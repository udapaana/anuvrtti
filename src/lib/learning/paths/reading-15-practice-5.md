## reading - Time Devours All

**Source:** Traditional Subhāṣita

@deva[कालः पचति भूतानि कालः संहरते प्रजाः]

Split:
- @deva[कालः पचति भूतानि] — no sandhi
- @deva[कालः संहरते प्रजाः] — no sandhi

Parse:
- @deva[कालः] — "Time" (nom.)
- @deva[पचति] — "cooks/ripens" (present)
- @deva[भूतानि] — "beings" (acc. pl. neut.)
- @deva[संहरते] — "gathers up" (middle voice)
- @deva[प्रजाः] — "creatures" (acc. pl.)

**Translation:** "Time cooks beings; Time gathers up creatures."

## reading - Time Never Sleeps

@deva[कालः सुप्तेषु जागर्ति कालो हि दुरतिक्रमः]

Split:
- @deva[कालो हि] = @deva[कालः हि] (visarga sandhi — we'll learn this)
- @deva[दुरतिक्रमः] = @deva[दुः + अतिक्रमः] (compound)

Parse:
- @deva[सुप्तेषु] — "while [beings are] asleep" (loc. pl.)
- @deva[जागर्ति] — "stays awake"
- @deva[हि] — "indeed"
- @deva[दुरतिक्रमः] — "hard to cross over"

**Translation:** "Time stays awake while [all] sleep; Time indeed is hard to overcome."

---

## concept - Locative Absolute

@deva[सुप्तेषु] is a **locative absolute** — "while X-ing":

@deva[सुप्तेषु जागर्ति] = "while [they are] sleeping, [Time] stays awake"

The locative can express simultaneous action.

## reading - Lift Yourself

**Source:** Bhagavad Gītā 6.5

@deva[उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत्]

Split:
- @deva[उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानम्] = @deva[उद्धरेत् आत्मना आत्मानम्] (त् → द् before a vowel; a + ā → ā)
- @deva[नात्मानमवसादयेत्] = @deva[न आत्मानम् अवसादयेत्] (a + ā → ā)

Parse:
- @deva[उद्धरेत्] — "one should lift up" (optative of उद् + हृ)
- @deva[आत्मना] — "by oneself" (instr.)
- @deva[आत्मानम्] — "oneself" (acc.)
- @deva[अवसादयेत्] — "one should cause to sink" (optative, causative of अव + सद्)

**Translation:** "One should lift oneself up by oneself; one should not let oneself sink."

## reading - Continued

@deva[आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः]

Split:
- @deva[आत्मैव] = @deva[आत्मा एव] (ā + e → ai)
- @deva[ह्यात्मनो] = @deva[हि आत्मनः] (i → y before a vowel; aḥ → o before a voiced sound)
- @deva[बन्धुरात्मैव] = @deva[बन्धुः आत्मा एव] (visarga → r before a vowel)
- @deva[रिपुरात्मनः] = @deva[रिपुः आत्मनः]

Parse:
- @deva[आत्मा] — "the self" (nom.)
- @deva[एव] — "alone, indeed"
- @deva[हि] — "for"
- @deva[आत्मनः] — "of oneself" (gen.)
- @deva[बन्धुः] — "friend"
- @deva[रिपुः] — "enemy"

**Translation:** "For the self alone is one's friend, the self alone one's enemy."

---

## concept - The Optative Mood

@deva[उद्धरेत्] "one should lift up" is the **optative** — expressing what ought to be done:

- @deva[गच्छेत्] — "one should go"
- @deva[वदेत्] — "one should speak"
- @deva[कुर्यात्] — "one should do"

We'll learn this fully later.
