---
key: rule:27
title: "Optional Transformation of @deva[उ] to @deva[व]"
pages: [021.png]
verdict: approved
---
The particle @deva[उ], preceded by a letter of the first five classes except @deva[ञ्] and followed by any vowel, is optionally changed to @deva[व्][^1]; @deva[किमु उक्तं], @deva[किम्वुक्तम्] and @deva[किमु उक्तं] (by @ref[26] c.).

---

[^1]: @deva[मय उञो वो वा] । Pāṇ. VIII. 3. 33.
