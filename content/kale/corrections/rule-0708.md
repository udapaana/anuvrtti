## (d) Past Active Participles.

Past Active participles are derived from the P. Passive Participles in @deva[त] or @deva[न] by adding to them the affix @deva[वत्]; e. g.

| Roots. | P. P. Ptc. | P. Ac. Ptc. | | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| @deva[भू] to be | @deva[भूत] | @deva[भूतवत्] | one or that | has been |
| @deva[कृ] to do | @deva[कृत] | @deva[कृतवत्] | ,, ,, | done |
| @deva[कॄ] to scatter | @deva[कीर्ण] | @deva[कीर्णवत्] | ,, ,, | scattered |
| @deva[छिद्] to cut | @deva[छिन्न] | @deva[छिन्नवत्] | ,, ,, | cut |
| etc. | etc. | etc. | | etc. |
