## GENDERS OF TATPURUSHA COMPOUNDS.

As a general rule a Tatpurusha compound follows the gender of the final noun.[^1]

**Exceptions:**[^2]

**(a)** Compounds with @deva[प्राप्त] and @deva[आपन्न] for their first member and the Prādi compounds follow the gender of the words they qualify; @deva[प्राप्तजीविकः नरः], @deva[प्राप्तजीविका स्त्री], @deva[निष्कौशाम्बिः पुरुषः] &c.

**(b)** A Tatpurusha ending in @deva[रात्र], @deva[अह्न] and @deva[अह] is *masculine*, except when @deva[रात्र] is preceded by a numeral and @deva[अह] by @deva[पुण्य] and @deva[सुदिन]; e. g. @deva[पूर्वरात्रः], @deva[मध्याह्नः], @deva[सप्ताहः], @deva[नवरात्रम्], @deva[गणरात्रम्], @deva[पुण्याहम्], @deva[सुदिनाहम्]; also that ending in @deva[पथ] (substituted for @deva[पथिन्], vide @ref[280]) preceded by a numeral or an indeclinable; as @deva[त्रयाणां पन्थाः त्रिपथम्], @deva[विरूपः पन्थाः विपथम्], a bad or a wrong way; but @deva[सुपन्थाः], @deva[अतिपन्थाः] as they do not end in @deva[पथ]; (see @ref[285])

**(c)**[^3] A collective Dwigu is *neu.*; but that ending in @deva[अ] is generally *feminine*, and that ending in @deva[आ] is optionally so in which case it takes the termination @deva[ई]; @deva[पञ्चगवं] a collection of five cows; @deva[त्रयाणां लोकानां समाहारः त्रिलोकी]; but @deva[पञ्चपात्रम्], @deva[त्रिभुवनम्], @deva[चतुर्युगम्] &c.; @deva[पञ्चानां खट्वानां समाहारः पञ्चखट्वी], @deva[पञ्चखट्वम्]. The @deva[न्] of final @deva[अन्] is dropped and the Dwigu is optionally *feminine* @deva[पञ्चतक्षी-क्षम्], (fr. @deva[पञ्च + तक्षन्] a carpenter).

**(d)**[^4] A Tatpurusha ending in @deva[उपज्ञा] and @deva[उपक्रम] is of the *neuter* gender, when the idea of @deva[प्राथम्य] 'being the first' of what is known or commenced (introduced) is meant to be expressed; @deva[पाणिनेरुपज्ञा पाणिन्युपज्ञं ग्रन्थः] the work (grammar) first taught or composed by Pāṇini. @deva[नन्दोपक्रमं द्रोणः] the *droṇa* (a measure of corn) first brought into use in the reign of King Nanda.

**(e)**[^5] A Tatpurusha ending in @deva[छाया] is *neuter* when the objects casting the shade are many; @deva[इक्षूणां इक्षुच्छायम्].

**(f)**[^6] A Tatpurusha having for its first member a synonym of @deva[राजन्] (and not @deva[राजन्] itself) or the words @deva[रक्षस्], @deva[पिशाच] &c. and @deva[सभा] for its latter member is *neuter*; as @deva[इनसभम्], @deva[ईश्वरसभम्], a king's council; but @deva[राजसभा]; @deva[रक्षःसभम्], @deva[पिशाचसभम्]; also that ending in @deva[सभा] when it means 'a multitude'; as @deva[स्त्रीसभम्] 'an assembly of women'; but @deva[धर्मसभा] in the sense of @deva[धर्मशाला].

**(g)**[^7] A Tatpurusha ending in @deva[सेना], @deva[सुरा], @deva[छाया], @deva[शाला] and @deva[निशा] is optionally *neuter*; @deva[ब्राह्मणसेना-नम्], @deva[यवसुरा-रम्] malt liquor, beer, @deva[कुड्यच्छाया-यम्], the shadow of a wall; @deva[गोशाला-लम्], @deva[श्वनिशा-शम्] (the 14th day of the dark half of a month, so called because certain dogs observe a fast on this night, according to the Sābarabhāṣya).

**N. B.** These rules hold good in the case of Tat. alone; so @deva[दृढसेनो राजा] (Bah.); @deva[असेना] (Neg. Tat.), @deva[परमसेना] (Karm.).
