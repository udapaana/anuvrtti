title: "Past Passive Participles of @deva[मन्], @deva[बुध], and @deva[पूज्] in Present Sense"
pages: [510.png]
verdict: approved
---
The past passive participles of the roots @deva[मन्], @deva[बुध] and @deva[पूज्] and their synonyms are used in the sense of the Present tense and are construed with the Genitive. (See @ref[857].)

For further particulars vide @ref[705]—@ref[707].
