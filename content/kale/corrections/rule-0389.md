---
key: rule:389
title: "Fourth Class (@deva[दिवादि]): Addition of @deva[य] (@deva[श्यन्])"
pages: [242.png, 243.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[य] (@deva[श्यन्]) is added on to the final of a root of the Fourth or @deva[दिवादि] class before the terminations; the radical vowel remains unchanged; e. g. @deva[कुप्+ति=कुप्+य+ति=कुप्यति].
