(a) @deva[भू] when it has a Subanta prefixed to it and is without a preposition takes the affix @deva[क्यप्] impersonally; @deva[ब्रह्मणो भावः] @deva[ब्रह्मभूयं] 'identity with Brahman.' When no Subanta is used prepositionally with it, it takes the affix @deva[यत्]; @deva[भव्य] or @deva[प्रभव्य].

(a) @deva[वद्] under similar circumstances takes either @deva[क्यप्] or @deva[ण्यत्] in a passive sense or impersonally; @deva[ब्रह्मोद्यं] or @deva[ब्रह्मवद्यं] 'expounding the Veda' (@deva[ब्रह्म वेदः तस्य वदनं] Sid. Kau.).
