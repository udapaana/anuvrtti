## IMPERFECT, PERFECT AND AORIST.

In Sanskrit there are three tenses denoting a past action, viz. the Imperfect, the Perfect, and the Aorist. Originally each of these three tenses had a signification of its own and was used in its proper sense in ancient writings.[^1] After Sanskrit ceased to be a spoken language the exact senses of these tenses were lost sight of and writers began to use them promiscuously, so that now any of these may be used to denote past time with certain limitations.
