@deva[मृज्] also takes this affix optionally; @deva[मृज्य] 'what is fit or ought to be cleansed;' optionally it takes @deva[ण्यत्] before which the final @deva[ज्] is changed to @deva[ग्]; @deva[मार्ग्य].
