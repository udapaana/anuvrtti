title: "Special Rules for @deva[अञ्च्], @deva[धृष्] and @deva[शस्]"
pages: [423.png]
verdict: approved
---
(a) @deva[अञ्च्] in the sense of 'to worship' takes @deva[इ]; @deva[अञ्चित] 'worshipped;' but @deva[अक्तः] 'gone'; with @deva[सम्]-@deva[समक्त] (@deva[समक्तौ शकुने पादौ])

(b) @deva[धृष्] and @deva[शस्] reject @deva[इ] when they express the idea of 'immodesty or rudeness;' @deva[धृष्ट] 'rude,' @deva[विशस्तः] 'ill-mannered;' but @deva[धर्षित] 'overpowered, trifled with,' &c, @deva[विशासित] 'tormented or ill-treated'.
