[^1] A *prâtipadika* ending in short @deva[ऋ] or @deva[न्] forms its *feminine* by the addition of @deva[ई]; @deva[कर्तृ-कर्त्री], @deva[दण्डिन्-दण्डिनी], @deva[शुनी], @deva[राज्ञी], @deva[परिदिवन्-परिदिव्नी]; &c.

*N. B.* Words like @deva[स्वसृ], @deva[ननान्दृ], @deva[तिसृ], @deva[दुहितृ], @deva[स्वसृ] &c. being themselves *feminine* do not take this termination.

**(a)** [^2] @deva[युवन्] forms its *feminine* by the addition of @deva[ति] before which the final @deva[न्] is dropped; @deva[युवतिः]

---

[^1]: @deva[ऋन्नेभ्यो ङीप्] । Pân. IV. 1. 5.

[^2]: @deva[यूनस्तिः] ।
