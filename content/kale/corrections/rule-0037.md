title: "Anusvāra to Class Nasal Conversion"
pages: [025.png]
verdict: approved
doubts:

---
An Anusvāra followed by any consonant except @deva[श्], @deva[ष्], @deva[स्] or @deva[ह्] is changed to the nasal of the class to which the following letter belongs, necessarily when in the middle, and optionally when at the end of a word[^1]; e.g. @deva[अन्क्] + @deva[इतः] = @deva[अं] + @deva[क्] + @deva[इतः] (by the preceding rule and then) @deva[अङ्कितः] marked (by this); so @deva[अन्च्] + @deva[इतः] = @deva[अञ्चितः] honoured; @deva[कुण्ठित] obstructed; @deva[शान्तः] calmed; @deva[गुम्फितः] woven; @deva[त्वम्] + @deva[करोषि] = @deva[त्वं करोषि] or @deva[त्वङ्करोषि] thou doest; similarly @deva[संयन्ता] or @deva[सय्ँयन्ता] a restrainer; @deva[संवत्सरः] or @deva[सँव्वत्सरः] a year; @deva[यं लोकम्] or @deva[यल्ँलोकम्] to which people.

---

[^1]: @deva[अनुस्वारस्य ययि परसवर्णः । वा पदान्तस्य ।] Pāṇ. VIII. 4. 48-52.
