## Vowel Changes in Causals

Roots ending in @deva[अम्], except @deva[अम्] 'to go' &c., @deva[कम्] 'to love,' @deva[चम्] 'to eat,' @deva[शम्] when it means 'to see' and @deva[यम्] when it does not mean 'to eat,' and the roots[^1] marked with an indicatory @deva[म्] do not change their vowel; i. e. their vowel takes its Guṇa substitute; @deva[गम्]—@deva[गमयति], @deva[क्रम्]—@deva[क्रमयति], @deva[घट्]—@deva[घटयति], @deva[जन्]—@deva[जनयति], @deva[व्यथ्]—@deva[व्यथयति]; @deva[जॄ]—@deva[जरयति]; @deva[श्रा][^2]—@deva[श्रपयति], @deva[जा][^2]—@deva[जपयति], &c. but @deva[कम्]—@deva[कामयते]; @deva[चम्]—@deva[चामयति], @deva[शम्] 'to see' @deva[शामयति]; but @deva[शमयति] in other cases; @deva[यम्]—@deva[यामयति], &c.; but @deva[यम्] 'to eat'—@deva[यमयति].

(a) The roots @deva[वम्], @deva[नम्], @deva[वन्], @deva[ज्वल्], @deva[ह्वल्], and @deva[ह्मल्], lengthen their vowel optionally when not preceded by a preposition; @deva[नमयति], @deva[नामयति], but @deva[प्रणमयति], &c.
