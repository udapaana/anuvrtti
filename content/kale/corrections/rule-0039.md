If @deva[ड्] or @deva[न्] at the end of a word be followed by @deva[स्], @deva[ध्] (changed to @deva[त्]) is optionally inserted between them;[^1] e. g. @deva[षड्] + @deva[सन्तः] = @deva[षड्सन्तः] or @deva[षड्] + @deva[ध्] + @deva[सन्तः] = @deva[षड्त्सन्तः] six good men; so @deva[सन्] + @deva[सः] = @deva[सन्सः] or @deva[सन्त्सः] that good man.

**(a)** @deva[ङ्], @deva[ण्] and @deva[न्] at the end of a word and preceded by a short vowel double themselves when followed by a vowel;[^2] e. g. @deva[प्रत्यङ्] + @deva[आत्मा] = @deva[प्रत्यङ्ङात्मा] the individual soul; so @deva[सुगण्णीशः] the best reckoner; @deva[सन्नच्युतः] good Achyuta.

---

[^1]: @deva[डः सि धुट्। नश्च] — Pāṇ. VIII. 3. 22, 32.

[^2]: @deva[ङमो ह्रस्वादचि ङमुण्नित्यम्] — Pāṇ. VIII. 3. 32.
