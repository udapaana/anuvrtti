## Monosyllabic Roots with Penultimate @deva[अ]

Monosyllabic roots, having a short @deva[अ] for their penultimate, change it to @deva[ए] and drop the reduplicative syllable before the weak terminations and before @deva[थ] when it takes @deva[इ], provided their initial letter undergoes no change in the reduplication, e. g. @deva[पत्] 1st dual @deva[पपत्]+@deva[इव]=@deva[पेत्]+@deva[इव]=@deva[पेतिव], @deva[पेतिथ] 2 sing, @deva[शेकिथ], @deva[शशक्थ] 2. sing. of @deva[शक्], but @deva[ननन्दिथ] 2nd dual of @deva[नन्द्] as the @deva[अ] here is prosodially long.

Exceptions:—Roots beginning with @deva[व] and the roots @deva[शस्] and @deva[दद्].
