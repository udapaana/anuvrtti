( *a* ) The @deva[म] of the termination @deva[मत्] is changed to @deva[व] when affixed to words ending in @deva[म्] or @deva[अ], short or long, or having either for their penultimate;[^1] @deva[किंवत्] from @deva[किम्], @deva[विद्यावत्], @deva[लक्ष्मीवत्], @deva[यशस्वत्], @deva[भास्वत्], &c.;[^2] @deva[राजन्+वत्=राजन्वत्]; as @deva[राजन्वान् देशः] 'a country having a good or just king'; ( *cf.* Rag. VI. 22. )=@deva[राजवत्]; as @deva[राजवान् देशः] 'a country governed by a king'; @deva[उदक+वत्=उदन्वत्] *m.* 'the sea'; @deva[उदकवत्] as @deva[उदकवान्] ( @deva[घटः] 'a jar' ) containing water.

Exceptions:—@deva[म] is not changed to @deva[व] in the case of the words @deva[यव], @deva[दल्मि], @deva[ऊर्मि], @deva[भूमि], @deva[कृमि], @deva[क्रुञ्चा], @deva[वशा], @deva[द्राक्षा], @deva[भ्रजि], @deva[व्रजि], @deva[ध्वजि], @deva[नजि], @deva[हरित्], @deva[ककुद्], @deva[गरुत्], @deva[इक्षु], @deva[द्रु] and @deva[मधु], as @deva[यवमान्], @deva[ऊर्मिमान्], &c.

( *b* )[^3] @deva[मत्] also becomes @deva[वत्] when added to words ending in any of the first four letters of a class; @deva[विद्युत्वान्], @deva[तडित्वान्] *m.* 'possessing lightning' *i. e.* 'a cloud', &c. The @deva[त्] of @deva[विद्युत्] &c. is not changed to @deva[द्] as it is not at the end of a @deva[पद्].

( *c* ) and when the whole expresses a @deva[संज्ञा] ( a name ); @deva[अहीवती], @deva[मुनीवती] &c.

[^1]: @deva[मादुपधायाश्च मतोर्वोऽयवादिभ्यः] । Pâṇ. VIII. 2. 9.

[^2]: @deva[राजन्वान्सौराज्ये] । Pâṇ. VIII. 2. 14. @deva[राजवानन्यत्र] । Sid. Kau.

[^3]: @deva[झयः] । @deva[संज्ञायाम्] । Pâṇ. VIII. 2. 10. 11.
