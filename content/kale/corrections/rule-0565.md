## Roots Taking the Fourth Form

(a) Aniṭ roots not belonging to any of the preceding Varieties take this Form. Aniṭ roots that optionally take any of the preceding Varieties and Veṭ roots optionally belong to this Variety.

### Exceptions:

(1) @deva[स्तु] and @deva[सु], when Parasmaipadi, belong to the Fifth Variety.

(2) Roots ending in @deva[ऋ] preceded by a conjunct consonant may take Fourth or Fifth Form in the Ātmanepada.
