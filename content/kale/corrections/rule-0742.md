## (3) By means of @deva[केलिमर्] (@deva[एलिम]).

A few transitive roots take the affix @deva[एलिम] having the same force as the affix @deva[य]; @deva[पचेलिम] fr. @deva[पच्], 'fit to ripen or to be cooked; as @deva[पचेलिमा माषाः]; @deva[भिदेलिमाः] (fr. @deva[भिद्]) @deva[सरलाः] 'the pine trees ought to be felled;' &c.
