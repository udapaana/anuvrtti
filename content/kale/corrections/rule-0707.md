title: "Past Passive Participles with Present Tense Sense"
pages: [429.png]
verdict: approved
---
The P. P. Participles of roots meaning 'to think or to wish,' 'to know,' 'to adore,' and of the roots @deva[इन्ध्], @deva[भी] etc. have the sense of the present Tense; @deva[राज्ञः मतः] 'he is honoured by the king,' @deva[सतां पूजितः इद्धः अग्निः] 'the fire is kindled,' so @deva[भीतः] etc
