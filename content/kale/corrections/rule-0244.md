---
key: rule:244
title: "Exceptions for Negative Tatpurusha"
pages: [148.png]
verdict: approved
---
All the rules given above for the change of the finals of the Tat. compounds do not hold good in the case of the Neg. Tat.;[^1] e. g. @deva[न राजा अराजा] one not a king; @deva[न सखा असखा], &c.

**(a)** But in the case of the word @deva[पथिन्] the final @deva[इन्] is optionally dropped and @deva[अ] added; @deva[अपथं] or @deva[अपंथाः] 'absence of a road'. @deva[पथिन्] when so modified in a Tat. is neu.;[^2] (but @deva[अपथः देशः]).

---

[^1]: @deva[नञस्तत्पुरुषात् । पथो विभाषा ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 71, 72.

[^2]: @deva[अपथं नपुंसकम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 4. 3.
