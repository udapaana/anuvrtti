---
key: rule:392
title: "Lengthening of @deva[अ] and Elision Before Terminations"
pages: [243.png]
verdict: approved
---
(a)[^1] The preceding @deva[अ] is lengthened before a conjugational termination beginning with a semivowel, a nasal or @deva[झ्] or @deva[भ्]; e. g. @deva[नयामि] &c.

(b) The final @deva[अ] is dropped before terminations beginning with @deva[अ]; @deva[नय+अन्ति=नयन्ति], &c.
