## Dative with @deva[राध्] and @deva[ईक्ष्] (Fortune Inquiry)

The roots @deva[राध्][^1] and @deva[ईक्ष्] both meaning 'to determine the good or bad fortune of,' govern the Dative of the person with reference to whom the inquiry is made; @deva[कृष्णाय राध्यति ईक्षते वा । पृष्टो गर्गः शुभाशुभं पर्यालोचयतीत्यर्थः ।] Sid. Kau.

---

[^1]: @deva[राधीक्ष्योर्यस्य विप्रश्नः ।] Pāṇ. I. 4. 39.
