## Optional First Aorist for @deva[घ्रा], @deva[धे], @deva[शो], @deva[सो], and @deva[छो]

The roots @deva[घ्रा], @deva[धे], @deva[शो], @deva[सो], and @deva[छो] belong to this Variety optionally. They optionally take the Sixth Form. @deva[धे] takes the Third Form also.

Paradigms.

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | @deva[अस्थाम्] | @deva[अस्थाव] | @deva[अस्थाम] |
| 2. | @deva[अस्थाः] | @deva[अस्थातम्] | @deva[अस्थात] |
| 3. | @deva[अस्थात्] | @deva[अस्थाताम्] | @deva[अस्थुः] |
