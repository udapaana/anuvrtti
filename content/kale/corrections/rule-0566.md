## Vowel Changes in Fourth Aorist

(a) In the Parasmaipada the radical vowel takes its Vṛddhi substitute; @deva[नी]-@deva[अनैषीत्]; @deva[कृ]-@deva[अकार्षीत्]; @deva[भञ्ज्]-@deva[अभांक्षीत्], &c.

(b) In the Ātmanepada, Guṇa is substituted for final @deva[इ] or @deva[उ], short or long; final @deva[ऋ] and the penultimate vowel remain unchanged; final @deva[ऋ] is changed to @deva[ईर्] or @deva[ऊर्] in accordance with @ref[394]; @deva[चि]-@deva[अचेष्ट], @deva[नी]-@deva[अनेष्ट], @deva[च्यु]-@deva[अच्योष्ट], @deva[सू]-@deva[असोष्ट], @deva[कृ] see below, @deva[भिद्]-@deva[अभित्त], @deva[स्तृ]-@deva[अस्तीर्ष्ट], @deva[वृ]-@deva[अवृष्ट].

(c) The Penultimate @deva[ऋ] of Aniṭ roots is optionally changed to @deva[र]; @deva[कृष्]-@deva[अकार्क्षीत्] or @deva[अक्राक्षीत्].
