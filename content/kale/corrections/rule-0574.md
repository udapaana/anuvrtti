## Vowel Changes in Fifth Aorist

(a) In the Parasmaipada, the final radical vowel and the penultimate @deva[अ] of roots ending in @deva[र्] or @deva[ल्] and that of @deva[वद्] and @deva[व्रज्] take their Vṛddhi substitute necessarily; @deva[लू]-@deva[अलाविषम्]; @deva[चर्]—@deva[अचारिषम्]; @deva[फल्]-@deva[अफालिषम्], &c.

(b) The penultimate short vowel of a root takes its Guṇa substitute; @deva[बुध्]-@deva[अबोधिषम्], &c.

(c) Vṛddhi is optionally substituted for the penultimate @deva[अ], not prosodially long, of roots having an initial consonant and not ending in @deva[र्] or @deva[ल्]; @deva[पठ्]—@deva[अपठिषम्], @deva[अपाठिषम्]; @deva[गद्]—@deva[अगदिषम्]—@deva[अगादिषम्].

(d) But the vowel of roots ending in @deva[ह्], @deva[म्], @deva[य्], and of the roots @deva[क्षण्], @deva[श्वस्], @deva[जागृ], @deva[श्वि], @deva[कट्] 'to cover, to surround,' @deva[चट्], 'to break, to pierce,' @deva[चत्], @deva[चद्], 'to ask, to beg,' @deva[पथ्] 'to go or move,' @deva[मथ्] 'to churn,' @deva[लग्] 'to stick or cling to,' @deva[हस्] and @deva[ह्लस्] 'to sound or to be diminished,' does not take its Vṛddhi substitute.

(e) In the Ātmanepada the radical vowel takes its Guṇa substitute; @deva[लू]—@deva[अलविष्ट].
