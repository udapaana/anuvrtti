---
key: rule:694
title: "Roots Optionally Taking @deva[न] or @deva[त]"
pages: [425.png]
verdict: approved
---
The roots @deva[नुद्], @deva[विद्], 6. A., @deva[उन्द्], @deva[त्रै], @deva[घ्रा] and @deva[ह्री] take @deva[न] or @deva[त]; @deva[नुन्न] or @deva[नुत्त], @deva[विन्न] or @deva[वित्त], @deva[त्राण] or @deva[त्रात], @deva[घ्राण] or @deva[घ्रात] and @deva[ह्रीण] or @deva[ह्रीत].
