§ 370. Several nouns, substantive and adjective, may be prefixed to the roots @deva[कृ], @deva[भू], and @deva[अस्] to form what are called in Sanskrit 'chvi-derivatives' (vide chap. IX. Sec. III.) @deva[कृष्णकरणम्] = @deva[कृष्णीकरणम्], @deva[घन] = @deva[भूत] = @deva[घनीभूतः]. Such nouns are also termed 'Gati.'
