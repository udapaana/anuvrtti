## Locative with Verbs of Love and Attachment

Verbs having the sense of 'love, regard for, attachment to' such as @deva[स्निह्], @deva[अनुरञ्ज्], @deva[अभिलष्], @deva[रम्], &c. and their derivatives generally govern the Locative; @deva[पिता पुत्रे स्निह्यति] a father loves his son; @deva[अस्ति मे सोदरस्नेहोपि एतेषु] (S'ak. I) I have a sisterly affection for them also; @deva[न खलु तापसकन्यकायां ममाभिलाषः] I do not, indeed, love the ascetic's daughter, @deva[अशुद्धप्रकृतौ राज्ञि जनता नानुरज्यते] people do not love a king whose ministers are corrupt; @deva[भ्रातुर्मृतस्य भार्यायां योनुरज्येत कामतः] (Manu. S. III. 179). @deva[रहसि रमते] (Māl. Mād. III. 2.) takes pleasure in solitude; @deva[रतः श्रेयसि] (Bhatt. I.) devoted to his welfare.

*Note*:—@deva[अनुरञ्ज्] and @deva[अभिलष्] are sometimes used with the Accusative also; @deva[समस्थमनुरज्यन्ति] (Rāma.); @deva[मानुषानभिलषन्ती] (Bhatt. IV. 22.)
