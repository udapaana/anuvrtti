---
key: rule:33
title: "Optional Aspiration of ह् After a Class Letter"
pages: [024.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[ह्] coming after any of the first four letters of a class is optionally changed to the soft aspirate (4th letter) of that class[^1]; @deva[वाक्] + @deva[हरिः] = @deva[वाग्हरिः] (see b. under @ref[22]), and by this @deva[वाग्घरिः] (@deva[वाचां हरिः]) the possessor of speech, Bṛhaspati.

---

[^1]: @deva[झयो होऽन्यतरस्याम्] । Pāṇ. VIII. 4. 62.
