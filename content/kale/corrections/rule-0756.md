@deva[वे], @deva[ज्या] and @deva[व्ये] do not take Samprasāraṇa; @deva[प्रवाय]; @deva[प्रज्याय] 'having become old;' @deva[उपव्याय] 'having covered;' but @deva[व्ये] with @deva[परि] or @deva[सम्], takes Samprasāraṇa optionally; @deva[परिव्याय] or @deva[परिवीय]; @deva[संव्याय] or @deva[संवीय].
