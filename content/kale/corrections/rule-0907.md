---
key: rule:907
title: "Impersonal @deva[भवितव्य] and @deva[भाव्य] for Probability"
pages: [512.png]
verdict: approved
---
The *neuter* forms @deva[भवितव्यं] and @deva[भाव्यं] are used impersonally in the sense of 'being,' or 'what must be or in all probability is,' the noun denoting the agent being put in the Instrumental case: @deva[अत्र केनापि कारणेन भवितव्यं] there must be some cause; @deva[अस्य शब्दानुरूपेण पराक्रमेण भवितव्यं] or @deva[भाव्यम्] in all probability his strength must be corresponding to his sound; @deva[आर्यया प्रवाहणमारूढया भवितव्यम्] the lady must (in all likelihood) be seated in the carriage, &c.
