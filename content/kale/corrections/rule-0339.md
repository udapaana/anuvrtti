title: "General Rules for Taddhita Affixes"
pages: [194.png, 195.png]
verdict: approved
---
The Taddhita affixes are added in various senses. They occasion various changes in the words to which they are added. The following general observations should be paid attention to.

**(a)** As a general rule the first vowel of a word takes its Vriddhi substitute before the terminations @deva[अ], @deva[य], @deva[इक], @deva[ईन], @deva[एय], @deva[त्य]. &c.; as @deva[अश्वपति + अ = आश्वपति + अ].

**(b)** Before terminations beginning with a vowel or @deva[य] (1) the final @deva[अ], @deva[आ], @deva[इ], and @deva[ई] are rejected; (2) @deva[उ] and @deva[ऊ] take their Guṇa substitute; (3) @deva[ओ] and @deva[औ] obey the ordinary rules of sandhi; @deva[आश्वपति + अ = आश्वपत] 'belonging to Asvapati,' &c. *m. n.*; @deva[मनु + अ = मानवः] 'a descendant of Manu'; @deva[गो + य = गव्यं] 'belonging to a cow', so @deva[नाव्यं] fr. @deva[नौ] &c.

**(c)** In the case of derivatives from compound words sometimes the initial vowel of the second word takes its Vriddhi substitute, and sometimes the Vriddhi is double; @deva[पूर्ववार्षिक] 'belonging to the last year'; similarly @deva[सुपांचालकः] &c. @deva[सौहार्दं] fr. @deva[सुहृद्], @deva[सौभाग्य] from @deva[सुभग], &c. When these terminations are added to a Dwa. comp. both the words of which are the names of deities, Vriddhi is substituted for the initial vowel of both; @deva[आग्निमारुतं कर्म] 'a sacrifice offered to Agni and the Marut deities,' &c.

**(d)** If the initial vowel of a word be preceded by the @deva[य्] and @deva[व्] of a preposition the @deva[य्] or @deva[व्] is first changed to @deva[इय्] or @deva[उव्] before Vriddhi substitute can take place; as @deva[व्याकरण+अ=वियाकरण+अ=वैयाकरणः]; @deva[स्वश्व+अ=सुवश्व+अ=सौवश्व]; similarly @deva[सौवस्तिक] from @deva[स्वस्ति], @deva[सौवर] fr. @deva[स्वर], &c.

**(e)** Before consonantal Tad. affixes, a final @deva[न्] is generally rejected; the final @deva[न्] with the preceding vowel is sometimes dropped before vowel terminations and before such as begin with @deva[य]; @deva[युवन्-युवत्वं], @deva[राजन्-राजकं], &c. @deva[आत्मन्-आत्म्य-आत्मीय]. There are various exceptions to this latter part of the rule; *e. g.* @deva[राजन्य] fr @deva[राजन्] &c.

*N. B.*—Other changes the student will easily note from the instances given.
