[^1]A Dwandwa compound of words denoting limbs of the body of an animal, players on musical instruments, parts of an army, inanimate objects (things or @deva[द्रव्य]s and not qualities) names of rivers differing in gender, as also of countries (and not villages), and insects, and animals between which there is natural antipathy, is always of this nature; @deva[पाणी च पादौ च पाणिपादम्] hands and feet; @deva[रथिकाश्च अश्वारोहाश्च रथिकाश्वारोहम्] chariot-warriors and horse-men @deva[मार्दंगिकाश्च पाणविकाश्च मार्दंगिकपाणविकम्] players on mridanga and panava (kinds of tabors); @deva[धानाश्च] fried barley or rice @deva[शष्कुल्यश्च] a kind of sweetmeat @deva[धानाशष्कुलि]; but @deva[रूपं च रसश्च रूपरसौ] form and flavour, as these denote qualities; @deva[गंगा च शोणश्च गंगाशोणम्] the rivers Ganges and Sona; but @deva[गंगायमुने] as these do not differ in gender; @deva[कुरवश्च कुरुक्षेत्रं च कुरुकुरुक्षेत्रम्] names of countries; but @deva[जाम्बवशालूकिन्यौ] (two villages of that name); @deva[मद्राश्च केकयाश्च मद्रकेकयः] (two names of the same gender); @deva[यूका] a louse @deva[च लिक्षा] a nit @deva[च यूकालिक्षम्]; @deva[अहिश्च नकुलश्च अहिनकुलम्] a snake and an ichneumon, &c.

[^1]: @deva[द्वन्द्वश्च प्राणितूर्यसेनाङ्गानाम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 4. 2, @deva[जातिरप्राणिनाम्] Pāṇ. II. 4. 6.; @deva[विशिष्टलिङ्गो नदीदेशोऽग्रामाः ।] Pāṇ. II. 4. 7., @deva[क्षुद्रजन्तवः] Pāṇ. II. 4. 8. @deva[येषां च विरोधः शाश्वतिकः] Pāṇ. II. 4. 9.
