---
key: rule:321
title: "Lengthening to @deva[ऊ] for @deva[उ]-Ending Words"
pages: [188.png, 189.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "(a) भद्रबाहुः printed with short ु although the rule gives ऊ: probable misprint for भद्रबाहूः."
  - "(b) \"take ई\" as printed though the forms end in ऊ."
---
[^1] A *prâtipadika* ending in @deva[उ] not preceded by @deva[य] and denoting mankind forms its *feminine* in @deva[ऊः]: *e. g.* @deva[कुरूः] 'a woman of the Kuru country'; but @deva[अध्वर्युः] 'the wife of an Adhvaryu'; also when it does not denote animal-kind[^2]; as @deva[अलाबूः], @deva[कर्कन्धूः]; except in the case of @deva[रज्जु] and @deva[हनु]; @deva[रज्जुः], @deva[हनुः].

**(a)** *Obs.* [^3] A *prâtipadika* ending in @deva[बाहु] and used as a proper name, and the word @deva[पङ्गूः] also form their *feminine* in @deva[ऊ]; as @deva[भद्रबाहुः] 'a woman of that name'; but @deva[वृत्तबाहुः] 'a woman having well-rounded arms'; @deva[पङ्गूः] ।

**(b)** @deva[कद्रु] and @deva[कमण्डलु] if used as proper names take @deva[ई]; @deva[कद्रूः] 'a woman of that name,' @deva[कमण्डलूः]; but @deva[कमण्डलुः], @deva[कद्रुः] in other cases.

[^1]: @deva[ऊङुतः] Pân. IV. 1. 66. @deva[उकारान्तादयोपधान्मनुष्यजातिवाचिनः स्त्रियामूङ्स्यात्] । Sid. Kau.

[^2]: @deva[अप्राणिजातेश्चारज्ज्वादीनामुपसंख्यानम्] । Pân. Vârtika.

[^3]: @deva[बाह्वन्तात्संज्ञायाम्] । Pân. IV. 1. 67. @deva[पङ्गोश्च] । Pân. IV. 1. 68. @deva[संज्ञायाम्] । Pân. IV. 1. 72.
