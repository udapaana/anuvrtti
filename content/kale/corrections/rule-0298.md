A certain number of compounds and other words of irregular formation are classed under the head of @deva[पृषोदर].[^1] Any word whose formation cannot be properly explained is included into this group. These are to be taken as found in the language. The principal of these are:—@deva[पृषतः उदरं पृषोदरम्] 'wind, air'; @deva[हन्ति गच्छतीति हसतीति वा हंसः][^2] (either from @deva[हन्] or @deva[हस्]); @deva[हिनस्तीति सिंहः] (fr. @deva[हिंस्] con. 7 to kill), @deva[गूढश्चासौ आत्मा गूढोत्मा] the soul that is concealed from the external senses; @deva[वारिणां वाहकाः बलाहकाः] 'clouds'; @deva[जीवनस्य मूतः] (a bag) @deva[जीमूतः] 'a cloud'; @deva[श्मानः] (dead bodies) @deva[शेरते अत्र श्मशानं] or @deva[शवानां शयनं] (Kās.); @deva[ऊर्ध्वं च तत् खं च ऊर्ध्वखं तत् लातीति उलूखलम्], 'a wooden mortar'; @deva[पिशितमाचामतीति पिशाचः], @deva[ब्रुवन्तोस्यां सीदन्तीति बृसी] 'the seat of ascetics or holy sages discussing philosophical subjects'; @deva[मयते असौ, मह्यां रौतीति वा, मयूरः] a peacock.

**(a)** @deva[तीर] becomes @deva[तार] optionally when compounded with the names of directions;[^3] as @deva[दक्षिणतीरम्] or @deva[दक्षिणतारम्]; @deva[उत्तरतीरम्-तारम्], &c.

**(b)** **Obs.**—@deva[दुर्] becomes @deva[दू] in the following cases—[^4]@deva[दुःखेन दाश्यते दूडाशः] 'difficult to be given or hurt'; @deva[दुःखेन नाश्यते दूणाशः] not easy to be destroyed; @deva[दुःखेन दभ्यते दूडभः] 'difficult to be injured or impelled;' @deva[दुःखेन ध्यायतीति दूढ्यः] &c.
