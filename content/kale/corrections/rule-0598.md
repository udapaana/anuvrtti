## Passive Forms of Tenth Class Roots

Roots of the tenth Class—

(a) The @deva[अय्] (i. e. @deva[अय] with the final @deva[अ] dropped) is optionally dropped in the General Tenses, except the Perfect. The Aorist forms, except that of the 3rd sing., are made up by adding the terminations of the Fifth Form; @deva[चुर्] 1 sing. @deva[चोरयांचक्रे]—@deva[बभूवे], @deva[चोरयामासे] Perf.; @deva[चोरयिताहे] @deva[चोरिताहे], 1st Fu. @deva[चोरयिष्ये], @deva[चोरिष्ये] 2nd Fu.; @deva[अचोरयिषि], @deva[अचोरिषि], Aor. @deva[चोरयिषीय], @deva[चोरिषीय] Ben.

(b) Roots which do not lengthen their penultimate @deva[अ] (see also @ref[603]) lengthen it optionally in the general Tenses of the Passive, except in Perf., when @deva[अय्] is dropped; @deva[कथ्]—@deva[अकथयिषि], @deva[अकाथिषि]; Aor. 1 sing., &c.

(c) The 3 per. sing. of the Pass. Aorist is formed by dropping @deva[अय्] necessarily and adding @deva[इ]; @deva[चोरय]—@deva[अचोर्]—@deva[अचोरि]; @deva[पीड्]—@deva[अपीडि]; @deva[पृ]—@deva[अपारि], &c.; @deva[रह्]—@deva[अरहि], @deva[अराहि] Aor. 3 sing.; @deva[रम्]—@deva[अरमि], @deva[अरामि] Aorist, 3rd sing. &c.
