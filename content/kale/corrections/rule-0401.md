Some roots of the 10th class exclusively take the Ātmanepada i. e. even when the fruit of the action expressed by them does not accrue to the agent. These are:—@deva[अर्थ्] to request, to desire, @deva[कुह्] to astonish, to deceive, @deva[चित्] to be conscious of, to think, @deva[दंश्] to bite, @deva[दंस्] (or @deva[दस्] according to some) to see, to bite, @deva[डप्] or @deva[डिप्] to accumulate, @deva[तन्त्र्] to support a family, @deva[मन्त्र्] to counsel secretly, @deva[मृग्] to search, to hunt, to seek, @deva[स्पृश्] to take, to string together, @deva[तर्ज्] and @deva[भर्त्स्] to reprove, @deva[बस्त्] and @deva[गन्ध्] to injure, to hurt, @deva[विष्क्] to kill (@deva[हिष्क्] according to some), @deva[निष्क्] to measure, @deva[लल्] to desire, @deva[कण्] to contract, @deva[तुण्] to fill, @deva[भ्रूण्] to fear, @deva[शल्] to praise, @deva[यक्ष्] to worship, @deva[स्यम्] to guess, @deva[गुर्] to strike, @deva[सम्] to look at, to inspect, @deva[कुत्स्] to reproach, @deva[त्रुट्] (according to some @deva[कुट्]) to cut, @deva[गल्] to drop down, @deva[भल्] to see, to expound, @deva[कूट्] to abstain from giving, to muddle, @deva[कुट्] to cut, @deva[वञ्च्] to deceive, @deva[वृष्] to have the power of generation, to be eminent, @deva[मुद्] to gratify, @deva[दिन्] to bewail, @deva[गृ] to know, @deva[विद्] to know, to be conscious of, @deva[मन्] to stop, @deva[यु] to censure, and @deva[कुस्म्] to smile improperly.
