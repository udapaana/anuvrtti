## Ablative with @deva[परा]+@deva[जि] (Disgust)

In the case of @deva[जि] with @deva[परा][^1] the thing unbearable is put in the Ablative case; @deva[अध्ययनात्पराजयते] finds study unbearable or difficult; @deva[तां पराजयमानां स प्रीतेः] (Bhatti. VIII. 71.) who got disgusted with the love (of Rāvaṇa); but @deva[शत्रून् पराजयते ।]

---

[^1]: @deva[पराजेरसोढः ।] Pāṇ. I. 4. 26.
