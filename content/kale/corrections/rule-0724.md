According to Vāmana, the roots @deva[शंस्], @deva[दुह्] and @deva[गुह्] take this affix optionally; @deva[शंस्]—@deva[शस्य] 'praiseworthy;' @deva[दुह्]—@deva[दुह्य], @deva[गुह्]—@deva[गुह्य]; optionally they take the affix @deva[ण्यत्] (to be given hereafter); @deva[शंस्य], @deva[दोह्य], @deva[गोह्य].
