Before these the final @deva[ई] and @deva[ऊ] of a prátipadika are optionally shortened, e. g. @deva[श्रीतरा] or @deva[श्रितरा], @deva[श्रीतमा] or @deva[श्रितमा]; @deva[घेमूतरा] (more lame) or @deva[घेमुतरा], @deva[घेमूतमा], @deva[घेमुतमा], &c.
