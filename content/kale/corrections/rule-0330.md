[^1] Of Bah. compounds having for their last member one of the words @deva[नासिका], @deva[उदर], @deva[ओष्ठ], @deva[जङ्घा], @deva[दन्त], @deva[कर्ण], @deva[शृङ्ग], @deva[अङ्ग], @deva[गात्र], @deva[कण्ठ] and @deva[पुच्छ] the *fem.* is formed either in @deva[आ] or @deva[ई]; @deva[तुङ्गनासिका-की]; @deva[कृशोदरा-री] 'a woman having a slender waist;' @deva[बिम्बोष्ठी-ष्ठा] 'with a lip as red as the *bimba* fruit,' @deva[दीर्घे जङ्घे यस्याः सा दीर्घजङ्घा-ङ्घी] 'one having long legs,' hence 'a she-camel;' @deva[स्वङ्गा-ङ्गी] ( @deva[शोभनं अङ्गं यस्याः सा] ) fair-bodied; @deva[सुपुच्छा-च्छी]; &c.

**(a)** but if @deva[पुच्छ] be preceded by @deva[कबर], @deva[मणि] and @deva[विष], or if @deva[पुच्छ] and @deva[पक्ष] be used in a Bah. involving comparison, the *fem.* is formed in @deva[ई] only; @deva[कबरपुच्छी], 'one having a variegated plumage' *i. e.* 'pea-hen', @deva[उलूकपक्षी शाला] 'a hall having its sides like the wings of an owl'; @deva[उलूकपुच्छी सेना] 'an army with its rear arrayed in the shape of the tail of an owl'.

---

[^1]: @deva[नासिकोदरौष्ठजङ्घादन्तकर्णशृङ्गाच्च] । Pân. IV. 1. 55. @deva[अङ्गगात्रकण्ठेभ्यो वक्तव्यम्] । @deva[पुच्छाच्च] । @deva[कबरमणिविषशरेभ्यो नित्यम्] । @deva[उपमानात्पक्षाच्च पुच्छाच्च] । Vârtikas.
