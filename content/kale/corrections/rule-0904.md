title: "Future Participle Expressing Intention or Purpose"
pages: [511.png]
verdict: approved
---
Besides showing simple futurity participle expresses intention or purpose; @deva[अनुयास्यन् मुनितनयाम्] wishing to follow the daughter of the sage; @deva[दास्यन्] wishing to give, @deva[वन्यान् विनेष्यन्निव दुष्टसत्त्वान्] wishing, as it were, to tame the wild beasts.
