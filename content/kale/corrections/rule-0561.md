## Aniṭ Roots Taking the Seventh Form

Aniṭ roots ending in @deva[श्], @deva[ष्], @deva[स्] and @deva[ह्] and having @deva[इ], @deva[उ], @deva[ऋ] or @deva[ऌ] for their penultimate take this Form. @deva[दृश्] is an exception; it therefore takes the Fourth Variety.
