## Dative with @deva[धृ] (Debt) and @deva[स्पृह्] (Desire)

The creditor (or the person to whom something is due) in the case of the root @deva[धृ][^1] 'to owe;' and the person or thing desired in the case of @deva[स्पृह्], are put in the Dative case; @deva[वृक्षसेचने द्वे धारयसि मे] 'you owe me two sprinklings of trees' (Sa'k.); @deva[भक्ताय धारयति मोक्षं हरिः]; (Sid. Kau.) @deva[तस्यै स्पृहयमाणोसौ] 'he longing for her (Bhatti VIII. 15.);' @deva[पुष्पेभ्यः स्पृहयति] 'he longs for flowers;' but @deva[पुष्पाणि स्पृहयति] where no longing is implied.

---

[^1]: @deva[धारेरुत्तमर्णः । स्पृहेरीप्सितः ।] Pāṇ. I. 4 35, 36.
