Besides the general sense given above the Present tense in Sanskrit has the following senses:—

(a) [^1]It is sometimes used in the sense of 'immediate futurity;' @deva[कदा गमिष्यसि] when wilt thou go? @deva[एष गच्छामि] Here I go (i. e. shall go); @deva[ऊर्ध्वं म्रिये मुहूर्ताद्धि] I shall die an hour after.

(b) It may also be used to denote an action which is recently completed; @deva[कदा त्वं नगरादागतोसि—अयमागच्छामि] when didst thou come from the city? Here I come (have come just now).

(c) In narrations it is used for the Past tense; @deva[गृध्रो ब्रूते कस्त्वं] the vulture says, 'who art thou'?

(d) Sometimes it is used to denote a habitual or repeated action; @deva[पशुवधेनासौ जीवति।]
