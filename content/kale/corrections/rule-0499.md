## Guṇa Substitution for Final Vowels in Perfect

Guṇa is substituted for the final vowel of roots ending in @deva[ऋ] preceded by a conjunct consonant, and in long @deva[ऋ] and of the roots @deva[ऋ], @deva[ऋच्छ्] and @deva[जागृ]; as @deva[सस्मरिव] 1 dual of @deva[स्मृ], &c. @deva[शॄ], @deva[दॄ] and @deva[पॄ] optionally take the Guṇa substitute before the weak terminations, and shorten their vowel when they do not; as @deva[शशरिव], @deva[शश्रिव]. 1 dual of @deva[शॄ]. &c.
