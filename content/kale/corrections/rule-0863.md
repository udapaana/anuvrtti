## Genitive with Superlatives; Ablative with Comparatives

With superlatives, and words having the sense of the superlative, the Genitive is used; @deva[नृणां ब्राह्मणः श्रेष्ठः]; @deva[अग्रणीर्मन्त्रकृतामृषीणाम्] (Rag. V. 4.) the chief of sages, the authors to the *Mantras*.

*Note*:—Words having the sense of comparatives are used with the Ablative and sometimes with the Instrumental; @deva[अयमस्माद् बलेन हीनः] or @deva[अधिकः] this person is superior or inferior to him in strength; similarly @deva[देवदत्तो यज्ञदत्तात्पटुः मूर्खो वा]; @deva[को नु स्वन्ततरो मया] who will have a happier end than I? The word @deva[अधिक] is used with the Genitive, the Locative or the Instrumental; @deva[सुतैर्हि तासामधिकोपि सोऽभवत्] he was more (i. e. dearer) to them than their sons; @deva[तेषामप्यधिका मासाः पञ्च च द्वादश क्षपाः] they passed five months and twelve nights more than (those years); @deva[कुडवेऽधिकः प्रस्थः] *A Prastha is larger than a Kudava*.
