## Ablative for Distance in Time and Space

(a) [^1] The place or time from which the distance of another place or point of time is to be expressed is put in the Ablative case; the distance in space being put in the Nominative or the Locative and that in time in the Locative; @deva[वनाद् ग्रामो योजनं योजने वा] (Sid. Kau.) the village is a *yojana* from the forest, @deva[गविधुमतः सांकाश्यं चत्वारि योजनानि चतुर्षु योजनेषु वा] (Mb); @deva[कार्तिक्या आग्रहायणी मासे] (Sid Kau.) the full moonday of Mārgaśīrsha is a month (at the interval of a month) from that of Kārtika; @deva[समुद्रात्परी क्रोशौ].

(b) The Ablative is also used in questions and answers; @deva[कस्मात् त्वं । नद्याः] whence art thou? from the river; @deva[कुतो भवान् । पाटलिपुत्रात्] where do you come from? Pāṭaliputra.

---

[^1]: @deva[यतश्चाध्वकालनिर्माणं तत्र पञ्चमी । तद्युक्ताद्ध्वनः प्रथमासप्तम्यौ । कालात्सप्तमी च वक्तव्या । प्रश्नाख्यानयोश्च ।] Vārtikas.
