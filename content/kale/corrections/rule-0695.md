---
key: rule:695
title: "Special Rules for @deva[प्याय्] with Contextual Conditions"
pages: [425.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[प्याय्] has @deva[पीन] when it is used with 'one's own limbs.' @deva[पीनं मुखं], and @deva[प्यान] or @deva[पीन] in other cases, as @deva[प्यानः] or @deva[पीनः स्वेदः]; when it is preceded by a preposition, @deva[ई] is not substituted for its vowel; @deva[प्रप्यानः]; but when it is used with @deva[अन्धु] or @deva[ऊधस्] with the preposition @deva[आ], @deva[ई] is necessarily substituted; @deva[आपीनः अन्धुः], @deva[आपीनं ऊधः].
