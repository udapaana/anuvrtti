## Causative: Roots @deva[हृ], @deva[कृ], @deva[अभिवद्], @deva[दृश्] in the Ātmanepada

The primitive subject of the verbs @deva[हृ] and @deva[कृ],[^1] of @deva[अभिवद्], and @deva[दृश्] when used in the Atm. is either put in the Accusative or the Instrumental; @deva[हारयति कारयति वा भृत्यं भृत्येन वा कटम्] he causes the servant to take or weave a mat; @deva[अभिवादयते दर्शयते देवं भक्तं भक्तेन वा] he makes the devotee bow down to, or see, God.

---

[^1]: @deva[हृक्रोरन्यतरस्याम्] Pāṇ. I. 4. 43. @deva[अभिवादिदृशोरात्मनेपदं वेति वाच्यम् ।] Vārt.
