Sup (@deva[सुप्])[^1] is the technical term for a case termination in Sanskrit. Declension consists in adding the case termination to the crude form or base.

---

[^1]: The general terminations as given by Pāṇini are—@deva[स्वौजसमौट्छष्टाभ्याम्भिस्ङेभ्याम्भ्यस्ङसिभ्याम्भ्यस्ङसोसाम्ङ्योस्सुप्] IV. 1. 2. i. e. Nom. @deva[सु औ जस्]; Acc. @deva[अम् औट् शस्]; Ins. @deva[टा भ्याम् भिस्]; Dat. @deva[ङे भ्याम् भ्यस्]; Ab. @deva[ङसि भ्याम् भ्यस्]; Gen. @deva[ङस् ओस् आम्]; Loc. @deva[ङि ओस् सुप्]. Some of these terminations, as in common with Pāṇini's system of nomenclature, have certain *Its* (servile letters) added or prefixed to them; *e. g.* @deva[उ] in @deva[सु], @deva[ज्] in @deva[जस्] &c. It will be easily seen that the term @deva[सुप्] is obtained by taking the first letter and the last *It* in the sūtra.
