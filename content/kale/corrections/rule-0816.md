## Instrumental for Cause, Motive, and Purpose

The Instrumental is also used to express the cause or motive or the object of purpose of an action as distinguished from the mere instrument of it;[^1] @deva[पुण्येन दृष्टो हरिः] Hari was seen by (virtue of) merit; @deva[तेनापराधेन दण्ड्योसि] thou art to be punished for that fault; @deva[अध्ययनेन वसति] dwells for the purpose of studying; also when the object to be accomplished is simply implied; @deva[अलं श्रमेण] away with your efforts i. e. they will not succeed; @deva[श्रमेण साध्यं नास्ति इत्यर्थः] (Sid. Kau).

---

[^1]: @deva[हेतौ ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. @deva[फलमपि इह हेतुः । द्रव्यादिसाधारणं निर्व्यापारसाधारणं च हेतुत्वम् । करणत्वं तु क्रियामात्रविषयं व्यापारनियतं च ।] Sid. Kau.
