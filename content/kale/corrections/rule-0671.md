## Extended Use of @deva[आन] for Habit and Capacity

[^1]The termination @deva[आन] may be added to any root in the sense of 'in the habit of' or 'to show a particular standard of age, or capacity to do a thing; *e. g.* @deva[भोगं भुंजानः] 'one habituated to enjoy pleasures;' @deva[कवचं बिभ्राणः] 'of a sufficient age *i. e.* young enough to bear armour;' @deva[शत्रुं निघ्नानः] 'able to kill the enemy,' &c.

[^1]: @deva[ताच्छील्यवयोवचनशक्तिषु चानश्] । Pāṇ. III. 2. 126.
