title: "Usage of भवत् as Honorific Second Person"
pages: [507.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[भवत्] is used in the second person like 'you' in English as a courteous form of address though it is to be regarded as a pronoun of the third person and ought to be treated as such, @deva[भवान् अत्र प्रष्टव्यः] you ought to be asked here; @deva[भवान् अपि तत्र गच्छतु] you may also go there.

(a) When respect is to be shown, @deva[अत्र] and @deva[तत्र] are prefixed to @deva[भवत्] according as the person with reference to whom it is used is near, or at a distance or absent; @deva[अत्रभवान् काश्यपः] the venerable Kāshyapa (who is near); @deva[इदमासनं अलङ्करोत्वत्रभवान्] may you occupy (lit. ornament) this seat; @deva[तत्रभवती इरावती] lady Irāvatī (who is not present). Sometimes @deva[तद्] is used with @deva[भवत्] to show respect; as @deva[यन्मां विधेयविषये स भवान् नियुङ्क्ते।] Māl. Mād. I.
