## IRREGULAR BASES:—

The vowel of the roots @deva[मा], @deva[हा] 'to go,' @deva[भृ], @deva[पृ] or @deva[पॄ] 'to fill' and @deva[ऋ] is changed to @deva[इ] in the reduplicative syllable.
