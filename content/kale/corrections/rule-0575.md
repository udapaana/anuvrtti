**Irregular Aorists of the Fifth Form.**

## Optional @deva[इ] for @deva[इट्] Substitution

The roots @deva[दीप्], @deva[जन्], @deva[पूर्], @deva[ताय्] and @deva[प्याय्] optionally substitute @deva[इ] for @deva[इट्] (third person sing. Ātm.).
