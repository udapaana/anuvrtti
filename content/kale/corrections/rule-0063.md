## Nouns ending in @deva[आ] Mas. and Fem.

@deva[गोपा] *mas.* a cow-herd.

**(a)** Mas. nouns ending in @deva[आ] take the general terminations. The final @deva[आ] is dropped before a vowel termination beginning with the Acc. plu.
