---
key: rule:96
title: "Monosyllabic Roots with Initial @deva[ब्], @deva[ग्], or @deva[द्]"
pages: [056.png]
verdict: approved
---
When a mono-syllabic root (or root-noun) begins with @deva[ब्], @deva[ग्] or @deva[द्] and ends with @deva[घ्], @deva[ढ्], @deva[ध्], @deva[भ्] or @deva[ह्], the @deva[ब्], @deva[ग्] or @deva[द्] is respectively changed to @deva[भ्], @deva[घ्] or @deva[ध्] before @deva[स्] or @deva[ध्व्] or when followed by nothing or when the final soft aspirate is changed to an unaspirate letter.[^1]

---

[^1]: @deva[एकाचो बशो भष् झषन्तस्य स्ध्वोः] — Pāṇ. VIII. 2. 37.
