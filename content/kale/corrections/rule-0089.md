  - "Neu. N. A. plu. printed कमलि (expected कमलि/कमल्लि?); kept as printed. The print labels this row 'N. N. & A.'; written N. A."

**(a)** After final @deva[ल्] the @deva[सु] of the loc. is changed to @deva[षु].

**(b)** @deva[ट्] (optionally changeable to @deva[ठ्]) may be optionally inserted between @deva[ण्] and @deva[सु].
