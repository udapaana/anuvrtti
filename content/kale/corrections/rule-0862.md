## Genitive with Certain Indeclinables

The Genitive is used with indeclinables like @deva[मध्ये], @deva[पारे], @deva[कृते], &c.; @deva[गङ्गाया मध्ये परे वा] in the middle or on the other side of the Ganges; @deva[अमीषां प्राणानां कृते] for the sake of this life.
