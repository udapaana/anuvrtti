**Obs:**—@deva[त्व] and @deva[त्व] (the 10th and 11th @deva[सर्वादि]s) the one @deva[उदात्त] (udātta), the other @deva[अनुदात्त] (anudātta), both synonyms of @deva[अन्य] are declined like @deva[सर्व]. The former is of the form @deva[त्वत्] according to some and then it is declined regularly by adding the terminations; e. g. @deva[त्वत् त्वतौ त्वतः] Nom. &c.
