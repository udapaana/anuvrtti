## Substitution of @deva[ख्या] and @deva[क्शा] for @deva[चक्ष्] in Perfect

@deva[ख्या] and @deva[क्शा] are optionally substituted for @deva[चक्ष्] in the Perfect, and necessarily in the other Non-conjugational tenses.

@deva[ख्या] and @deva[क्शा] take both the Padas.

**Par.**

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | @deva[आचख्यौ] | @deva[आचख्यिव] | @deva[आचख्यिम] |
|  | @deva[आचक्शौ] | @deva[आचक्शिव] | @deva[आचक्शिम] |
| 2. | @deva[आचख्याथ], @deva[आचख्यिथ] | @deva[आचख्यथुः] | @deva[आचख्य] |
|  | @deva[आचक्शाथ], @deva[आचक्शिथ] | @deva[आचक्शाथुः] | @deva[आचक्श] |
| 3. | @deva[आचख्यौ] | @deva[आचख्यतुः] | @deva[आचख्युः] |
|  | @deva[आचक्शौ] | @deva[आचक्शतुः] | @deva[आचक्शुः] |
