---
key: rule:283
title: "Adding @deva[अ] to Specific Finals"
pages: [169.png]
verdict: approved
doubts:
  - "\"devine\", \"Bramhan\": as printed, kept"
---
[^1] @deva[अ] is added to the finals of the following—

**(a)** @deva[वर्चस्] preceded by @deva[ब्रह्मन्] or @deva[हस्तिन्]; @deva[ब्रह्मवर्चसम्] 'the devine Majesty of Bramhan or the glory of a Brâhmana'; 'pre-eminence or sanctity arising from sacred knowledge'; @deva[हस्तिवर्चसम्] 'the splendour or magnificence of an elephant'.

**(b)** @deva[तमस्] preceded by @deva[अव], @deva[सम्] and @deva[अन्ध]; *e. g.* @deva[अवततं तमः अवतमसम्] 'slight darkness;' @deva[संततं तमः संतमसम्] 'great or continuous darkness; @deva[अन्धं तमः अन्धतमसम्] 'pitchy darkness.'

**(c)** @deva[रहस्] preceded by @deva[अनु], @deva[अव] or @deva[तप्त]; @deva[अनुगतं रहः अनुरहसम्] 'secret, solitary'; @deva[अवततं रहः अवरहसं] 'a little secret,' or 'desolate'; @deva[तप्तं रहः तप्तरहसम्] 'a hot secluded place.'

**(d)** @deva[उरस्] in the Loc. and preceded by @deva[प्रति]; @deva[उरसि प्रति प्रत्युरसम्] 'against the breast'

**(e)** @deva[गो] preceded by @deva[अनु], when length is implied; @deva[अनुगव यानम्] 'a vehicle measuring as much as a bull in length.'

---

[^1]: @deva[ब्रह्महस्तिभ्यां वर्चसः] । @deva[अवसमन्धेभ्यस्तमसः] । @deva[अन्ववतप्ताद्रहसः] । @deva[प्रतेरुरसः सप्तमीस्थात्] । @deva[अनुगवमायामे] । Pān. V. 4. 78. 79; 81-83.
