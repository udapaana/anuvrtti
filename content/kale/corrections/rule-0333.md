---
key: rule:333
title: "Feminine of @deva[पाद्] Compounds"
pages: [192.png, 193.png]
verdict: approved
---
A Bah.[^1] ending in @deva[पाद्] ( substituted for @deva[पाद] ) optionally forms its *fem.* in @deva[ई] before which it is changed to @deva[पद्]; @deva[व्याघ्रस्य इव पादौ यस्याः सा व्याघ्रपाद्-पदी], @deva[द्विपाद्-पदी] and in @deva[आ] when @deva[पाद्] means 'a foot of a Vedic verse'; @deva[द्विपदा ऋक्], @deva[एकपदा] &c.

**(a)** But if @deva[पाद्] be preceded by one of the words @deva[कुम्भ], @deva[शूल], @deva[तृण], @deva[हंस], @deva[काक], @deva[कृष्ण], @deva[सूकर], &c. the *fem.* is necessarily formed in @deva[ई], @deva[पाद्] changing into @deva[पद्] as before; @deva[कुम्भपदी] 'a woman whose feet are as big as a pitcher.'

**(b)** In other cases the *fem.* of *prâtipadikas* ending in @deva[पाद्] is formed by adding @deva[आ]; @deva[हस्तिपादा], @deva[अजपादा], &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[पादोऽन्यतरस्याम्] । @deva[टाबृचि] । Pân. IV. 1. 8, 9.
