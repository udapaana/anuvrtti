## Vowel Changes in Benedictive Before Parasmaipada Terminations

The final @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] is lengthened; final @deva[ऋ] short is changed to @deva[रि], and final @deva[ॠ] changed to @deva[ईर्] or to @deva[ऊर्] when preceded by a labial or @deva[व्] before the Par. terminations of the Benedictive, and the @deva[य] of the Passive.

**Examples:**
- @deva[जि]—@deva[जीयात्]
- @deva[स्तु]—@deva[स्तूयात्]
- @deva[कृ]—@deva[क्रियात्]
- @deva[कॄ]—@deva[कीर्यात्]
- @deva[पॄ]—@deva[पूर्यात्], &c.
