**(a)** The @deva[स्] of the nom. sing. of @deva[तद्] and @deva[एतद्] *mas.* is dropped before a consonant when they do not end in @deva[क] or are not used in a negative Tatpurusha;[^1] *e. g.* @deva[स शंभुः], @deva[एष विष्णुः]; but @deva[एषको रुद्रः] this Rudra; @deva[असोऽशिवः] that is not Śiva (a neg. Tat.); @deva[एषोऽत्र].

**(b)** Sometimes in poetry the @deva[स्] of @deva[सः] and @deva[एषः] is considered as not existing when followed by a vowel other than @deva[अ], so that the two vowels may combine in order to meet the exigencies of the metre;[^2] *e. g.* @deva[सेमानविड्ढि प्रभृतिं य ईशिषे] &c. Ṛg. II. 24. 1.

@deva[सैष दाशरथी रामः सैष राजा युधिष्ठिरः।]
@deva[सैष कर्णो महात्यागी सैष भीमो महाबलः॥]

---

[^1]: @deva[एतत्तदोः सुलोपोऽकोरनञ्समासे हलि] — Pāṇ. VI. 1. 132.

[^2]: @deva[सोऽचि लोपे चेत्पादपूरणम्] — Pāṇ. VI. 1. 134.
