title: "Irregular Dwandwa Compounds"
pages: [117.png, 118.png]
verdict: approved
---
The following compounds are always what they are laid down to be ( though they do not conform to the proper rules ); viz.

**(a)** Samāhāra Dwandwas:—@deva[गावश्च अश्वाश्च गवाश्वम्]; @deva[पुत्राश्च पौत्राश्च पुत्रपौत्रम्]; so @deva[स्त्रीकुमारम्], @deva[उष्ट्रखरम्] camels and asses, @deva[उष्ट्रशशम्] camels and rabbits, @deva[मांसशोणितम्], @deva[दर्भशरम्] holy grass and reeds, @deva[तृणोलपम्] grass and soft grass or shrubs, @deva[दासीदासम्] &c.

**(b)** Itaretara Dwandwas:—@deva[दधिपयसी] curds and milk, @deva[इध्माबर्हिषी] holy sticks and grass, @deva[सर्पिर्मधुनी], @deva[मधुसर्पिषी] honey and ghee, @deva[शुक्लकृष्णौ], @deva[अध्ययनतपसी], @deva[आद्यवसाने], @deva[उलूखलमुसले], @deva[ऋक्सामे] (@deva[ऋक्+सामन्]) Rik and the Sāman verses, @deva[वाङ्मनसे] (@deva[वाक्+मनस्]) speech and mind (the @deva[न्] of @deva[सामन्] dropped and @deva[अ] added to @deva[मनस्] irregularly by Pān. V. 4. 77).
