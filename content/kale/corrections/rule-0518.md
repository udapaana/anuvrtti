## Perfect Base of @deva[ऊर्णु]: @deva[ऊर्णुनु]

@deva[ऊर्णु] forms its base as @deva[ऊर्णुनु]. Its vowel is optionally not guṇated before a strong termination when it takes @deva[इ].

| 2. | @deva[ऊर्णुनुविथ] @deva[ऊर्णुनविथ] | @deva[ऊर्णुनुवथुः] | @deva[ऊर्णुनुव] |
| 3. | @deva[ऊर्णुनाव] | @deva[ऊर्णुनुवतुः] | @deva[ऊर्णुनुवुः] |
