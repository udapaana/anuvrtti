Decline similarly @deva[विश्वपा] the protector of the world, @deva[शंखध्मा] a conch-shell-blower, @deva[सोमपा] one who quaffs the soma-juice, @deva[धूम्रपा] one who inhales smoke, @deva[बलदा] strength-giver, or Indra, and other comp. nouns derived from roots ending in @deva[आ].

**(a)** If the latter member be not a root the final @deva[आ] is not dropped; e. g. @deva[हाहा] name of a Gandharva, Acc. plu. @deva[हाहान्]; D. Ab. G. and L. sing. @deva[हाहै], @deva[हाहाः], @deva[हाहाः] and @deva[हाहे] respectively. The rest like @deva[गोपा].
