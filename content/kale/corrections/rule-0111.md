**Obs.** The @deva[शा] of @deva[उक्थशास्] uttering hymns, becomes @deva[श] before the consonantal terminations, except that of the Nom. sing.; e. g. Nom. @deva[उक्थशाः] sing. @deva[उक्थशासौ] dual.; Instr. sing. @deva[उक्थशासा]; dual @deva[उक्थशोभ्याम्]; Loc. plu. @deva[उक्थशाःसु-स्सु].
