## Particles and Prepositions Governing Accusative

The particles[^1] @deva[उभयतः], @deva[सर्वतः], @deva[उपर्युपरि], @deva[अधोधः], @deva[अध्यधि] and @deva[धिक्]; @deva[अभितः], @deva[परितः], @deva[समया], @deva[निकषा], @deva[हा] and @deva[प्रति] meaning 'to,' and @deva[अन्तरा] 'between,' and @deva[अन्तरेण] 'without,' 'regarding to,' govern the Accusative; @deva[उभयतः कृष्णं गोपाः] the cowherds are on both sides of Krishna—@deva[सर्वतः प्रासादं जाग्रति दण्डधारिणः] guards keep vigil on all sides of the palace; @deva[उपर्युपरि लोकं हरिः] Hari is over all the worlds; @deva[अधोधो लोकं पातालः] Pātāla is below the world. @deva[अध्यधि लोकम्]; @deva[धिग्वो जाल्मान्] fie upon you rogues; @deva[धिक्सानुजं कुरुपतिं] fie upon the lord of the Kurus, with all his brothers; (@deva[धिक्]) is used sometimes with the Nom. and sometimes with the Voc. @deva[धिगर्थाः कष्टसंश्रयाः] fie upon wealth which is attended with troubles; @deva[धिङ् मूर्ख] fie upon thee fool; @deva[रक्षांसि वेदिं परितो निरास्थदङ्गान्ययाक्षीदभितः प्रधानम्] (Bhatti I. 12) he dispelled the demons from around the altar and offered sacrifices to the minor deities arranged round the principal one. @deva[अभितस्तं पृथासूनुः स्नेहेन परितस्तरे] Kir. XI. 8. @deva[ग्रामं समया-निकषा व्रजति] goes near to the village. Vide Sis. I. 68, VI. 73. @deva[हा कृष्णाभक्तं] woe to him who is not a devotee of Krishna; @deva[मन्दौत्सुक्योऽस्मि नगरगमनं प्रति] I have but a faint desire to go to the town; @deva[अन्तरा त्वां मां हरिः]; @deva[हरिमन्तरेण न सुखं] happiness is not possible without Hari; @deva[देवीं वसुमतीमन्तरेण] with reference to queen Vasumati.

Some of the indeclinables given above are used with the Genitive; e. g. @deva[उपर्युपरि सर्वेषामादित्य इव तेजसा] (he stood) very high above all by his lustre, like the sun, &c.
