@deva[न][^1] is substituted for @deva[त] when it immediately follows a final @deva[द्] or @deva[र्]; this final @deva[द्] is also changed to @deva[न्]: @deva[भिद्]-@deva[भिन्न], @deva[शॄ]-@deva[शीर्ण]; @deva[तुर्व्]-@deva[तूर्ण] (See @ref[698])

**Exceptions:—**

(a) @deva[भिद्] has @deva[भित्त] when it means 'a part, a portion,' @deva[भिन्न] in other senses

(b) @deva[विद्] 6 P. A. takes @deva[त] in the sense of 'fit for enjoyment or famous;' @deva[वित्तं] 'wealth, property,' @deva[वित्तः] 'famous'; @deva[विन्न] in other cases.

(c) @deva[मद्], @deva[पुर्] and @deva[मूर्च्छ्]; @deva[मत्त], @deva[पूर्त] 'filled,' (also @deva[पूर्ण] when it means the same as @deva[पू]), @deva[मूर्त].

[^1]: @deva[रदायां निष्ठातो नः पूर्वस्य च दः । संयोगादेरातो धातोर्यण्वतः । ल्वादिभ्यः ।] Pāṇ. VIII. 2. 42–44.
