  - "बहुदाण्डिका नगरी and बहुदाण्डिको ग्रामः: printed so on scan 161 (बहुदण्डी between them has no ā); probably misprints for बहुदण्डिका, बहुदण्डिको"
---
A *fem.* Bah. ending in @deva[इन्] takes this @deva[क] necessarily;[^1] as @deva[बहुदाण्डिका नगरी] 'a city having many ascetics'; @deva[बहुवाग्मिका सभा] 'an assembly having many eloquent speakers in it'; but @deva[बहुदण्डी] or @deva[बहुदाण्डिको ग्रामः] (vide @ref[263] below), as it is *mas.*

---

[^1]: @deva[इनः स्त्रियाम् ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 152.
