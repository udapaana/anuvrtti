**(a)** @deva[च्] or @deva[ज्] is changed to @deva[क्] when followed by a hard consonant or by nothing, and to @deva[ग्] when followed by a soft consonant.[^1]

**(b)** The ending @deva[श्] or @deva[छ्] of root-nouns and the final of @deva[व्रश्च्], @deva[भ्रस्ज्], @deva[सृज्], @deva[मृज्], @deva[यज्], @deva[राज्] and @deva[भ्राज्] are changed to @deva[ष्] when followed by any consonant except a nasal or a semi-vowel or by nothing[^2]; the @deva[ष्] is changed to @deva[ट्] or @deva[ड्] when at the end of a word, and to @deva[ड्] when followed by a soft consonant. The @deva[ज्] of @deva[परिव्राज्] is similarly changed to @deva[ट्] or @deva[ड्].

**(c)** But the @deva[श्] of the root-nouns @deva[दिश्], @deva[दृश्], @deva[स्पृश्] and @deva[सृश्] is changed to @deva[क्] as also the @deva[ष्] of @deva[दधृष्] 'a bold or impudent man' and the @deva[क्ष्] of such words as @deva[विपक्ष्] &c. The @deva[श्] of @deva[नश्] is optionally changed to @deva[ट्] or @deva[क्], as also the @deva[क्ष्] of @deva[तक्ष्] and @deva[गोरक्ष्]. The @deva[ज्] of @deva[ऋत्विज्] is changed to @deva[क्].

**(d)** @deva[त्] may optionally be inserted between @deva[ट्] and the loc. pl. term. @deva[सु].

**(e)** Before vowel terminations final @deva[छ्] may optionally be changed to @deva[श्].

---

[^1]: @deva[चोः कुः] — Pāṇ. VIII. 2. 30.

[^2]: @deva[व्रश्चभ्रस्जसृजमृजयजराजभ्राजच्छशां षः] — Pāṇ. VIII. 2. 36.
