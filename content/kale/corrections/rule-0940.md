---
key: rule:940
title: "Second Future for Imperfect with Verbs of Remembering"
pages: [524.png]
verdict: approved
---
When the word @deva[यत्] is not used with roots meaning 'to remember,' such as @deva[स्मृ], &c. the Second Future is used in the sense of the Imperfect; @deva[स्मरसि कृष्ण गोकुले वत्स्यामः] Krishna, do you remember that we dwelt in Gokula?
