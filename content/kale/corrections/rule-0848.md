## Genitive with Words Ending in @deva[-तस्] and Direction Words

[^1] Words ending in the termination @deva[तस्] and showing direction and others having the same sense, such as @deva[उपरि], @deva[उपरिष्टात्], @deva[अधः], @deva[अधस्तात्], @deva[पुरः], @deva[पुरस्तात्], @deva[पश्चात्], @deva[अग्रे], &c. are used with the Genitive; @deva[ग्रामस्य दक्षिणतः उत्तरतः] &c. to the south or to the north of the village, &c; @deva[अर्कस्योपरि] (S'ak. II 8.) on the Arka plant; @deva[तरूणामधः] (S'ak. I.) under the trees; @deva[तस्य स्थित्वा कथमपि पुरः] (Meg.) standing before him with great difficulty, &c.

(a) [^2] Words ending in @deva[एन] such as @deva[दक्षिणेन], @deva[उत्तरेण], &c. are used with the Genitive or the Accusative; @deva[दक्षिणेन ग्रामं ग्रामस्य वा] to the south of the village; @deva[उत्तरेण स्रवन्तीं] (Māl. Mād IX. 24.) to the north of the river; @deva[दण्डकान्दक्षिणेनाहं] (Bhatti. VIII.108.); @deva[धनपतिगृहानुत्तरेण] (Meg. 80) to the north of Kubera's palace.

---

[^1]: @deva[षष्ठ्यतसर्थप्रत्ययेन] Pāṇ. II. 3. 30.
[^2]: @deva[एनपा द्वितीया । ] Pāṇ. II. 3. 31. @deva[एनपेति योगविभागात्षष्ठ्यपि ।] Sid. Kau.
