@deva[दा] 'to give,' and @deva[दे], form their P. P. Ptc. as @deva[दत्त]; the @deva[द] of @deva[दत्त] is dropped after a preposition ending in a vowel; @deva[प्रत्त]-@deva[अवत्त] &c; in this latter case the preceding @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] is lengthened; @deva[नीत्त], @deva[सूत्त], &c.; @deva[द] may optionally be retained also; @deva[प्रदत्त], @deva[अवदत्त], @deva[सुदत्त],[^1] &c.

[^1]: @deva[अवदत्तं विदतश्च प्रदतश्चादिकर्मणि । सुदत्तमनुदन्तश्च निदत्तमिति चेष्यते ॥]
