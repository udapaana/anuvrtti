[^1]A root may be used in its gerundive form in @deva[अम्] with its object prefixed to it if that object be a part of the body which can be severed without fatal effects; @deva[भ्रूविक्षेपं कथयति] 'narrates contracting the eyebrows; but @deva[शिर उत्क्षिप्य] (not @deva[शिरउत्क्षेपम्]) as the severance of the head would cause death.

(a) Similarly when a part of the body is completely hurt or pressed in the action, the gerund in @deva[अम्] is used with that part; @deva[उर-प्रतिपेषं युध्यन्ते] i. e. @deva[कृत्स्नमुरः पीडयन्तः] 'afflicting the whole bosom;' @deva[उरोविदारं प्रतिचस्करे नखैः ।]
