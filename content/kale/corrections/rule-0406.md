title: "Addition of @deva[नु] and @deva[उ] in Fifth and Eighth Classes"
pages: [256.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[नु] and @deva[उ] are added on to the roots of the 5th and 8th classes respectively before the terminations.[^1]

[^1]: @deva[स्वादिभ्यः श्नुः] । @deva[तनादिकृञ्भ्य उः] । Pāṇ. III. 1. 73, 79.
