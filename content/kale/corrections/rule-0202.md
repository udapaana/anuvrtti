The final vowel of a feminine noun ending in any of the affixes @deva[आ], @deva[ई] or @deva[ऊ] and forming the second member of a compound used adjectively is shortened; the @deva[ओ] of @deva[गो] is changed to @deva[उ] under the same circumstances;[^1] e. g. @deva[प्राप्त + जीविका = प्राप्तजीविकः] (Tat.), @deva[अतिमालः] (Tat.); @deva[पञ्चगुः] something bought for five cows; @deva[बह्व्यो नाड्यो यस्मिन् बहुनाडिः] many-veined @deva[देहः] (Bah.); @deva[चित्रा गावो यस्य चित्रगुः] having variegated cows, &c.; but @deva[कल्याणपञ्चमीकः] as the @deva[ई] is not final.

**(a)** If the final @deva[ई] or @deva[ऊ] be not a fem. termination, it remains unchanged; @deva[सुष्ठु धीः सा सुधीः], @deva[बहुतन्त्रीर्धमनी].

---

[^1]: @deva[गोस्त्रियोरुपसर्जनस्य ।] Pāṇ. I. 2. 48.
