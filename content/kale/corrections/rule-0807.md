## Passive Construction with Double Accusative Roots

When these verbs are used in the passive,[^1] the secondary object in the case of the first twelve roots and the principal one in that of the last four is put in the Nominative case, the other Accusative remaining as before; @deva[धेनुः पयो दुह्यते], @deva[दशरथः रामं ययाचे कौशिकेन], @deva[उदधिः सुधां ममन्थे देवैः] &c, @deva[तेन गावः ग्रामं नीयन्ते ह्रियन्ते कृष्यन्ते उह्यन्ते वा], &c.
