---
key: rule:29
title: "No Lingual Substitution Before ष्"
pages: [022.png]
verdict: approved
---
If a letter of the group @deva[त्] be followed by @deva[ष्] it does not substitute its corresponding lingual[^1]; @deva[सन्] + @deva[षष्ठः] = @deva[सन्षष्ठः] sixth good man.

---

[^1]: @deva[तोः षि] । Pāṇ. VIII. 4. 43.
