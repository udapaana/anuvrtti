@deva[प्रति] meaning 'very little' is compounded with a noun and is placed last;[^1] @deva[शाकस्य लेशः शाकप्रति] 'very little vegetable'. But @deva[वृक्षं वृक्षं प्रति विद्योतते विद्युत्] where @deva[प्रति] means 'towards'.

---

[^1]: @deva[सुप्प्रतिना मात्रार्थे] । Pāṇ. II. 1. 9.
