# Chapter VIII. Formation of Feminine Bases

Feminine bases are derived from the masculine by the addition of the affixes @deva[आ] ( @deva[टाप्], @deva[डाप्], @deva[चाप्] ), @deva[ई] ( @deva[ङीप्], @deva[ङीष्], @deva[ङीन्] ), @deva[ऊ] ( @deva[ऊङ्] ) and @deva[ति].
