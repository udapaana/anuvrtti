title: "Feminine with @deva[न्] Insertion"
pages: [188.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] The words @deva[अन्तर्वत्] and @deva[पतिवत्] form their *feminine* in @deva[ई] but have a @deva[न्] prefixed to the @deva[ई], @deva[अन्तर्वत्नी] 'a woman big with a child' @deva[पतिवत्नी] 'a woman whose husband is living'. But if the word @deva[पति] means 'a lord or master of' @deva[ई] only is added; as @deva[पतिमती पृथ्वी] ( the earth having a king ).

---

[^1]: @deva[अन्तर्वत्पतिवतोर्नुक्] । Pân. IV. 1. 32.
