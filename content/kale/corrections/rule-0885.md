title: "एक and अपर/अन्य for 'Some—Others'"
pages: [507.png]
verdict: approved
---
The pronouns @deva[एक] and @deva[अपर] or @deva[अन्य] are used in the plural in the sense of 'some—others;' @deva[विधवायाः पुनरुद्वाहः सशास्त्र इत्येके शास्त्रप्रतिषिद्ध इत्यन्ये कलौ निषिद्ध इत्यपरे] some think that widow-remarriage is sanctioned by the Ś'astras, some say that it is prohibited by them, while others hold that it is not allowed in the Kali age. @deva[केचित्] may take the place of @deva[एके].
