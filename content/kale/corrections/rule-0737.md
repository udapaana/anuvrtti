title: "Roots Not Changing @deva[च्] or @deva[ज्] Before @deva[ण्यत्]"
pages: [437.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "याज्यम्/याच्यम् first example is blurred"
---
(a) The roots @deva[यज्], @deva[याच्], @deva[रुच्], @deva[प्रवच्], @deva[त्यज्], and @deva[पच्] do not change their @deva[च्] or @deva[ज्] to @deva[क्] or @deva[ग्] before @deva[ण्यत्]; @deva[याज्यम्], @deva[याच्यम्]; @deva[रोच्यम्], @deva[प्रवाच्यम्] (@deva[ग्रन्थविशेषः]), @deva[अर्च्य], @deva[त्याज्य], @deva[पाच्य].

(b) @deva[वच्] does not change its @deva[च्] to @deva[क्] before @deva[ण्यत्] when the meaning is 'what ought to be spoken out, speech,' @deva[वाच्यं]; but @deva[वाक्यं]; a sentence.'

(c) @deva[वञ्च्] when it means 'to go' does not change @deva[च्] its to @deva[क्]; @deva[वञ्च्यम्]; when it means 'to bend' the @deva[च्] is changed to @deva[क्]; @deva[वङ्क्यं काष्ठम्].

(d) @deva[युज्] when it is preceded by @deva[प्र] and @deva[नि], takes the affix @deva[ण्यत्] in the sense of 'what is possible or capable of;' and does not change its @deva[ज्] to @deva[ग्]; @deva[प्रयोक्तुं शक्य प्रयोज्यः], @deva[नियोक्तुं शक्यः नियोज्यः भृत्यः ।]

(e) @deva[भुज्] has @deva[भोज्यं] meaning 'food,' and @deva[भोग्यं] 'what is fit to be enjoyed.'
