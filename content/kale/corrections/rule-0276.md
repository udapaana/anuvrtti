---
key: rule:276
title: "Numeral Compounds with Relations/Learning and Rivers"
pages: [166.png]
verdict: approved
---
**(a)** A numeral may optionally be compounded with a noun, showing relation by blood or by some kind of learning, to form an Avya. comp.;[^1] @deva[द्वौ मुनी वंश्यौ द्विमुनि], @deva[व्याकरणस्य त्रिमुनि]; *e. g.* @deva[त्रिमुनि व्याकरणम्], 'Sans. Grammar of which the three sages, Pânini, Kâtyâyana and Patanjali, are the authors in succession.'

**(b)** and with names of rivers, this comp. also shows an aggregate;[^2] @deva[सप्तगंगम्], @deva[द्वियमुनम्].

---

[^1]: @deva[संख्या वंश्येन] । Pân. II. 1. 19. @deva[वंशो द्विधा विद्यया जन्मना च] । Sid. Kau.

[^2]: @deva[नदीभिश्च] । Pân. II. 1. 20 @deva[समाहारे चायमिष्यते] । Vârt.
