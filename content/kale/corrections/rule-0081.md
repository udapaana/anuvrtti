Nouns ending in @deva[ऋ] *mas. fem.* and *neu.*

Nouns derived from roots with the affix @deva[तृ] (@deva[तृच्] and @deva[तृन्] Pāṇ. III. 1. 133. and 2. 135), such as @deva[कर्तृ] a maker, &c. and the words @deva[स्वसृ] f, @deva[नप्तृ], @deva[नेष्टृ], @deva[क्षत्तृ], @deva[होतृ], @deva[पोतृ], and @deva[प्रशास्तृ] as also @deva[उद्गातृ][^1] change their @deva[ऋ] to @deva[आ] in the Nom. sing. and to @deva[आर्] in the first five inflections. The @deva[ऋ] is lengthened in the Acc. and Gen. pl. and changed to @deva[उर्] before the Ab. and Gen. sing.; the Voc. sing. ends in @deva[अर्].
