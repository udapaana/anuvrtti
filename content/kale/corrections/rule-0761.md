**(b) The Adverbial Indeclinable Participle**

**or**

**The Gerund in @deva[अम्].**

---

There is another Indeclinable Participle having the same sense as that in @deva[त्वा], formed with the affix @deva[अम्]. Before this the root or the derivative verbal base generally undergoes the same changes as it does before the @deva[इ] of the Pass. Aorist 3rd sing.; @deva[नी]—@deva[नायम्] 'having carried or led'; @deva[दा]—@deva[दायम्] 'having given'; @deva[भू]—@deva[भावम्], @deva[भिद्]—@deva[भेदम्], @deva[ग्रह्]—@deva[ग्राहम्], @deva[गम्]—@deva[गमम्], &c.
