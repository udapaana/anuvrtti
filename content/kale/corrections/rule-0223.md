Names of quarters and numerals form a Karm. compound with any @deva[सुबन्त] (a declinable word) but only when the whole compound is an appellation;[^1] e. g. @deva[सप्तर्षयः] 'Name of a constellation of stars,' 'Ursa Major;' @deva[पञ्चजनाः][^2] &c., @deva[पूर्वेषुकामशमी] 'Name of a town in the east;' @deva[उत्तरा वृक्षाः] 'Northern trees; @deva[पञ्च ब्राह्मणाः] &c. The compound is forbidden in other cases.

**(a)**[^3] But words denoting a cardinal point or numerals may be compounded with any other noun when a Taddhita termination is to be affixed to the compound so formed (or it conveys in addition to its sense as a Karm. a derivative meaning such as is usually denoted by a Tad. aff.) or when the compound itself becomes the first member of another compound or when the compound forms a proper name; @deva[पूर्वा शाला पूर्वशाला]; @deva[पूर्वस्यां शालायां भवः पौर्वशालः] 'being in the eastern hall' from @deva[पूर्वशाला+अ] (i. e. Tad. aff. @deva[ञ] added by @deva[दिक्पूर्वपदादसंज्ञायां ञः] Pāṇ. IV. 2. 107); so @deva[षष्+मातृ=षण्मातृ] 'six mothers'+Tad. @deva[अ]=@deva[षाण्मातुरः] the son of six mothers; @deva[पूर्वा शाला प्रिया यस्य स पूर्वशालाप्रियः] where @deva[पूर्वशाला] is a @deva[पूर्वपद] and not used independently; @deva[उत्तरध्रुवः], @deva[दक्षिणध्रुवः] &c. which are names.

---

[^1]: @deva[दिक्संख्ये संज्ञायाम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 1. 61.

[^2]: These are gods, men, Gandharvas, serpents, and Pitṛis, according to some; and Brāhmaṇas, Kshatriyas, Vaishyas, Śūdras and Nishādhas or barbarians, according to others. (Vide Shankarāchārya on Brah. Sū. I. 4. 11.-13.)

[^3]: @deva[तद्धितार्थोत्तरपदसमाहारे च ।] Pāṇ. II. 1. 51.
