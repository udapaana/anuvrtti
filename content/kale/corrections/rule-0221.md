**(a)**[^1] A word expressive of the standard of comparison (@deva[उपमान]) may be compounded in a Karmadhāraya with another denoting the common quality or ground of comparison; as @deva[घन इव श्यामः घनश्यामः] dark like a cloud. Such compounds are called @deva[उपमानपूर्वपदकर्मधारय].

**(b)**[^2] Similarly a noun denoting the person or thing compared (@deva[उपमेय]) may be compounded with words indicative of excellence, such as @deva[व्याघ्र],[^3] @deva[सिंह], @deva[चन्द्र], @deva[कमल], &c. no mention of the common attribute (of similarity) being made; as @deva[पुरुषो व्याघ्र एव पुरुषव्याघ्रः] a man like a tiger (i. e. as bold as a tiger); @deva[मुखं चन्द्र इव मुखचन्द्रः] a moon-like face i. e. as delightful as the moon; @deva[मुखं कमलमिव मुखकमलम्] &c.; these are called @deva[उपमानोत्तरपदकर्मधारय].

**Note. 1.**—The difference between the two is this:—In the former the common quality of comparison is actually expressed; while in the latter it is left to be understood; in fact if it be expressed there can be no compound; as @deva[पुरुषः व्याघ्र इव शूरः ।]

**Note. 2.**—The above Karmadhārayas may be dissolved as @deva[मुखमेव चन्द्रः मुखचन्द्रः], @deva[मुखमेव कमलं मुखकमलम्], &c. Dissolved in either way there will be no difference in the formation of the compounds; but there will be difference both in the meaning and in the metaphor. In one case prominence will be given to @deva[चन्द्र] or the moon and the figure will be 'upamā (a simile),' while in the other to @deva[मुख] or the face in which case the figure will be 'Rūpaka.'[^4] Compounds like @deva[पाद एवपद्मं पादपद्मम्], @deva[विद्या एव धनं विद्याधनं], &c. are termed @deva[अवधारणापूर्वपदकर्मधारय]s.

[^1]: @deva[उपमानानि सामान्यवचनैः ।] Pāṇ. II. 1. 55.

[^2]: @deva[उपमितं व्याघ्रादिभिः सामान्याप्रयोगे ।] Pāṇ. II. 1. 56.

[^3]: All these words are included under what is called the group of @deva[व्याघ्र]. The more important of these words are @deva[व्याघ्र], @deva[सिंह], @deva[ऋक्ष], @deva[ऋषभ], @deva[चन्दन], @deva[वृक], @deva[वृष], @deva[वराह], @deva[हस्तिन्], @deva[रुरु], @deva[पृषत्], @deva[पुण्डरीक], &c.; @deva[चन्द्र], @deva[पद्म], @deva[कमल], @deva[किसलय], &c.; cf. @deva[स्युरुत्तरपदे व्याघ्रपुंगवर्षभकुञ्जराः । सिंहशार्दूलनागाद्याः पुंसि श्रेष्ठार्थगोचराः ॥] Amara III. 1. 59.

[^4]: So when the common attribute is applicable in its expressed (@deva[वाच्य]) sense to the @deva[उपमेय] as in @deva[मुखपद्मं सहास्यं] dissolve the comp. as @deva[मुखं पद्ममिव] and when to the @deva[उपमान] as in @deva[मुखपद्मं विकसितं] dissolve as @deva[मुखमेव पद्मम्].
