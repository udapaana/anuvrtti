[^1]The infinitive is also used with:—

(a) verbs meaning to be able, to make bold, to know, to be wearied, to strive, to begin, to set about, to bear, to be pleased and to be; @deva[न शक्नोति शिरोधरां धारयितुं] (Kād.) is not able to support his neck; @deva[जानासि कोपं निग्रहीतुं] you know how to restrain anger; @deva[अङ्गदेन समं योद्धुमघटिष्ट] (Bhaṭṭi. XV. 77) he strove to fight with Angada; @deva[गन्तुं व्यवस्येद्भवान्] (Meg. 22) thou wilt try to go; @deva[वक्तुं प्रक्रमेथाः] (Meg. 103.) begin to speak; @deva[अस्ति-भवति-विद्यते वा भोक्तुमन्नं] (Sid. Kau) there is food to eat, &c.

(b) [^2]words like @deva[अलं] and others meaning sufficient or able, proficient or skilled in, &c.; @deva[पर्याप्तोसि प्रजाः पातुं] (Rag. X. 25) thou art able to protect the creation; @deva[कः समर्थो दैवमन्यथा कर्तुं] who is able to change destiny; @deva[प्रासादास्त्वां तुलयितुमलं] (Meg. 66) the palaces are able to stand comparison with thee; @deva[भोक्तुं प्रवीणः कुशलः पटुर्वा] (Sid. Kau.) skilled in eating.

(c) [^3]words having the sense of 'it is time to do anything;' @deva[कालः समयो वेला अनेहा वा भोक्तुम्] (Sid. Kau.) it is time to take food.
