## Periphrastic Perfect Participles

The participles of the Periphrastic Perfect Par. and Atm. are formed by the addition of the Perf. Participial forms of the auxiliary verbs @deva[कृ], @deva[अस्] or @deva[भू] to the base in @deva[आम्] *e. g.* @deva[दयामासिवस्], @deva[उन्दांबभूवस्]. @deva[गण्]—@deva[गणयामासिवस्], @deva[गणयांबभूवस्] &c.
