## Dative with Implied Infinitive

The object governed by an infinitive mood not actually used but implied, is put in the Dative;[^1] @deva[फलेभ्यो याति] i. e. (@deva[फलानि आहर्तुं]) 'goes for (in order to bring) fruits;' @deva[नृसिंहाय नमस्कुर्मः] (i. e @deva[नृसिंहमनुकूलयितुं]) 'We bow down to Nrisimha (to propitiate him)'

(a) The Dative of an abstract noun formed from a root may be used to express the sense of the infinitive of the same root; @deva[यागाय याति] 'goes to perform a sacrifice;' @deva[त्यागाय संभृतार्थानां] (Rag. I. 7.) 'who had amassed wealth in order to give it away,' &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[क्रियार्थोपपदस्य च कर्माणि स्थानिनः । तुमर्थाच्च भाववचनात् ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 14, 15.
