---
key: rule:194
title: "Vowel Changes in Deity Names"
pages: [118.png]
verdict: approved
---
**(a)** When the names of deities well known to be in constant association with each other are compounded, @deva[आ] is substituted for the final vowel of the preceding word, except in the case of the word @deva[वायु];[^1] e.g. @deva[मित्रावरुणौ], @deva[सूर्याचन्द्रमसौ], @deva[अग्नामरुतौ], &c. but @deva[अग्निवायू] or @deva[वाय्वग्नी].

**(b)** The word @deva[अग्नि] followed by the word @deva[सोम] or @deva[वरुण] lengthens its @deva[इ]; as @deva[अग्नीषोमौ], @deva[अग्नीवरुणौ].

---

[^1]: @deva[देवताद्वंद्वे च ।] Pān. VI. 3. 26. @deva[वायुशब्दप्रयोगे प्रतिषेधः ।] Vārt. @deva[ईदग्नेः सोमवरुणयोः ।] Pān. VI. 3. 27.
