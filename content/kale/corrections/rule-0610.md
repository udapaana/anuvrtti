## Anomalous Causal Forms

The following roots form their Causal base anomalously:—

- @deva[इ] 'to go'—@deva[गमयति], with @deva[अधि] 'to remember.'—@deva[अधिगमयति],—'to study' @deva[अध्यापयति]. with @deva[प्रति]—@deva[प्रत्याययति].
- @deva[क्नू] or @deva[क्नूय्] 'to sound,' &c.—@deva[क्नोपयति] 'he causes to sound.'
- @deva[क्ष्माय्] 'to tremble'—@deva[क्ष्माययति] „ „ „ tremble.
- @deva[गुह्] 'to conceal'—@deva[गूहयति] „ „ „ conceal.
- @deva[चि] 5 cl. 'to collect'—@deva[चापयति-ते] @deva[चाययति-ते] „ collect.
- „ 10 cl.—@deva[चपयति-ते] @deva[चययति-ते] „ „
- @deva[जागृ] 'to awake'—@deva[जागरयति] he rouses
- @deva[दुष्]—'to sin, to be unchaste'—@deva[दूषयति-ते] he causes to sin, but @deva[दुषयति-ते], @deva[दोषयति-ते] in the sense of 'he corrupts or makes depraved.'
- @deva[धू] 'to shake'—@deva[धूनयति-ते] he causes to shake.
- @deva[प्री] 'to please'—@deva[प्रीणयति] „ „ „ please.
- @deva[भी] 'to fear.'—@deva[भाययति-ते] he frightens with. @deva[भापयते], @deva[भीषयते] he inspires fear.
- @deva[भ्रस्ज्]—'to fry'—@deva[भर्जयति-ते], @deva[भ्रज्जयति-ते] he causes to fry.
- @deva[मृज्]—'to wipe'—@deva[मार्जयति].
- @deva[रञ्ज्]—'to dye'—@deva[रञ्जयति] he dyes or paints: also in the sense of 'he propitiates or satisfies': cf. '@deva[ब्रह्मापि नरं न रञ्जयति]' Bh. Nî Śa. 3. But @deva[रजयति] only, in the sense of 'he hunts deer'; (Vide Kir. VI. 34).
- @deva[रुह्]—'to grow'—@deva[रोहयति-ते], @deva[रोपयति-ते] he plants or causes to grow.
- @deva[ला]—'to take' and @deva[ली]—'to embrace,' 'to adhere'—@deva[लालयति-ते], @deva[विलापयति-ते]; and @deva[लीनयति], @deva[लापयति] in the sense of 'melting an unctuous substance'
- @deva[वा] 'to blow'—@deva[वापयति] he causes to blow or move. @deva[वाजयति] he shakes.
- @deva[स्मि] 'to smile'—@deva[विस्माययति] he causes a smile by, or astonishes or frightens by. @deva[विस्मापयते] he astonishes.
- @deva[वी]—@deva[वापयति], @deva[वाययति] he causes to conceive; @deva[वाययति-ते] in other senses.
- @deva[शद्] 'to fall'—@deva[शातयति] he causes to fall, cuts down. @deva[शादयति] he causes to go.
- @deva[सिध्] 'to be accomplished'—@deva[साधयति] he accomplishes or prepares. @deva[सेधयति], he makes perfect, &c. with reference to sacred rites or things only; as @deva[सेधयति तापसं तपः] &c.
- @deva[स्फाय्]—'to swell'—@deva[स्फावयति], he causes to swell.
- @deva[स्फुर्]—'to tremble; to shine forth.' @deva[स्फोरयति], @deva[स्फारयति] he causes to tremble or shine
- @deva[हन्] 'to strike or kill'—@deva[घातयति] he causes to strike, &c.
