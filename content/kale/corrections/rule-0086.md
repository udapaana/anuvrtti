## Words ending in @deva[ओ] and @deva[औ].

Vṛddhi (@deva[औ]) is substituted for the @deva[ओ] of words ending in @deva[ओ] in the first five inflections except the Acc. sing.; @deva[आ] is substituted for @deva[ओ] in the Acc. sing. and plural.[^1] Nouns ending in @deva[औ] are regularly declined.

@deva[गो] mas. fem. a bull or a cow.

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. V. | @deva[गौः] | @deva[गावौ] | @deva[गावः] |
| A. | @deva[गाम्] | ,, | @deva[गाः] |
| I. | @deva[गवा] | @deva[गोभ्याम्] | @deva[गोभिः] |
| D. | @deva[गवे] | ,, | @deva[गोभ्यः] |
| Ab. | @deva[गोः] | ,, | ,, |
| G. | ,, | @deva[गवोः] | @deva[गवाम्] |
| L. | @deva[गवि] | ,, | @deva[गोषु] |

Decline similarly @deva[स्मृतो] (@deva[स्मृत उ शंकरः येन]) and @deva[द्यो] f. the sky. The neu. @deva[प्रद्यो] (@deva[प्रकृष्टा द्योः यस्मिन् तत्]) changed to @deva[प्रद्यु] should be declined like @deva[मधु].

@deva[ग्लौ] mas. the moon.

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. V. | @deva[ग्लौः] | @deva[ग्लावौ] | @deva[ग्लावः] |
| A. | @deva[ग्लावम्] | ,, | ,, |
| I. | @deva[ग्लावा] | @deva[ग्लौभ्याम्] | @deva[ग्लौभिः] |
| D. | @deva[ग्लावे] | ,, | @deva[ग्लौभ्यः] |
| Ab. | @deva[ग्लावः] | ,, | ,, |
| G. | ,, | @deva[ग्लावोः] | @deva[ग्लावाम्] |
| L. | @deva[ग्लावि] | ,, | @deva[ग्लौषु] |

Decline similarly @deva[नौ] f. a boat, a ship. The neu. @deva[सुनौ] (@deva[सुष्ठु नौः यस्मिन्]) changed to @deva[सुनु] should be declined like @deva[मधु].
