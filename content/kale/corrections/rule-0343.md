When added to words denoting qualities @deva[मत्] is dropped;[^1] *e. g.* @deva[शुक्लो गुणोऽस्यास्तीति शुक्लः पटः] a white ( *i. e.* possessed of the white colour ) garment, so @deva[कृष्णः] &c.

@deva[य] ( @deva[यप्] )—is added to @deva[रूप] in the sense of 'a stamped coin, or beautiful'; @deva[हिम्य] 'possessing snow, snowy', @deva[गुण्यः] 'possessing merits.'

@deva[युस्]—is added to @deva[ऊर्णा], @deva[शुभं], @deva[अहं], and @deva[शं]; @deva[ऊर्णायुः] 'woolen', @deva[शुभंयुः] 'possessing bliss, fortunate', @deva[अहंयुः] 'proud'; @deva[शंयुः] 'happy.'

@deva[र]—is added to @deva[पाण्डु], @deva[मधु], @deva[सुषि], @deva[ऊष], @deva[नग], @deva[मुष्क], @deva[पांसु], @deva[ख], @deva[सुख] and @deva[कुञ्ज] ( @deva[कुञ्जो हस्तिहनुः] ); @deva[पाण्डुर] 'possessing paleness,' *i. e.* 'pale'; @deva[मधुर] 'sweet,' &c.

@deva[ल] ( @deva[लच्] )—@deva[अंसलः] 'having muscular shoulders' *i. e.* 'strong,' @deva[वत्सल] 'compassionate,' @deva[फेनल] 'foamy'; it is also added to words ending in @deva[आ] and denoting a limb of the body of an animal; @deva[चूडालः] 'having a crest.'

@deva[व]—@deva[केशवः] 'having beautiful and luxuriant hair,' also ( @deva[केशिन्], @deva[केशिक], @deva[केशवत्] in this sense ); @deva[मणिवः] 'a kind of serpent,' @deva[हिरण्यवः] 'one of the nine treasures of Kubera'; @deva[अर्णस्+व=अर्णवः] 'the sea.'

@deva[वल] ( @deva[वलच्] )—@deva[दन्तावलः] 'an elephant'; @deva[शिखावलः] 'a peacock'; @deva[रजस्वला], @deva[कृषीवलः] 'a husbandman,' @deva[आसुतीवलः] 'a sacrificial priest' 'a distiller of spirit'; @deva[परिषद्वलः] 'a king;' and @deva[ऊर्जस्वलः] 'powerful.'

@deva[विन्]—[^2] is added to @deva[माया], @deva[मेधा], @deva[स्रज्] and words ending in @deva[स्]; @deva[मायाविन्] 'a magician;' &c., @deva[मेधाविन्] 'talented,' @deva[स्रग्विन्] 'possessing a garland,' @deva[तेजस्विन्] 'lustrous,' &c.; also to @deva[आमय] the final @deva[अ] being lengthened; @deva[आमयाविन्] 'diseased.'

@deva[श]—@deva[लोमशः] 'hairy,' 'a monkey'; @deva[रोमशः]; @deva[कपिशः] 'tawny.'

N. B.—[^3] These affixes are prohibited after Karmadhāraya compounds.

## SECTION III. Affixes forming Adverbs.

@deva[अक] ( @deva[अकच्] )—is inserted before the final vowel of indeclinables without any change of meaning; @deva[उच्चैः]-@deva[उच्चकैः]; @deva[नीचैः]-@deva[नीचकैः].

@deva[एन]—is added to nouns indicating 'a direction' in the sense of 'not far from'; @deva[पूर्वेण ग्रामं] 'to the east of the village not far from it'; @deva[अपरेण ग्रामं], &c.

@deva[तस्] ( @deva[तासि] )—has the sense of the Ablative; @deva[आदितः] 'from the beginning,' @deva[मध्यतः], @deva[स्वरतः], @deva[वर्णतः] &c.; sometimes this is added in the sense of the Gen.; as @deva[देवा अर्जुनतोभवन्] 'the gods declared themselves on the side of Arjuna'; ( @deva[तासिल्] )—@deva[परितः] 'on all sides'; @deva[अभितः] 'on both sides'.

@deva[ना]—@deva[विना] 'without', @deva[नाना] 'in various ways', &c.

@deva[वत्]—[^4] is affixed in the sense of 'equally with' or 'like to' when the equality or likeness refers to an action; @deva[ब्राह्मणेन तुल्यं ब्राह्मणवदधीते]; but @deva[पुत्रेण तुल्यः स्थूलः] and not @deva[पुत्रवत् स्थूलः]; similarly @deva[क्षत्रियवत्]; or (2) 'as in the case of that' @deva[मथुरायामिव मथुरावत् स्रुघ्ने प्राकारः]; @deva[चैत्रस्य इव चैत्रवत्] ( @deva[मैत्रस्य गावः] ); (3) @deva[विधिमर्हति विधिवत् पूज्यते].

@deva[शस्]—@deva[अल्पशः] 'little by little'; @deva[बहुशः] &c.

@deva[च्वि]—is added to nouns or indeclinables to express that a person or thing, not being like what is denoted by the base to which it is added, becomes or is made like it.[^5]

[^6] Before this the ending @deva[अ] or @deva[आ] is changed to @deva[ई], but not that of indeclinables; @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] is lengthened and @deva[ऋ] changed to @deva[री]; final @deva[न्] and the ending consonant of @deva[अरुस्], @deva[मनस्], @deva[चक्षुस्], @deva[चेतस्], @deva[रहस्], and @deva[रजस्] are dropped and then the preceding rules are applied to the penultimate vowel. After this the termination is dropped and the verbal or other forms of @deva[कृ], @deva[भू] and @deva[अस्] are added to the base regarded as a preposition; @deva[अकृष्णः कृष्णः सम्पद्यते तं करोति] ( @deva[कृष्ण+च्वि=कृष्णी+च्वि=कृष्णी+करोति] ) @deva[कृष्णीकरोति]; @deva[न ब्रह्मा अब्रह्मा अब्रह्मा ब्रह्मा सम्पद्यते ब्रह्मीभवति]; similarly @deva[गङ्गीस्यात्]; @deva[दोषाभूतमहः] 'the day is changed into the night', @deva[दिवाभूता रात्रि] 'the night has become like the day;' @deva[शुचीभवति], @deva[पटूस्यात्], @deva[मात्रीभवति], @deva[मात्रीकरोति], &c. @deva[अरूकरोति], @deva[उन्मनीस्यात्], @deva[उच्चक्षूकरोति], @deva[विचेतीकरोति], @deva[विरजीकरोति], &c.

@deva[आ]—is affixed like @deva[च्वि] to (1) @deva[दुःख] when the meaning is 'troubling one who ought not to be troubled'; @deva[दुःखाकरोति स्वामिनम्];—(2) @deva[सुख] and @deva[प्रिय] in the sense of 'pleasing one who ought to be pleased,' &c.; @deva[सुखाकरोति], @deva[प्रियाकरोति गुरुं] ( @deva[अनुकूलाचरणेन आनन्दयति] Sid. Kau. );—(3) @deva[शूल]; @deva[शूलाकरोति मांसम्] *i. e.* 'roasts it';—(4) @deva[सत्यः] @deva[सत्याकरोति भाण्डं वणिक्] *i. e.* 'settles its price';—(5) and to onomatopœic words not followed by @deva[इति] and containing more than one vowel, the words being reduplicated; @deva[पटत्]—@deva[पटपटाकरोति] 'utters the words @deva[पटत्], @deva[पटत्].'

@deva[सात्]—is optionally affixed like @deva[च्वि] but when the change meant is complete;[^7] ( @deva[कृत्स्नं शस्त्रं अग्निः सम्पद्यते] ) @deva[अग्निसाद्भवति] 'is completely changed to fire;' ( also @deva[अग्नीभवति] ); @deva[भस्मसात् करोति] 'completely reduces to ashes;' in the case of this affix the forms of @deva[पद्] with @deva[सम्] are also added; @deva[अग्निसात्सम्पद्यते] @deva[अग्निसाद्भवति शस्त्रम्], @deva[जलसात्सम्पद्यते], @deva[जलीभवति लवणम्]; @deva[सात्] also conveys the sense of 'making over or delivering something to another' &c.; @deva[राजसात्सम्पद्यते], @deva[राजसात्करोति]; @deva[सात्] and @deva[त्रा] are similarly added when something is to be given to another; @deva[विप्रत्राकरोति], @deva[विप्रत्रासम्पद्यते]; @deva[विप्रसात्करोति] &c.

N. B. The derivatives formed by means of the affix @deva[सात्] do not share the properties of prepositions; the verbal indecl., therefore, from @deva[अग्निसात्करोति] is @deva[अग्निसात्कृत्वा] and not—@deva[कृत्य].

Adverbs of Time Irregularly formed from Nouns:—

@deva[सामने अहनि सद्यः] 'the same day'; @deva[पूर्वस्मिन्वत्सरे परुत्] 'last year'; @deva[पूर्वतरे वत्सरे परारि] 'the year before the last;' @deva[अस्मिन्संवत्सरे ऐषमः] 'this year;' @deva[परस्मिन्नहनि परेद्यवि] 'the other day', @deva[अस्मिन्नहनि अद्य] 'to-day'; @deva[पूर्वस्मिन्नहनि पूर्वेद्युः] 'yesterday;' @deva[अन्यस्मिन्नहनि अन्येद्युः] 'the other or following day;' @deva[उभयोरह्नोः उभय-ये-द्युः] 'both the days.'
