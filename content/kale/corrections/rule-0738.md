Roots ending in @deva[उ], short or long, take the affix @deva[ण्यत्] in the sense of 'what ought or must necessarily be done;' @deva[लू]—@deva[लाव्यं] 'what must necessarily be cut off;' @deva[पाव्यं] 'what must necessarily be purified;' @deva[सू] with @deva[आ]—@deva[आसाव्यं], @deva[यु] 'to mix,' @deva[याव्य], &c.

(a) The roots @deva[वप्], @deva[रप्], @deva[लप्], @deva[भप्] and @deva[चप्] also do the same; @deva[वाप्यं] 'what must necessarily be sown;' @deva[राप्यम्], 'what must be spoken of distinctly;' @deva[लाप्यम्], @deva[भाप्यम्], @deva[चाप्यम्].
