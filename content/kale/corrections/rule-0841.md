## Ablative with @deva[अप], @deva[परि], @deva[आ], @deva[प्रति]

The ablative is used with—[^1]

(a) The prepositions @deva[अप] and @deva[परि] meaning 'away from, without' and @deva[आ] meaning 'as far as' or 'including, comprehending'; @deva[यत्संप्रत्यप लोकेभ्यो लंकायां वसतिर्भयात्] (Rām.) now that he dwelt in Lankā, in terror, away from the worlds; @deva[अप हरेः संसारः] *Samsāra* exists outside Hari; @deva[अप त्रिगर्तेभ्यो वृष्टो देवः] it rained everywhere, except in the country of Trigarta. Similarly @deva[परि हरेः संसारः परि त्रिगर्तेभ्यो वृष्टो देवः] (Vop.) &c. @deva[आमुक्ते संसार], @deva[आसकलाद् ब्रह्म] Brahman pervades every thing; @deva[आ परितोषाद् विदुषां] until the learned are satisfied.

(b) @deva[प्रति] meaning 'the representative of, in exchange for or giving in return for;' @deva[प्रद्युम्नः कृष्णात्प्रति] (Sid. Kau.) Pradyumna is the representative of Krishna, @deva[तिलेभ्यः प्रतियच्छति माषान्] gives māshas in return for sesamum.
