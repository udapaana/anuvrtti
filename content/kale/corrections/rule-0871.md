## Ablative/Locative for Interval of Time or Space

[^1] Words expressive of the interval of time or space are used with the Ablative or the Locative; @deva[अद्य भुक्त्वायं द्व्यहे द्व्यहाद्वा भोक्ता] having dined to-day he will dine again after two days; @deva[इहस्थोयं क्रोशात् क्रोशे वा लक्ष्यं विध्येत्] standing here he will hit a mark two miles distant.

---

[^1]: @deva[सप्तमीपञ्चम्यौ कारकमध्ये ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 5.
