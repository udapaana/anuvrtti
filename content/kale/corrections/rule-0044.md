@deva[च्] is necessarily inserted between @deva[छ्] and the preceding vowel, short or long; and optionally when a long vowel at the end of a word, except that of the particle @deva[मा] and the preposition @deva[आ], precedes it;[^1] e. g. @deva[शिव] + @deva[छाया] = @deva[शिवच्छाया] Śiva's shadow; so @deva[स्वच्छाया]; @deva[चेच्छियते] what is cut again and again; @deva[लक्ष्मी] + @deva[छाया] = @deva[लक्ष्मीछाया] or @deva[लक्ष्मीच्छाया]; @deva[मा] + @deva[छिदत्] = @deva[माच्छिदत्] may he not cut; @deva[आ] + @deva[छादयति] = @deva[आच्छादयति] he covers.

---

[^1]: @deva[छे च]। Pāṇ. VI. 1. 73. Properly @deva[त्] is inserted, but it is changed to @deva[च्] by @ref[28]. @deva[आङ्माङोश्च। दीर्घात्। पदान्ताद्वा]। Pāṇ. VI. 1. 74-76.
