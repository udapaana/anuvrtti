## II. ADVERBS

§ 372. Adverbs are either primitive or derived from nouns, pronouns or numerals. As adverbs may also be further used the Acc. sing. neu. of nouns and adjectives; and sometimes the sing. of other cases also; @deva[सत्यम्] 'truthfully,' @deva[मृदु] 'softly,' @deva[सुखम्] 'happily,' @deva[लघु] 'quickly,' @deva[निर्भरम्], @deva[अवश्यम्], @deva[अत्यन्तम्], @deva[बलवत्] 'strongly,' @deva[भूयः], 'again' &c.; @deva[दुःखेन] 'painfully,' @deva[सुखेन], @deva[धर्मेण] 'virtuously, justly,' @deva[दक्षिणेन], @deva[उत्तरेण], @deva[अशेषेण], @deva[चिरेण] 'after a long time,' @deva[क्षणेन] &c.; @deva[चिराय], @deva[चिररात्राय] 'for a long time,' @deva[अर्थाय] 'for the sake of,' @deva[बलात्] 'forcibly,' @deva[हर्षात्], @deva[शोकात्], @deva[दूरात्], @deva[तस्मात्], @deva[कस्मात्] &c.; @deva[चिरात्] 'for a long time,' @deva[दूरात्], @deva[उत्तरात्] &c.; @deva[स्थाने] 'properly,' @deva[दूरे], @deva[प्रभाते], @deva[प्राह्णे], @deva[अग्रे], @deva[एकपदे] 'at once,' @deva[सपदि], @deva[ऋते], @deva[समीपे], @deva[अभ्यासे] 'near,' &c.

N. B. Adverbs formed from pronouns and numerals are given in their proper places; while those formed from nouns are given in Chapter IX.
