## Genitive with Words of Equality/Likeness

[^1] In the case of words denoting equality or likeness, such as @deva[तुल्य], @deva[सदृश], &c., the person or thing with whom or which any object is compared is put in the Genitive case, except in the case of @deva[तुला] and @deva[उपमा]; @deva[तुल्यः सदृशः समो वा कृष्णस्य कृष्णेन वा] equal to or like Krishna; @deva[कोन्योस्ति सदृशो मम] who else is equal to me? but @deva[कृष्णस्य तुला उपमा वा नास्ति] (Sid. Kau.)

*Obs.*—The words @deva[तुला] and @deva[उपमा], however, are found used by good authors with the Instrumental against Pāṇini's rule; @deva[तुलां यदारोहति दन्तवाससा] (Kum. V. 34.) which rises to the high position of being compared with your lip; @deva[स्फुटोपमं भूतिसितेन शंभुना] (Sis. I. 4.) clearly deserving to be compared with Śambhu white with ashes; see Rag. VIII. 15.

---

[^1]: @deva[तुल्यार्थैरतुलोपमाभ्यां तृतीयान्यतरस्याम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 72.
