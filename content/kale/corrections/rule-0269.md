title: "Formation Rules for Avyayībhāva"
pages: [163.png, 164.png]
verdict: approved
---
In forming the Avyayībhāva compounds the following rules should be observed:—

**(a)** The final long vowel is shortened, and the ending @deva[ए] or @deva[ऐ] is changed to @deva[इ] and @deva[ओ] to @deva[उ]; @deva[गोपायति गाः पातीति वा गोपाः] । @deva[तस्मिन्निति अधिगोपम्], @deva[अनुविष्णु] after Vishnu, @deva[उपगु] near a cow, &c.

**(b)** The final @deva[न्] of *mas.* and *fem.* nouns in @deva[अन्] is dropped; and optionally that of *neuter* nouns ending in @deva[अन्];[^1] @deva[उपराजम्]; @deva[उपचर्मम्-र्म].

**(c)** @deva[अ] is optionally substituted for the final of @deva[नदी], @deva[पौर्णमासी], @deva[आग्रहायणी] and @deva[गिरि];[^2] @deva[उपनदम्-दि], @deva[उपपौर्णमासम्-सि], @deva[उपाग्रहायणम्-णि] 'near to the full-moon day of Mārgas'irsha'; @deva[उपगिरम्-रि].

**(d)** It is also optionally added to all nouns ending in any of the first four letters of a class;[^3] @deva[उपसमिधम्-मित्].

**(e)** @deva[अ] is attached to the final of the following nouns when compounded in an Avyaya.[^4]:—@deva[शरद्], @deva[विपाश्], @deva[अनस्], @deva[मनस्], @deva[उपानह्], @deva[अनडुह्], @deva[दिव्], @deva[हिमवत्], @deva[दिश्], @deva[दृश्], @deva[विश्], @deva[चेतस्], @deva[चतुर्], @deva[तद्], @deva[यद्], @deva[कियत्], @deva[जरस्] ( substituted for @deva[जरा] ) &c; @deva[शरदः समीपम् उपशरदम्], @deva[प्रतिविपाशम्], 'towards the Vipāśā;' @deva[दिशोर्मध्ये अपदिशम्] between two divisions of the compass, @deva[उपजरसम्] towards old age, @deva[अध्यात्मम्] on the soul, &c.; and to @deva[अक्षि] when preceded by @deva[प्रति], @deva[पर] changed to @deva[परो], @deva[सम्] and @deva[अनु], the @deva[इ] of @deva[अक्षि] being dropped; @deva[अक्ष्णः प्रति प्रत्यक्षम्] before one's eyes; @deva[अक्ष्ण परं परोक्षम्] out of sight; @deva[समक्षम्], and @deva[अन्वक्षम्] afterwards, forthwith.

---

[^1]: @deva[अनश्च] । @deva[नपुंसकादन्यतरस्याम्] Pāṇ. V. 4. 108, 109.

[^2]: @deva[नदीपौर्णमास्याग्रहायणीभ्यः] । @deva[गिरेश्च सेनकस्य] Pāṇ. V. 4. 110, 112.

[^3]: @deva[झयः] । Pāṇ. V. 4. 111.

[^4]: @deva[अव्ययीभावे शरत्प्रभृतिभ्यः] । Pāṇ. V. 4. 107.
