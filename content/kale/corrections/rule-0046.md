Visarga, followed by @deva[च्], @deva[छ्], @deva[ट्], @deva[ठ्], and @deva[त्], @deva[थ्], themselves not followed by a sibilant, is changed to @deva[स्];[^1] e. g. @deva[विष्णुस्त्राता] Viṣṇu, the protector: @deva[हरिश्चरति], Hari walks: @deva[रामष्टीकते] Rāma goes; (Vide @ref[28].); but @deva[कः त्सरुः] (as the @deva[त्] is followed by @deva[स्]); and optionally when followed by @deva[श्], @deva[ष्] or @deva[स्];[^2] @deva[रामः स्थाता] or @deva[रामस्स्थाता] Rāma who would stand; @deva[हरिःशेते] or @deva[हरिश्शेते] &c.

**(a)** Visarga, not belonging to an indeclinable is changed to @deva[स्] when preceded by @deva[अ], and to @deva[ष्], when preceded by @deva[इ] or @deva[उ], short or long and followed by the terminations @deva[पाश], @deva[कल्प], @deva[क] and @deva[काम्य];[^3] e. g. @deva[पयस्पाशम्] bad milk; @deva[यशस्कल्पम्] little short of fame: @deva[यशस्कम्] with fame; @deva[यशस्काम्यति] he desires fame; but @deva[प्रातःकल्पम्] almost dawn (where @deva[प्रातः] is an indec.); @deva[सर्पिष्पाशम्] bad ghee, @deva[सर्पिष्कल्पम्], @deva[सर्पिष्कम्], @deva[सर्पिष्काम्यति]. If the visarga, however, is a substitute for a final @deva[र्], it is not changed to @deva[स्] before @deva[काम्य];[^4] @deva[गीः काम्यति] speech desires.

**(b)** The Visarga of @deva[नमः] and @deva[पुरः] when prefixed as indeclinables to verbs beginning with @deva[क्] or @deva[ख्] and @deva[प्] or @deva[फ्] is optionally changed to @deva[स्] in the case of the former and necessarily in that of the latter;[^5] e. g. @deva[नमस्करोति] or @deva[नमःकरोति], @deva[पुरस्करोति] puts in the front; but @deva[पुरः प्रवेष्टव्याः] towns fit to be entered (as @deva[पुर्] is a noun).

**(c)** Visarga, preceded by @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] and not belonging to a termination, is changed to @deva[ष्] except in the case of @deva[मुहुः]; often, if followed by a hard consonant of the guttural or the labial class;[^6] @deva[निः] + @deva[प्रत्यूह] = @deva[निष्प्रत्यूहम्] without obstacle. @deva[आविष्कृतम्] disclosed; @deva[दुष्कृतम्] a bad action; but @deva[मुहुः कृतम्]; @deva[आग्निः करोति] as the Visarga is substituted for @deva[स्] a term. so @deva[मातुः कृपा] and not @deva[मातुष्कृपा]. @deva[भ्रातुष्पुत्रः] is an exception.

**(d)** @deva[तिरस्][^7] optionally retains its @deva[स्] when followed by @deva[क्], @deva[ख्], @deva[प्] or @deva[फ्]; @deva[तिरः करोति] or @deva[तिरस्करोति] hides or scorns

@deva[द्विः], @deva[त्रिः] and @deva[चतुः]; all of them adverbs showing frequency, change their Visarga to @deva[ष्] optionally under the same circumstances;[^8] @deva[द्विष्करोति] or @deva[द्विःकरोति] does twice; but @deva[चतुष्कपालम्] having four parts (where @deva[चतुः] is not an adverb).

**(e)** The ending @deva[इः] or @deva[उः] of a word changes its visarga to @deva[ष्] optionally under the same circumstances when the presence of the following word is necessary for the completion of the sense;[^9] @deva[सर्पिष्करोति] or @deva[सर्पिःकरोति] turns into ghee; @deva[धनुष्करोति] or @deva[धनुःकरोति] makes a bow; but @deva[तिष्ठतु सर्पिः पिब स्वमुकदम्], as there is no connection here between the words @deva[सर्पिः] and @deva[पिब].

And necessarily when at the end of the first member of a compound; as @deva[सर्पिष्कुण्डिका] a pot for clarified butter; but @deva[परमसर्पिः कुण्डिका] a big ghee-pot (as the word @deva[सर्पिः] is an *uttara pada*).

**(f)** Visarga coming after the @deva[अ] of a word other than an indeclinable and forming the first member of a compound is changed to @deva[स्] when followed by a derivative of the roots @deva[कृ], @deva[कम्], and by @deva[कंस], @deva[कुंभ], @deva[पाश], @deva[कुशा] and @deva[कर्णी];[^10] e. g. @deva[अयस्कारः] an iron-smith; @deva[अयस्कामः] one who desires iron; @deva[अयस्कंसम्] an iron pot; @deva[अयस्पात्रम्]; @deva[अयस्कुशा] a bridle; @deva[अयस्कर्णी] a kind of iron pot; but @deva[गीःकारः] Bṛhaspati; @deva[स्वःकामः] one desiring heaven (where @deva[स्वर्] is an indec.); @deva[यशः करोति] (as there is no comp.); and @deva[परमयशःकारः] producing great fame (as the word @deva[यशस्] is not the first member of the com.).

**(g)** The words @deva[अधः] and @deva[शिरः] change their visarga to @deva[स्] when followed by @deva[पद] under the same circumstances as those mentioned above;[^11] @deva[अधस्पदम्], @deva[शिरस्पदम्] but @deva[अधःपदं] when there is no comp.; @deva[परमशिरः पदम्] (as @deva[शिर] is the latter member @deva[उत्तरपद]).
