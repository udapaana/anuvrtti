title: "Optional Feminine of @deva[मनु]"
pages: [189.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] The *feminine* of @deva[मनु] is optionally formed by adding @deva[ई] before which the final @deva[उ] is changed to @deva[औ] or @deva[ऐ]; @deva[मनावी], @deva[मनायी] or @deva[मनुः] ।

---

[^1]: @deva[मनोरौ वा] । Pân. IV. 1. 38. @deva[मनुशब्दस्यौकारादेशः स्यादुदात्तैकारश्च] । Sid. Kau.
