---
key: rule:476
title: "Insertion of @deva[न्] in @deva[मस्ज्] and @deva[नश्]"
pages: [300.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[न्] is inserted before the ending consonant of the root @deva[मस्ज्] before which @deva[स्] is dropped, and after the vowel of @deva[नश्] when they are followed by any consonant except a nasal or a semi-vowel: @deva[मङ्क्ता] &c.; @deva[नंष्टा], @deva[नशिता]. The @deva[स्] of @deva[मस्ज्] is changed to @deva[ज्] when not dropped.
