title: "Active Use of Past Passive Participles"
pages: [510.png, 511.png]
verdict: approved
---
Many past passive participles are used actively in which case they may govern the Accusative case, like a Perfect tense active; @deva[आरूढमद्रीन्] (Rag. VI. 77.) which had ascended the mountains; similarly @deva[गगनमध्यमारूढः सविता]; @deva[आपादमुत्तीर्णः] crossed i. e. got over the calamity; @deva[यमुनाकच्छमवतीर्णः] descended to the bank of the Yamunā, &c.
