Roots ending in @deva[आ][^1] (@deva[ए], @deva[ऐ] and @deva[ओ] changeable to @deva[आ]) and beginning with a conjunct consonant containing a semi-vowel also substitute @deva[न] for @deva[त]; @deva[द्रा] 'to run, to sleep,' @deva[द्राण]; @deva[ग्लै] 'to fade,' @deva[ग्लान]; @deva[स्त्यै]-@deva[स्त्यान] 'collected to a mass,' &c

**Exceptions:—**@deva[ख्या] 'to name,' &c. @deva[ध्यै] 'to contemplate,' @deva[व्ये] and @deva[ह्वे]; @deva[ख्यात], @deva[ध्यात], @deva[वीत] and @deva[हूत].

[^1]: @deva[रदायां निष्ठातो नः पूर्वस्य च दः । संयोगादेरातो धातोर्यण्वतः । ल्वादिभ्यः ।] Pāṇ. VIII. 2. 42–44.
