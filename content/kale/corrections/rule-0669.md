## Formation of Present Ātmanepada Participle

The participles of the Present Atm. is formed by adding @deva[आन] to the root which undergoes the same changes before it, as before the termination @deva[अते] or @deva[अन्ते] of the 3rd pl. pre. @deva[आन] is changed to @deva[मान], in the case of roots of the 1st, 4th, 6th and 10th classes, and all derived roots, or rather when the base ends in @deva[अ], before which the @deva[अ] of the base remains unchanged; *e. g.* @deva[एध्] (1. cl.) @deva[एधमान] 'growing;' @deva[वन्द्] (1 cl.) @deva[वन्दमान] 'saluting'; @deva[शी] (2. cl.) @deva[शयान] 'lying down;' @deva[द्विष्] (2. cl.) @deva[द्विषाण], @deva[हन्] with @deva[आ] (2. cl) @deva[आघ्नान] 'killing;' @deva[धा] (3. cl.) @deva[दधान] 'holding; @deva[हु] (3. cl.) @deva[जुह्वान]; @deva[दिव्] (4. cl.) @deva[दीव्यमान] 'playing;' @deva[सु] (5. cl.) @deva[सुन्वान] 'extracting;' @deva[तुद्] (6. cl.) @deva[तुदमान] 'giving pain;' @deva[रुध्] (7. cl.) @deva[रुन्धान] 'obstructing;' @deva[कृ] (8. cl) @deva[कुर्वाण] 'doing;' @deva[तन्] (8. cl.) @deva[तन्वान] 'stretching;' @deva[क्री] (9. cl.) @deva[क्रीणान] 'buying;' @deva[चुर्] (10, cl) @deva[चोरयमाण] 'stealing;' &c. @deva[बुध्] (1. cl)—cau. @deva[बोधयति]-@deva[बोधयमान] 'causing to know;'—desiderative @deva[बुबोधिषति]-@deva[बुबोधिषमाण] 'being desirous to know,' &c.
