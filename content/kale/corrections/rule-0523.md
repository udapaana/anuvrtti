## Substitution of @deva[पी] for @deva[प्यै] in Perfect and Frequentative

@deva[पी] is substituted for @deva[प्यै] 'to grow fat' in the Perfect and in the Frequentative; @deva[पिप्ये] 1 sing., @deva[पिप्यिध्वे]-@deva[ढ्वे] 2 pl.
