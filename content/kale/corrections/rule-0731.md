The roots @deva[पू], @deva[नी] and @deva[जि], preceded by @deva[वि], take this affix, when they are connected with the words @deva[मुञ्ज], @deva[कल्क] and @deva[हल] respectively, @deva[विपूयो मुञ्जः] 'The Munja grass to be prepared for weaving into a rope' (@deva[मुञ्जादिकरणाय शोधयितव्य इत्यर्थः] Sid. Kau.); @deva[विनीयः कल्कः] 'sin which ought to be destroyed;' @deva[विजित्यो हलिः] 'The plough to be pulled with great force,' @deva[बलेन कृष्टव्यः], in other cases these take the affix @deva[यत्]; @deva[विपत्य], @deva[विनेय], @deva[जेय].
