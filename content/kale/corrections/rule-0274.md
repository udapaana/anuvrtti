key: rule:274
title: "Optional Avyayībhāva Compounds with Ablative/Accusative"
pages: [165.png, 166.png]
verdict: approved
---
**(a)** The prepositions @deva[अप], @deva[परि] and @deva[बहिः] and words derived from the root @deva[अञ्च्] ( @deva[प्राक्], @deva[प्रत्यच्], @deva[उदच्], @deva[अवाच्], @deva[तिर्यच्], &c. ), are optionally compounded with a noun in the Ablative;[^1] @deva[अपविष्णु] or @deva[अपविष्णोः] 'away from Vishṇu'; @deva[परिविष्णु] or @deva[परिविष्णोः]; @deva[बहिर्वनम्] or @deva[बहिर्वनात्]; @deva[प्राग्वनम्] or @deva[प्राग्वनात्] 'to the east of the forest' &c

**(b)** @deva[आ] showing limit, inclusive and exclusive, is optionally compounded with a noun in the Ablative; and so are @deva[अनु], @deva[अभि], and @deva[प्रति] all meaning 'towards' with a noun in the Accusative;[^2] @deva[आमुक्ति] or @deva[आमुक्तेः] 'until final liberation' @deva[संसारः], @deva[आबालं] or @deva[आबालेभ्यः] up to the child; @deva[हरिभक्तिः]; @deva[अभ्यग्नि] or @deva[अग्निमभि] towards the fire @deva[शलभाः पतन्ति]; @deva[प्रत्यग्नि] or @deva[अग्निं प्रति], @deva[अनुवनम्] or @deva[वनमनु].

**(c)** @deva[अनु] meaning 'towards' or 'alongside of,' 'lengthwise,' is also similarly compounded; @deva[अनुवनम्] ( @deva[वनस्य समीपं अशनिर्गतः] 'the thunderbolt passed ) towards the forest'; @deva[गङ्गाया अनु अनुगङ्गम् वाराणसी] 'Varânasi is alongside the Ganges' ( @deva[गंगादैर्घ्यसदृशदैर्घ्योपलक्षिता इत्यर्थः] S. K. )

---

[^1]: @deva[विभाषा] । @deva[अपपरिबहिरञ्चवः पञ्चम्या] Pāṇ. II. 1. 11-12.

[^2]: @deva[आङ्मर्यादाभिविध्योः] । @deva[लक्षणेनाभिप्रती आभिमुख्ये] । @deva[अनुर्यत्समया] । @deva[यस्य चायामः] Pāṇ. II. 1. 13-16.
