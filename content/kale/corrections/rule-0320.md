---
key: rule:320
title: "Optional @deva[ई] for @deva[उ]-Ending Adjectives"
pages: [188.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] Adjectives ending in @deva[उ] not preceded by a conjunct consonant, except @deva[खरु], take @deva[ई] optionally; *e g* @deva[मृदु-मृदुः-द्वी], @deva[पटुः-ट्वी], @deva[बहुः-ह्वी]; but @deva[खरुः] 'a girl who chooses her husband' ( @deva[पतिवरा कन्या] Sid. Kau. ), @deva[पाण्डुः] and not @deva[पाण्ड्वी] as the @deva[उ] is preceded by a conjunct consonant; @deva[आखुः] as it is a noun.

---

[^1]: @deva[वोतो गुणवचनात्] Pân. IV. 1. 44. @deva[खरुसंयोगोपधान्न] । Vârtika.
