The following roots insert @deva[इ] optionally:—

(a) @deva[दम्], @deva[शम्], @deva[पुर्], @deva[दस्], @deva[स्पश्], @deva[छद्], @deva[ज्ञप्], @deva[रुष्], @deva[अम्], @deva[घुष्], with @deva[सम्], @deva[स्वन्] with @deva[आ], and @deva[हृष्] 4. 1. Par. when used with @deva[लोमन्] 'the hair,' or when it means 'to be surprised or disappointed;' @deva[दान्त], @deva[दमित]; (see @ref[696] a.); @deva[शान्त], @deva[शमित]; @deva[पूर्ण] (see @ref[688]), @deva[पूरित]; @deva[दस्त] 'wasted, perished,' @deva[दासित]; @deva[स्पष्ट], @deva[स्पशित]; @deva[छन्न], @deva[छादित]; @deva[ज्ञप्त], @deva[ज्ञपित]; @deva[रुष्ट], @deva[रुषित]; @deva[आन्त] (See @ref[696] a), @deva[अमित]; @deva[सङ्घुष्ट] @deva[सङ्घुषित]; @deva[आस्वान्त], @deva[आस्वनित]; @deva[हृष्ट]-@deva[हृषित]-@deva[लोमन्] 'horripilated with joy,' @deva[हृष्टो]-@deva[हृषितो मैत्रः] @deva[विस्मितः प्रतिहतो वा].

(b) @deva[क्लिश्] and @deva[पू] take @deva[इ] optionally before @deva[त] and @deva[त्वा]; @deva[क्लिष्ट], @deva[क्लिशित]; @deva[पूत], @deva[पवित].

(c) The following roots admit of @deva[इ] optionally before @deva[त] when the P. P. Participle is used impersonally, or conveys the sense of beginning to perform the action or to undergo the state expressed by the root; @deva[तृष्], @deva[स्वर्], @deva[धृष्], @deva[फल्], @deva[भिद्], @deva[मूर्च्छ्], @deva[स्विद्], 1. 4. cl. @deva[स्फुर्च्छ्], @deva[स्फुर्ज्]. @deva[क्ष्विद्] and @deva[क्ष्विद्] 1. 4. P. 1 A.; @deva[स्विद्]-@deva[प्रस्वेदितः] or @deva[प्रस्विन्नः चैत्रः] 'Chaitra has begun to perspire;' @deva[प्रस्वेदितं] or @deva[प्रस्विन्नमनेन] 'he has perspired,' &c.: @deva[स्विद्] 4 cl. has @deva[स्विदित] also.

N. B. When the participle is not used in the sense given above these roots reject @deva[इ]; @deva[क्ष्विद्]-@deva[क्ष्विण्ण]; 'perspired, sounded inarticulately,' &c.
