---
key: rule:646
title: "Non-Desiderative Uses of @deva[य] Nominal Verbs"
pages: [392.png]
verdict: approved
---
This form of the Nominal verb has not always a desiderative meaning.

(a) The affix @deva[य] is added in the sense of 'treating or considering like;' @deva[पुत्रीयति छात्रम्] 'he treats the pupil as a son,' @deva[विष्णूयति द्विजम्] 'he treats the Brâhmaṇa like Vishṇu,' @deva[प्रासादीयति कुट्यां भिक्षुः] 'the beggar considers his hut a palace,' @deva[कुटीयति प्रासादे राजा] 'the king in his palace considers himself to be in a hut.'

(b) It is added to @deva[नमस्], @deva[वरिवस्] and @deva[चित्र] in the senses of 'adoring,' 'serving' and 'striking with wonder' respectively, @deva[नमस्यति देवान्] 'he adores the gods', @deva[वरिवस्यति गुरुम्] 'he serves the preceptor', @deva[चित्रीयते लोकान्] 'he strikes the people with wonder.' It is also added to @deva[तपस्] in the sense of 'practising,' @deva[तपस्यति].
