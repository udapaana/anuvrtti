## Relative Agrees with its Antecedent

The relative agrees with its antecedent in gender, number and person, the cases of the relative and its antecedent being determined by their relation to their respective clauses; @deva[यस्यास्ति वित्तं स नरः कुलीनः] he who has wealth has a noble family; @deva[यस्य बुद्धिर्बलं तस्यः यधेन युज्यते लोके बुधस्तत्तेन योजयेत्] what, which is fit to be united with anything, a wise man should unite with it &c.
