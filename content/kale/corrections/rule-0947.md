title: "Imperative with @deva[मा] for Present Sense"
pages: [526.png]
verdict: approved
---
The Imperative has sometimes the force of the present when used in combination with the particle @deva[मा]; @deva[मा भवतु] no, it is not so; @deva[मा च ते नियतः शत्रुन्मन्युर्भवतु पार्थिव।]
