title: "Radical @deva[व्] Changing to @deva[ऊ] Before @deva[त] or @deva[न]"
pages: [426.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "first root वर् (possibly बर्) blurred"
---
A radical @deva[व्] preceding or following a vowel is sometimes changed to @deva[ऊ] before @deva[त] or @deva[न]; when it is preceded by @deva[र्] it is dropped; @deva[वर्]—@deva[ऊर्ण], @deva[त्वर्]—@deva[तूर्ण], @deva[तुर्व्]—@deva[तूर्ण], @deva[सिव्]—@deva[स्यूत], @deva[दिव्]—@deva[द्यूत] or @deva[द्यून] (See @ref[693], c.).
