## Exceptions and Special Uses of Frequentatives

(a)[^1] A Frequentative may be formed from @deva[अट्] 'to go,' @deva[ऋ] 'to go,' @deva[अश्] 'to eat' and @deva[ऊर्णु] 'to conceal,' though these begin with a vowel; and from @deva[सूच्] cl. 10 'to hint,' @deva[सूत्र्] cl. 10 'to string together,' and @deva[मूत्र्] cl. 10. though they belong to the 10th cl.

(b) The Frequentative of roots signifying motion conveys the notion of tortuous motion and not of repetition.[^2] The Frequentative of roots @deva[लुप्] 'to cut,' @deva[सद्] 'to sink down,' @deva[चर्] 'to go,' @deva[जप्] 'to mutter prayers,' @deva[जभ्] 'to yawn,' @deva[दह्] 'to burn,' @deva[दंश्] 'to bite,' and @deva[गॄ] 'to swallow,' imply reproach on the manner of doing the act;[^3] @deva[लोलुप्यते] 'he cuts awkwardly,' @deva[सासद्यते] 'falls down badly,' @deva[चञ्चूर्यते] &c.

[^1]: @deva[सूचिसूत्रिमूत्र्यट्यर्त्यशूर्णोतिभ्यो यङ् वाच्यः] । Vārt. on the above.

[^2]: @deva[नित्यं कौटिल्ये गतौ] । Pāṇ. III. 1. 23.

[^3]: @deva[लुपसदचरजभदहदशगॄभ्यो भावगर्हायाम्] । Pāṇ III. 1. 24
