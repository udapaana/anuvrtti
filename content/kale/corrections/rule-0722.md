title: "@deva[हन्] Taking @deva[यत्] or @deva[ण्यत्] Optionally"
pages: [433.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[हन्] takes the affix @deva[यत्] optionally, before which @deva[वध्] is substituted for it; @deva[हन्+य=वध्य]='what ought to be killed;' it optionally takes @deva[ण्यत्], in which case @deva[घात्] is substituted for it; @deva[घात्यः].
