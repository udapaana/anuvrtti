The roots @deva[गद्], @deva[मद्], @deva[चर्] and @deva[यम्], take the affix @deva[य] when not preceded by a preposition; @deva[गद्]—@deva[गद्य] 'what ought to be spoken or told;' @deva[मद्य], @deva[चर्य], @deva[यम्य]. @deva[चर्] with @deva[आ] may take this affix, if it does not mean 'a preceptor;' in which case it takes the affix @deva[ण्यत्] (@deva[य]) @deva[आचर्यो देशः] 'a country fit to be gone to, but @deva[आचार्यः] 'a preceptor.'
