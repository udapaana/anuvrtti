## (f) Potential Participles (and Verbal Adjectives).

The Potential Participle is formed by means of the affixes @deva[तव्य][^1], @deva[अनीय], @deva[त] and rarely @deva[एलिम], added to a root or derivative verb. This is Passive when the verb is transitive and impersonal when the verb is intransitive. It is also used like an adjective when denoting fitness, &c.

[^1]: @deva[तव्यत्तव्यानीयरः ।] Pāṇ. III. 1. 96. @deva[केलिमर उपसंख्यानम् ।] Vārtika.
