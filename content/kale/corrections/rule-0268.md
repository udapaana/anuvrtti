## IV. AVYAYĪBHĀ'VA OR THE ADVERBIAL COMPOUNDS.

An Avyayībhāva comp. consists of two members the first of which is, in most cases, an indeclinable ( a preposition or an adverb ) and the second a noun, the whole being treated like the Nom. sing. of *neu*. nouns. the comp so formed is indeclinable; *e. g.* @deva[अधिहरि] 'in Hari'; @deva[अन्तर्गिरि] 'in a mountain,' &c.
