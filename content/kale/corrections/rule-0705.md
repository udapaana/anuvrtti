The P. P. Participles of roots implying motion, of intransitive roots, and of the roots @deva[श्लिष्], @deva[शी], @deva[स्था], @deva[आस्], @deva[वस्] 'to dwell,' @deva[जन्], @deva[रुह्], and @deva[जॄ] 4. cl. have an active sense; @deva[गतोऽहं मद्रपुरम्] 'I went to Madras;' @deva[म्लानो बालः] 'the boy has become languid,' @deva[लक्ष्मीमाश्लिष्टो हरिः] 'Hari embraced Lakshmī;' @deva[शेषमधिशयितः] 'slept on Sesha;' @deva[वैकुंठमधिष्ठितः] 'dwelt in Vaikuntha;' @deva[शिवमुपासितः] 'served Shiva,' @deva[हरिदिनमुपोषितः] 'observed a fast on the day sacred to Hari,' @deva[राममनुजातः] 'was born after Rāma;' @deva[गरुडमारूढः] sat on Garuda; @deva[विश्वमनुजीर्णः] 'grew old after the world.'
