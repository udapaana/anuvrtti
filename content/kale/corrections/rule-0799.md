## Intransitive Verbs with Accusative of Space and Time

[^1] Intransitive roots govern the Accusative nouns denoting space or country, or duration of time and distance; @deva[कुरून् स्वपिति] he sleeps in the country of the Kurus; @deva[तत्र कतिपयान् दिवसान् अवसत्] there he dwelt for some days; @deva[गोदोहमास्ते] he sits down till a cow is milked; @deva[कोशं प्रतिष्ठते] he walks for a Kosa; @deva[कोशं कुटिला नदी] the course of the river is winding for a Kosa; but @deva[मासस्य द्विरधीते] studies twice a month; @deva[कोशस्यैकदेशे पर्वतः] the hill is situated in a part of a Kosa.

---

[^1]: @deva[कालाध्वनोरत्यन्तसंयोगे ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 5.
