**Conjugation of the Parasm. Fre. Base.**

## Conjugation of Parasmaipada Frequentatives

In the Conjugational Tenses the Parasm. Frequentatives follow the conjugation of the special base of a root of the 3rd class, @deva[ई] is optionally prefixed to the terminations of the singulars of the Present, of the 2nd and 3rd singulars of the Imperf. and of the 3rd sing. of the Impera.; when @deva[ई] is prefixed to these, a penultimate short vowel does not take Guṇa; @deva[दा]—@deva[दादाति] or @deva[दादेति]; @deva[वृत्]—@deva[वर्वर्ति] or @deva[वरिवर्ति] or @deva[वरीवर्ति] or @deva[वर्वृतीति] or @deva[वरिवृतीति] or @deva[वरीवृतीति], @deva[कृ]—@deva[चर्कर्ति] or @deva[चर्करीति], @deva[चरिकर्ति] or @deva[चरिकरीति], @deva[चरीकर्ति] or @deva[चरीकरीति].
