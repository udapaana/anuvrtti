The words @deva[वर] and @deva[प्रवर] when used in the sense of the superlative govern the Genitive or the Locative; @deva[पुत्र स्पर्शवतां वरः] 'a son is the best of things possessed of touch;' @deva[चतुष्पदां गोः प्रवरा लोहानां काञ्चनं वरं] 'the cow is the best of quadrupeds and gold of metals'; the neu. sing. of @deva[वर] is used (with a word expressive of negation) in the sense of 'better and not, or but not;' @deva[अकरणान्मन्दकरणं वरं] doing any thing slowly is better than not doing it at all; @deva[अजातमृतमूर्खाणां वरमाद्यौ न चान्तिमः] of (the three kinds of) sons—not born, born and dead, and foolish, the first two are better, but not the last. @deva[याच्ञा मोघा वरमधिगुणे नाधमे लब्धकामा।] Meg. I. 6. @deva[वरं प्राणैः वियोगः न तु मानहानिः] better death than disgrace.
