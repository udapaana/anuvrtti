## THE SECOND OR SIMPLE FUTURE.

[^1]When the close proximity of a future action is intended, the Second Future or the Present may be used; @deva[कदा गमिष्यसि] when will you go? @deva[एष गच्छामि] or @deva[गमिष्यामि] I shall just go.
