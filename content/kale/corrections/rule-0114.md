The penultimate @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] of the words @deva[पिपठिष्] wishing to read, @deva[सजुष्] m. f. a companion, @deva[चिकीर्ष्] wishing to do, @deva[सुपिस्] one stepping well, @deva[आशिष्] f. a blessing, @deva[सुतुस्], cutting well, and also of @deva[गिर्], speech, @deva[धुर्] a yoke, @deva[पुर्] a town all fem. is lengthened before consonantal terminations; their final is changed to visarga in the Nom. Sing. e. g. @deva[पिपठिष्]—

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. V. | @deva[पिपठीः] | @deva[पिपठिषौ] | @deva[पिपठिषः] |
| A. | @deva[पिपठिषम्] | @deva[पिपठिषौ] | @deva[पिपठिषः] |
| I. | @deva[पिपठिषा] | @deva[पिपठीर्भ्याम्] | @deva[पिपठीर्भिः] |
| D. | @deva[पिपठिषे] | @deva[पिपठीर्भ्याम्] | @deva[पिपठीर्भ्यः] |
| L. | @deva[पिपठिषि] | @deva[पिपठिषोः] | @deva[पिपठीष्षु-ःषु][^1] |
