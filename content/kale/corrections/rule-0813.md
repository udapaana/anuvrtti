## Instrumental with Time/Space for Accomplishment

When the accomplishment of an object is to be expressed,[^1] the Instrumental of words expressing the length of time or space is used; @deva[अह्ना क्रोशेन वाऽनुवाकोधीतः] the section (of the Veda) was studied by him in a day, or a Kosa (i. e. by going over it); but @deva[मासमधीतो नायातः] as the accomplishment does not take place.

---

[^1]: @deva[अपवर्गे तृतीया ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 6. @deva[अपवर्गः फलप्राप्तिः । तस्यां द्योत्यायां कालाध्वनोरत्यन्तसंयोगे तृतीया स्यात् ।]
