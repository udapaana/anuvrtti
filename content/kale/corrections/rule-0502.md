## Samprasāraṇa in Perfect Tense

The change of a semi-vowel to its corresponding vowel is called Samprasâraṇa. Samprasâraṇa generally takes place before weak terminations in the case of the following roots—@deva[वच्], @deva[यज्], @deva[वप्], @deva[वह्], @deva[वस्] 'to dwell,' @deva[वे], @deva[ज्ये], @deva[ह्वे], @deva[श्वि], @deva[वद्], @deva[स्वप्], @deva[ज्या], @deva[वश्], @deva[व्यच्], @deva[प्रच्छ्], @deva[व्रश्च्], @deva[भ्रस्ज्], @deva[ग्रह्] and @deva[व्यध्]. In the case of the Perfect the roots @deva[प्रच्छ्], @deva[व्रश्च्] and @deva[भ्रस्ज्] form an exception.
