The final @deva[अय] of roots of the tenth class and of causals, the final @deva[अ] of Ātm. Freq. Bases, when the @deva[य] is preceded by a vowel and the whole @deva[य] when it is preceded by a consonant, are dropped before @deva[अनीय]. To Desiderative bases it is added without any change; e. g.

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| @deva[कथ्]— | @deva[कथनीय] | what must, or ought to be told. |
| @deva[चुर्]— | @deva[चोरणीय] | ,, ,, stolen. |
| @deva[बोधय] cau. of @deva[बुध्]— | @deva[बोधनीय] | ,, ,, made to know. |
| @deva[बोबुध्य] Ātm. Freq. Base of ,, | @deva[बोबुधनीय] | ,, ,, known frequently. |
| @deva[बोभूय]—Ātm. freq. Base of @deva[भू]— | @deva[बोभूयनीय] | what must or ought to be frequently. |
| @deva[बुबोधिष्] Desi. Base of @deva[बुध्] | @deva[बुबोधिषणीय] | ,, ,, desired to know. |
