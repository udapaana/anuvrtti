(a) The penultimate nasal of a root is dropped before @deva[ना] &c. as @deva[ग्रथ्नामि], @deva[ग्रथ्नीवः], @deva[ग्रथ्नीमः] &c. from @deva[ग्रन्थ्] 'to put together.'

(b) In the case of roots ending in a consonant, the imperative second person singular termination is @deva[आन] instead of @deva[हि]; as @deva[मुषाण] from @deva[मुष्] 'to steal'.
