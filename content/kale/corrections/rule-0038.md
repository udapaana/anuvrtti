If @deva[ङ्] and @deva[ण्] be followed by a sibilant, @deva[क्] and @deva[ट्] respectively are suffixed to them optionally; and hard unaspirates are optionally changed into hard aspirates when followed by a sibilant;[^1] @deva[प्राङ्] + @deva[षष्ठः] = @deva[प्राङ्षष्ठः], @deva[प्राङ्क्षष्ठः], @deva[प्राङ्ख्षष्ठः]; the sixth man gone before; @deva[सुगण्] + @deva[षष्ठः] = @deva[सुगण्षष्ठः], @deva[सुगण्ट्षष्ठः], @deva[सुगण्ठ्षष्ठः], the sixth good counter.

---

[^1]: @deva[ङ्णोः कुक्टुक् शरि] — Pāṇ. VIII. 3. 28.
