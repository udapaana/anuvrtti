title: "Formation Conditions for Dwigu"

---
A Dwigu compound is possible (a) under the circumstances mentioned in @ref[223] (a) i. e.—

(1) when a Taddhita affix is to be added to the compound so formed; @deva[षण्णां मातृणामपत्यं षाण्मातुरः] 'the son of six mothers, Kārtikeya' (vide Ku. IX); @deva[पञ्चकपालः] &c; or (2) when the compound itself becomes the first member of another compound, as @deva[पञ्चगावः धनं यस्य असौ पञ्चगवधनः], @deva[पञ्चनावप्रियः] &c.

(b) And when the compound denotes an aggregate; in this case it is singular;[^1] as @deva[त्रयाणां भुवनानां समाहारः त्रिभुवनम्] the three worlds (taken collectively); as @deva[पञ्चपात्रम्], @deva[पञ्चगवम्], &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[द्विगुरेकवचनम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 4. 1.
