The @deva[मत्वर्थीय] affixes or affixes expressive of possession have the following additional senses:—@deva[भूमन्] or 'greatness, eminence,' @deva[निन्दा] or 'depreciation,' @deva[प्रशंसा] or 'praise,' @deva[नित्ययोग] or 'constant connection,' @deva[अतिशायन] or 'excess over,' @deva[सम्बन्ध] or 'being in connection with,' and @deva[अस्तिविवक्षा] or to express the meaning of 'possession.'[^1] The instances in order are—@deva[यवमान्], @deva[कंकुदावर्तिनी कन्या], @deva[रूपवान्], @deva[क्षीरिणो वृक्षाः], @deva[उदरिणी कन्या], @deva[दण्डी].

## SECTION II. Affixes showing Possession ( @deva[मत्वर्थीय] ).

@deva[अ] ( @deva[अच्] )—is affixed to words included in the @deva[अर्शआदि] group[^2]; @deva[अर्शसः] ( @deva[अर्शांसि अस्य विद्यन्ते] ) 'suffering from piles;' @deva[जटा अस्यास्तीति जटः] 'having matted hair'; @deva[उरसः] 'having a prominent breast.'

@deva[आट] and @deva[आल]—are attached to @deva[वाच्] in the sense of 'speaking much or speaking badly'; @deva[वाचाटः] or @deva[वाचालः] a prattler, a garrulous or talkative person.

@deva[आलु]—@deva[हृदयालुः] one who possesses a 'heart' *i. e.* 'a kind-hearted man;' this is added to @deva[शीत], @deva[उष्ण] and @deva[तृप्र] in the sense of 'not capable of enduring;' @deva[शीतं न सहते शीतालुः] 'unable to endure cold'; similarly @deva[उष्णालुः], @deva[तृप्रालुः] ( @deva[तृप्रः पुरोडाशः तं न सहते । तृप्रं दुःखं इति माधवः] S. K. ).

@deva[इत]—is affixed to @deva[तारका] and other words[^3] in the sense of 'that is obtained or possessed by'; @deva[तारका अस्य संजाताः तारकितं नभः] 'the sky studded with stars'; @deva[फलानि अस्य संजातानि असौ फलितः वृक्षः]; similarly @deva[पुष्पित], @deva[सुखित], @deva[दुःखित] &c.

@deva[इन्] and @deva[इक] ( @deva[इनि], @deva[ठन्] )—are added to words ending in @deva[अ], and to the words of the @deva[व्रीह्यादि] group[^4]; @deva[दण्ड अस्यास्तीति दण्डिन्] or @deva[दण्डिक] 'one who has a staff.' @deva[धनिन्], @deva[सुखिन्], @deva[दुःखिन्] &c.; @deva[व्रीहिन्], @deva[व्रीहिक], @deva[मायिन्], @deva[मायिक], @deva[शालिन्], @deva[मालिन्], &c.; when added to @deva[वात] and @deva[अतिसार], @deva[क] is prefixed to it; @deva[वाताकिन्], 'suffering from gout, rheumatic; @deva[अतिसारकिन्], 'one suffering from diarrhœa'; it is added to @deva[पुष्कर][^5] and other words when the place is meant; @deva[पुष्करिणी] 'the place where lotuses grow'; hence 'a tank or a lake'; similarly @deva[कुमुदिनी], @deva[पद्मिनी] &c.; it is also added to @deva[अर्थ] and words ending in @deva[अर्थ]; @deva[अर्थिन्] one having some object in view; @deva[धान्यार्थिन्] &c.; and to @deva[वर्ण-वर्णिन्] 'an ascetic.'

@deva[इन]—is affixed to @deva[फल], @deva[बर्ह] and @deva[मल]; @deva[फलिनः] 'bearing fruit, fruitful,' @deva[बर्हिण] 'a peacock;' @deva[मलिन] 'dusky'.

@deva[इल]—is added to @deva[तुन्द], @deva[उदर], @deva[पिचण्ड], @deva[यव], @deva[व्रीहि] and @deva[प्रज्ञा] optionally; to @deva[पिच्छ], @deva[उरस्], @deva[ध्रुवक], @deva[वर्ण], @deva[उदंक] and @deva[पङ्क] necessarily; to @deva[सिकता], @deva[शर्करा] and @deva[फेन] optionally; @deva[तुन्दिल] 'having a protuberant belly, corpulent,' @deva[उदरिल], @deva[पिचण्डिल], (have the same sense); @deva[प्रज्ञिल] 'intelligent;' @deva[पिच्छिल] 'slimy, slippery;' @deva[उरसिल] 'having a broad chest;' @deva[पङ्किल] 'muddy' @deva[सिकतिल] 'sandy,' @deva[शर्करिल], @deva[फेनिल], &c.

@deva[उर]—@deva[दन्तुर] 'having projecting teeth, hence also rugged.' &c.

@deva[ऊल]—is added to @deva[बल] and @deva[वात] in the sense of 'not enduring'; as @deva[बलूलः] 'not able to face the army of the enemy,' 'one who cannot withstand the strength of another'; @deva[वातूल] 'one that cannot bear the wind'. When added to @deva[वात] it has also the sense of 'a collection.' @deva[वातूलः] 'a hurricane.'

@deva[ग्मिन्]—This is added to the word @deva[वाच्] in a good sense, as @deva[आट] or @deva[आल] is added in a bad sense; @deva[वाग्मिन्] 'eloquent, an orator.'

@deva[मत्] ( @deva[ड्मतुप्] )—is added to @deva[कुमुद्], @deva[नड्] and @deva[वेतस्]; @deva[कुमुद्वत्] 'a place abounding in lotuses'; @deva[नड्वत्] 'abounding in the *nada* grass,' @deva[वेतस्वत्] 'abounding in canes.'

@deva[मत्] ( @deva[मतुप्] ) is the general term expressing possession;[^6] it is added in the sense of 'it has that or that is in it'; *e. g.* @deva[गावोऽस्यास्मिन्वा सन्तीति गोमान्] 'possessing cows,' &c. It is specially added to the words @deva[रस], @deva[रूप], @deva[वर्ण], @deva[गन्ध], @deva[स्पर्श], @deva[स्नेह], @deva[शब्द] and @deva[स्व]; @deva[रसवान्], @deva[रूपवान्], &c; @deva[स्ववान्].
