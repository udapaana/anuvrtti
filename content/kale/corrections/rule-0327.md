key: rule:327
title: "Feminine of Bahuvrīhi Ending in @deva[अन्]"
pages: [190.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] A Bahuvrîhi ending in @deva[अन्] forms its *fem.* in @deva[आ] optionally; as @deva[सु+पर्वन्=सुपर्वन्-र्वा], @deva[बहु+यज्वन् ज्वा], &c. but if the word ending in @deva[अन्] be such as drops its @deva[अ] before the @deva[आ] of the Inst. sing. it takes @deva[ई] also optionally; *e. g.* @deva[बहु+राजन्—बहुराजन्—राजा--राज्ञी] &c.

**(a)** [^2] If a Bah. ends in @deva[वन्], the @deva[न्] is optionally changed to @deva[र्]; as @deva[बहुधीवन्-बहुधीवा] or @deva[बहुधीवरी] a town in which there are many fishermen.

---

[^1]: @deva[वनो न हश इति वक्तव्यम्] । Vârtika.

[^2]: @deva[बहुव्रीहौ वा] । Vârt. on Pân. IV. 1. 7.
