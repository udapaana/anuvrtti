When @deva[अ] or @deva[आ] is followed by @deva[इ], @deva[उ], @deva[ऋ] or @deva[ऌ], short or long, the @deva[गुण] letter corresponding to the latter takes the place of both;[^1] e.g. @deva[उप] + @deva[इन्द्रः] = @deva[उपेन्द्रः] Vishṇu; @deva[परम] + @deva[ईश्वरः] = @deva[परमेश्वरः] the great lord; @deva[रमा] + @deva[इच्छा] = @deva[रमेच्छा], the wish of Ramā; @deva[यथा] + @deva[ईप्सितं] = @deva[यथेप्सितम्] according to wish or desire; @deva[हित] + @deva[उपदेशः] = @deva[हितोपदेशः] friendly instruction; @deva[कृष्ण] + @deva[ऊरुः] = @deva[कृष्णोरुः] Kṛshṇa's thigh; @deva[गङ्गा] + @deva[उदकं] = @deva[गङ्गोदकम्], @deva[महा] + @deva[ऊरुः] = @deva[महोरुः]. @deva[कृष्ण] + @deva[ऋद्धिः] = @deva[कृष्णर्द्धिः] Kṛshṇa's prosperity; @deva[महा] + @deva[ऋषिः] = @deva[महर्षिः], a great sage; @deva[तव] + @deva[ऌकारः] = @deva[तवल्कारः]. The ḷkāra pronounced by you.

**(a)** If a consonant be followed by homogeneous consonants, except the nasals, the semivowels and @deva[ह्], the first of them is optionally dropped;[^2] @deva[कृष्ण] + @deva[ऋद्धिः] = @deva[कृष्णर्द्धिः] by the general rule; then @deva[कृष्णर्] + @deva[द्] + @deva[ध्] + @deva[इः] = @deva[कृष्णर्धिः] by this rule, the @deva[द्] being dropped; and also a third form @deva[कृष्णर्द्द्धिः] (vide @ref[22], sec. d., below).

**(b)** A consonant of the first five classes except @deva[ञ्] coming after a semivowel is optionally doubled; so @deva[तवल्कारः] also becomes @deva[तवल्क्कारः], @deva[तवल्ल्कारः] and @deva[तवल्ल्क्कारः], 4 forms altogether.

**Exceptions:** Vṛddhi substitute takes place in the following cases:[^3]

**(a)** When a word ending in @deva[अ] is followed by @deva[ऊह], and @deva[प्र] by @deva[ऊह], @deva[ऊढ] and @deva[ऊढि]; e. g. @deva[प्रष्ठ] + @deva[ऊहः] = @deva[प्रष्ठौहः], chief guess (or this may be the acc. pl. of @deva[प्रष्ठवाह्] a young bull trained to the yoke); @deva[प्र] + @deva[ऊहः] = @deva[प्रौहः] principal reasoning. Similarly @deva[प्रौढः] full grown; @deva[प्रौढिः]; but @deva[प्रोढवान्] (as @deva[ऊढवान्] is not mentioned in the Sūtra).

**(b)** @deva[अक्ष] + @deva[ऊहिनी] = @deva[अक्षौहिणी] a complete army.[^4] (The change of @deva[न्] to @deva[ण्] will be explained further on).

**(c)** When @deva[स्व] is followed by @deva[ईर] and @deva[ईरिन्] both derived from @deva[ईर्] to go; e. g. @deva[स्व] + @deva[ईरः] = @deva[स्वैरः], one acting wilfully, self-willed; @deva[स्व] + @deva[ईरिणी] = @deva[स्वैरिणी], so @deva[स्वैरं], @deva[स्वैरी] (@deva[स्वेन ईरितुं शीलमस्य इति]).

**(d)** When @deva[अ] is followed by @deva[ऋतः] and there is an Inst. Tat.; as @deva[सुख] + @deva[ऋत] = @deva[सुखार्तः] blessed with happiness (@deva[सुखेन ऋतः]); but @deva[परमर्तः] very adorable (@deva[परमश्चासौ ऋतश्च]).

**(e)** When the word @deva[ऋण] comes after @deva[प्र], @deva[वत्सतर], @deva[कम्बल], @deva[वसन] and @deva[ऋण]; @deva[प्र] + @deva[ऋणं] = @deva[प्रार्णम्], principal debts, so @deva[वत्सतरार्णम्] debt for a calf; @deva[ऋणार्णम्] (a debt contracted to liquidate a previous debt); @deva[दशार्णः] (Name of a country; lit. the country with ten fortresses); also @deva[दशार्णा नदी] (lit. the river into which ten other rivers flow).

**(f)** When[^5] a preposition ending in @deva[अ] is followed by a root having an initial @deva[ऋ]; as @deva[उप] + @deva[ऋच्छति] = @deva[उपार्च्छति]; @deva[प्रार्च्छति]; but if the root be a denominative, the Vṛddhi is optional; @deva[प्र] + @deva[ऋषभीयति] = @deva[प्रर्षभीयति] or @deva[प्रार्षभीयति] acts like a bull; but @deva[उप] + @deva[ॠकारीयति] = @deva[उपर्कारीयति], as the @deva[ॠ] is long; also @deva[प्र]-@deva[ऌकारीयति] = @deva[प्रा]-@deva[प्रल्कारीयति] very much wishes for @deva[ऌ] lkāra; for @deva[ऌ] is grammatically considered to be similar to @deva[ऋ].
