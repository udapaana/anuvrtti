title: "10th Class and Causals Preserving @deva[अय्] Before @deva[य]"
pages: [443.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "प्रबेभिदय्य: second syllable (बे vs बो) blurred."
---
Roots of the Tenth class and Causals preserve their @deva[अय्] before @deva[य], if it be preceded by a short penultimate vowel; if not, it is dropped; @deva[चोरय]—@deva[प्रचोर्य]; @deva[बोधय]—@deva[प्रबोध्य]; @deva[कॄ]—cau. @deva[विकार्य]; @deva[नी] cau. with @deva[आ]—@deva[आनाय्य], &c.; but @deva[गण्]—@deva[विगणय्य], @deva[प्रणमय्य], @deva[प्रकथय्य]; @deva[प्रबेभिदय्य] 'having again and again caused to be broken.'
