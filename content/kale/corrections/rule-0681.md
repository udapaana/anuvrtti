title: "Affix @deva[त] Is Weak with Exceptions"

The affix @deva[त] is weak.

**Exceptions:—**

(a) The roots @deva[शी], @deva[स्विद्] 1 cl., @deva[मिद्], @deva[क्ष्विद्], @deva[धृष्] and @deva[मृष्] admit of the Guṇa change before @deva[त] when @deva[इ] is prefixed to it; @deva[पू] 1. A also does the same when it takes @deva[इ] (see @ref[686]b).

(b) Such of the roots of the 1st class as have @deva[उ] for their penultimate change their vowel to Guṇa optionally, when the affix @deva[त] is added with the intermediate @deva[इ], and the P. P. Participle is used impersonally, or when it conveys the sense of beginning to perform the action or undergoing the condition expressed by the root; @deva[मुद्] 'to delight' @deva[मुदित], but 'to begin to delight' @deva[प्रमुदित] or @deva[प्रमोदित]; @deva[प्रमुदितं] or @deva[प्रमोदितं साधुना]; @deva[प्रमुदितः] or @deva[प्रमोदितः साधुः]; similarly, @deva[द्युत्]-@deva[प्रद्युतित] or @deva[प्रद्योतित] &c.
