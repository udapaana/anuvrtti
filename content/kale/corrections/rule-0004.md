The consonants are divided into @deva[स्पर्श] or mutes (those involving a complete closure or contact and not an approximate one of the organs of pronunciation), @deva[अन्तस्थ] (or intermediate, i.e. the Semivowels,) and @deva[ऊष्मन्] or sibilants.

These are represented by thirty-three syllabic signs arranged as below:—

**(a) Mutes:**

(1) @deva[कवर्ग] or the group @deva[कु]—@deva[क्] @[k], @deva[ख्] @[kh], @deva[ग्] @[g], @deva[घ्] @[gh], @deva[ङ्] @[ṅ].

(2) @deva[चवर्ग] or the group @deva[चु]—@deva[च्] @[c], @deva[छ्] @[ch], @deva[ज्] @[j], @deva[झ्] @[jh], @deva[ञ्] @[ñ].

(3) @deva[टवर्ग] or the group @deva[टु]—@deva[ट्] @[ṭ], @deva[ठ्] @[ṭh], @deva[ड्] @[ḍ], @deva[ढ्] @[ḍh], @deva[ण्] @[ṇ].

(4) @deva[तवर्ग] or the group @deva[तु]—@deva[त्] @[t], @deva[थ्] @[th], @deva[द्] @[d], @deva[ध्] @[dh], @deva[न्] @[n].

(5) @deva[पवर्ग] or the group @deva[पु]—@deva[प्] @[p], @deva[फ्] @[ph], @deva[ब्] @[b], @deva[भ्] @[bh], @deva[म्] @[m].

These are also called the five classes designated as @deva[कवर्ग], @deva[चवर्ग], @deva[टवर्ग], @deva[तवर्ग], and @deva[पवर्ग] respectively.

**(b) Semivowels**

@deva[य्] @[y], @deva[र्] @[r], @deva[ल्] @[l], @deva[व्] @[v].

**(c) Sibilants**

@deva[श्] @[ś], @deva[ष्] @[sh], @deva[स्] @[s].

**(d) Sonant Aspirate**

@deva[ह्] @[h].

Besides these we have two more characters occurring in the Veda, viz. @deva[ळ] and @deva[ळ्ह] (often substituted for @deva[ड] and @deva[ढ]; as @deva[ईळे] for @deva[ईडे], @deva[मीळ्हुषे] for @deva[मीढुषे] &c.). In Marāthi @deva[ळ] is generally substituted for the final @deva[ल] of Sanskrit words.
