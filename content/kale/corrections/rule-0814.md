## Instrumental with Defective Limbs

Words expressing some defect in a limb of the body govern the Instrumental of the defective limb;[^1] @deva[अक्ष्णा काणः] blind of one eye; so @deva[पादेन खञ्जः] &c

---

[^1]: @deva[येनाङ्गविकारः ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 20.
