The penultimate @deva[इ], @deva[उ], @deva[ऋ] or @deva[ऌ] of a root, followed by @deva[र्] or @deva[व्] is lengthened when a consonant follows;[^1] e. g. @deva[उर्द्] 1. A. to measure, to play, @deva[ऊर्दते]; similarly @deva[कुर्द्], @deva[खुर्द्], @deva[गुर्द्] all A. and meaning to play, @deva[हुर्छ्], to act dishonestly, to be crooked, @deva[मुर्छ्] to faint; @deva[स्फुर्छ्] to spread, to forget; @deva[स्फुर्ज्] to thunder, to shine; @deva[उर्व्], @deva[तुर्व्], @deva[थुर्व्], @deva[दुर्व्], @deva[धुर्व्] all meaning to kill, @deva[गुर्व्] to try, @deva[मुर्व्], &c., all Parasm. and belonging to the 1st class, lengthen their penultimate vowel; @deva[दिव्] 4. P. @deva[दीव्यति]; similarly @deva[सिव्] has @deva[सीव्यति], @deva[ष्ठिव्], @deva[ष्ठीव्यति], &c.
