## Root @deva[विश्] with @deva[अभिनि]: Accusative and Locative

@deva[विश्][^1] with @deva[अभिनि] governs the Accusative; @deva[अभिनिविशते सन्मार्गम्] he pursues the path of goodness; @deva[धन्या सा गणिकादारिका यामेव भवन्मनोऽभिनिविशते] happy is that harlot girl on whom you have fixed your mind (see Bhatti. VIII. 80.). Rarely this governs the Loc. @deva[अभिनिविशते पापे] fondly resorts to sin. @deva[विश्] with a preposition governs the Acc. but with @deva[उप] meaning 'to sit' takes the Loc. @deva[आसनेऽस्मिन्नुपविश] sit on this seat.

---

[^1]: @deva[अभिनिविशश्च] Pāṇ. I. 4. 47.
