## Nouns with Single Inflection Treated as Indeclinables

Besides these there are in Sanskrit a few nouns having one inflection only which are treated as indeclinable (nipātas), e. g. @deva[अन्यत्] another (reason), @deva[अस्तम्] setting, @deva[अस्ति] that exists, @deva[ओम्] the well-known sacred syllable om, @deva[चनस्] satisfaction, food, @deva[चाटु] coaxing, @deva[नमस्] a bow, @deva[नास्ति] non-existence, @deva[भूर्] the earth, @deva[भुवर्] the sky, @deva[वदि] the dark fortnight, @deva[शम्] happiness, @deva[शुदि] or @deva[सुदि] the bright fortnight, @deva[संवत्] a year, @deva[स्वाहा] food offered to gods, @deva[स्वधा] food offered to the manes, @deva[स्वर्] heaven, @deva[स्वस्ति] happiness &c.
