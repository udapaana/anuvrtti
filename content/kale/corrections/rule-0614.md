## Meaning of Desiderative Forms

The Desiderative expresses the notion that a person or thing wishes (or is about) to perform the action, or to be in the condition denoted by the root or the desiderative base; @deva[पिपठिषति] 'he wishes to study' from @deva[पठ्]; @deva[मुमूर्षति] 'he is about to die' fr. @deva[मृ]; &c.
