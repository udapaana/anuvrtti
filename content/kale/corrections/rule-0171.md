# Chapter VI.
# DEGREE OF COMPARISON.

@deva[तर] and @deva[तम] are the most common terminations of the comparative and the superlative degrees respectively.[^1] They are added to that crude form of words which they assume, before the Inst. dual termination; e. g. @deva[अयमेतयोरतिशयेन लघुः लघुतरः], @deva[अयमेषामतिशयेन लघुतमः]; similarly @deva[युवन्],—@deva[युवतर], @deva[युवतम]; @deva[विद्वस्]-@deva[विद्वत्तर], @deva[विद्वत्तम]; @deva[प्राच्]-@deva[प्राक्तर], @deva[प्राक्तम]; @deva[धनिन्]-@deva[धनितर], @deva[धनितम]; @deva[धर्मभुध्]—@deva[धर्मभुत्तर], @deva[धर्मभुत्तम]; @deva[गुरु] - @deva[गुरुतर], @deva[गुरुतम], &c; @deva[अति]-@deva[अतितर] @deva[अतितम]; @deva[उत्]-@deva[उत्तर], @deva[उत्तम], &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[अतिशायने तमबिष्ठनौ । द्विवचनविभज्योपपदे तरबीयसुनौ । तिङश्च । तरप्तमपौ घः] Pāṇ. V. 3. 55. 57. 56; I. 1. 22. When atiś'aya or excess of the one thing over many is to be shown @deva[तम] and @deva[इष्ठ] are the affixes used; and when out of two things one is to be separated as possessing some quality in excess @deva[तर] and @deva[ईयस्] are affixed. @deva[तर] and @deva[तम] may also added to verbs.
