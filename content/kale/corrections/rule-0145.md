## 8—Reciprocal Pronouns.

Reciprocity is expressed by the repetition of the pronominal adjectives @deva[अन्य], @deva[इतर] and @deva[पर]; e. g. @deva[अन्योन्य], @deva[इतरेतर], and @deva[परस्पर]. These are generally used in the sing. and also adverbially e. g. @deva[परस्परेण स्पृहणीयशोभं] &c. (Rag. VII. 14); @deva[परस्परं विवदन्ते] &c. They are more commonly used as the first members of compounds; as @deva[अन्योन्यशोभाजननाद् बभूव] &c. (Ku. I, 44; @deva[इतरेतरयोगाः] (Sis. X. 24.) &c.
