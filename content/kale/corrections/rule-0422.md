**IRREGULAR BASES.**

Many of the roots of the 2nd class are of irregular conjugation. We will treat of them in alphabetical order.

@deva[अद्] P. 'to eat' forms the 2 and 3 sing. Imperf. as @deva[आदः] and @deva[आदत्] respectively. In other respects it is regular.

**Present.**

| | S. | D. | P. |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | @deva[अद्मि] | @deva[अद्वः] | @deva[अद्मः] |
| 2. | @deva[अत्सि] | @deva[अत्थः] | @deva[अत्थ] |
| 3. | @deva[अत्तिः] | @deva[अत्तः] | @deva[अदन्ति] |

**Imperfect.**

| | S. | D. | P. |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | @deva[आदम्] | @deva[आद्व] | @deva[आद्म] |
| 2. | @deva[आदः] | @deva[आत्तम्] | @deva[आत्त] |
| 3. | @deva[आदत्] | @deva[आत्ताम्] | @deva[आदन्] |

**Imperative.**

| | S. | D. | P. |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | @deva[अदानि] | @deva[अदाव] | @deva[अदाम] |
| 2. | @deva[अद्धि] | @deva[अत्तम्] | @deva[अत्त] |
| 3. | @deva[अत्तु] | @deva[अत्ताम्] | @deva[अदन्तु] |
