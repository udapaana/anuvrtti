## Prepositions Used with Accusative: @deva[अति], @deva[अनु], @deva[अभि], @deva[उप], @deva[परि], @deva[प्रति]

The following prepositions are used with the Accusative.[^1]

(a) @deva[अति] 'superior to, higher than,' @deva[अनु] 'just after, after, by the side of, inferior to,' @deva[अभि] 'close to' and @deva[उप] 'near, inferior to;' e. g. @deva[अति देवान् कृष्णः] Krishna excels gods in might; @deva[अतिरामं गोविन्दः] Govinda is superior to Rāma; @deva[जपमनु प्रावर्षत्] it rained just after the muttering of prayers; @deva[सर्वं मामनु ते] every thing of thee is after mine; @deva[अनु पितरं गच्छति सुतः] the son imitates his father; @deva[न भवान् अनु रामं चेत्] if you are not inferior to Rāma; so @deva[अनु हरि सुराः], @deva[भक्तो हरिं अभि] the devotee is close to Hari; @deva[उपशूरं न ते वृत्तं] your act is not like that of a hero (lit. is inferior to his); @deva[उपहरिं सुराः] &c.

(b) The prepositions @deva[अभि], @deva[अनु], @deva[परि] and @deva[प्रति] are used with the Accusative when they refer to particular things; @deva[गिरि-अभि-अनु-परि-प्रति-वियोतते विद्युत्] the lightning flashes towards the mountain; also when they mean 'to every one,' &c. @deva[वृक्षं वृक्षमभि-अनु-परि-प्रति-सिंचति] waters each and every tree; so @deva[अभि-अनु-परि-प्रति स्त्रीं स्त्रीं जातमन्मथः ।]

(c) @deva[अनु], @deva[परि] and @deva[प्रति] also govern the Accusative when they imply 'one's own share;' @deva[लक्ष्मीः हरिमनु-परि-प्रति] Lakshmi is the proper share (property) of Hari.
