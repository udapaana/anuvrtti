title: "@deva[न] → @deva[ण] in @deva[वाहन]"
pages: [180.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] The @deva[न] of @deva[वाहन] is changed to @deva[ण] when preceded by a word denoting an object that can be borne; @deva[इक्षुवाहणम्]; but @deva[इन्द्रवाहनम्] ( @deva[इन्द्रस्वामिकं वाहनमित्यर्थः] Sid. Kau. ).

---

[^1]: @deva[वाहनमाहितात्] । Pān. VIII. 4. 8.
