## II. INDECLINABLE PARTICIPLES.

## (a) Indeclinable Past Participles.

The Indeclinable Past Participles are of the nature of gerunds. They fall under two heads:—(1) Those derived by affixing @deva[त्वा] to the simple root, and (2) Those derived by means of @deva[य] affixed to the root compounded with prepositions or words used prepositionally; @deva[गम्]—@deva[गत्वा] 'having gone;' @deva[भू] with @deva[अनु]—@deva[अनुभूय] 'having experienced;' &c.
