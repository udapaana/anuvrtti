Additional instances of the aluk of the Loc.

**(a)** Aluk is necessary in the case of the words—(1) @deva[गो] and @deva[युध्] followed by @deva[स्थिर], as @deva[गविष्ठिरः], stationary in the sky, @deva[युधिष्ठिरः] firm in battle; (2) @deva[हृद्] and @deva[दिव्] followed by @deva[स्पृश्]; @deva[हृदिस्पृक्], @deva[दिविस्पृक्] (@deva[हृदयं दिवं च स्पृशतीति]); (3) @deva[अन्त] and @deva[मध्य] followed by @deva[गुरु]; @deva[मध्येगुरुः], @deva[अन्तेगुरुः] and (4) words expressive of parts of the body except @deva[मूर्धन्] and @deva[मस्तक] and not followed by @deva[काम]; @deva[कण्ठेकालः], @deva[उरसिलोमा] (having the hair on the breast: Bah.), but @deva[मूर्ध-मस्तक-शिखः], @deva[मुखे कामो अस्य मुखकामः].

**(b)**[^1] When a noun in the Loc. is compounded with a verbal derivative and the whole is a name, aluk takes place in most cases; e. g. @deva[स्तम्बेरमः] (@deva[स्तम्बे] a kind of grass @deva[रमते असौ]) an elephant; @deva[कर्णेजपः] one who whispers into the ear, a spy; @deva[खेचरः] a heavenly being; @deva[पंकेरुहं] a lotus; @deva[कुशेशयम्], @deva[जलेशयः] a fish; but @deva[कुरुचरः], @deva[सरसिजं] or @deva[सरोजम्] &c.

**(c)** There is optional aluk of the Loc. of a word expressive of time when compounded with @deva[काल] and words ending in the affixed @deva[तर], @deva[तम] and @deva[तन]; @deva[पूर्वाह्ण-ह्णे-काले]; @deva[पूर्वाह्ण-ह्णे-तरे] &c. @deva[पूर्वाह्ण-ह्णे तने].

**(d)**[^2] When the compound is not a name, aluk is necessary when @deva[ज] comes after @deva[प्रावृद्], @deva[शरद्], @deva[काल] and @deva[दिव्]; and optional when it comes after @deva[वर्ष], @deva[क्षर], @deva[शर] and @deva[वर]; as @deva[प्रावृषि-शरदि-काले-दिवि-जः]; @deva[वर्षेजः] or @deva[वर्षजः] produced in the rainy season, &c.

**Exceptions**[^3]:—No aluk takes place in the case of verbal derivatives ending in @deva[इन्] and the words @deva[सिद्ध], @deva[बद्ध], and @deva[स्थ] in the ordinary language; as @deva[स्थण्डिलशायी] 'an ascetic,' @deva[साङ्काश्यसिद्धः]; @deva[चक्रबद्धः]; @deva[समस्थः]; but @deva[कृष्णोऽस्याखरेष्ठः] &c.

**(e)**[^4] When a noun in the Loc. case ending in @deva[अ] or a consonant and not denoting time is compounded with @deva[शय], @deva[वास] and @deva[वासिन्] and with @deva[बन्ध] aluk is optional; @deva[खे-खशयः], @deva[ग्रामे-मवासः], @deva[ग्रामे-मवासी], @deva[हस्ते-स्तबन्धः], but @deva[भूमिशयः], @deva[गुप्तिबन्धः] &c.
