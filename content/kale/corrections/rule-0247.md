Bahuvrīhi compounds are divided into two classes, @deva[समानाधिकरणबहुव्रीहि] and @deva[व्यधिकरणबहुव्रीहि].[^1]

**(a)** That is a Samānādhikaraṇa Bah. in which both the members are in apposition to each other i. e. have the same case relation when dissolved. There are six kinds of this according as the @deva[यत्] is in any one of the six oblique cases; e. g. @deva[प्राप्तं उदकं यं असौ प्राप्तोदको ग्रामः]; @deva[ऊढः रथः येन असौ ऊढरथः अनड्वान्], @deva[उपहृतः पशुः यस्मै सः उपहृतपशुः रुद्रः], @deva[उद्धृतः ओदनः यस्याः सा उद्धृतोदना स्थाली], @deva[पीतं अम्बरं अस्य स पीताम्बरो हरिः], @deva[वीराः पुरुषाः यस्मिन् स वीरपुरुषो ग्रामः ।]

---

[^1]: Strictly speaking this is not a division of the Bah. but an exception to the general rule. We have given it as a separate division to avoid confusion.
