Names of directions may form a Bahuvrīhi compound and signify the point of direction between them;[^1] @deva[दक्षिणस्याः पूर्वस्याश्च दिशोऽन्तरालं दक्षिणपूर्वा]; so @deva[उत्तरपूर्वा], &c. But if the words so compounded are not the names of quarters, no compound is possible; as @deva[ऐन्द्र्याश्च कौबेर्याश्चान्तरालं दिक्] the point between the East and the North, and not @deva[ऐन्द्रीकौबेरी] as these are not the names of the Eastern and Northern quarters.

---

[^1]: @deva[दिङ्नामान्यन्तराले ।] Pāṇ. II. 2. 26.
