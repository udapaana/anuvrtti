## Transitive Verbs Govern Accusative Case

All transitive verbs govern an Accusative; @deva[पुष्पाण्यवचिनोति] collects flowers; @deva[अप एव ससर्जादौ] (the creator) created water first, &c. Several transitive verbs govern what is called in English a factitive object besides a direct object; @deva[त्वामामनन्ति प्रकृतिं त्वामेव पुरुषं विदुः] Kum. II. 13 they consider thee to be Prakriti, they know thee to be Purusha; @deva[कुमारं नेतारं कृत्वा] having made Kumāra the leader (of the forces); @deva[नाम्ना तमात्मजन्मानं अजं चकार] made his son Aja by name.
