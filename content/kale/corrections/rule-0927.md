[^1]The Imperfect is optionally used for the Perfect when the particles @deva[ह] and @deva[शश्वत्] are used in a sentence, @deva[इति ह अकरोत्] or @deva[चकार] @deva[शश्वदकरोत्] or @deva[चकार].

(a) [^2]It may also be optionally used in asking questions referring to a very recent time; @deva[अगच्छत् किं] (*Qu.*) (*Ans*) @deva[अगच्छत्] or @deva[जगाम किं? जगाम]; but when the question refers to a very remote time the Perfect alone ought to be used; @deva[कृष्णः कंसं जघान किं? जघान।]
