## Reduplicative @deva[इ] and @deva[उ] Changes

When initial @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] of a root takes Guṇa or Vṛddhi substitute, the reduplicative @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] is changed to @deva[इय्] or @deva[उव्]; otherwise the two vowels combine to form long @deva[ई] or @deva[ऊ]; as @deva[इएष्]+@deva[अ]=@deva[इयेष], @deva[उओख्]+@deva[अ]=@deva[उवोख]; @deva[ईषिव]; &c.
