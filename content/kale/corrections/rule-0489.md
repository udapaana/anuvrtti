## Reduplicative Perfect Formation

The Reduplicative Perfect is formed of all monosyllabic roots beginning with consonants, as well as of those beginning with the vowels @deva[अ] or @deva[आ], and @deva[इ], @deva[उ], and @deva[ऋ] short.

Exceptions—@deva[दय्], @deva[अय्], @deva[कास्], @deva[आस्]. These take the Periphrastic Perfect necessary.
