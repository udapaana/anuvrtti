## Dative with @deva[रुच्] and Roots of Pleasing

The roots @deva[रुच्][^1] and others having the same sense govern the Dative of the person or thing pleased or satisfied; @deva[हरये रोचते भक्तिः] 'Hari likes devotion;' @deva[अपां हि तृप्ताय न वारिधारा स्वादुः सुगन्धिः स्वदते तुषारा] 'a stream of sweet water, cool and perfumed, is not liked by one who has already drunk enough of water.'

---

[^1]: @deva[रुच्यर्थानां प्रीयमाणः] Pāṇ. I. 4. 33.
