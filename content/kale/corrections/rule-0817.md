## Instrumental: Excelling, Resemblance, Swearing, Rejoicing, Motion, Price

The Instrumental is used with words expressing the idea of—

(a) excelling; @deva[पूर्वान् महाभाग तयातिशेषे] Oh fortunate one, you excel your ancestors in that (devotion); @deva[धाम्नातिशाययति धाम सहस्रधाम्नः] (Mu 3. 17) he surpasses by his lustre, the lustre of the sun; @deva[दूरीकृता खलु गुणैरुद्यानलता वनलताभिः ।] Ś'ak. I.

(b) resemblance, likeness, equality; @deva[स्वरेण पितरमनुहरति] resembles his father in voice; @deva[देहबन्धने स्वरेण च रामभद्रमनुहरति] (Utta. 4.); @deva[अस्य मुखं मातुः मुखेन संवदति] his face resembles that of his mother; @deva[विष्णुना सदृशो वीर्ये] equal to Vishnu in valour.

(c) swearing; @deva[भरतेनात्मना चाहं शपे] I swear by Bharata and myself; @deva[शापितासि मम जीवितेन] I conjure thee by my life.

(d) rejoicing, being pleased; @deva[भक्त्या गुरौ मय्यनुकम्पया च प्रीतास्मि] I am pleased with thee by thy devotion to your preceptor and compassion upon me; @deva[कापुरुषः स्वल्पकेनापि तुष्यति] a low person is satisfied with little.

(e) motion (the conveyance or the part of the body on which a thing is carried being put in the Instrumental case); @deva[रथेन संचरते] 'he moves about in a chariot.'

(f) price (real or metaphorical) at which a thing is bought; @deva[शतेन क्रीतः] bought for a hundred (rupees, etc.), @deva[स्वप्राणव्ययेनापि रक्षणीयाः सुहृदसवः] 'the life of a friend ought to be saved even at the cost of one's life.'
