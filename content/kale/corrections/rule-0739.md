The following words are irregularly derived by means of the affix @deva[ण्यत्]; @deva[आनाय्यः] 'what ought to be brought from the Gārhapatya i. e. the Dakshināgni;' (fr. @deva[नी] with @deva[आ]) @deva[दक्षिणाग्निविशेष एवेदम् । स हि गार्हपत्यादानीयतेऽनित्यश्च सततमज्वलनात् ।] (Sid. Kau.;) but @deva[आनेय] in other cases; as 'a jar,' @deva[प्रणाय्यः] (fr. @deva[नी] with @deva[प्र]) @deva[चोरः प्रीत्यनर्ह इत्यर्थः] (Sid. Kau); 'disgusted with worldly pleasures;' as @deva[प्रणाय्योन्तेवासी विरक्त इत्यर्थः ।] but @deva[प्रणेय] in other cases.
