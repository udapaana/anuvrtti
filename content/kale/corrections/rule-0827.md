## Dative for Purpose, Result, Evil Portent, Benefit

[^1] (a) A noun expressive of the purpose for which anything is done, or the result to which anything leads or the effect for which anything exists, is put in the Dative case; @deva[मुक्तये हरिं भजति] 'worships Hari for final beatitude;' @deva[भक्तिर्ज्ञानाय कल्पते सम्पद्यते जायते वा] 'devotion leads to knowledge;' @deva[मूत्राय कल्पते जायते सम्पद्यते यवागूः] (Mb.) 'water gruel tends to (produce) urine,' @deva[कुण्डलाय हिरण्यं] (Mah. Bhās.) 'gold for Kundala (a kind of ornament);' @deva[यूपाय दारु] 'wood for a sacrificial post,' &c.

*N. B.* The roots @deva[भू] or @deva[अस्] are often omitted after a Dative used in this sense @deva[काव्यं यशसे] (@deva[भवति]), poetry is for fame.'

(d) [^2] An evil foreboded by a portentous phenomenon is also put in the Dative case; @deva[वाताय कपिला विद्युत्] 'the tawny lightning forebodes a storm.'

(c) [^3] The word @deva[हित] is used with the Dative; @deva[ब्राह्मणाय हितं] 'good for a Brāhmaṇa.'

[^1]: @deva[तादर्थ्ये चतुर्थी वाच्या । क्लृपि संपद्यमाने च ।] Vārtika.
[^2]: @deva[उत्पातेन ज्ञापिते च ।] Vārt. @deva[वाताय कपिलाविद्युदातपायातिलोहिनी । पीता वर्षाय विज्ञेया दुर्भिक्षाय सिता भवेत् ।] Mah. Bhās.
[^3]: @deva[हितयोगे च ।] Vārt.
