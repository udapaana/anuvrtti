Neither Guṇa nor Vṛddhi is substituted for the vowel of a few roots of the 6th class even before a strong termination except the @deva[अ] of the 1st and 3rd person sing. of the Perfect, the @deva[अय] of the causal and the @deva[इ] of the 3rd pers. sing. of the Passive Aorist. These are @deva[कुट्], @deva[पुट्], @deva[कुच्], @deva[गुज्], @deva[छुर्], @deva[स्फुट्], @deva[त्रुट्], @deva[लुट्], @deva[स्फुर्], @deva[गुर्], @deva[नू], @deva[धू], @deva[कु], and a few more not often to be met with.
