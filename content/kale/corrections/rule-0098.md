## IRREGULAR BASES.

**(1)** The @deva[स्] of @deva[तुरासाह्] 'Indra' is changed to @deva[ष्] before the consonantal terminations;[^1] e. g.

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. V. | @deva[तुराषाट्] | @deva[तुरासाहौ] | @deva[तुरासाहः] |
| A. | @deva[तुरासाहम्] | ,, | ,, |
| I. | @deva[तुरासाहा] | @deva[तुराषाड्भ्याम्] | @deva[तुराषाड्भिः] |
| L. | @deva[तुरासाहि] | @deva[तुरासाहोः] | @deva[तुराषाट्सु-ट्त्सु] |

---

[^1]: Pāṇ. VIII. 3. 56.
