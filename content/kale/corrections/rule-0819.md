## Dative Case: Sampradāna (Indirect Object)

The primary sense of the Dative case is Sampradāna (@deva[संप्रदानं]).[^1] The indirect object of the root @deva[दा] is called Sampradāna; also the person or thing with reference to whom or which an action is done; @deva[विप्राय गां ददाति] 'he gives a cow to a Brāhmaṇa;' @deva[युद्धाय संनह्यते] 'prepares for battle;' @deva[न शूद्राय मतिं दद्यात्] 'The Śūdra should not be instructed in the Veda, &c'

But in the case of the root @deva[यज्][^2] the proper object is put in the Instrumental case and the indirect object in the Accusative; @deva[पशुना रुद्रं यजते] 'he sacrifices a bull to Rudra.'

Note:—Though the root @deva[दा] ought to govern the Dat. of the indirect object, it is sometimes found used with the Gen. or the Loc. @deva[राज्यं शिबीनां ऋद्धं वै ददामि तव खेचर] 'Oh sky-wanderer, I will give you the prosperous kingdom of the Sibis;' @deva[यस्त्वं रामे पृथिवीं दातुमिच्छासि] 'thou who art thinking of bestowing the earth upon Rāma,' &c.
