## Dative/Instrumental with @deva[परि]+@deva[क्री] (Hire)

The root @deva[क्री][^1] with @deva[परि] 'to hire, as a servant,' &c., optionally governs the Dative (and optionally the Inst.) of the price at which he is hired; @deva[शतेन शताय वा परिक्रीतः ।] Sid. Kau.

---

[^1]: @deva[परिक्रयणे संप्रदानमन्यतरस्याम् ।] Pāṇ. 4. 44. @deva[नियतकालभृत्यास्वीकरणं परिक्रयणं ।] Sid. Kau.
