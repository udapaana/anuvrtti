## Irregular Frequentative Forms

The following roots form their Frequentatives irregularly.

| Roots | Atm. Fre. | Par. Freq. |
|---|---|---|
| @deva[ऊर्णु] to cover | @deva[ऊर्णोनूयते] |  |
| @deva[कु] 1 cl. to sound | @deva[कोकूयते] |  |
| @deva[खन्] to dig | @deva[चङ्खन्यते] or @deva[चंखन्यते] or @deva[चाखायते] | @deva[चंखनीति], @deva[चङ्खन्ति], &c. |
| @deva[गॄ] to swallow | @deva[जेगिल्यते] | @deva[जागर्ति] |
| @deva[चर्] to walk | See @ref[630] a. | @deva[चञ्चरीति] or @deva[चञ्चर्ति] |
| @deva[चाय्] to worship | @deva[चेकीयते] | @deva[चेकीयति] or @deva[चेकेति] |
| @deva[जन्] to be born | See @ref[630] a. | @deva[जञ्जनीति] or @deva[जञ्जन्ति], &c. |
| @deva[द्युत्] to shine | @deva[देद्युत्यते] | @deva[देद्युतीति] or @deva[देद्योति] |
| @deva[फल्] to expand | See @ref[630] a. | @deva[पंफुलीति] or @deva[पंफुल्ति] |
| @deva[शी] to lie down | @deva[शाशय्यते] | @deva[शेशयीति] or @deva[शेशेति] |
| @deva[श्वि] to swell | @deva[शोश्वियते] or @deva[शोशूयते] | @deva[शेश्वयीति] or @deva[शेश्वेति] |
| @deva[सन्] to obtain | @deva[संसन्यते] or @deva[सासायते] | @deva[संसनीति] or @deva[संसन्ति] |
| @deva[हन्] to injure | @deva[जेघीयते] | @deva[जघनीति] or @deva[जघान्ति] |
| in other cases | @deva[जङ्घन्यते] or @deva[जंघन्यते] |  |
