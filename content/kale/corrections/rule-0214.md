[^1]A word expressive of a period of time that has passed since a certain occurrence is compounded with another expressive of the occurrence; as @deva[मासो जातस्त यस्य स मासजातः] 'one since whose birth a month has elapsed'; so @deva[द्व्यहजातः]; @deva[संवत्सरमृतः] &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[कालाः परिमाणिना ।] Pāṇ. II. 2. 5.
