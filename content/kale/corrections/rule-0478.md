**Terminations of the Second Future:—**

| | Parasm. | | | Ātmane | | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | S. | D. | P. | S. | D. | P. |
| 1. | @deva[स्यामि][^1] | @deva[स्यावः] | @deva[स्यामः] | @deva[स्ये] | @deva[स्यावहे] | @deva[स्यामहे] |
| 2. | @deva[स्यसि] | @deva[स्यथः] | @deva[स्यथ] | @deva[स्यसे] | @deva[स्येथे] | @deva[स्यध्वे] |
| 3. | @deva[स्यति] | @deva[स्यतः] | @deva[स्यन्ति] | @deva[स्यते] | @deva[स्येते] | @deva[स्यन्ते] |

[^1]: These terminations, it will be seen, are obtained by adding those of the Present to @deva[स्य] with its @deva[अ] lengthened before initial @deva[म] and @deva[व], and dropped before a vowel.
