title: "Roots @deva[सु] and @deva[यज्] Taking Affix @deva[वत्] in Active Sense"
pages: [428.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "जॄ (long ṛ) blurred"
  - "वत्/अत्: printed with halant in both printings (not वतु/अतृ)"
---
(a) The roots @deva[सु] and @deva[यज्] take the affix @deva[वत्] like @deva[त] in an active sense; @deva[सुन्वन्] 'one who extracted Soma juice,' @deva[यज्वन्] 'one who has offered a sacrifice;' @deva[अत्] is added to @deva[जॄ] optionally in the same sense; @deva[जीर्ण] or @deva[जरन्] 'one who has become old' (also @deva[जीर्णवत्]).
