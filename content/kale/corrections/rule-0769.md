[^1]With the words @deva[समूल], @deva[अकृत] and @deva[जीव] are used respectively the gerunds in @deva[अम्] of the roots @deva[हन्], @deva[कृ] and @deva[ग्रह्] in a cognate sense; @deva[समूलघातं हन्ति] 'kills destroying the roots i e totally;' @deva[अकृतकारं करोति] 'does what ought not to have been done;' @deva[जीवग्राहं गृह्णाति] 'captures him so as to preserve him i e. alive.'

(a) The gerund of @deva[हन्] and @deva[पिष्] is used with words signifying instruments (of action); @deva[पादघातं हन्ति]=@deva[पादेन हन्ति] 'strikes (kicking) with foot;' @deva[उदपेषं पिनष्टि]=@deva[उदकेन पिनष्टि] 'grinds with (using) the water.'

(b) Similarly the gerund of @deva[वृत्] and @deva[ग्रह्] is used with @deva[हस्त] and its synonyms, and that of @deva[पुष्] with @deva[स्व]; @deva[हस्तवर्तं वर्तयति]; so @deva[करवर्तम्], @deva[हस्तेन गुलिकां करोतीत्यर्थः ।] Sid. Kau.; @deva[हस्तग्राहं गृह्णाति]; similarly @deva[पाणिग्राहम्], @deva[करग्राहम्], &c.
