title: "Comparative and Superlative Sense Through Case Usage"
pages: [508.png]
verdict: approved
---
The sense of the comparative and the superlative may also be expressed by the particular case used; @deva[अस्य हृदयं पाषाणात्कठिनं] his heart is harder than stone; @deva[छात्राणां छात्रेषु वा चैत्रः पटुः] Chaitra is the cleverest of all students.
