## List of Common Sanskrit Prepositions

§ 366. The following are the prepositions (upasargas) commonly prefixed to roots:—

### @deva[अति]

'beyond, over'; @deva[अतिक्रमः] 'overstepping' or 'going beyond,' @deva[अतिसर्जन] 'a gift, &c.'

### @deva[अधि]

'over, above, &c.;' @deva[अधिगमः] 'going up, acquisition,' @deva[अधिकार] 'high office or power,' @deva[अधिक्षेपः] 'censure,' &c.

### @deva[अनु]

'after, behind, along, &c.' @deva[अनुक्रमणं] 'following,' @deva[अनुकृतिः] 'imitation,' @deva[अनुग्रहः] 'favour,' &c.

### @deva[अप]

'away from, away.' @deva[अपनयनं] 'the act of taking away,' @deva[अपहृ] 'to plunder, seize' &c., @deva[अपकारः] 'harm, wrong' &c.

### @deva[अपि] (sometimes @deva[पि])

'near to,' 'over,' 'taking to,' &c.; @deva[अपिगम्] 'to be resolved into or reduced to,'[^1] @deva[अपिधानं] or @deva[पिधानं] 'a covering,' @deva[अप्ययः] 'destruction,' &c.

This prep. is more commonly used as an independent adverb having a cumulative force in classical Sanskrit.

### @deva[अभि]

'towards, near to,' &c.; @deva[अभिगम्] 'to go towards,' @deva[अभिजनः] 'noble descent or family,' @deva[अभिमानः] 'self-respect,' @deva[अभिभू] 'to defeat,' &c.

### @deva[अव] (sometimes @deva[व]; see @deva[अपि] and ft. note)

'away, down,' &c. @deva[अव]—or @deva[वगाह्] 'to plunge into,' @deva[अवतारः] 'descent,' @deva[अवगीत] 'reproached,' @deva[अवमन्] to disrespect,' &c.

### @deva[आ]

'up to, towards, all round, a little,' &c.; @deva[आच्छाद्] 'to cover all round,' @deva[आकारः] 'form or shape (within due bounds),' @deva[आकाशः] 'that which shines all round' i. e. 'the ether'; @deva[आकम्प्] 'to shake a little,' &c.

### @deva[उत्]—@deva[द्]

'upon,' &c.; @deva[उद्गम्] 'to go up,' @deva[उद्यमः] 'industry,' @deva[उत्सर्गः] 'pouring out, hence a gift, a general rule,' &c.

### @deva[उप]

'near to, towards, by the side of,' &c.; @deva[उपया] 'to go near to,' @deva[उपकृति] f. 'an obligation,' @deva[उपरति] f. 'death,' @deva[उपस्थानं] 'praise, worship,' @deva[उपमिति] f. 'comparison,' &c.

### @deva[दुस्]—@deva[र्]

'bad, hard to be done,' &c.; @deva[दुराचारः] 'bad conduct,' @deva[दुष्कर] 'hard to be done,' @deva[दुःसह] 'difficult to be borne,' &c.

### @deva[नि]

'in, into, great, opposed to,' &c.; @deva[निकृ] 'to insult,' @deva[निकेत] 'a house,' @deva[निचय] 'a heap, a great collection,' @deva[निपीत] 'drunk in,' @deva[निदेश] 'a command,' &c.

### @deva[निस्]—@deva[र्]

'out of, away from, without,' &c.; @deva[निःसृ] 'to issue out,' @deva[निर्गम] 'a passage out,' @deva[निर्दोषः] 'out of i. e. free from blame,' @deva[निः शङ्क] 'without doubt,' &c.

### @deva[परा]

'away, back, opposed to,' &c.; @deva[पराकृ] 'to reject, to despise,' @deva[पराक्रम] 'to act bravely,' @deva[परागत] 'gone away,' @deva[पराञ्च्] 'turn back,' @deva[पराजय] 'what is opposed to victory; defeat,' &c.

### @deva[परि]

'all round, about,' &c.; @deva[परिधा] 'to place all round i. e. to put on or wear,' @deva[परिधिः] 'a wall &c. that surrounds,' @deva[परिणामः] 'ripening, maturity,' @deva[परिगणना] 'counting all round, i. e. a complete enumeration,' &c.

### @deva[प्रति]

'towards, back, in return, in opposition to,' &c.; @deva[प्रतिगम्] 'to go towards,' @deva[प्रतिभाषण] 'a speech in return, an answer,' @deva[प्रति—ती—कारः] 'an act in opposition to i. e. a remedy' &c.

### @deva[वि]

'apart, separate from, reverse to,' &c.; @deva[विचल्] 'to move apart,' @deva[वियुज्] 'to be separated,' @deva[विक्री] opp. of @deva[क्री] 'to sell,' 'to buy' &c.; sometimes this has an intensive force.

### @deva[सम्]

'together with, excellent, full,' &c. @deva[संगम्], 'to be united,' @deva[संस्कारः] 'perfection,' @deva[संस्कृति] 'refinement,' @deva[संहारः] 'destruction, contraction,' &c.

### @deva[सु]

'well, thoroughly,' &c. (in this sense it is opposed to @deva[दुस्]); @deva[सुकृतं] 'done well,' @deva[सुशासित] 'thoroughly trained, well governed,' &c. It is also used in the sense of 'very, excessively;' @deva[सुमहत्] 'very great.'

[^1]: Cf. @deva[कारणेन अपिगच्छत् कारणं], &c. Shārīra Bhāshya. According to the grammarian Bhāguri the prepositions @deva[अपि] and @deva[अव] may optionally lose their @deva[अ]—@deva[वष्टि भागुरिरल्लोपमवाप्योरुपसर्गयोः] । (Sid. Kau.)
