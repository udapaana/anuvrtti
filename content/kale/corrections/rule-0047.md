Visarga (substituted for @deva[स्] and not @deva[र्]) preceded by short @deva[अ] and followed by short @deva[अ] or a soft consonant is changed to @deva[उ]; @deva[शिवः] + @deva[अर्च्यः] = @deva[शिव] + @deva[उ] + @deva[अर्च्यः] = @deva[शिवो] + @deva[अर्च्यः] = @deva[शिवोर्च्यः] Śiva is adorable; (see @ref[25]); @deva[देवः] + @deva[वन्द्यः] = @deva[देवो वन्द्य] God should be saluted; but @deva[तिष्ठतु पय अ३ग्निदत्त] (where the @deva[अ] after @deva[पयः] is *pluta*); @deva[प्रातः] + @deva[अत्र] = @deva[प्रातरत्र] (for here the visarga is substituted for @deva[र्]); so @deva[प्रातर्गच्छ] &c.
