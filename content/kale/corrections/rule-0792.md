## Relative with a Predicate Noun of Different Gender

When the relative has for its predicate a noun differing in gender from the antecedent the relative generally takes the gender of the antecedent noun, the demonstrative pronoun following that of the noun it qualifies; @deva[परगुणासहिष्णुत्वं हि यत्स दुर्जनानां स्वभावः]—non-endurance of the merits of another is but the nature of the wicked; @deva[शैत्यं हि यत्सा प्रकृतिर्जलस्य].
