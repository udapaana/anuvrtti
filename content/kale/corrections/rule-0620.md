## Pada Selection in Desideratives

Roots in their Desiderative forms take the same terminations, Parasm. or Atmane. that they do in the primitive. The roots @deva[ज्ञा], @deva[श्रु], @deva[स्मृ] and @deva[दृश्] take Atm. terminations in the Desiderative.
