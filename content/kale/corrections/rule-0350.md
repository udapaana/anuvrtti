## II. Feminine Words

Verbal derivatives formed by the terminations @deva[अनि], @deva[मि], @deva[नि], @deva[ति], @deva[ई] and @deva[ऊ]; as @deva[अवनिः], @deva[भूमिः], @deva[ग्लानिः], @deva[गतिः], @deva[लक्ष्मीः], @deva[चमूः], &c.

**Exceptions:**—@deva[वह्नि], @deva[अग्नि] and @deva[घृणि], all *mas.*
