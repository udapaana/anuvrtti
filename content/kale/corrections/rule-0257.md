The following words lose their final vowel when at the end of a Bahuvrīhi:—

**(a)**[^1] The word @deva[पाद] when it is preceded by a numeral or @deva[सु] or when the compound involves comparison and it is not preceded by a word of the @deva[हस्त्यादि] group (@deva[हस्तिन्], @deva[अज], @deva[कुसूल], @deva[अश्व], @deva[कपोत], @deva[जाल], @deva[गण्ड], @deva[दासी], @deva[गणिका] and some others). @deva[द्वौ पादौ यस्य द्विपात्] a biped; @deva[सुपात्] fair-footed; (@deva[व्याघ्रस्य इव पादौ यस्य],) &c. but @deva[हस्तिपादः], @deva[कुसूलपादः], &c.

**(b)** @deva[पाद] becomes @deva[पद्] and the comp. takes @deva[ई] in the *fem.* in the case of the words @deva[कुम्भपदी], and others (i. e. @deva[कुम्भपदी], @deva[एकपदी], @deva[जालपदी], @deva[सूत्रपदी], @deva[शतपदी], @deva[विपदी], @deva[द्विपदी], @deva[त्रिपदी], @deva[दासीपदी], @deva[विष्णुपदी], @deva[सुपदी], &c.;)[^2] but @deva[कुम्भपादः] *mas.*

**(c)**[^3] @deva[दन्त] necessarily when it is preceded by a numeral or @deva[सु] indicating age, or when the whole compound forms a feminine name; and optionally when it is preceded by @deva[श्याव], @deva[अरोक], a word ending in @deva[अम], @deva[शुद्ध], @deva[शुभ्र], @deva[वृष], or @deva[वराह]; @deva[द्वौ दन्तौ यस्य स द्विदन्] 'a child so small as to have two teeth only,' @deva[षड् दन्ता अस्य][^4] @deva[षोडन्]; @deva[शोभना दन्ता अस्य सुदन्], @deva[सुदती] (i. e. youthful) *fem.*; but @deva[द्विदन्तः करी], @deva[सुदन्तः] (having beautiful teeth) @deva[पुरुषः], @deva[अयोदती], @deva[फलदती] (both proper names,) &c. but @deva[समदन्ती] having even rows of teeth; @deva[श्यावा] (black) @deva[दन्ता यस्य स शावदन्-न्तः], @deva[अरोकदन्-दन्तः] having black or thick-set teeth; @deva[कुमडलाग्रदन्-न्तः], having teeth like the points of buds, @deva[शुभ्रदन्-न्तः] &c.

**(d)**[^5] @deva[ककुद्] 'when the compound denotes a particular stage of growth;' @deva[अजातं ककुदं यस्य सः अजातककुद्] 'a bull that has not got his hump as yet' i. e. 'who is very young'; also when it is preceded by @deva[त्रि] and the compound designates the mountain of that name; as @deva[त्रिककुद्] 'name of a mountain with three peaks'; but @deva[त्रिककुदः] 'having three humps'.

**(e)**[^6] @deva[काकुद] 'the palate' (@deva[काकुदं तालु] Kās.) necessarily when preceded by @deva[उत्] and @deva[वि] and optionally when preceded by @deva[पूर्ण]; as @deva[उत्काकुद], @deva[विकाकुद]; @deva[पूर्णकाकुद-कुदः].
