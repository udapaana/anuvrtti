## OTHER CHANGES IN CONNECTION WITH COMPOUNDS.

[^1] The word @deva[पाद्] is changed to @deva[पद्] when followed by @deva[आजि], @deva[आति], @deva[ग] and @deva[उपहत]; and to @deva[पद्] necessarily when followed by @deva[हिम], @deva[काषिन्], and @deva[हति], and optionally when followed by @deva[घोष], @deva[मिश्र], @deva[शब्द] and @deva[निष्क]; as @deva[पादाभ्यामजतीति पदाजिः], @deva[पद्भ्यामततीति पदातिः] @deva[पद्भ्यां गच्छतीति पद्गः] all meaning one walking on foot, a pedestrian; a foot-soldier', &c.; @deva[पदोपहतः] 'beaten down by the feet'; @deva[पद्धिमम्] 'coldness of the feet;' @deva[पादौ कषितुं शीलमस्य पत्काषी] 'one accustomed to tax his legs, a footman'; @deva[पदा हतिः पद्धतिः] a beaten-track, a way, a road; @deva[पद्घोषः] or @deva[पादघोषः], @deva[पन्मिश्रः] or @deva[पादमिश्रः]; @deva[पच्छब्दः] or @deva[पादशब्दः] @deva[पन्निष्कः] or @deva[पादनिष्कः] 'quarter of a Nishka' ( a gold coin ).
