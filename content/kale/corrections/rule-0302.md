title: "@deva[न] → @deva[ण] in @deva[वन] with Prepositions and Plants"
pages: [179.png, 180.png]
verdict: approved
---
**Obs.**—[^1] The @deva[न] of @deva[वन] is changed to @deva[ण] necessarily when preceded by @deva[प्र], and optionally when compounded with names of herbs or plants containing two or three vowels; @deva[प्रवणम्], @deva[कार्ष्यवणम्], &c. @deva[दूर्वावणम्-वनम्], @deva[शिरीषवणम्-वनम्]; but @deva[देवदारुवनम्] ( as the word contains more than three vowels ); exceptions:—@deva[इरिकावनम्], @deva[मिरिकावनम्], and @deva[तिमिरावनम्].

---

[^1]: @deva[प्रनिरन्तः शरेक्षुप्लक्षाम्रकार्ष्यखदिरपीयूक्षाभ्योऽसंज्ञायामपि] । @deva[विभाषौषधिवनस्पतिभ्यः] । Pān. VIII. 4. 5-6.
