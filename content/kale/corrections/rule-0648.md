## Formation with @deva[काम्य] Affix

To express the sense of wishing for that which is denoted by the noun; the affix @deva[काम्य] is also added like the above and the base so derived conjugated in the Par. as before, @deva[पुत्रकाम्यति] 'he wishes for a son,' @deva[यशस्काम्यति] 'he wishes for fame,' @deva[सर्पिष्काम्यति] 'he wishes for ghee.'
