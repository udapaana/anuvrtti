## THE MOODS.

## THE IMPERATIVE MOOD

[^1]The Imperative Mood does not express merely command, but also entreaty, benediction, courteous enquiry, gentle advice, ability, &c.

(a) In the second person this Mood is used to express command, entreaty, gentle advice and benedictions or blessings; @deva[गच्छ] (@deva[त्वं]) @deva[कुसुमपुरं] go to Kusumapura; @deva[परित्रायध्वं परित्रायध्वं] help! help! @deva[क्षमस्वापराधं] O God! forgive my faults, &c.; @deva[शुश्रूषस्व गुरून् कुरु प्रियसखीवृत्तिं सपत्नीजने] (S'ak. IV.) serve your elders and treat your co-wives as if they were your friends; @deva[एधि कार्यकरस्त्वं मे गत्वा प्रवद राघवं] be thou my messenger, go to Rāghava, and say to him; @deva[अनन्यभाजं पतिमाप्नुहीति सा तथ्यमेवाभिहता हरेण] she was addressed the truth by Hara when he said 'do thou obtain a husband not devoted to any other lady.'

(b) In the third person it is often used to express a blessing and sometimes gentle command; @deva[विधत्तां सिद्धिं नो…प्रकीर्णः पुष्पाणां हरिचरणयोरञ्जलिरयम्] may this handful of flowers scattered on the feet of Hari give us success; @deva[पर्जन्यः कालवर्षी भवतु] may rain pour down in time; @deva[पश्चात्तिष्ठन्तु वीरा शकनरपतयः] (Mud. V. II.).

(c) In the first person it expresses a question, necessity, ability, &c; @deva[किं करवाणि ते] what should I do for you? @deva[अधुनाहं गच्छानि] I must go now; @deva[करवामैतद्द्वयं देवि प्रियं तव] we will (are able to) do this thing, oh queen, which is agreeable to you; @deva[नहि प्रेष्यवधं घोरं करवाण्यस्तु ते मतिः] (Bhaṭṭi XX. 6.) let your thought be 'I must not commit the horrible murder of an ambassador.'
