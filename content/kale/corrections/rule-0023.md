---
key: rule:23
title: "Optional Non-combination of Final @deva[इ]/@deva[उ]/@deva[ऋ]/@deva[ऌ]"
pages: [017.png, 018.png]
verdict: approved
---
**(a)** @deva[इ], @deva[उ], @deva[ऋ] or @deva[ऌ], short or long, at the end of a word followed by a dissimilar vowel except in a compound, are optionally not combined, and when so they are shortened if long[^1] e.g. @deva[चक्री] + @deva[अत्र] = @deva[चक्र्यत्र], Vishṇu here, and @deva[चक्रि अत्र]; but @deva[वापी] + @deva[अश्वः] = @deva[वाप्यश्वः] a horse in a well, @deva[गौरी] + @deva[औ] (a term.) @deva[गौर्यौ].

**(b)** @deva[अ], @deva[इ], @deva[उ] and @deva[ऌ], short or long, at the end of a word followed by a short @deva[ऋ] are also optionally not combined, and when not combined are shortened if long[^2]; e.g. @deva[ब्रह्मा] + @deva[ऋषिः] = @deva[ब्रह्मर्षिः] A Brāhmaṇa saint, and @deva[ब्रह्म ऋषिः]; @deva[सप्तर्षीणाम्] of the seven sages, and @deva[सप्त ऋषीणाम्] (a comp. expression).

---

[^1]: @deva[इकोऽसवर्णे शाकल्यस्य ह्रस्वश्च ।]

[^2]: @deva[ऋत्यकः] Pāṇ. VII. 1. 127.
