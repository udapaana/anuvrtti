## Formation of Perfect Participles

The participle of the Perf. Par. and Atm. is formed by adding @deva[वस्] and @deva[आन] generally to that form of the root which it assumes before the termination of the 3rd per. plural. If this form consists of one syllable only or when the root ends in @deva[आ], @deva[वस्] has the intermediate @deva[इ] added to it. @deva[इ] is optionally prefixed to @deva[वस्] in the case of the roots @deva[गम्], @deva[हन्], @deva[दृश्], @deva[विश्] and @deva[विद्] (6 cl.); @deva[जन्] and @deva[खन्], and @deva[गम्] and @deva[हन्] when they do not take @deva[इ], form this participle from that base which they would assume before the 2nd per. sing. termination, *e. g.*
