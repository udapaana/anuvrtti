---
key: rule:282
title: "Adding @deva[अ] with Vowel/Consonant Dropping"
pages: [168.png, 169.png]
verdict: approved
---
The following words take the suffix @deva[अ] before which the final vowel, with the following consonant, if any, is dropped.[^1]

**(a)** @deva[सामन्] and @deva[लोमन्] when preceded by @deva[प्रति], @deva[अनु] or @deva[अव]; @deva[प्रतिसामं], @deva[साम अनुगतः अनुसामः] friendly; @deva[अवरं साम अवसामम्] a bad hymn; @deva[प्रतिलोमम्] 'in inverse order, inversely'; @deva[अनुलोमम्] 'in natural order, successively, directly.'

**(b)** @deva[भूमि] preceded by @deva[कृष्ण], @deva[उदच्], @deva[पाण्डु] or a numeral; @deva[कृष्णा भूमिः यस्य स कृष्णभूमः]; similarly @deva[उदीची भूमिः यस्य स उदग्भूमः]; @deva[पाण्डुभूमिः यस्य स पाण्डुभूमः]; @deva[द्वे भूमी यस्य स द्विभूमः] [@deva[प्रासादः]] 'having two floors.'

**(c)** The words @deva[नदी] and @deva[गोदावरी] preceded by a numeral; @deva[पञ्चनदम्], @deva[सप्तगोदावरम्].

**(d)** @deva[अक्षि] when used in a metaphorical sense, and not meaning the eye'; as @deva[गवामक्षीव गवाक्षः] *lit.* resembling the eye of a bull: hence 'a round window.'

**(e)** @deva[अध्वन्] when preceded by a preposition; @deva[प्रगतोऽध्वानं] a carriage that has reached a road, @deva[प्रकृष्टो] ( distant ) @deva[अध्वा] journey @deva[वा प्राध्वः] ।

**(f)** [^2] also @deva[नाभि] in compounds like @deva[पद्मनाभः] ।

---

[^1]: @deva[अच्च प्रत्यन्ववपूर्वात्सामलोम्नः] । Pân. V. 4. 75. @deva[कृष्णोदक्पाण्डुसंख्यापूर्वाया भूमेरजिष्यते] । @deva[संख्याया नदीगोदावरीभ्यां च] । Vârtikas. @deva[अक्ष्णोऽदर्शनात्] । Pân. V. 4. 76. @deva[उपसर्गादध्वनः] । Pân. V. 4. 85.

[^2]: @deva[अजिति योगविभागादन्यत्रापि] । Sid. Kau.; this is obtained by separating the portion @deva[अच्] from the Sûtra '@deva[अच् प्रत्यन्वव०]' But this is no sanction for forming new compounds ending in @deva[नाभ]. It is only a device used by grammarians to explain such words as @deva[नलिननाभ] &c.
