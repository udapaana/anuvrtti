Instances of the Aluk of the Instrumental[^1] (i. e. in which the noun in the Inst. does not drop its case term.)—@deva[अञ्जसा कृतम् अञ्जसाकृतम्] 'done honestly'; @deva[ओजसाकृतम्] 'done with might'; @deva[पुंसानुजः] 'one having an elder brother'; @deva[जनुषान्धः] 'blind from birth'; @deva[मनसागुप्ता], @deva[मनसाज्ञायी], when proper names, otherwise @deva[मनोगुप्ता], @deva[मनोज्ञायी], &c. @deva[आत्मन्] followed by an ordinal number retains its case ending; as @deva[आत्मना पञ्चम आत्मनापञ्चमः ।]

---

[^1]: @deva[ओजःसहोम्भस्तमसस्तृतीयाया ।] Pāṇ. VI. 3. 3. @deva[अञ्जस उपसंख्यानम् ।] Vārt.
