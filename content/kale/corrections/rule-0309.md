---
key: rule:309
title: "Insertion of @deva[आयन्] Before @deva[ई]"
pages: [184.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] Some *prâtipadikas* ending in the Tad. affix @deva[य] insert @deva[आयन्] before @deva[ई]; @deva[गार्ग्यायणी] ( the grand-daughter of @deva[गर्ग] ), @deva[लोहित्यायनी], @deva[कात्यायनी]. &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[सर्वत्र लोहितादिकतन्तेभ्यः] । Pān. IV. I. 18.
