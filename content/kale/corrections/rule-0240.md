---
key: rule:240
title: "Addition of @deva[अ] to Final Member"
pages: [146.png, 147.png]
verdict: approved
---
The following words have an @deva[अ] added to them when at the end of a Tatpurusha compound:—

**(a)** @deva[गो] except when a Tad. aff. is added and then dropped;[^1] e. g. @deva[परमगवः] an excellent bull, @deva[पञ्चगव]-(a collection of five cows) @deva[धनः], but @deva[द्विगुः] exchanged or bartered for two cows.

**(b)**[^2] @deva[उरस्] meaning 'chief or eminent'. @deva[अश्वानां उर इव अश्वोरसम्] 'the chief of horses i. e. an excellent horse.'

**(c)**[^3] The words @deva[अनस्] @deva[अश्मन्], @deva[अयस्] and @deva[सरस्] when they denote a class or form names; @deva[उपानसम्] (@deva[उपगतं अनः]) 'a cart-load,' @deva[महानसः] 'a kitchen'; @deva[अमृताश्मः] 'a kind of stone like the moon-stone' [in this case the final @deva[अन्] is dropped]; @deva[कालायसम्] 'black iron', @deva[मण्डूकसरसम्] 'a lake full of frogs'; @deva[जलसरसम्] 'a lake of that name.'

**(d)**[^4] @deva[नौ] at the end of a Dwigu, except when the Tad. affix is dropped), as @deva[द्वाभ्यां नौभ्यामागतः द्विनावरूप्यः] (when the aff. is not dropped), @deva[द्विनावम्] 'a collection of two boats', so @deva[त्रिनावम्] &c; but @deva[पञ्चभिः नौभिः क्रीतः पञ्चनौः], also when it is preceded by @deva[अर्ध]; as @deva[नावः अर्धम् अर्धनावम्] the *neuter* is irregular (@deva[लोकात्] Sid. Kau).

**(e)**[^5] @deva[खारी] (a measure of corn) under similar circumstances takes the @deva[अ] optionally, the final @deva[ई] being dropped before it; @deva[द्विखारम्-रिः], @deva[अर्धखारम्-रि].

**(f)**[^6] @deva[अञ्जलि], preceded by @deva[द्वि] or @deva[त्रि] in a Dwigu, optionally takes @deva[अ], except when the Tad. aff. is dropped, before which the final @deva[इ] is dropped; @deva[द्व्यञ्जलं-लि] 'two handfuls'; but @deva[द्वाभ्यामञ्जलिभ्यां क्रीतः द्व्यञ्जलिः ।]

[^1]: @deva[गोरतद्धितलुकि ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 92.

[^2]: @deva[अग्राख्यायामुरसः ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 93.

[^3]: @deva[अनोश्मायः सरसां जातिसंज्ञयोः ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 94.

[^4]: @deva[नावो द्विगोः । अर्धाच्च ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 99. 100.

[^5]: @deva[खार्याः प्राचाम् ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 101.

[^6]: @deva[द्वित्रिभ्यामञ्जलेः ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 102.
