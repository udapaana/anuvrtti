**Non-Conjugational Tenses.**

## Non-Conjugational Tenses of Desideratives

(*a*) The Perfect is formed by adding @deva[आम्] to the base and appending the Perfect forms of the auxiliary verbs @deva[अस्], @deva[भू], and @deva[कृ] (see @ref[490], @ref[526]).

(*b*) In the Aorist the Desiderative Bases take the terminations of the Vth Variety.

(*c*) In the Benedictive the Parasmai. terminations are added without @deva[इ] and the Atm. ones with the intermediate @deva[इ].

(*d*) The remaining tenses have no peculiarities.
