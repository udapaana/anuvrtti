---
key: rule:894
title: "आस् and स्था with Present Participles for Continuity"
pages: [509.png]
verdict: approved
---
The roots @deva[आस्] and @deva[स्था] are generally used with present participles to show the continuity of the action denoted by them; @deva[पशूनां वधं कुर्वन् आस्ते] used to kill (always kept on killing) animals; @deva[तं प्रतिपालयन् तस्थौ] remained waiting for him.
