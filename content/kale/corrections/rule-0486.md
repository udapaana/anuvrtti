## Substitution of @deva[गा] for @deva[इ] with @deva[अधि]; @deva[ई] for Final Vowel of @deva[दा], @deva[धा], &c.

In the case of @deva[इ] with @deva[अधि], @deva[गा] is optionally substituted for @deva[इ] in the Conditional and the Aorist. @deva[ई] is substituted for the final vowel of the verbs @deva[दा] 3 P. Â. 1 P. @deva[धा], @deva[दो], @deva[दे], @deva[धे], @deva[मा], @deva[स्था], @deva[गा] (substituted for @deva[इ] 2 P. and @deva[इ] with @deva[अधि],) @deva[पा], @deva[हा], and @deva[सो] before a consonantal weak termination. All terminations added to @deva[गा] substituted for @deva[इ] are weak.
