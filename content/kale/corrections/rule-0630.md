## Nasal Insertion in Ātmanepada Frequentatives

When a root ends in a nasal preceded by @deva[अ] and the nasal is not dropped, @deva[न्] (changeable to an Anusvāra or the nasal of the class to which the letter following it belongs) is inserted between the reduplicative @deva[अ] and the first radical consonant; the @deva[अ] of the reduplicative syllable remains unchanged (against @ref[629] b.).

@deva[यम्]—@deva[यम्य], @deva[ययम्य] = @deva[यंयम्यते] or @deva[यँय्यम्यते], @deva[जन्]—@deva[जन्य]—@deva[जजन्य] = @deva[जंजन्यते] or @deva[जञ्जन्यते], but when @deva[जन्] = @deva[जाय्], the A. Freq. base is @deva[जाजाय], 3rd sing. @deva[जाजायते].

(a) The same rule applies to the roots @deva[चर्], @deva[फल्], @deva[जप्], @deva[जभ्], @deva[दह्], @deva[दंश्], @deva[भञ्ज्] and @deva[पश्]. @deva[चर्] and @deva[फल्] after inserting @deva[न्] change the @deva[अ] of the following syllable to @deva[उ]; @deva[चर्]=@deva[चर्य]—@deva[चचर्य]=@deva[चंचुर्य] or @deva[चञ्चुर्य]=@deva[चंचूर्यते] or @deva[चञ्चूर्यते] by @ref[394]. @deva[फल्]=@deva[फल्य]=@deva[पफल्य]=@deva[पंफुल्यते] or @deva[पम्फुल्यते]; @deva[दह्]=@deva[दह्य]—@deva[ददह्य]=@deva[दंदह्यते] or @deva[दन्दह्यते] @deva[जप्]—@deva[जंजप्यते] or @deva[जञ्जप्यते].
(b) @deva[नी] is inserted after the @deva[अ] of the reduplicative syllable (which then remains unchanged) in the case of the roots @deva[वञ्च्], @deva[स्रंस्], @deva[ध्वंस्], @deva[भ्रंस्], @deva[कस्], @deva[पत्], @deva[पद्] and @deva[स्कन्द्]; @deva[वञ्च्]—@deva[वञ्च्य]—@deva[ववञ्च्य]—@deva[वनीवञ्च्यते], @deva[स्रंस्]—@deva[स्रस्य]—@deva[सनीस्रस्यते]; @deva[ध्वंस्]=@deva[दनीध्वस्यते], @deva[भ्रंस्]=@deva[बनीभ्रस्यते]. @deva[कस्]=@deva[कनीकस्यते], @deva[पत्]=@deva[पनीपत्यते], @deva[पद्]=@deva[पनीपद्यते], @deva[स्कन्द्]=@deva[कनीस्कद्यते].
