title: "Feminine of Dwigu Compounds"
pages: [193.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] A Dwigu ending in @deva[अ] forms its *fem.* in @deva[ई]; as @deva[त्रिलोकी]; but if the ending word be one of those included in the Ajâdi group ( see foot-note on p. 108 ) its *fem.* is formed in @deva[आ]; @deva[त्रिफला], @deva[त्र्यनीका] ( consisting of three battalions ) @deva[सेना], &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[द्विगोः] । @deva[अपरिमाणविस्ताचितकम्बल्येभ्यो न तद्धितलुकि] । Pân. IV. 1. 21. 22.
