Irregular Bases.

@deva[पूषन्], @deva[अर्यमन्] and nouns ending in @deva[हन्] lengthen their @deva[अ] in the Nom. sing. only; the @deva[न्] of @deva[हन्] is changed to @deva[ण्] after @deva[ह्]; e. g.
