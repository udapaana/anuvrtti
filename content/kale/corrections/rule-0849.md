## Genitive/Ablative with @deva[दूर], @deva[अन्तिक] and Synonyms

[^1] The words @deva[दूर] and @deva[अन्तिक] and their synonyms govern either the Genitive or the Ablative; @deva[ग्रामात् ग्रामस्य वा वनं दूरं—निकटं—समीपं वा] the forest is distant from or near the village. @deva[रामाद्रुद्रस्य यो दूरं पापाद्दुःखस्य सोन्तिकं] he who is away from Rāma or Śiva is near sin. @deva[प्रत्यासन्नो माधवीमण्डपस्य] close to the bower of the Mādhavī creeper; @deva[तस्य सकाशं], &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[दूरान्तिकार्थैः षष्ठ्यन्यतरस्याम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 34.
