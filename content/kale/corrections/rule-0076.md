Decline (@deva[यान्ति अनेन इति]) @deva[ययी] a way or horse, (@deva[पाति लोकं इति]) @deva[पपी] the sun, &c. similarly

**Obs.** Decline (@deva[बह्वयः श्रेयस्यः यस्य]) @deva[बहुश्रेयसी] mas. one having many good wives, and (@deva[लक्ष्मी अतिक्रान्तः]) @deva[अतिलक्ष्मी] mas. like @deva[नदी] except in the Acc. plu. which is @deva[बहुश्रेयसीन्] and @deva[अतिलक्ष्मीन्] respectively. @deva[अतिलक्ष्मी] fem. should be declined like @deva[लक्ष्मी].

The word @deva[वातप्रमी], derived with the affix @deva[क्विप्], is declined like @deva[प्रधी] which see.
