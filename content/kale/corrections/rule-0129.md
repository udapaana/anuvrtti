So असृज् and असन् are kept, as the cropped ends of ours, and शकृत् (in ours, line 2; absent from alt's list) is kept. Not a union of two readings — but a human should confirm."
  - "Alt p. 83 lacks शकृत् in the opening list (has शकन् among the forms)."

  - "निशा L. plu.: second form read निच्छु (could be निच्सु — the glyph is small in both printings); third form cut in ours, read निट्सु-ट्त्सु from alt p. 84 (ट् vs द् uncertain)."
  - "असृज् L. plu. -अससु: last syllables small on alt p. 85."
  - "दोस् L. plu. दोःषु-ःषु-षषु kept from the draft: ours reads like दोष्षु-ःषु-षसु; blurred in both printings."
  - "p. 85 (दन्त, नासिका, मास, हृदय, असृज्) exists only in the alt printing; read from it."
  - "शकृत् A./I. plu. cells cut at the right edge of scan 083 (शकानि, शकभिः) — from the draft's alt reading."
  - "मांस L. plu. मांसेषु,मान्सु-मान्त्सु as printed."
