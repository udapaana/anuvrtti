## Instrumental with Characteristic Attributes

A characteristic attribute indicative of the existence of a particular state or condition is put in the Instrumental;[^1] @deva[जटाभिः तापसः] he is an ascetic (which is apparent) from his matted hair (@deva[जटाज्ञाप्यतापसत्वविशिष्ट इत्यर्थ] Sid. Kau.).

---

[^1]: @deva[इत्थंभूतलक्षणे ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 21.
