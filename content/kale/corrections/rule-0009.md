---
key: rule:9
title: "Homogeneous and Heterogeneous Letters: Savarṇa and Asavarṇa"
pages: [007.png]
verdict: approved
---
Those letters are called @deva[सवर्ण] or homogeneous whose place of pronunciation is the mouth and the effort required to pronounce them is the same or equal.[^1] Letters which are not @deva[सवर्ण] or similar are called @deva[असवर्ण] or heterogeneous.

---

[^1]: @deva[तुल्यास्यप्रयत्नं सवर्णम्] — Pāṇ. I. 1. 9.
