## Locative/Genitive with @deva[अपराध्] (to offend)

The root @deva[राध्] with @deva[अप] 'to offend' is generally construed with the Locative and sometimes with the Genitive; @deva[कस्मिन्नपि पूजार्हे अपराद्धा शकुन्तला] Śakuntalā has offended some one deserving respect; @deva[न तु ग्रीष्मस्यैवं सुभगमपराद्धं युवतिषु] (Śak. III. 9.), @deva[किंपुनरसुरावलेपेन भवतीनामपराद्धं ।] Vik. I.
