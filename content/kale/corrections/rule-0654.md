## @deva[य] Affix for 'Becoming What It Was Not Before'

The affix @deva[य] is added to a few nouns such as @deva[भृश] 'much,' @deva[मन्द] 'slow,' '@deva[पण्डित]' 'learned,' @deva[सुमनस्] 'generous-minded,' @deva[उन्मनस्] 'agitated,' &c. in the sense of 'becoming what it was not before,' or 'what it was not like to before,' @deva[भृशः भवति] @deva[भृशायते] 'what was not much now becomes much,' @deva[उन्मनायति] 'he (who was not agitated before) becomes agitated,' similarly @deva[सुमनायते] &c.
