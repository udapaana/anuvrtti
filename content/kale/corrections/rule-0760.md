---
key: rule:760
title: "Desiderative and Frequentative Bases with @deva[य]"
pages: [443.png, 444.png]
verdict: approved
---
Desiderative bases add @deva[य] immediately and Freq. bases add it after dropping their @deva[य], when it is preceded by a consonant and dropping the final @deva[अ] when it is preceded by a vowel; @deva[बुध्]—@deva[प्रबुबोधिष्य]; Fre. @deva[प्रबोबुध्य]; @deva[भू] Fre. @deva[प्रबोभूय्य] &c.
