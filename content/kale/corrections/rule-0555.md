## Roots Not Shortening Penultimate Vowel

The roots @deva[शास्], @deva[एज्], @deva[काश्], @deva[क्रीड्], @deva[क्षीब्], @deva[खाद्], @deva[खेल्], @deva[ढौक्], @deva[ताय्], @deva[दाश्], @deva[देव्], @deva[नाथ्], @deva[प्रोथ्], @deva[बाध्], @deva[याच्], @deva[योध्], @deva[राध्], @deva[राज्], @deva[लाघ्], @deva[लेप्], @deva[लोक्], @deva[लोच्], @deva[वेप्], @deva[वेल्], @deva[श्लाघ्], @deva[श्लोक्], @deva[सेक्], @deva[सेव्], @deva[हेष्], and others less important, do not shorten their penultimate; 3 sing. @deva[अशशासत्], @deva[ऐजिजत्], @deva[अचकाशत्], @deva[अचिक्रीडत्], @deva[अचिक्षिवत्], @deva[अचखादत्], @deva[अचिखेलत्], &c.
