§ 371. Like prepositions may be further used the words to which the Taddhita affix @deva[सात्] is affixed, @deva[अग्निसात्कृ] 'to consign to flames;' @deva[भस्मसात्कृत] 'reduced to ashes,' @deva[राजसात्कृता] 'made over to the king.' &c. (vide p. 216.)
