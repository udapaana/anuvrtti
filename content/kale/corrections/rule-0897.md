---
key: rule:897
title: "Intransitive Past Passive Participles with Nominative Agent"
pages: [510.png]
verdict: approved
---
In the case of past passive participles of intransitive roots the agent is put in the Nominative case; @deva[तदा प्ररुदितो राजा रक्षसां] then the king of demons wept; @deva[सत्यं मृतोयं पापः] &c.
