title: "Impersonal Potential for Precepts"
pages: [529.png]
verdict: approved
---
Sometimes the Potential is used without a subject when it is used in the sense of 'a precept or advice'; @deva[आपदर्थे धनं रक्षेद्दारान् रक्षेद्धनैरपि। आत्मानं सततं रक्षेद्दारैरपि धनैरपि॥] (a man) should save money for adversity, he should save his wife at the cost of his wealth and himself even at the expense of his wife and wealth; @deva[यद्यद्रोचेत विप्रेभ्यस्तत्तद्दद्यादमत्सरः] (one) should serve Brāhmaṇas with all that they like, without being jealous.
