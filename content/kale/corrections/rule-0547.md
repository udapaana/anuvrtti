## Formation of Third Aorist: Reduplication and Vowel Changes

(a) The root is first reduplicated and then the augment @deva[अ] and the terminations are added as in the 2nd Variety.

(b) Final @deva[इ] and @deva[उ] are changed to @deva[इय्] and @deva[उव्] before @deva[अ] and final @deva[ओ] is dropped.

Paradigms.

**@deva[श्रि] P. A. 'to go,' &c.**

**Par.**

| | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | @deva[अशिश्रियम्] | @deva[अशिश्रियाव] | @deva[अशिश्रियाम] |
| 2. | @deva[अशिश्रियः] | @deva[अशिश्रियतम्] | @deva[अशिश्रियत] |
| 3. | @deva[अशिश्रियत्] | @deva[अशिश्रियताम्] | @deva[अशिश्रियन्] |
