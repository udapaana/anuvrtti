title: "@deva[यदि] and @deva[चेत्] with Potential and Conditional"
pages: [533.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[यदि] and @deva[चेत्] 'if' are usually used with the Potential or the Conditional; as @deva[यदि सोत्र सन्निहितो भवेत् तर्हि मम साहाय्यं कुर्यात्] if he were here he would assist me; @deva[यदि देवदत्तोत्राभविष्यन्नूनमेतदकरिष्यत्] had Devadatta been here, he would have undoubtedly done this; but they are also construed with the Present Indicative; @deva[यदि जीवति भद्राणि पश्यति] if he lives he will see prosperity; @deva[यदि मया देवपादानां प्रयोजनमस्ति] if Your Majesty has any thing to do with me, &c; @deva[शापितोसि मम जीवितेन यदि वाचा न कथयसि] I conjure you by my life if you will not tell it in words. @deva[चेत्] is never used at the beginning of a sentence; @deva[तं चेत्सहस्रकिरणो धुरि नाकरिष्यत्] (S'ak. VII. 4) if the thousand-rayed one (sun) did not place him at the yoke of his car, @deva[अयि रोषमुरीकरोषि] (have recourse to) @deva[नो चेत्] &c Bha. V. I. 44.
