The word @deva[क्रोष्टु] a jackal, is declined like a word ending in @deva[ऋ] necessary in the first five inflections and optionally before the vowel terminations beginning with the Inst. sing. except that of the Gen. plu.[^1] e. g.

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. | @deva[क्रोष्टा] | @deva[क्रोष्टारौ] | @deva[क्रोष्टारः] |
| V. | @deva[क्रोष्टो] | ,, | ,, |
| A. | @deva[क्रोष्टारम्] | ,, | @deva[क्रोष्टून्] |
| I. | @deva[क्रोष्ट्रा-ष्टुना] | @deva[क्रोष्टुभ्याम्] | @deva[क्रोष्टुभिः] |
| D. | @deva[क्रोष्ट्रे-ष्टवे] | ,, | @deva[क्रोष्टुभ्यः] |
| Ab. | @deva[क्रोष्टुः-क्रोष्टोः] | ,, | ,, |
| G. | ,, | @deva[क्रोष्ट्रोः-ष्ट्वोः] | @deva[क्रोष्टूनाम्] |
| L. | @deva[क्रोष्टरि-ष्टौ] | ,, | @deva[क्रोष्टुषु] |

**(a)** @deva[क्रोष्टु] becomes @deva[क्रोष्टृ] in the *fem.* also (by @deva[स्त्रियां च] Pāṇ. VIII. 1. 96.) which with the *fem.* term. @deva[ई] becomes @deva[क्रोष्ट्री] and is declined like @deva[नदी].

**Obs.**—The word @deva[प्रियक्रोष्टु] *neu.* is declined like @deva[मधु] and optionally like @deva[क्रोष्टु] *mas.* before the vowel terminations from the I. sing.; e. g. D. sing. @deva[प्रियक्रोष्ट्रे-ष्टवे-ष्टुने].

[^1]: @deva[तृज्वत्क्रोष्टुः । विभाषा तृतीयादिष्वचि] । Pāṇ. VII. 1. 95, 97.
