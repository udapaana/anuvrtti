---
key: rule:390
title: "Sixth Class (@deva[तुदादि]): Addition of @deva[अ] with Vowel Changes"
pages: [242.png, 243.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[अ] is added on to the roots of the sixth or @deva[तुदादि] class before which the penultimate vowel remains unchanged, and the final @deva[इ], @deva[उ], short or long, @deva[ऋ] and @deva[ॠ] are changed to @deva[इय्], @deva[उव्], @deva[रिय्] and @deva[इर्] respectively; e. g. @deva[क्षिप्+ति=क्षिप्+अ+ति=क्षिपति]; @deva[धु+ति=धुव्+अ+ति=धुवति]; @deva[रि+अ+ति=रियति]; @deva[मृ+अ+ते=म्रियते]; @deva[गॄ+अ+ति=गिर्+अ+ति=गिरति], &c.
