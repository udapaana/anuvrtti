## Bases derived from the root @deva[अञ्च्] 'to go' to worship.

Nouns derived form the root @deva[अञ्च्] 'to go' by means of such affixes as @deva[क्विन्], @deva[क्विप्] &c. such as @deva[प्राञ्च्] (@deva[प्रा-अञ्च्]) eastern, @deva[प्रत्यञ्च्] (@deva[प्रति-अञ्च्]) western, @deva[सम्यङ्] (@deva[सह-अञ्च्]) a companion, @deva[तिर्यञ्च्] (@deva[तिरस्-अञ्च्]) going away, @deva[सम्यञ्च्] (@deva[सम्-अञ्च्]) going with or well, @deva[विष्वञ्च्] (@deva[विष्वञ्च्-अञ्च्]) following, @deva[देवद्र्यञ्च्] (@deva[देव-अञ्च्]) adoring a deity, @deva[सर्वद्र्यञ्च्] (@deva[सर्व-अञ्च्]) going everywhere, @deva[उदञ्च्] northern, @deva[अन्वञ्च्] following, @deva[उदद्र्यञ्च्], @deva[अदमुयञ्च्], @deva[अमुमुयञ्च्] going to that (all these derived from @deva[अदस्+अञ्च्]), @deva[गोअञ्च्] &c.

**(a)** In the Nom sing. @deva[अञ्च्] becomes @deva[अङ्]. The nasal of @deva[अञ्च्] is dropped before all terminations beginning with the Acc. plu. and the preceding semi-vowel is changed to its corresponding long vowel before the vowel terminations after which @deva[अ] is dropped. When @deva[अञ्च्] is not preceded by a semi-vowel, its @deva[अ] is changed to @deva[ई] if not preceded by a long vowel; otherwise it remains unchanged. @deva[तिर्यञ्च्] becomes @deva[तिरश्च्] under similar circumstances. In other respects these words are declined like words ending in @deva[च्].
