title: "Potential or Imperative with Verbs of Wishing"
pages: [528.png, 529.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1]When words having the sense of 'to wish,' such as @deva[इष्], @deva[कम्]. &c. are used, the Potential or Imperative is used; @deva[इच्छामि भवान्भुञ्जीत] or @deva[भुङ्क्ताम्] I wish you should dine; @deva[इच्छामि सोमं पिबेत् पिबतु वा भवान्] (Sid. Kau) I wish Your Honour will drink Soma.

(a) But when the agents of both the actions are the same, the Potential alone is used in the sense of the Infinitive; @deva[भुञ्जीयेतीच्छति] (Sid. Kau.) i. e. @deva[भोक्तुमिच्छति] wishes that he will eat (wishes to eat).
