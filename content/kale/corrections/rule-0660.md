## Roots That Are Also Prātipadika (@deva[यक्] Derivatives)

There are several roots[^1] which are also *Pratipadikas* and which may be regarded as Nominal verbal bases derived from those *Pratipadikas* by means of the affix @deva[य] (@deva[यक्]). The more important of these are given below:—

| | |
|---|---|
| @deva[कण्डू]-@deva[कण्डूयति-ते] he scratches. | @deva[सपर]—@deva[सपर्यति] he worships. |
| @deva[मंतु]-@deva[मंतूयति] he offends against, he becomes angry, also @deva[मंतूयते] according to @deva[चन्द्र]. | @deva[भिषज्]-@deva[भिषज्यति] treats medically. |
| | @deva[इषुध]-@deva[इषुध्यति] it contains arrows. |
| | @deva[गद्गद]—@deva[गद्गद्यति] he stammers. |
| @deva[वल्गु]—@deva[वल्गूयति] he becomes handsome, mild or gentle, honours | @deva[केला], @deva[खेला]—@deva[केलायति], @deva[खेलायति] he sports or becomes merry. |
| @deva[असु]—@deva[असूयति-ते] (@deva[अस्यति]) he is jealous, he envies, &c. | @deva[हृणी]—@deva[हृणीयते] he is angry or feels ashamed. |
| @deva[लेला]—@deva[लेलायति] he shines. | @deva[रेखा]—@deva[रेखायति] he approaches. |
| @deva[उषस्]—@deva[उषस्यति] it dawns. | @deva[मही]—@deva[महीयते] he is adored, &c. |
| @deva[मेधा]—@deva[मेधयति] he is quick in understanding, | @deva[तिरस्]—@deva[तिरस्यति] he disappears. |
| | @deva[अगद]-@deva[अगद्यति] he becomes healthy. |
| @deva[सुख]—@deva[सुख्यति] feels happy. | @deva[उरस्]—@deva[उरस्यति] he becomes powerful. |
| @deva[दुःख]—@deva[दुःख्यति] feels unhappy. | @deva[पयस्]—@deva[पयस्यति] it spreads. |

[^1]: In the Kaumudī these roots are classed separately under the head of '@deva[कण्ड्वादिगण]' or the group of roots beginning with @deva[कण्डू].
