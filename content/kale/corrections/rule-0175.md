title: "Dropping Possessive Affixes Before @deva[ईयस्] and @deva[इष्ठ]"
pages: [111.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "footnote printed \"तुरिष्ठेमेय सु\" (gap); kept तुरिष्ठेमेयस्सु"
---
The possessive affixes at the end of words and the affix @deva[तृ] are dropped before @deva[ईयस्] and @deva[इष्ठ][^1] and then these terminations are applied subject to the above rule; e. g. @deva[मतिमत्] talented @deva[मतीयस्], @deva[मतिष्ठ]; @deva[मेधाविन्]-@deva[मेधीयस्], @deva[मेधिष्ठ]; @deva[धनिन्]-@deva[धनीयस्]-@deva[धनिष्ठ]; @deva[कर्तृ]-@deva[करीयस्], @deva[करिष्ठ] (@deva[अतिशयेन कर्ता]); @deva[स्तोतृ]-@deva[स्तवीयस्], @deva[स्तविष्ठ]; so @deva[स्रजीयस्], @deva[स्रजिष्ठ] from @deva[स्रग्विन्] one having a garland &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[विन्मतोर्लुक् । तुरिष्ठेमेयस्सु ।] Pāṇ. VI. 4, 154.
