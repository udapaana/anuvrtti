title: "Changes Before Feminine Affix @deva[ई]"
pages: [180.png, 181.png]
verdict: approved
---
Before the affix @deva[ई]—

**(a)** nouns ending in consonants assume that form which they take before the termination of Inst. sing.; @deva[प्रत्यञ्च्-प्रतीची], @deva[राजन्-राज्ञी], @deva[मघवन्-मघोनी], @deva[श्वन्-शुनी], @deva[अर्यमन्-अर्यम्णी], @deva[विद्वस्-विदुषी], &c. There are some exceptions, @deva[अर्वन्-अर्वणी] &c.

**(b)** the preceding @deva[अ] or @deva[ई] is dropped; as @deva[गौर-गौरी], @deva[औत्स-औत्सी], @deva[पार्वती]. &c.

**(c)** if a *Prâtipadika*[^1] end in @deva[य], part of a Taddhita affix, that @deva[य] is dropped @deva[गार्ग्य + ई = गार्गी] 'the daughter of Garga.' &c.

**(d)** the final @deva[य] of the words @deva[सूर्य], @deva[तिष्य] the constellation of stars called @deva[पुष्य], @deva[अगस्त्य] and @deva[मत्स्य][^2] is dropped; as @deva[सौरी], @deva[मत्सी], &c.

**(e)** in the case of the participles of the present and the future @deva[न्] is inserted, as in the Nom. dual of the *neu.* ( vide @ref[311]. *a. b.* ); for instances, see @ref[236].

---

[^1]: @deva[हलस्तद्धितस्य] । Pān VI. 4. 150. For the meaning of the term *Prâtipadika* vide @ref[52].

[^2]: @deva[सूर्यतिष्यागस्त्यमत्स्यानां य उपधायाः] । Pān VI. 4. 149.
