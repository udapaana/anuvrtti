---
key: rule:460
title: "Tenth Class Roots Preserving @deva[अय्] in General Tenses"
pages: [297.png]
verdict: approved
---
Roots of the tenth class preserve their @deva[अय्] (i. e. @deva[अय] with the final @deva[अ] dropped) with all the changes that the root undergoes before it in the general tenses.
