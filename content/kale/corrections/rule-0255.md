title: "Transformations of @deva[धर्म], @deva[धनुष्], @deva[जम्भ], and @deva[ईर्म]"
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "read entirely from the alt printing (our scans lack leaf 156)"
  - "शतधन्वा-नुः: printed short form after the hyphen looks like -नुः (for शतधनुः); uncertain"
  - "निवृत्तिधर्मा: old-style वृ ligature, read as निवृत्ति"
---
**(a)**[^1] @deva[धर्म] when preceded by a single member in a Bahuvrīhi becomes @deva[धर्मन्]; @deva[कल्याणं धर्मः यस्य स कल्याणधर्मा]; similarly @deva[समानधर्मा] (see Māl. Māḍ. Act. I. Intro.); but @deva[परमः स्वः धर्मः यस्य स परमस्वधर्मः]; @deva[परमस्वधर्मा] may also be correct if @deva[परमस्व] be regarded as one word compounded in a Karmadhāraya. @deva[संदिग्धसाध्यधर्मा], @deva[निवृत्तिधर्मा], @deva[अनुच्छित्तिधर्मा] are to be similarly explained.

**(b)** @deva[धनुष्] at the end of a Bah. becomes @deva[धन्वन्]; as @deva[अधिज्यं धनुर्यस्य सः अधिज्यधन्वा] 'one whose bow is strung'; so @deva[शार्ङ्गधन्वा] (@deva[शृङ्गस्य इदं शार्ङ्गं]) 'one whose bow is made of horn'; i. e. the god Vishṇu. This change is optional when the whole compound is a name; @deva[शतधन्वा-नुः].

**(c)** Similarly @deva[जम्भ] 'a tooth', 'food, &c. becomes @deva[जम्भन्] when preceded by @deva[सु], @deva[हरित], @deva[तृण] or @deva[सोम]; as @deva[शोभनः जम्भः अस्य सुजम्भा] one having beautiful teeth; similarly @deva[हरितजम्भा] m.; @deva[तृणं भक्ष्यं यस्य, तृणमिव दन्ता यस्येति वा तृणजम्भा], @deva[सोमजम्भा] 'one whose food is the holy *soma* juice'; but @deva[पतितजम्भः].

**(d)** The word @deva[ईर्म] n. a wound, preceded by @deva[दक्षिण] becomes @deva[ईर्मन्] when the wound is inflicted by a hunter; @deva[दक्षिणे ईर्म यस्य दक्षिणेर्मा मृगः] a stag wounded on the right side by a hunter. See Bhaṭṭi. IV. 44.

---

[^1]: @deva[धर्मादनिच् केवलात् ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 124. @deva[धनुषश्च । वा संज्ञायाम् । जम्भा सुहरिततृणसोमेभ्यः । दक्षिणेर्मा लुब्धयोगे ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 132, 133, 125, 126.
