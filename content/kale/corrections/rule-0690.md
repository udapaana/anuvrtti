The roots given at @ref[414] and @deva[ज्या] substitute @deva[न] for @deva[त].[^1]

| Roots | | P. P. Ptc. | Roots | | P. P. Ptc. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| @deva[री] | to flow, to move | @deva[रीण] | @deva[जॄ] | to grow old | @deva[जीर्ण] |
| @deva[ली] | to melt, etc. | @deva[लीन] | @deva[दॄ] | to tear | @deva[दीर्ण] |
| @deva[व्ली] | to go, to hold | @deva[व्लीन] | @deva[नॄ] | to lead | @deva[नीर्ण] |
| @deva[प्ली] | to go, to move | @deva[प्लीन] | @deva[पॄ] | to fill, to gratify | @deva[पूर्ण] |
| @deva[धू] | to shake | @deva[धून] | @deva[भॄ] | to bear, to nourish | @deva[भूर्ण] |
| @deva[पू] | to destroy | @deva[पून] | @deva[मॄ] | to hurt, to kill | @deva[मूर्ण] |
| @deva[लू] | to cut | @deva[लून] | @deva[वॄ] | to choose | @deva[वूर्ण] |
| @deva[ऋ] | to go | @deva[ईर्ण] | @deva[शॄ] | to tear asunder | @deva[शीर्ण] |
| @deva[कॄ] | to scatter | @deva[कीर्ण] | @deva[स्तॄ] | to scatter | @deva[स्तीर्ण] |
| @deva[गॄ] | to utter a sound, to praise, etc. | @deva[गीर्ण] | @deva[ज्या] | to grow old | @deva[जीन] |

[^1]: @deva[रदायां निष्ठातो नः पूर्वस्य च दः । संयोगादेरातो धातोर्यण्वतः । ल्वादिभ्यः ।] Pāṇ. VIII. 2. 42–44.
