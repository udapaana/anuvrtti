**SECTION II.**

**GOVERNMENT.**

## Section II. Government: the Kārakas

The only portion of Syntax treated separately in Sanskrit grammars is the Kārakaprakaraṇa or the chapter on Government. Kāraka is the name given to the relation subsisting between a noun and a verb in a sentence. There are six Kārakas in Sanskrit belonging to the first seven cases, except the Genitive, which is, therefore, not a Kāraka case. These are @deva[कर्ता], @deva[कर्म], @deva[करण], @deva[संप्रदान], @deva[अपादान] and @deva[अधिकरण].
