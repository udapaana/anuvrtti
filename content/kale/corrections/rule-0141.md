## 6—Indefinite Pronouns.

Indefinite pronouns are formed by the addition of @deva[चित्] or @deva[चन] or @deva[अपि] and sometimes of @deva[स्वित्] to the various cases of the pronoun @deva[किम्] in all the genders; e. g. @deva[कश्चित्], @deva[कश्चन] a certain one, @deva[कोपि], @deva[केनापि], @deva[कयाचन], @deva[कयापि]; @deva[कास्वित्], &c.
