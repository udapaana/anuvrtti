Roots of the 8th class ending in a nasal, except @deva[सन्] and the roots @deva[मन्], @deva[वन्] and @deva[हन्], drop their nasal necessarily; @deva[गम्], @deva[नम्], @deva[यम्] and @deva[रम्] do it optionally; @deva[तन्] with @deva[वि]—@deva[वितत्य]; @deva[मन्] with @deva[अव]—@deva[अवमत्य]; @deva[यम्] with @deva[नि]—@deva[नियम्य] or @deva[नियत्य]; @deva[रम्] with @deva[वि]—@deva[विरम्य] or @deva[विरत्य]; @deva[नम्] with @deva[प्र]—@deva[प्रणम्य] or @deva[प्रणत्य], &c
