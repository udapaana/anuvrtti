If a consonant, except @deva[र्] or @deva[ह्], coming at the end of a word, be followed by a nasal, the nasal of its class is optionally substituted for it[^1]; @deva[एतद्] + @deva[मुरारिः] = @deva[एतन्मुरारिः] and @deva[एतद्मुरारिः] (see @ref[22] sec. b.) this Murāri; @deva[षड्] + @deva[मासाः] = @deva[षण्मासाः] and @deva[षड्मासाः] six months.

**N.B.** If the nasal belong to a termination the change is necessary; @deva[तन्मात्रम्] that alone; @deva[चिन्मात्रं] mere knowledge. @deva[वाक्] + @deva[मय] = @deva[वाङ्मय]. The word @deva[ककुद्मत्] (see Rag. IV. 22.) is irregularly formed.

[^1]: @deva[यरोऽनुनासिकेऽनुनासिको वा ।] Pāṇ. VIII. 4. 45.
