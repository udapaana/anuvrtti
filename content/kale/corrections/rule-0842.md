## Ablative with Debt as Cause

When a word expressive of 'a debt,' is merely intended to be stated as a cause,[^1] it is put in the Ablative case; @deva[शताद्बद्धं द्रव्यं] a thing mortgaged for a hundred rupees, @deva[ऋणाद्बद्ध इव] tied down as it were by the debt he owes.

---

[^1]: @deva[अकर्तर्यृणे पञ्चमी ।] Pāṇ. II. 4. 25.
