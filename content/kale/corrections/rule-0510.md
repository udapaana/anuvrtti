## Vowel-Dropping Roots in Perfect

The @deva[अ] of the roots @deva[गम्], @deva[हन्], @deva[जन्], @deva[खन्] and @deva[घस्], is dropped before vowel weak terminations except those of the 2nd variety of the Aorist; the @deva[ह्] of @deva[हन्] is then changed to @deva[घ्]; and @deva[जन्] and @deva[घस्] become @deva[ज्] and @deva[क्ष्] respectively.
