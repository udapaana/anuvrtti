---
key: rule:237
title: "Upapadas with @deva[त्वा]/@deva[य] Indeclinables"
pages: [144.png]
verdict: approved
---
Some *upapadas* such as @deva[उच्चैः], @deva[नीचैः], @deva[तिर्यक्], @deva[मुखतः], &c. also optionally form compounds with the verbal indeclinable in @deva[त्वा] (changeable to @deva[य]); as @deva[उच्चैःकृत्य] (or @deva[उच्चैः कृत्वा]), @deva[तिर्यक्कृत्य], @deva[मुखतोभूय], @deva[नानाकृत्य], @deva[एकधाभूय], &c. For further particulars vide the Chapter on verbal derivatives.
