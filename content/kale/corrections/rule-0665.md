## Primary Nominal Bases: Kṛt Affixes

The Kṛt affixes (Vide @ref[337]) are added to roots or to their modified forms to form nouns, adjectives, and indeclinables; *e. g.* @deva[कृ]—@deva[कार], @deva[कर्तृ], @deva[करण], @deva[कुर्वत्], @deva[करिष्यत्], @deva[चकृवस्], @deva[कृत्वा], @deva[कर्तुम्] &c. These are called Kṛdantas or Primary Nominal Bases as distinguished from the Secondary Derivatives formed with the Taddhita affixes.
