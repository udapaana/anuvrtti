Nouns ending in @deva[म्]. There are a few nouns derived from the roots ending in @deva[म्]. They have no other peculiarity than this, that they change their @deva[म्] to @deva[न्] before consonantal terminations, e. g. @deva[प्रशाम्] m. f. one who is tranquil.

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. | @deva[प्रशान्] | @deva[प्रशामौ] | @deva[प्रशामः] |
| A. | @deva[प्रशामम्] | ,, | ,, |
| I. | @deva[प्रशामा] | @deva[प्रशान्भ्याम्] | @deva[प्रशान्भिः] |
| L. | @deva[प्रशामि] | @deva[प्रशामोः] | @deva[प्रशान्सु] or @deva[प्रशांसु] |
