The @deva[न] is not changed to @deva[अ] or @deva[अन्] in the following cases[^1]:—@deva[नभ्राट्] 'that which does not shine,' 'a cloud;' @deva[नपात्] (pre. p.) 'one who does not protect;' @deva[नवेदाः] 'one who does not know;' @deva[नासत्याः] [ @deva[न सत्याः असत्याः न असत्याः नासत्याः][^2] ] the two Aswins, the physicians of the gods. @deva[नमुचिः] [ @deva[न मुञ्चतीति] ] name of a demon slain by Indra; @deva[नकुलः] [ @deva[न कुलमस्य], for the mungoose is supposed to belong to no particular species of animals ]; @deva[नखं] [ @deva[न खमस्य] that which leaves no cavity, or does not go to heaven being burnt down with the dead body ]; @deva[नपुंसकं] [ @deva[न स्त्री पुमान्] ]; @deva[नक्षत्रं] [ @deva[न क्षरतीति] ] that which does not move from its proper place, hence a star; @deva[नक्रः] [ @deva[न क्रामतीति] that which does not go far off from water ]; @deva[नाकः] [ @deva[न कं अकं न अकं अस्मिन्] ]; @deva[नग] when it does not qualify an animal retains its @deva[न] optionally as @deva[नगः] or @deva[अगः] 'a mountain or a tree;' but @deva[अगः वृषलः] 'a Sudra that cannot move.'

**N. B.** It should be noted that some of these are Bahuvrihi compounds.

---

[^1]: @deva[नभ्राण्नपान्नवेदानासत्यानमुचिनकुलनखनपुंसकनक्षत्रनक्रनाकेषु प्रकृत्या ।] Pāṇ. VI. 3. 75. @deva[नगोऽप्राणिष्वन्यतरस्याम् ।] Pāṇ. VI. 3. 77.

[^2]: @deva[अत्र बहुवचनमविवक्षितं] (is not the point) @deva[तेन 'नासत्यावश्विनौ दस्रौ' इति सिद्धम् ।] Tatvabodhinī.
