**(b) Participles of the Perfect.**

## Weak Terminations in Perfect and Past Participles

The terminations of the Perf. and the Past participles are weak, and therefore the radical vowel does not take its Guṇa substitute before these. The penultimate nasal is generally dropped (see @ref[584]).
