## Formation of Ātmanepada Frequentative Base

The Atm. Frequentative base is formed by adding @deva[य] to the root before which it undergoes the same changes as before the @deva[य] of the Passive; @deva[दा]—@deva[दीय], @deva[चि]—@deva[चीय], @deva[नी]—@deva[नीय], @deva[भू]—@deva[भूय], @deva[स्मृ]—@deva[स्मर्य], @deva[ऋ]—@deva[अर्य], @deva[कॄ]—@deva[कीर्य], @deva[धे]—@deva[धीय] &c.; @deva[भिद्]—@deva[भिद्य], @deva[पॄ]—@deva[पूर्य], @deva[बन्ध्]—@deva[बध्य], @deva[नन्द्]—@deva[नन्द्य], &c.

(a) @deva[घ्रा] and @deva[ध्मा] change their vowel to @deva[ई], and @deva[ऋ] preceded by one radical consonant is changed to @deva[री], and not to @deva[रि]; @deva[घ्रा]—@deva[घ्रीय], @deva[ध्मा]—@deva[ध्मीय], @deva[कृ]—@deva[क्रीय].

(b) The roots @deva[व्यच्], @deva[व्यध्], @deva[स्यम्], @deva[स्वप्], @deva[ग्रह्], @deva[प्रच्छ्], @deva[भ्रस्ज्] and @deva[व्रश्च्] take Samprasāraṇa; @deva[ज्या] and @deva[व्ये] substitute @deva[ई], and @deva[ह्वे], @deva[ऊ] for their final vowel; @deva[शास्] becomes @deva[शिष्] and @deva[प्याय्] @deva[पी]; @deva[व्यच्]—@deva[विच्य], @deva[स्वप्]—@deva[सुप्य], @deva[ग्रह्]—@deva[गृह्य], @deva[ह्वे]—@deva[हूय], @deva[ज्या]—@deva[जीय], @deva[शास्]—@deva[शिष्य], @deva[प्याय्]—@deva[पीय].

(c) @ref[395] should be observed.
