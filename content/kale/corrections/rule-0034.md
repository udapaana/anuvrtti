---
key: rule:34
title: "Final Consonant Devoicing and Softening"
pages: [024.png]
verdict: approved
---
Any consonant, except a nasal or a semi-vowel, is changed to the first of its class, when followed by a hard consonant, and to the first or third when followed by nothing; @deva[वाक्], @deva[वाग्].
