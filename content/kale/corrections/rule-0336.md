title: "Feminine of Participles and Adjectives"
pages: [193.png]
verdict: approved
---
Participles ( those of the Pres. and Fut. ) of Parasm. roots take @deva[ई] in the *fem.*, @deva[न्] being inserted before @deva[त्] as in the Nom. or Acc. dual of the *Neu.* ( see @ref[116] ) and so do adjectives ending in a consonant; @deva[पचन्ती]; @deva[याती-न्ती], @deva[शासती], @deva[ददती], @deva[दीव्यन्ती], @deva[महती], &c.
