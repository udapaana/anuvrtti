title: "Guṇa and Vṛddhi: Vowel Grades"
pages: [011.png]
verdict: approved
---
The vowels @deva[अ], @deva[ए], @deva[ओ] and the syllables @deva[अर्] and @deva[अल्] are called Guṇa; the vowels @deva[आ], @deva[ऐ], @deva[औ] and the syllables @deva[आर्] and @deva[आल्] are called Vṛddhi.[^1] The Guṇa and Vṛddhi vowels and syllables that are substituted for the simple vowels will appear from the following table:

| Simple V. | @deva[अ] | @deva[इ] & @deva[ई] | @deva[उ] & @deva[ऊ] | @deva[ऋ] & @deva[ॠ] | @deva[ऌ] |
|-----------|----------|---------------------|---------------------|---------------------|----------|
| Guṇa | @deva[अ] | @deva[ए]            | @deva[ओ]            | @deva[अर्]          | @deva[अल्] |
| Vṛddhi | @deva[आ] | @deva[ऐ]            | @deva[औ]            | @deva[आर्]          | @deva[आल्] |

---

[^1]: @deva[वृद्धिरादैच्। अदेङ् गुणः। उरण् रपरः] Pāṇ. I, 1, 2; I, 51.
