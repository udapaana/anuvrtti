The words @deva[मुख], @deva[नयन], @deva[लोह], @deva[धन], @deva[मांस], @deva[रुधिर], @deva[कार्मुक] 'a bow', &c., @deva[विवर], @deva[जल], @deva[हल], @deva[धन], @deva[बल], @deva[अन्न], @deva[कुसुम], @deva[शुल्ब] 'copper', @deva[पत्तन], @deva[रण], and their synonyms.

**Exception:**—@deva[सीर] 'a plough', @deva[अर्थ] 'wealth', @deva[ओदन] 'cooked rice', @deva[आहव] 'a battle', @deva[संग्राम] 'a battle', all *mas.*, and @deva[आजि] 'war' and @deva[अटवी] 'a forest', both *fem.*
