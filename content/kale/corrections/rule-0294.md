[^1] @deva[सह] forming the first member of a compound, is changed to @deva[स]—

**(a)** when the whole compound is a name; *e. g.* @deva[सपलाशम्]; but @deva[सहयुध्वा] a comrade in battle ( Upapada comp. ).

**(b)** when it means 'including a certain literary work,' or 'in addition to;' as @deva[समुहूर्तं] ( Avya. comp. ) @deva[ज्योतिषमधीते] 'he studies the science of astronomy, including that of finding out the auspicious times;' @deva[सद्रोणा खारी] 'a *khâri* with a drona in addition to it.'

**(c)** and when the object denoted by the latter member is not visibly seen, but is to be inferred: as @deva[सराक्षसीका] ( Bah. comp. ) @deva[निशा] 'a night in which presence of demoness is to be inferred'.

---

[^1]: @deva[सहस्य सः संज्ञायाम्] । @deva[ग्रन्थान्ताधिके च] । @deva[द्वितीये चानुपाख्ये] । Pān. VI. 3. 78–80.
