The root @deva[वस्] when preceded by @deva[अमा] 'with' takes the affix @deva[ण्यत्] before which the penultimate @deva[अ] is optionally changed to Vṛiddhi; @deva[अमा सह वसतोऽस्यां चन्द्रार्कौ] @deva[अमावस्या] or @deva[वास्या] 'the day on which the sun and the moon are with each other i. e. are in conjunction.'

(a) @deva[सृज्] when preceded by @deva[पाणि] or the preposition @deva[समव] takes the affix @deva[ण्यत्]; as @deva[पाणिभ्यां सृज्यते पाणिसर्ग्या रज्जुः]; similarly @deva[समवसर्ग्या].
