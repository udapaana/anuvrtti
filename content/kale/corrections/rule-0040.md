**Ob.** If the ending @deva[न्] of a word be followed by @deva[श्], @deva[त्] is optionally inserted between them;[^1] e. g. @deva[सन्] + @deva[शंभुः] = @deva[सन्शंभुः] and @deva[सन्त्छंभुः] Śambhu existing for all times; and @deva[सन्त्शंभुः] = @deva[सन्त्शंभुः] and @deva[सन्त्छंभुः] (by @ref[35]); then by @ref[28] (a) @deva[न्] being changed to @deva[ञ्] (and @deva[त्] to @deva[च्]) @deva[सञ्शंभुः], @deva[सञ्च्शंभुः] and @deva[सञ्च्छंभुः] and finally by dropping @deva[च्] optionally (by @ref[20] a.) @deva[सञ्शंभुः], @deva[सञ्च्शंभुः] and @deva[सञ्छंभुः], @deva[सञ्च्छंभुः].

---

[^1]: @deva[शि तुक्] — Pāṇ. VIII. 3. 31.
