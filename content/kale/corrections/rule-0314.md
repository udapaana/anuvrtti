title: "Feminine of Color Words"
pages: [187.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] *Prâtipadikas* expressive of colour, having @deva[त] for their penultimate and ending in an Anudâtta vowel, except @deva[असित] black and @deva[पलित] grey, and the word @deva[पिशङ्ग] form their *feminine* in @deva[ई] or @deva[आ]; @deva[एता] or @deva[एती] 'variegated' from @deva[एत]; @deva[रोहिता], @deva[रोहिती]; @deva[पिशङ्गी]-@deva[पिशङ्गा]; but @deva[असिता], @deva[पलिता], @deva[श्वेता] ( as the @deva[त] here is Udâtta ).

**(a)**[^2] But if the *Prâtipadika* has no @deva[त] for its penultimate, it simply takes @deva[ई]; @deva[कल्माषी] variegated, @deva[सारङ्गी]; but @deva[कृष्णा] @deva[कपिला] ( as the last vowels are not Anudâtta ).

[^1]: @deva[वर्णादनुदात्तात्तोपधात्तो नः] । Pân. IV. 1. 39. @deva[पिशङ्गादुपसंख्यानम्] । @deva[असितपलितयोर्न] । Vârtikas.

[^2]: @deva[अन्यतो ङीष्] । Pân. IV. 1. 40.
