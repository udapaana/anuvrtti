## Ablative with Concealment

When concealment is to be had recourse to,[^1] that whose sight one desires to avoid is put in the Ablative case; @deva[मातुर्निलीयते कृष्णः] 'Krishna conceals himself from his mother; but @deva[चोरान्न दिदृक्षते].

---

[^1]: @deva[अन्तर्धौ येनादर्शनमिच्छति ।] Pāṇ. I. 4. 28.
