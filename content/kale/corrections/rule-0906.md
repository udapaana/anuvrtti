title: "Impersonal Use of Potential Passive Participle"
pages: [512.png]
verdict: approved
---
Occasionally this participle is used impersonally in the *neuter* gender and singular number; @deva[तत्रभवता तपोवनं गन्तव्यम्] his honour should go to the penance grove; @deva[मया चाण्डालैः सह स्थातव्यं] I should have to dwell in the company of Chāṇḍālas &c.
