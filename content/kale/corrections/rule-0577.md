## Vowel Changes in @deva[ऊर्णु]

The vowel of @deva[ऊर्णु] takes Vṛddhi optionally in the Parasm. i. e. optionally takes Guṇa substitute; also it optionally remains unchanged before @deva[इ] (Vide @ref[466], @ref[518]).
