## Formation of Present Passive Participle

The participle of the Pre. Passive is formed by adding @deva[मान] to the Passive base in @deva[य]; *e. g.* @deva[बुध्यमान] 'who or what is known,' @deva[अद्यमान] 'what is eaten,' @deva[दीयमान] who or what is given, @deva[चीयमान] 'who or what is gathered,' @deva[क्रियमाण] 'what is done,' @deva[कॄ]—@deva[कीर्यमाण] 'what is scattered,' @deva[चोर्यमाण] 'who or what is stolen' @deva[बुध्]—cau. Pass. @deva[बोध्यमान] 'who or what is caused to know,' Des. Pass. @deva[बुबोधिष्यमाण] 'who or what is desired to know,' &c.
