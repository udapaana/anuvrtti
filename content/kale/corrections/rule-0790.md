## Predicate Participle Agrees with the Subject

When a past or potential passive participle is used as predicate with a noun in apposition to the subject, the participle agrees with the subject; @deva[कृताः शरव्यं हरिणा तवासुराः] (S'ak. VI.) the demons are made the marks of your arrows by Hari.

**Concord of Relative with its Antecedent.**
