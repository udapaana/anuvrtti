key: rule:263
title: "General Optional @deva[क] for Bahuvrīhi"
pages: [161.png]
verdict: approved
---
And as a general rule, when the final word of a Bah. comp. does not undergo any additions or alterations mentioned in the foregoing rules, it optionally takes the affix @deva[क].[^1] @deva[महायशस्कः]-@deva[महायशाः] 'of mighty glory'; but @deva[उत्तरपूर्वा], @deva[व्याघ्रपात्], @deva[सुगन्धिः] &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[शेषाद्विभाषा ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 154.
