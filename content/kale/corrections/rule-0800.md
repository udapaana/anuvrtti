## Verbs of Motion Govern Accusative of Destination

Roots having the sense of motion, real or metaphorical govern the Accusative of the place to which it is directed. @deva[ग्रामं गच्छति] goes to a village; @deva[अधिज्यधन्वा विचचार दावम्] with his bow strung he roamed all over the forest; @deva[आनन्दस्य परां कोटिमध्यगच्छन्] they reached the highest point of joy; @deva[मनसा कृष्णमेति] goes to Krishna (thinks of him) mentally; @deva[इति चिन्तयन्नेष स निद्रां ययौ] while thus pondering he sank into sleep.

(a) When the motion is real, the Dative may also be used; @deva[ग्रामाय ग्रामं वा गच्छति]; but not of words denoting 'a road;' @deva[पन्थानं गच्छति] only; but @deva[उत्पथेन पथे गच्छति] he reaches the main road by taking a by-path.
