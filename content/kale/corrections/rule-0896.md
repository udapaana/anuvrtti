## THE PAST PASSIVE PARTICIPLES.

The past passive participle is very frequently used to supply the place of a verb; sometimes in conjunction with the auxiliary verbs @deva[अस्] and @deva[भू]. The past passive participle agrees adjectivally with the object in gender, number and case, the agent being put in the Instrumental, while the past active participle is treated exactly like the verb in the past tense; @deva[तेन कार्यं कृतं] by him the work was done; @deva[तेन बन्धनानि छिन्नानि] by him the bonds were cut; @deva[आदिष्टास्मि देव्या धारिण्या] I am commanded by queen Dhāriṇī; @deva[स कार्यं कृतवान्] he did the work; @deva[रामः दैत्यान् हतवान्] Rāma killed the Rākshasas; @deva[कृतवत्यासि नावधीरणां] thou didst never despise me, &c.
