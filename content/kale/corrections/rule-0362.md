# Chapter XI. Avyayas or Indeclinables

That is an *Avyaya* whose form remaining the same in all the genders, numbers and cases, undergoes no change.[^1] The Avyayas may be divided into simple and compound ones. The latter are treated of in the chapter on compounds (Avyayībhāvas and a few Bahuvrīhis and Tatpurusha).

[^1]: @deva[सदृशं त्रिषु लिङ्गेषु सर्वासु च विभक्तिषु । वचनेषु च सर्वेषु यन्न व्येति तदव्ययम् ॥] @deva[स्वरादिनिपातमव्ययम्] । Pāṇ. I. 1. 37.
