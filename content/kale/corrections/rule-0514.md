## Changes to @deva[जि], @deva[हि], and @deva[चि] after Reduplication

@deva[जि] and @deva[हि] are respectively changed to @deva[गि] and @deva[घि], and @deva[चि] optionally to @deva[कि], after the reduplicative syllable in the Perf. and the Desiderative.

| 1. | @deva[जिगाय] | @deva[जिग्यिव] | @deva[जिग्यिम] |
