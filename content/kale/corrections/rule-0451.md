title: "@deva[निज्], @deva[विज्], @deva[विष्] with Guṇa in Reduplicative Syllable"
pages: [283.png, 284.png]
verdict: approved
---
The @deva[इ] of the reduplicative syllable of @deva[निज्], @deva[विज्] and @deva[विष्], takes its Guṇa substitute before all terminations and the radical @deva[इ] is not guṇated before vowel strong terminations.
