Words ending in @deva[इस्] and @deva[उस्]; in @deva[मन्], and @deva[अस्], and having two vowels in them; in @deva[त्र] and such as have @deva[ल] for their penultimate; @deva[सर्पिस्] 'liquid ghee,' @deva[ज्योतिष्], @deva[धनुष्], @deva[चर्मन्], @deva[वर्मन्] 'an armour,' @deva[यशस्], @deva[मनस्], @deva[पत्र], @deva[छत्र], &c., @deva[कुल], @deva[कूल], @deva[स्थल], &c.

**Exceptions:**—

**(*a*)** @deva[छदिस्] *fem.* 'the roof of a carriage or house' and @deva[सीमन्] *fem.* 'a boundary.'

**(*b*)** @deva[भूत्र], @deva[अमित्र] ( @deva[न मित्रम्] ), @deva[छात्र] 'pupil', @deva[पुत्र], @deva[मन्त्र], @deva[वृत्र] 'name of a demon' and @deva[उष्ट्र] all *mas.*; @deva[यात्रा], @deva[मात्रा], @deva[भस्त्रा] 'a smith's bellows', @deva[दंष्ट्रा], @deva[वरत्रा], 'a leather strap', all *fem.*

**(*c*)** and @deva[तूल], @deva[उपल], @deva[ताल], @deva[कुसुल] 'a granary or store house for corn', @deva[तरल] 'the middle gem of a necklace', @deva[कम्बल], @deva[देवल] 'a Brâhmaṇa who attends on an idol', and @deva[वृषल], which are all *mas.*
