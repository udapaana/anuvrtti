**(a)** Visarga, preceded by any vowel except @deva[अ] or @deva[आ] and followed by a vowel or soft consonant, is changed to @deva[र्]; @deva[हरिः] + @deva[जयति] = @deva[हरिर्जयति] Hari conquers; so @deva[भानुरुदेति] the sun rises; @deva[गौरागच्छति] a cow or bull comes.

*Exception*:—The Visarga of the particles @deva[भोः], @deva[भगोः] and @deva[अघोः] obeys @ref[48]; e. g. @deva[भो] + @deva[अच्युत] = @deva[भो अच्युत] or @deva[भोयच्युत] Oh Achyuta; @deva[भगो नमस्ते] Bhago! a bow to thee; @deva[अघो याहि] Oh you go.

**(b)** The @deva[न्] of @deva[अहन्], not followed by a declensional termination, is always changed to @deva[र्] but if followed by @deva[पति][^1] and other words such as @deva[गिर्], @deva[धुर्] &c. the change is optional and if by @deva[रूप] and @deva[रात्रि], visarga is substituted for it; @deva[अहः], @deva[अहरहः] day by day; @deva[अहःपतिः] or @deva[अहर्पतिः] the lord of the day, the sun; @deva[गीर्पतिः] or @deva[गीष्पतिः] Bṛhaspati; @deva[धूर्पतिः] or @deva[धूष्पतिः] a leader; but @deva[अहोभ्याम्] Inst. dual; @deva[अहोरूपम्] the form of the day @deva[गतमहो रात्रिरेषा]; @deva[अहोरात्रः] day and night; @deva[अहोरथन्तरम्] a protection of Sāman to be chanted by day.

**(c)** @deva[र्] or @deva[ढ्] followed by @deva[र्] or @deva[ढ्] respectively is dropped,[^2] and the preceding @deva[अ], @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] if short is made long; @deva[पुनर्] + @deva[रमते] = @deva[पुना रमते] sports again; @deva[हरिः] + @deva[रम्य] = @deva[हरिर्] + @deva[रम्यः] = @deva[हरी रम्यः] Hari is graceful; but @deva[वृढ्] + @deva[ढः] = @deva[वृढः] (form @deva[वृह्] + @deva[त]) p. p. of @deva[वृह्] to grow.

---

[^1]: @deva[अहरादीनां पत्यादिषु वा रेफः] — Vārt.

[^2]: @deva[रारि। ढ्लोपे पूर्वस्य दीर्घोऽणः] — Pāṇ. VIII. 3. 14. VI. 3. 111.
