**(a) Formation of the Causal Base.**

## Formation of Causal Base Like Tenth Class

The Causal Base of a root is formed like that of a root of the Tenth class. In the case of roots of the Tenth class the causal form is identical with the primitive. The Causal form takes either pada. Thus from @deva[बुध्] comes the causal base @deva[बोधय], @deva[बोधयति-ते] 'he causes to know'; @deva[क्षुभ्]—@deva[क्षोभयति] 'he shakes or agitates,' @deva[गण]—@deva[गणयति] 'he causes to count,' @deva[नी]—@deva[नाययति] 'he makes another lead or carry'; @deva[कृ] 'to do' and @deva[कॄ] to scatter—@deva[कारयति] 'he causes to do or scatter' @deva[कॄत्]—@deva[कीर्तयति], &c.
