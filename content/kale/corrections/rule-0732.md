The following words are derived from the root @deva[ग्रह्] with the affix @deva[क्यप्]:—@deva[अवगृह्यं], @deva[प्रगृह्यं], @deva[पदं] 'two technical terms in grammar;' @deva[गृह्यकाः] 'birds restrained from free motion, such as parrots, &c.;' '@deva[पञ्जरादिबन्धेन परतन्त्रीकृता इत्यर्थः ।] Sid. Kau. @deva[ग्रामगृह्या सेना] 'an army stationed outside a village'; @deva[आर्यैर्गृह्यते आर्यगृह्यः तत्पक्षाश्रित इत्यर्थः] (Sid. Kau.) 'siding with the noble.' See Rag. II. 43.
