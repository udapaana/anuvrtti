## THE PERFECT PARTICIPLES.

The use of the perfect participles is very limited; it is used in the sense of 'who or what has done &c.'; @deva[तं तस्थिवांसं नगरोपकण्ठे] (Rag. V. 61.) him who had halted in the vicinity of the city; @deva[श्रेयांसि सर्वाण्यधिजग्मुषस्ते] (Rag. V. 34.) of thee who hast obtained all good things, @deva[स शुश्रुवांस्तद्वचनं] (Bhaṭṭi. I. 20) when he heard his words; &c.
