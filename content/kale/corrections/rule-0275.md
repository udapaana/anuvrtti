title: "@deva[पार] and @deva[मध्य] Compounds"
pages: [166.png]
verdict: approved
---
The words @deva[पार] and @deva[मध्य] optionally form Avya. compounds with any nominal base, and are placed first, the final @deva[अ] being changed to @deva[ए] when thus compounded;[^1] *e. g.* @deva[पारे-मध्ये-गंगात्] from the middle of the Gangetic stream; optionally of course, the Gen. Tat.; also @deva[गङ्गापारात्], @deva[गङ्गामध्यात्]. In this case the Ab. termination is irregularly retained. If the final noun has the sense of the Loc. the final vowel may be changed to @deva[अम्]; as @deva[पारे-मध्ये-गङ्गम्]; *cf.* @deva[पारेसमुद्रम्] Bhaṭṭi V. 4.

---

[^1]: @deva[पारेमध्ये षष्ठ्या वा] । Pān. II. 1. 18.
