Two adjectives may also be compounded into what is called the @deva[विशेषणोभयपदकर्मधारयः] as @deva[शुक्लकृष्णः], @deva[कृष्णसारंगः].

**(a).**[^1] Two participles expressive of two consecutive actions of the same individual may be compounded together, the one expressive of the previous action being placed first; @deva[आदौ स्नातः पश्चादनुलिप्तः स्नातानुलिप्तः] 'first bathed and then anointed'; similarly @deva[पीतोद्गीर्णम्] 'first drunk and afterwards vomited out', @deva[पीतप्रतिबद्धः], @deva[गृहीतप्रतिमुक्तः] (Rag. II. 1. IV. 43), &c.

**(b).** In the case of the two sets of words given above (@ref[222] g.) forming compounds with one another those beginning with @deva[पूर्व] are placed first. In the case of @deva[एक] compounded with @deva[वीर] it may precede or follow it; as @deva[वीरैकः] or @deva[एकवीरः]; the former, however, is preferable. In the case of the first set of words viz. @deva[एक]......@deva[केवल] the one succeeding precedes the one preceding when compounded together; as @deva[पुराणजरत्], @deva[केवलपुराणम्], &c.

**(c)**[^2] A past participle is compounded with another with the negative particle prefixed to it; '@deva[कृताकृतम्],' what is done and not done, i. e. 'partially done'.

**(d).**[^3] The word @deva[युवन्] (m. f.) enters into a Karm. comp. with the words @deva[खलति], @deva[पलित], @deva[वलित] having wrinkles, and @deva[जरती], and is placed first; i. e. @deva[युवा+खलतिः युवखलतिः] 'a young bald-headed man';-@deva[ती] 'a young bald-headed woman'; @deva[युवजरती] 'a young woman looking old'; @deva[युवपलितः] 'a young man whose hair is grey', &c.

---

[^1]: See Pāṇ. II. 1. 49. (@deva[पूर्वकाल-पूर्वत्वस्य ससंबन्धिकत्वात्पूर्वकालोऽपरकालेन समस्यते ।] Tatv. quoted above)

[^2]: @deva[केन नञ्विशिष्टेनानञ् ।] Pāṇ. II. 1. 60.

[^3]: @deva[युवाखलतिपलितवलितजरतीभिः ।] Pāṇ. II. 67.
