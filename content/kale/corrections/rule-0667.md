## Formation of Present Parasmaipada Participle

The participle of the present Parasm. is formed by the addition of the affix @deva[अत्] to that form of a root, primitive or derivative, which it assumes before the third person plural termination of the Present Tense. If the base ends in @deva[अ], the @deva[अ] is dropped; *e. g.*

@deva[भू] (1 cl.)—@deva[भव्]+@deva[अन्ति] Pre. 3rd pl. @deva[भव्]+@deva[अत्]=@deva[भवत्] Pre. P. being

@deva[स्था] (1 cl.)—@deva[तिष्ठ्]+@deva[अन्ति] „ @deva[तिष्ठ्]+@deva[अत्]=@deva[तिष्ठत्] „ standing

@deva[द्विष्] (2 cl.)—@deva[द्विष्]+@deva[अन्ति] „ @deva[द्विष्]+@deva[अत्]=@deva[द्विषत्] „ hating.

(a) The term. @deva[अत्] is optionally changed to @deva[वस्] when added to the root @deva[विद्]; @deva[विद्वत्] or @deva[विद्वस्] 'knowing.'

(b) The affix @deva[अत्] when added to @deva[द्विष्] and @deva[सु] 'to extract Soma juice in a sacrifice' has the sense of an agent; as @deva[द्विषत्] *m.* 'an enemy.' @deva[यज्ञे सर्वे सुन्वन्तः] 'all are the extractors of Soma juice in a sacrifice.

(c) When affixed to @deva[अर्ह्] it has the sense of 'fitness, respect;' @deva[अर्हत्] 'one deserving respect, respectable.'

(d) @deva[इ] 2 P. and @deva[धृ] cau. take the affix @deva[अत्] when the idea of difficulty is not present; @deva[अधीयत्] 'studying without experiencing any difficulty;' @deva[धारयत्] 'holding or bearing with ease;' but @deva[कृच्छ्रेण अधीते], @deva[कृच्छ्रेण धारयति].
