In Sanskrit simple words, whether substantives, adjectives, verbs or indeclinables, have the power of entering into combination with one another and forming what are called 'Samāsas'[^1], compound words or, in short, compounds.

**(a)** A compound thus formed may further be compounded with another simple or compound word, and this again may become the member of a new compound.

---

[^1]: From @deva[अस्] with @deva[सम्] to throw together.
