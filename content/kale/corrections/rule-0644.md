## Restriction on @deva[य] Affix with @deva[म्]-Ending Nouns

The affix @deva[य] is not added to nouns ending in @deva[म्] and to indeclinables; @deva[कामिच्छति], @deva[स्वरिच्छति] (he longs for heaven).
