## Samprasāraṇa in Third Aorist

The roots @deva[ह्वे] and @deva[स्वप्] cau. take Samprasāraṇa; @deva[श्वि] does it optionally; @deva[ह्वे]-@deva[हु]-@deva[हावय्]—@deva[हाव्] or @deva[हव्], then @deva[जुहव्], @deva[जुहाव्] by @ref[550]; then @deva[अजुहावम्], @deva[अजूहवम्]; @deva[स्वप्]—@deva[स्वापय्]—@deva[स्वाप्]—@deva[सुप्]—@deva[सुषुप्]—@deva[सूषुप्]; @deva[असूषुपम्]; @deva[श्वि]—@deva[अशूशवम्], @deva[अशिश्वयम्] 1st sing.
