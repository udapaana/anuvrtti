## Genitive with Verbs Meaning 'to Injure, Punish'

[^1] The roots @deva[जस्], @deva[हन्] with @deva[नि] or @deva[प्र] or with both, @deva[नट्], @deva[क्राथ्] and @deva[पिष्] govern the Genitive when meaning to injure, to punish, &c; @deva[चौरस्योज्जासयति राजा] a king punishes a thief; @deva[निजौजसोज्जासयितुं जगद्रुहां] (Sis. I. 37.) to kill the enemies of the world (the demons) by his own power; @deva[मन्योरुज्जासयात्मनः] kill (drive away) your anger; @deva[राक्षसानां निहनिष्यति] or @deva[प्रहणिष्यति] or @deva[निप्रहणिष्यति] or @deva[प्रणिहनिष्यति रामः] Rāma will kill the Rākshasas; @deva[वृषलस्य—उन्नाटयति—क्राथयति] does injury to a Vrishala; @deva[साहसिकस्य पिनष्टि गजः] &c; in other senses they govern the Accusative; @deva[धानाः पिनष्टि] he grinds fried rice.

---

[^1]: @deva[जासिनिप्रहणनाटक्राथपिषां हिंसायाम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 56.
