## 8th Class Roots Ending in Nasals

Roots of the 8th class ending in @deva[ण्] or @deva[न्] drop their nasal and substitute optionally @deva[थास्] and @deva[त] for the terminations @deva[इष्ठाः] and @deva[इट्] of the 2nd and 3rd person sing. Ātm. @deva[सन्] lengthens its vowel after dropping @deva[न्].
