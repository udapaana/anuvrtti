## Locative/Genitive with @deva[आयुक्त] and @deva[कुशल] (appointed/devoted)

[^1] The words @deva[आयुक्त] and @deva[कुशल] meaning 'appointed or devoted to' are construed with the Locative or the Genitive; @deva[आयुक्तः कुशलो वा हरिपूजने हरिपूजनस्य वा] appointed to worship Hari; @deva[कुशलोन्वेषणस्याहमायुक्तो दूतकर्मणि ।] (Bhatti. VIII. 115.) In other senses they are construed with the Locative; @deva[आयुक्तो गौः शकटे] a bull harnessed to the yoke of a carriage; @deva[कर्मणि कुशलः] expert in the performance of an act.

---

[^1]: @deva[आयुक्तकुशलाभ्यां चासेवायाम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 40.
