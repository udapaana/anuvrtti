title: "Combination of Pronouns"
pages: [507.png, 508.png]

The pronouns @deva[अस्मत्], @deva[युष्मत्], @deva[यत्] and @deva[किम्] are often used in combination with other pronouns; @deva[सोहं—रघूणामन्वयं वक्ष्ये] that I will describe the race of the Raghus; @deva[सोहं सर्वाधमो लोके] that I am the most degraded of all the people; @deva[स त्वं प्रशस्ते महिते मदीये—अग्न्यगारे—वसन्] that thou dwelling in my fire-sanctuary; &c.; @deva[ते वयं दमयन्त्यर्थे चरामः पृथिवीमिमाम्] we, of this description, roam over the earth for (in search of) Damayantī; sometimes @deva[अस्मत्] and @deva[युष्मत्] may be understood; @deva[सा क्षिप्रमातिष्ठ रथं गजं वा] i. e. @deva[सा त्वं] that thou quickly sit in a chariot or mount an elephant. @deva[सोयं पुत्रस्तव मदस्रुचां वारणानां विजेता] this is that son of thine, the subduer of elephants shedding ichor; @deva[तथा विनाकृतः पुत्रैर्योहमिच्छामि जीवितुम्] I, who wish to live still, even though deprived of my sons in that manner, &c. &c.
