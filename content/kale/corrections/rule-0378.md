# Chapter XII. Conjugation of Verbs

§ 378. There are in Sanskrit—

(a) Two kinds of verbs, Primitive and Derivative.

(b) Six Tenses (@deva[कालाः]) and four Moods (@deva[अर्थाः]) which are as follow:—[^1]

| TENSES | TECHNICAL NAME | MOODS | TECHNICAL NAME |
|---|---|---|---|
| @deva[वर्तमानः] or Present | @deva[लट्] | @deva[आज्ञा] or Imperative | @deva[लोट्] |
| @deva[भूतः] or Aorist | @deva[लुङ्] | @deva[विधि] or Potential | @deva[विधिलिङ्] |
| @deva[अनद्यतनभूतः] or Imperfect | @deva[लङ्] | @deva[आशीः] or Benedictive | @deva[आशीर्लिङ्] |
| @deva[परोक्षभूतः] or Perfect | @deva[लिट्] | @deva[संकेत] or Conditional | @deva[लृङ्] |
| @deva[अनद्यतनभविष्यन्] or 1st Future | @deva[लुट्] | | |
| @deva[भविष्यन्] or 2nd Future | @deva[लृट्] | | |

@deva[लेट्] or the Subjunctive is used only in the Veda and is therefore termed 'The Vedic Subjunctive.'

Note:—The ten tenses and moods are technically called the ten Lakaras in Sanskrit grammars.

(c) Three Voices (@deva[प्रयोगाः]), the Active Voice (@deva[कर्तरीप्र०]) as @deva[रामः सत्यं भाषते]. the Passive Voice (@deva[कर्मणीप्र०]), as @deva[हरिणा फलं भक्ष्यते], and the Impersonal Construction (@deva[भावेप्र०]) @deva[रामेण गम्यते].

(d) Two sets of personal terminations, the one called 'Parasmaipada,' the other 'Ātmanepada.' Some roots take exclusively the Par. terminations and some the Ātm. ones; while there are others which take either. Several roots again, though Parasmaipadi, become Ātmanepadi and *vice versa*, when preceded by certain prepositions or in particular senses. These will be considered in a separate chapter.

[^1]: These technical names are given in the following Kārikā.

@deva[लट् वर्तमाने लेट् वेदे भूते लुङ्लङ्लिटस्तथा । विध्याशिषोस्तु लिङ्लोटौ लुट् लृट् लृङ् च भविष्यति ॥]

This terminology of Pāṇini, it will be seen, is artificial. Other grammarians use different names. The ten Lakaras of Pāṇini are according to their nomenclature, as follow.—@deva[भवति] (@deva[वृत्तिः]), @deva[अद्यतनी], @deva[ह्यस्तनी], @deva[परोक्षा], @deva[श्वस्तनी], @deva[भविष्यन्ती], @deva[पञ्चमी], @deva[सप्तमी], @deva[क्रियातिपत्ति] and @deva[आशीः]—'Apte's Guide'.
