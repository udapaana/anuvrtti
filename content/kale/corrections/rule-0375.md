§ 375. The following particles are used with certain words—

@deva[अद्]—@deva[अद्भुतं] 'a wonder'.

@deva[का]—@deva[कापुरुषः] 'a bad man,' @deva[कोष्णं] 'luke-warm,' @deva[काजलं] 'a little water'.

@deva[कु]—@deva[कुकृत्यं] 'a bad deed'.

@deva[चन], @deva[चित्]—@deva[किंचित्], @deva[कश्चित्], @deva[कश्चन], @deva[किंचन] &c.

@deva[न]—is generally changed to @deva[अ] or @deva[अन्] when prefixed to words beginning with a consonant or a vowel respectively. This particle has six different senses[^1] (1) @deva[सादृश्य] 'likeness' or 'resemblance'; as @deva[अब्राह्मणः] 'one not a Brāhmaṇa, but resembling a Brāhmaṇa' (wearing the sacred thread, &c.) i. e. 'a Kshatriya or a Vaiśya' (2) @deva[अभाव] 'absence or negation'; @deva[अज्ञानं] 'the absence of knowledge'; (3) @deva[अन्यत्व] 'difference from'; as @deva[अयं अपटः] 'this is something different from a cloth' i. e. 'a jar' or so; (4) @deva[अल्पता] 'littleness' or 'smallness'; as @deva[अनुदरा कन्या] 'a girl having a slender waist'; (5) @deva[अप्राशस्त्य] 'badness, unfitness', &c.; @deva[अकार्यं] 'something unfit to be done', @deva[अकालः] 'an improper time, not a favourable opportunity,' and lastly (6) @deva[विरोध] or 'opposition'; @deva[अनीतिः] 'non-morality'; immorality @deva[असुरः] 'opposed to a god' i. e. 'a demon'.

@deva[स्म]—is generally used as an expletive. Used with a form of the present tense of a verb, it gives it the sense of the past tense; as @deva[भवति स्म] i. e. @deva[अभवत्]. When used with the particle @deva[मा] it has an intensive force; as @deva[मा स्म शोके मनः कृथाः] &c.

@deva[वित्]—is added to @deva[किं] and other indeclinables and asks a question or implies a doubt; @deva[किंस्वित्], @deva[अहोस्वित्], &c.

@deva[स्वी]—is used with @deva[कृ] and its derivatives like a preposition in the sense of 'acceptance'; @deva[स्वीकारः], @deva[स्वीकृतम्], &c.

[^1]: These are given in the following couplet:— @deva[तत्सादृश्यमभावश्च तदन्यत्वं तदल्पता । अप्राशस्त्यं विरोधश्च नञर्थाः षट् प्रकीर्तिताः ॥]
