(a) [^1]The gerunds of @deva[अस्] and @deva[तृष्] are used with nouns denoting a period of time intervening between the repetition or performance of the action; @deva[द्व्यहात्यासं] or @deva[द्व्यहमत्यासं गाः पाययति] 'he makes the cows drink water allowing two days to intervene, i. e every third day' @deva[अद्य पाययित्वा द्व्यहमतिक्रम्य पुनः पाययतीत्यर्थः ।] (Sid. Kau.); similarly @deva[द्व्यहतर्षम्] or @deva[द्व्यहंतर्षम्].

(b) [^2]Similarly the gerunds of @deva[दिश्] with @deva[आ], and @deva[ग्रह्] are used with @deva[नामन्] and in the sense of the Acc.; @deva[नामदेशमाचष्टे]; @deva[नामग्राहं मामाह्वयति], &c.

(c) The gerund of @deva[भू] may optionally be used after @deva[तूष्णीं] and @deva[अन्वच्]; @deva[तूष्णींभूय]-@deva[भूत्वा]-@deva[भावं]; @deva[अन्वग्भूय], @deva[अन्वग्भूत्वा], @deva[अन्वग्भावम्].
