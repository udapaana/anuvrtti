---
key: rule:942
title: "Simple Future for Wonder, Doubt, and Certainty"
pages: [524.png, 525.png]
verdict: approved
---
When the idea of wonder is to be expressed and the words @deva[यच्च], @deva[यत्र] and @deva[यदि] do not occur in a sentence, the Simple Future should be used; @deva[आश्चर्यमन्धो नाम कृष्णं द्रक्ष्यति] it is a wonder that a blind man sees Hari.

(a) The Simple Future is also used when the particles @deva[उत] and @deva[अपि] expressing a doubt are used; @deva[उत दण्डः पतिष्यति] will the stick fall? @deva[अपि धास्यति द्वारं] will he close the door?

(b) This Future is also used when the particle @deva[अलं] meaning sure or able is used; @deva[अलं कृष्णो हस्तिनं हनिष्यति] Krishna is sure or able to kill the elephant.
