**By means of the affix @deva[ण्यत्].**

Roots ending in @deva[ऋ], and those ending in a consonant take the affix @deva[ण्यत्] (@deva[य]) in the same sense as @deva[यत्]. Before this affix the ending @deva[च्] and @deva[ज्] of a root are changed to @deva[क्] and @deva[ग्] respectively and the final vowel and the penultimate @deva[अ] take Vṛiddhi substitute; any other penultimate vowel generally takes Guṇa.

@deva[कृ]—@deva[कार्यं] 'what ought to be done,' @deva[धृ]—@deva[धार्यं] 'what ought to be worn,' &c.; @deva[ग्रह्]—@deva[ग्राह्यं], @deva[दभ्]—@deva[दाभ्यं] 'what ought to be impelled,' &c.; @deva[वच्]—@deva[वाक्यं] 'what is arranged, a sentence,' @deva[पच्]—@deva[पाक्यं] 'what is to be cooked,' @deva[मृज्]—@deva[मार्ग्यं] 'what is to be purified,' &c.
