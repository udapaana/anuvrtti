title: "Masculine Pluralia Tantum"

The words @deva[दाराः] 'a wife', @deva[अक्षताः] 'uninjured rice,' @deva[लाजाः] 'fried rice,' @deva[असवः] 'life ( the vital airs in the body )' and @deva[गृहाः] 'a house,' which are always used in the plural.
