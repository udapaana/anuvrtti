## Other Words Used Prepositionally (Gati)

§ 369. There are several other words, also styled Gati by Sanskrit grammarians, used prepositionally. These are prefixed to certain roots only. We give the more important of them below.

(a) @deva[अच्छ][^1] 'towards' is prefixed to @deva[वद्] and to roots implying motion; @deva[अच्छगम्य]—@deva[गत्य] 'going near,' @deva[अच्छपतत्] 'flying towards,' @deva[अच्छोद्य] 'having spoken to.'

(b) (1) @deva[अन्वाजे] 'giving strength to the weak,' @deva[अलं] in the sense of 'decorating,' @deva[ऊरी], @deva[उररी], @deva[ऊररी] all implying 'assent, acceptance or promise,' @deva[खात्] and similar imitative sounds, @deva[असत्] and @deva[सत्] in the sense of 'dishonouring or honouring;' @deva[प्राध्वं] denoting 'fastening,' &c. are prefixed to @deva[कृ]; @deva[अन्वाजेकरणम्], @deva[ऊरीकरणम्], @deva[असत्कारः], @deva[सत्कृत्य], @deva[खात्कृत्य], @deva[प्राध्वंकरणम्], &c.

(2) @deva[नमः], @deva[प्रादुः], @deva[मिथ्या], @deva[वशे], @deva[साक्षात्] and some other words may be optionally prefixed to @deva[कृ] or remain separate; @deva[नमस्कारः], @deva[वशेकृ] or @deva[वशे कृ] 'to bring under subjection,' @deva[साक्षात् कृ] or @deva[साक्षात्कृ] 'to make manifest,' &c.

(c) @deva[अन्तर्] is prefixed to roots meaning 'to go,' @deva[धा], @deva[भू], and similar roots; @deva[अन्तरित्य] 'having disappeared,' @deva[अन्तर्धानं] 'disappearance,' @deva[अन्तर्भूत], &c.

(d) @deva[अस्तम्] is prefixed to roots implying motion; @deva[अस्तमयः] 'setting,' @deva[अस्तंगत] 'set,' @deva[अस्तंनी] 'to cause to set, to lead to destruction,' &c.

(e) @deva[आविः], and @deva[प्रादुः] are prefixed to @deva[कृ], @deva[अस्] and @deva[भू] and @deva[तिरस्] to @deva[भू], @deva[धा] and similar roots, and optionally to @deva[कृ], @deva[आविष्करणं], @deva[आविर्भवनं] 'manifestation,' @deva[प्रादुर्]—@deva[आविर्भूत] 'manifested,' &c.; @deva[तिरोभूय] 'vanishing out of sight,' @deva[तिरोधानं] 'disappearance,' &c.

(f) @deva[पुरः] is prefixed to @deva[कृ], @deva[भू], @deva[गम्] &c.; @deva[पुरस्कृत] 'placed before, headed by,' @deva[पुरोगत] 'gone in the front,' &c.

[^1]: @deva[अच्छगत्यर्थवदेषु] । Pāṇ. 1. 4. 69.
