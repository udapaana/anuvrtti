title: "Present for Past with @deva[स्म] Particle"
pages: [518.png, 519.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1]The particle @deva[स्म] when used with the Present converts it into a Past tense; @deva[कस्मिंश्चिदधिष्ठाने मित्रशर्मा नाम ब्राह्मणः प्रतिवसति स्म] in a certain village there dwelt a Brāhmaṇa Mitraśarmā by name; @deva[पौराः शतशोभिधावन्ति स्म] the citizens ran in hundreds. The particle @deva[स्म] may not necessarily be joined with it; @deva[त्वं स्म वेत्थ महाराज यत्स्माह न विभीषणः, मन्त्रे स्म हितमाचष्टे], &c.

## Footnotes

[^1]: @deva[लट् स्मे।] Pān. III. 2. 118.
