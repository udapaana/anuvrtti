## Irregular Perfect Participles from @deva[ऋ]-Ending Roots

Roots ending in @deva[ॠ] (@deva[तॄ] and @deva[जॄ] included) form their Perf. P. irregularly; @deva[वस्] is added to the root, which then undergoes the changes mentioned in @ref[394], and is finally reduplicated; when a root is Atm. it is first reduplicated and @deva[आन] afterwards added, the final @deva[ॠ] changing as before; *e. g.* @deva[कॄ]+@deva[वस्]=@deva[कीर्वस्], by reduplication @deva[चिकीर्वस्]; @deva[कॄ]—by reduplication @deva[चकॄ]+@deva[आन]=@deva[चकिराण]; similarly @deva[तॄ]—@deva[तितीर्वस्]; @deva[ततिराण]; @deva[शॄ]—@deva[शिशीर्वस्] @deva[शशिराण]; @deva[पॄ]—@deva[पुपूर्वस्], @deva[पपुराण], &c.
