(a) @deva[ऋ][^1] when it means 'to incur debt' substitutes @deva[न]; @deva[ऋण] 'debt;' but @deva[ऋत], 'gone.'

(b) @deva[क्षि], when the P. P. Participle has an active sense, substitutes @deva[न] and lengthens its @deva[इ]; @deva[क्षीण], reduced, emaciated; but when a curse is implied, or pity expressed, the change is optional; @deva[क्षीणायुः] or @deva[क्षितायुः भव] i. e. 'die;' @deva[क्षीण] or @deva[क्षितः अयं तपस्वी] 'oh, the poor ascetic has grown thin' but @deva[क्षितः कामो मया].

(c) @deva[दिव्][^2] takes @deva[न] when it does not mean 'to gamble;' @deva[द्यून] 'a sport in which nothing is staked,' but @deva[द्यूतं] 'gambling.'

(d) @deva[वा][^2] with @deva[निर्] takes @deva[न] except when it has @deva[वात] for its subject; @deva[निर्वाणोऽग्निः] 'the fire is extinguished;' @deva[निर्वाणो मुनिः] the sage is absolved; but @deva[निर्वातः वातः]

(e) @deva[श्यै] takes @deva[न] when the notion of touch is not present; @deva[संश्यानो वृश्चिकः] 'a scorpion contracted through cold;' when it means 'to be coagulated or cold' its @deva[य्] takes @deva[संप्रसारण] (@deva[ई]); @deva[शीनं घृतं], but @deva[शीतं उदकं] and not @deva[शीनं] as the idea of touch is present here; also when preceded by @deva[प्रति]; @deva[प्रतिशीनं घृतं], &c.; with @deva[अभि] and @deva[अव] the change is optional; @deva[अभिश्यानं] or @deva[अभिशीनं घृतं], @deva[अवश्यानो] or @deva[अवशीनो वृश्चिकः]; but @deva[समवश्यातः]

[^1]: @deva[ऋणमाधमर्ण्ये ।] Pāṇ. VIII. 2. 60.

[^2]: @deva[दिवोऽविजिगीषायाम् । विर्वाणोऽवाते ।] Pāṇ. VIII. 2. 49–50.
