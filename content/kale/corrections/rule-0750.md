  - "क्लिन्त्वा / क्लिस्त्वा in the parenthesis is blurred."
  - "द्युत्वा at the end: ू vs ु ambiguous (from दिव्, expected द्यूत्वा?) — written as the glyph appears (द्युत्वा)."
  - "द्युत् in 'द्युत्—द्युतित्वा' blurred but consistent."

Guṇa is optionally substituted for the penultimate @deva[इ] and @deva[उ], roots beginning with any consonant and ending in any except @deva[य्] or @deva[व्] when @deva[त्वा] has @deva[इ] prefixed to it; @deva[लिख्]—@deva[लिखित्वा] or @deva[लेखित्वा]; @deva[क्लिद्]—@deva[क्लिदित्वा] or @deva[क्लेदित्वा] (also @deva[क्लिन्त्वा]); @deva[लुभ्]—6. P. @deva[लुभित्वा] or @deva[लोभित्वा]; @deva[द्युत्]—@deva[द्युतित्वा], @deva[द्योतित्वा]; @deva[रिष्]—@deva[रिषित्वा], @deva[रेषित्वा] and @deva[रिष्ट्वा]; so @deva[रुष्], &c., but @deva[दिव्]—@deva[देवित्वा], @deva[द्युत्वा].
