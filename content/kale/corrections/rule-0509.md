## Nasal-Dropping Roots in Perfect

**IRREGULAR BASES**

The roots @deva[श्रन्थ्], @deva[ग्रन्थ्], @deva[दम्भ्] and @deva[स्वञ्ज्] drop their nasal optionally before the terminations of the Perfect. @deva[श्रन्थ्], @deva[ग्रन्थ्] and @deva[दम्भ्] obey @ref[500], even before the strong terminations when their nasal is dropped.
