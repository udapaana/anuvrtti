## Admission of Intermediate @deva[इ] before Terminations

Special rules about the admission of the intermediate @deva[इ] before the terminations @deva[व], @deva[म], @deva[थ], @deva[से], @deva[वहे], @deva[महे], @deva[ध्वे]; see (§ @ref[457]).[^1]

(a) All roots, whether Seṭ or Aniṭ, except @deva[कृ], @deva[सृ], @deva[भृ], @deva[वृ], @deva[स्तु], @deva[द्रु], @deva[स्रु] and @deva[श्रु], admit @deva[इ]. But @deva[कृ] with @deva[सम्], and @deva[वृ] admit it before @deva[थ]; as @deva[संचस्करिथ], @deva[ववरिथ].

(b) Aniṭ roots ending in short @deva[ऋ], except @deva[ऋ], reject it before @deva[थ]; as @deva[सस्मर्थ] from @deva[स्मृ]; but @deva[आरिथ] from @deva[ऋ].

(c) Aniṭ roots with a final vowel or with a penultimate @deva[अ] admit it optionally before @deva[थ].

[^1]: @deva[कृसृभृवृस्तुद्रुस्रुश्रुवो लिटि ।] Pāṇ. VII. 2. 13. @deva[अजन्तोऽकारवान्वायस्तास्यनिद् थलि वेडयम् । ऋदन्त इदृङ् नित्यानिद् काद्यन्यो लिटि सेड् भवेत् ॥] (Sid. Kau.)
