## Locative with @deva[उप] (exceeding) and @deva[अधि] (master of)

[^1] The prepositions @deva[उप] in the sense of 'exceeding' and @deva[अधि] in that of 'the master of' govern the Locative; @deva[उपपरार्धे हरेर्गुणाः] the merits of Hari exceed a parārdha; @deva[अधि भुवि रामः] or @deva[अधि रामे भूः] Rāma is the lord of the earth; in other senses these prepositions are used with the Accusative, for which see @ref[805].

---

[^1]: @deva[यस्मादधिकं यस्य चेश्वरवचनं तत्र सप्तमी ।] Pāṇ. II 3. 9.
