title: "@deva[अप्] (Water): Plural-Only Declension"
pages: [080.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[अप्] f. water, is declined in the plural; it lengthens its @deva[अ] in the N. and changes its @deva[प्] to @deva[द्] before consonantal terminations. @deva[आपः-अपः-अद्भिः-अद्भ्यः-अद्भ्यः-अपाम्-अप्सु].
