## Five Mātrā Gaṇas

In the case of metres regulated by syllabic instants each line is divided into groups of four mātrās called the Mātrā Gaṇas; one @deva[मात्रा] or instant is allotted to a short vowel and two to a long one. The mātrā gaṇas are five in number. These may be symbolically represented thus:—

@deva[म] – –  @deva[स] ⏑ ⏑ –  @deva[ज] ⏑ – ⏑  @deva[भ] – ⏑ ⏑  @deva[न] ⏑ ⏑ ⏑ ⏑
