title: "Gutturals to Palatals and @deva[ह्] to @deva[ज्] in Reduplicative Syllable"
pages: [282.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "the alt printing p.282 omits the हु-जुहु example; ours prints it"
---
The guttural of the reduplicative syllable is changed to the corresponding palatal (subject to the above rule) and @deva[ह्] to @deva[ज्]; e. g. @deva[कम्]-@deva[ककम्]-@deva[चकम्], @deva[खन्]-@deva[खखन्]-@deva[कखन्]=@deva[चखन्], @deva[हु]-@deva[जुहु] &c.
