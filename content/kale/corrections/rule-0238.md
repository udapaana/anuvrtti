## GENERAL RULES APPLICABLE TO THE TATPURUSHA COMPOUNDS.

The word @deva[अङ्गुलि] at the end of a Tatpurusha compound changes its final to @deva[अ] when preceded by a numeral or an indeclinable,[^1] as @deva[द्वे अङ्गुली प्रमाणमस्य द्व्यङ्गुलं दारु] 'a piece of wood measuring two fingers,' @deva[निर्गतमङ्गुलिभ्यो निरङ्गुलम्] &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[तत्पुरुषस्याङ्गुलेः संख्याव्ययादेः ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 86.
