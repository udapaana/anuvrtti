title: "Potential or Second Future for Expectation Without @deva[यद्]"
pages: [528.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1]When the sense of 'I expect' is implied, the Potential or the 2nd Future may be used provided the word @deva[यद्] is not used; @deva[संभावयामि भुञ्जीत भोक्ष्यते वा भवान्] I expect you will eat; but @deva[संभावयामि यद्भुञ्जीथास्त्वम्] (Sid. Kau).

---

## Footnotes

[^1]: @deva[विभाषा धातौ संभावनवचनेऽयदि।] Pān. III. 3. 155.
