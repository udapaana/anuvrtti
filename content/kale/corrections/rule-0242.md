key: rule:242
title: "@deva[महत्] → @deva[महा] in Compounds"
pages: [148.png]
verdict: approved
---
The word @deva[महत्] is changed to @deva[महा] when it forms the first member of a Karmadhāraya or a Bahuvrīhi compound, or when the termination @deva[जातीय] follows,[^1] as @deva[महादेवः] the great god, @deva[महाबाहुः] a great arm (Tat.) or one having a great arm (Bah.); @deva[महाजातीयः]; but @deva[महतः सेवा महत्सेवा] (Gen. Tat.).

**Exception:**—When it is followed by @deva[घास], @deva[कर] and @deva[विशिष्ट] the change takes place necessarily; @deva[महतो महत्या वा करः महाकरः]; similarly @deva[महाघासः], @deva[महाविशिष्टः].

---

[^1]: @deva[आन्महतः समानाधिकरणजातीययोः ।] Pāṇ. VI. 3. 46. and the Vārtikas on it.
