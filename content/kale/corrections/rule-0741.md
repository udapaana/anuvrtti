The roots @deva[भू] and @deva[गै] take the affix @deva[यत्]; @deva[वच्] and @deva[स्था] take @deva[अनीय]; and @deva[जन्], @deva[प्लु] and @deva[पत्] take @deva[ण्यत्], in an active sense; @deva[भवतीति भव्यः] (also @deva[भव्यमनेन]); @deva[गायतीति गेयः] 'one who sings' (also @deva[गेयं साम अनेन]); @deva[प्रवचनीयः] 'one who speaks' @deva[उपस्थानीय] 'one who stands by'. @deva[जन्यः], @deva[प्लाव्यः], @deva[पात्यः].
