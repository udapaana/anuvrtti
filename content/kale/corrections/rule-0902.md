key: rule:902
title: "Past Passive Participles with Auxiliary Verbs @deva[अस्] and @deva[भू]"
pages: [511.png]
verdict: approved
---
The past passive participle active and passive, may be used with the auxiliaries @deva[अस्] and @deva[भू] in any tense, the meaning of the participle changing accordingly; @deva[गतोस्मि] or @deva[गतवानस्मि] I have or am gone; so @deva[गतवानभवं] or @deva[गतवानासं] or @deva[गतोभवं] I had or was gone; so @deva[कृतवानस्मि]. @deva[गतो वनं श्वो भवितेति रामः] that Rāma is to go to the forest to-morrow; @deva[संप्राप्तः कीर्तिमतुलां भविष्यसि] thou wilt obtain great glory, &c.
