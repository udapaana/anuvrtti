## Ātmanepada for Exchange of Action

When the notion of @deva[कर्मव्यतिहार] *i. e.* doing what is not proper for one to do, or the exchange of duties is to be implied, the Atm. is used, @deva[ब्राह्मणः सस्यानि व्यतिलुनीते] 'the Brāhmana reaps corn' (which is the work of a Śûdra and not his): @deva[धर्मः व्यतिस्ते] 'the religious duty is changed' (as when a S'ûdra discharges the duties enjoined upon a Vaisya, &c. @deva[संप्रहरन्ते राजानः] 'the kings exchange blows.

(a) But verbs implying motion, or meaning to kill, the roots @deva[हस्] and other similar roots do not take the Atm. even when an exchange of action is implied, @deva[व्यतियच्छन्ति], @deva[व्यतिघ्नन्ति], @deva[व्यतिहसन्ति], @deva[व्यतिजल्पन्ति].
