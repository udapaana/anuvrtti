Sometimes the second member of an Upapada Tat. may be a *Namulanta* or the verbal indeclinable in @deva[अम्]; as @deva[स्वादुंकारं भुङ्क्ते] 'he eats having sweetened the food;' @deva[अग्रेभोजं] 'having dined first;' sometimes this compound is optional. @deva[मूलकोपदंशं] or @deva[मूलकेन उपदंशं भुङ्क्ते] 'he eats the condiment with a radish,' &c.
