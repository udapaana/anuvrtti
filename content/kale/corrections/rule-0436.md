@deva[विद्] 'to know' takes optionally the terminations of the Perfect in the Present Tense. Its Imperative forms are optionally made up by adding @deva[आम्] to it and then appending the forms of the Imperative of @deva[कृ].

**Present.**

| | S. | D. | P. |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | @deva[वेद्मि]-@deva[वेद] | @deva[विद्वः]-@deva[विद्व] | @deva[विद्मः]-@deva[विद्म] |
| 2. | @deva[वेत्सि]-@deva[वेत्थ] | @deva[वित्थः]-@deva[विदथुः] | @deva[वित्थ]-@deva[विद] |
