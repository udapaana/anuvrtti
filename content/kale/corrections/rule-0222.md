An adjective may be compounded with the noun it qualifies in most cases[^1] as @deva[नीलं च तदुत्पलं च नीलोत्पलं], a blue lotus &c.; @deva[कृष्णश्चासौ सर्पश्च कृष्णसर्पः] where the comp. is necessary; but @deva[जामदग्न्यो रामः ।] Such compounds are called @deva[विशेषणपूर्वपदकर्मधारय]s.

As a general rule in such compounds the qualifying word is placed first; but there are several exceptions which are given below.

**(a)**[^2] The qualified word is placed first and is always of the mas. gender if it denotes a class or species, when compounded with certain adjectives; e. g. @deva[इभयुवतिः] 'a young female elephant', @deva[अग्निस्तोकः] 'a small fire', @deva[उदश्वित्कतिपयम्] 'butter-milk mixed with a little water', @deva[गोगृष्टिः] 'a cow that has brought forth a calf for the first time', @deva[गोधेनुः] 'a milch-cow (@deva[नवप्रसूतिका])'; @deva[गोवशा] 'a barren cow', @deva[गोवेहत्] (@deva[गर्भघातिनी गौः]), @deva[गोबष्कयणी] 'a cow whose calf is one year old,' @deva[कठश्रोत्रियः] 'an agnihotrin Brāhmaṇa of the Kaṭha branch of the Yajurveda,' @deva[कठाध्यापकः] 'a teacher of the Kaṭha branch,'[^3] @deva[गो-मतल्लिका-मचर्चिका-प्रकाण्डं] (also mas. according to some)—@deva[उद्धः] (@deva[गवोद्ध])-@deva[तल्लज] 'an excellent cow'. The last mentioned five words, all of which denote excellence, retain their own gender invariably; as @deva[ब्राह्मणमतल्लिका] 'an eminent Brāhmaṇa' &c.; but @deva[कुमारी मतल्लिका], as no class is meant here.

**(b)**[^4] The words @deva[कडार], @deva[खञ्ज], @deva[खोड] lame, @deva[काण], @deva[कुण्ठ] blunt, dull, @deva[खलति] bald-headed, @deva[गौर], @deva[वृद्ध], @deva[भिक्षुक] @deva[पिंग], @deva[पिंगल], @deva[तनु], @deva[जरठ] hard, stiff, @deva[बधिर], @deva[कुब्ज] and @deva[वर्बर], may optionally precede the words in apposition to them; as @deva[जैमिनिकडारः] or @deva[कडारजैमिनिः] Jaimini who is turned tawny on account of his having practised penance in the sun,' &c.

**(c)**[^5] Words expressive of the persons or things condemned or reproached are placed first; as @deva[वैयाकरणखसूचिः] 'a bad grammarian,' 'one who has forgotten his grammar and therefore can make no use of his knowledge;' (@deva[पृष्टः सन् प्रश्नं विस्मारयितुं खं सूचयति अभ्यासवैधुर्यात्स एकमुच्यते ।] Tatv.); so @deva[मीमांसकादुर्दुरूढः] 'a mīmāṃsaka who is a sceptic' or 'an unbeliever'; except in the case of the words @deva[पाप], @deva[अणक] and @deva[किम्]; as @deva[पापनापितः] 'a bad barber,' @deva[अणककुलालः] 'a mean, silly potter,' @deva[कुत्सितः राजा किराजः], @deva[किंसखा] (@deva[कुत्सितः सखा]), &c.

**(d)**[^6] Words in apposition to @deva[वृन्दारक], @deva[नाग] and @deva[कुञ्जर] are also placed first; e. g. @deva[नृपवृन्दारकः] 'an eminent king,' @deva[तापसकुञ्जरः], @deva[पुरुषनागः], &c.

**(e)**[^7] @deva[कतर] and @deva[कतम] are compounded only when they ask a question with reference to a class (@deva[जाति] family), and are placed first; e. g. @deva[कतर-कतम-कठः] 'which Brāhmaṇa of the Kaṭha branch'? @deva[कतर-कतम-कलापः] 'which Brāhmaṇa of the Kalāpa branch'? But @deva[कतरः पुत्रः] 'which son'?

**(f)**[^8] The word @deva[कुमार] (and also @deva[कुमारी] changed to @deva[कुमार]) is placed first when compounded with one of the words @deva[श्रमणा], @deva[प्रव्रजिता], @deva[कुलटा], @deva[गर्भिणी], @deva[तापसी], @deva[दासी], @deva[अध्यापक], @deva[पण्डित], @deva[पटु], @deva[मृदु], @deva[कुशल], @deva[चपल] and @deva[निपुण]; as @deva[कुमारश्रमणा] 'a female mendicant,' @deva[कुमारप्रव्रजिता] 'a young female ascetic'; @deva[कुमारमृदुः-द्वी] 'a delicate boy or girl,' @deva[कुमारगर्भिणी], @deva[कुमाराध्यापकः], &c.

**(g)**[^9] The words @deva[एक], @deva[सर्व], @deva[जरद्], @deva[पुराण], @deva[नव] and @deva[केवल]; and the words @deva[पूर्व], @deva[अपर], @deva[चरम], @deva[जघन्य] 'hindmost,' @deva[समान], @deva[मध्य], @deva[मध्यम] and @deva[वीर] are always placed first in a Karmadhāraya compound. @deva[अपर] when followed by @deva[अर्ध] is changed to @deva[पश्च]; as @deva[एकनाथः] 'sole master or lord', @deva[सर्वशैलाः], @deva[जरद्वैयायिकः] 'an old logician,' @deva[पुराणमीमांसकाः] 'old mīmāṃsā philosophers,' @deva[नवपाठकाः], @deva[पूर्ववैयाकरणाः] 'the old grammarians,' @deva[अपराध्यापकः] @deva[अपरश्चासौ अर्धश्च पश्चार्धः] 'the hinder part or side of the body;' @deva[चरमराजः] 'the last king,' @deva[समानाधिकरणम्] 'being in the same category or predicament'; @deva[वीरैकः] 'a sole warrior i. e. an eminent warrior', &c. The form @deva[एकवीर] (found used by great writers) also may be defended.[^10]

**(h)**[^11] The words @deva[सत्], @deva[महत्], @deva[परम], @deva[उत्तम] and @deva[उत्कृष्ट] are compounded with another noun when they are used as terms of praise or excellence; @deva[सद्वैद्यः] 'an excellent physician' @deva[महावैयाकरणः] &c. but @deva[उत्कृष्टो गौः] where @deva[उत्कृष्ट] means 'uplifted, or 'drawn out of.'
