## Roots with Two Causal Forms

The roots @deva[गुप्], @deva[विच्छ्], @deva[धूप्], @deva[पण्], @deva[पन्], and @deva[ऋत्] have two forms in the causal; @deva[गोपयति-ते], @deva[गोपाययति-ते]; @deva[विच्छयति-ते], @deva[विच्छाययति-ते]; &c.
