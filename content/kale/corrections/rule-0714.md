## (2) Participles in @deva[य] (@deva[यत्], @deva[क्यप्] and @deva[ण्यत्]).

**By means of @deva[यत्] (@deva[य]).**

The affix @deva[य] (@deva[यत्])[^1] is added on to roots ending in a vowel in the sense of 'fit for, or fit to be, or ought to be.' Before this @deva[य] the radical vowel undergoes Guṇa substitute and final @deva[आ] (@deva[ए], @deva[ऐ] and @deva[ओ] changeable to @deva[आ]) is changed to @deva[ए].

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| @deva[दा]— | @deva[देय] | what is fit or ought to be given. |
| @deva[धे]— | @deva[धेय] | ,, ,, sucked. |
| @deva[गै]— | @deva[गेय] | ,, ,, sung. |
| @deva[छो]— | @deva[छेय] | ,, ,, cut. |
| @deva[चि]— | @deva[चेय] | ,, ,, collected. |
| @deva[नी]— | @deva[नेय] | ,, ,, led or carried. |

[^1]: @deva[अचो यत् ।] Pāṇ. III. 1. 97.
