The roots @deva[श्वि], @deva[डी], @deva[शी], @deva[पू] and @deva[जॄ], Seṭ roots ending in consonants, roots of the 10th class and all derivative verbs, admit @deva[इ] before @deva[त्वा]; roots of the 10th cl. preserve their @deva[अय्] before @deva[त्वा]; @deva[श्वि]—@deva[श्वयित्वा], @deva[डी]—@deva[डयित्वा], @deva[जॄ]—@deva[जरीत्वा] or @deva[जरित्वा], @deva[नृत्]—@deva[नर्तित्वा], @deva[व्यच्]—@deva[विचित्वा], @deva[लज्ज्]—@deva[लजित्वा], @deva[जीव्]—@deva[जीवित्वा], &c., @deva[चुर्]—@deva[चोरयित्वा]; @deva[कथ्]—@deva[कथयित्वा], @deva[बुध्]—cau. @deva[बोधयित्वा]; desi. @deva[बुबोधिषित्वा]; Ātm. fre. @deva[बोबुधित्वा], &c.
