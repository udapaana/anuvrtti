---
key: rule:277
title: "River Compounds for Place Names"
pages: [166.png]
verdict: approved
---
Any noun may be compounded with the name of a river, into an Avya. comp. to form a name;[^1] @deva[उन्मत्तगंगम्], a place where the Ganges is very boisterous.' so @deva[लोहितगंगम्], &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[अन्यपदार्थे च संज्ञायाम्] । Pân. II. 1. 21.
