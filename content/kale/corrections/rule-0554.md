## Roots Preserving Vowel in Reduplicative Syllable

The roots given under @ref[400] preserve their vowel unchanged; i. e. do not substitute @deva[इ] in the reduplicative syllable, &c. @deva[कथ्]—@deva[अचकथत्], @deva[वर्]—@deva[अववरत्], @deva[शठ्]—@deva[अशशठत्], @deva[रह्]—@deva[अररहत्], @deva[पत्]—@deva[अपपतत्], @deva[स्पृह्]—@deva[अपस्पृहत्], @deva[सूच्]—@deva[असुसूचत्].
