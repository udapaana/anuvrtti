The intermediate @deva[इ] is optionally lengthened in the case of @deva[वृ] and roots ending in @deva[ॠ], except in the Perfect, the Benedictive Ātm. and the Aorist Par.; @deva[वरितास्मि], @deva[वरीतास्मि], 1 sing. @deva[कॄ]–@deva[करितास्मि], @deva[करीतास्मि] 1 sing.
