## Radical Vowel Changes Before @deva[स्] in Desideratives

The radical vowels undergo the following changes before @deva[स्]—

(a) The @deva[स्] with @deva[इ] is strong and without @deva[इ] weak. The usual rule as to Guṇa substitute holds good in the one case and does not in the other; @deva[वृत्]—@deva[विवर्तिष्], @deva[विवृत्स्]; @deva[दृ]—@deva[दिदरिष्], &c.

(b) The final @deva[इ] and @deva[उ] and the penultimate @deva[अ] of @deva[हन्] and @deva[गम्] (substituted for @deva[इ] 2 Par. 'to go,' and for @deva[इ] with @deva[अधि] 'to remember' or 'to study') are lengthened and final @deva[ऋ], short or long, changed to @deva[ईर्], or to @deva[ऊर्] after labials or @deva[व्] when the @deva[स्] is unaugmented; @deva[जि]—@deva[जिगीष्], @deva[द्रु]—@deva[दुद्रूष्], @deva[कृ]—@deva[चिकीर्ष्], @deva[तॄ]—@deva[तितीर्ष्], @deva[मृ]—@deva[मुमूर्ष्], @deva[पॄ]—@deva[पुपूर्ष्], &c.

(c) The roots @deva[रुद्], @deva[विद्] and @deva[मुष्] do not change their vowel to Guṇa; and the roots @deva[ग्रह्], @deva[स्वप्] and @deva[प्रच्छ्] take Samprasāraṇa; @deva[रुरुदिष्], @deva[विविदिष्], @deva[मुमुषिष्], @deva[जिघृक्ष्], @deva[सुषुप्स्], @deva[पिपृच्छिष्].

(d) Roots with an initial consonant, and having @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] short for their penultimate, and ending in any consonant, except @deva[य्] or @deva[व्], change their vowel to Guṇa optionally when @deva[इ] is prefixed to @deva[स्]; @deva[द्युत्]—@deva[दिद्युतिष्], @deva[दिद्योतिष्]; @deva[मुद्]—@deva[मुमुदिष्] or @deva[मुमोदिष्] &c.
