## Admission of @deva[इ] by @deva[गम्], @deva[हन्] and Aniṭ Roots

**Irregular Bases**

@deva[गम्] P. @deva[हन्] and Aniṭ roots ending in @deva[ऋ] admit @deva[इ] in the Second Future and the Conditional; @deva[गम्] P. (also that substituted for @deva[इ] 'to go' and with @deva[अधि] 'to remember') also admits it in the Desiderative, @deva[गमिष्यामि], @deva[हनिष्यामि], @deva[करिष्यामि], &c. Sec. Fu. 1 sing. @deva[अगमिष्यम्], @deva[अकरिष्यम्], @deva[अहनिष्यम्], &c. Con. 1 sing.
