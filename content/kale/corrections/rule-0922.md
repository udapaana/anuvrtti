With the words @deva[यावत्], @deva[तावत्] and others having a similar sense, the Present is sometimes used in the sense of the Future perfect; @deva[यावत्स त्वां न पश्यति तावद्दूरमपसर] before he sees you, move away (before he shall have seen you, &c.).

(b) [^1]With the particles @deva[पुरा] and @deva[यावत्] the Present has the sense of the Future when certainty is indicated; @deva[यावद्यते साधयितुं त्वदर्थम्।] (Rag. V. 25.) I will endeavour to accomplish your object. @deva[पुरा सप्तद्वीपां जयति वसुधाम्] (S'ak. VII. 33) he will conquer the earth consisting of seven continents. @deva[पुरानुशेते तव चञ्चलं मनः।] Kir. VIII. 8.
