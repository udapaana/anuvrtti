## Vowel Changes Before @deva[क्यङ्]

Before this affix the final @deva[अ] of a nominal base is lengthened, @deva[आ] remains unchanged, other final letters undergo the same changes as they do before the other @deva[य] (@deva[क्यच्]). The final @deva[स्] of a noun is changed to @deva[आ] optionally and that of @deva[अप्सरस्] and @deva[ओजस्] necessarily; @deva[कृष्ण इव आचरति], @deva[कृष्णायते] 'he acts like @deva[कृष्ण];' @deva[यशस्]—@deva[यशायते], @deva[यशस्यते] 'he behaves like one who is famous,' @deva[विद्वस्]—@deva[विद्वायते], @deva[विद्वस्यते] 'he acts like a learned man, &c.; but @deva[ओजस्]—@deva[ओजायते] 'acts like one who is lustrous' (powerful); @deva[अप्सरस्]—@deva[अप्सरायते] 'she behaves like an Apsaras.'

(a) A *feminine* noun not having @deva[ऋ] for its penultimate drops its *fem.* term., @deva[कुमारीव आचरति]—@deva[कुमारायते] 'he behaves like a girl,' @deva[हरिणीव आचरति] @deva[हरिणायते] 'she acts like a female deer'; @deva[गुर्वीव आचरति] @deva[गुरूयते] 'acts like a stout woman' But @deva[पाचिका इव आचरति] @deva[पाचिकायते] 'she acts like a female cook,' and not @deva[पाचकायते].

(b) @deva[सपत्नी] has @deva[सपत्नायते], @deva[सपत्नीयते] and @deva[सपतीयते] she acts like a co-wife, @deva[युवति] has @deva[युवायते] 'she behaves like a young woman.'
