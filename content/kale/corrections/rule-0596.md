**(2, 3) The two Futures, (4) the Conditional and (5) the Benedictive.**

## Futures, Conditional and Benedictive in Passive

### (a) Ordinary Formation

The forms of the two Futures, Conditional and the Benedictive of the Passive are made up in the same way as those of the Active, every root being supposed Ātmanepadi; @deva[बुध्]—@deva[बोधिताहे], @deva[बोधिष्ये], @deva[अबोधिष्ये], @deva[बोधिषीय]; @deva[तुद्]—@deva[तोत्ताहे], @deva[तोत्स्ये], @deva[अतोत्स्ये]; &c.

### (b) Optional Forms with Vṛddhi

Roots ending in a vowel[^1] and the roots @deva[हन्], @deva[ग्रह्], and @deva[दृश्] optionally form the two Futures, the Conditional and the Benedictive of the Passive by changing their vowel to Vṛddhi and appending the Ātmanepada termination of those tenses with @deva[इ] prefixed to them invariably; in the case of roots ending in @deva[आ] (and in @deva[ए], @deva[ऐ] and @deva[ओ] changeable to @deva[आ]) @deva[य्] is inserted between the root and this @deva[इ]; @deva[दा]—@deva[दायिताहे], @deva[दाताहे]; @deva[दायिष्ये], @deva[दास्ये]; @deva[अदायिष्ये], @deva[अदास्ये]; @deva[दायिषीय]; @deva[दासीय]; and similarly @deva[ह्वे]—@deva[ह्वायिताहे], @deva[ह्वाताहे]; &c. @deva[नी]—@deva[नायिताहे], @deva[नेताहे]; @deva[नायिष्ये], @deva[नेष्ये]; @deva[अनायिष्ये], @deva[अनेष्ये]; @deva[नायिषीय], @deva[नेषीय]; @deva[हन्]—@deva[घातिताहे], [^2] @deva[हन्ताहे]; @deva[घानिष्ये], @deva[हनिष्ये]; @deva[अघानिष्ये], @deva[अहनिष्ये]; @deva[घानिषीय], @deva[वधिषीय], 1 sing.; @deva[ग्रह्]—@deva[ग्राहिताहे], @deva[ग्रहीताहे]; @deva[ग्राहिष्ये], @deva[ग्रहीष्ये]; @deva[अग्राहिष्ये], @deva[अग्रहीष्ये]; @deva[ग्राहिषीय], @deva[ग्रहीषीय] &c. @deva[दृश्]—@deva[दर्शिताहे], @deva[द्रष्टाहे]; @deva[दर्शिष्ये], @deva[द्रक्ष्ये]; @deva[अदर्शिष्ये], @deva[अद्रक्ष्ये]; @deva[दर्शिषीय], @deva[दृक्षीय], &c.

[^1]: @deva[नू] and @deva[धू] take Vṛddhi in this case though it is usually forbidden with them; see @ref[463]; @deva[दृश्] takes Guṇa only.

[^2]: The @deva[ह्] of @deva[हन्] is changed to @deva[घ्] immediately before @deva[न्] and before an affix containing a mute @deva[ञ्] or @deva[ण्], i. e. @deva[य] of the Passive, &c.
