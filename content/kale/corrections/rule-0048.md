---
key: rule:48
title: "Visarga after @deva[आ]: Dropping and @deva[य्]"
pages: [033.png]
verdict: approved
---
Visarga preceded by @deva[आ] is dropped necessarily when followed by a soft consonant, and optionally when followed by a vowel; it is also optionally dropped when preceded by @deva[अ] and followed by a vowel except @deva[अ]; when it is not dropped it is changed to @deva[य्] in both these cases; @deva[देवाः] + @deva[नम्याः] = @deva[देवा नम्याः]; @deva[देवा] + @deva[इह] = @deva[देवा इह], @deva[देवायिह]।
