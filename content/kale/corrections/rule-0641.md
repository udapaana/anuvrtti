## Formation of Nominal Verbs Expressing Wish (@deva[क्यच्])

When the sense of 'wish' is to expressed, a nominal verb may be derived from any *Subanta* or nominal stem by affixing @deva[य] to it. The derivative verbal base derived in this manner is conjugated in the Parasm. only.
