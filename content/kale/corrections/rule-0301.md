---
key: rule:301
title: "Vowel Lengthening and @deva[न] → @deva[ण] Before @deva[वन]"
pages: [179.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] When the words @deva[पुरग], @deva[मिश्रक], @deva[सिध्रक], @deva[सारिक] and @deva[कोटर] are followed by @deva[वन] in a compound they lengthen their ending vowel, and the @deva[न] of @deva[वन] is changed to @deva[ण] ( also after @deva[अग्रे] ); @deva[पुरगावणम्], @deva[मिश्रकावणम्], @deva[सिध्रकावणम्], @deva[सारिकावणम्], @deva[कोटरावणम्]; but @deva[असिपत्रवनम्]; @deva[वनस्याग्रे अग्रेवणम्].

---

[^1]: @deva[वनं पुरगामिश्रकासिध्रकासारिकाकोटराग्रेभ्यः] । Pān. VIII. 4. 4.
