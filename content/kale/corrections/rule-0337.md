# Chapter IX. Secondary Nominal Bases Derived by the Addition of the Taddhita or Secondary Affixes

The terminations used to form derivative bases, in Sanskrit are distinguished into two classes, (1) *Krit* ( @deva[कृत्] ) or Primary Affixes and (2) *Taddhita* ( @deva[तद्धित] ) or Secondary affixes. Krit are those affixes that are added to verbs, and the Nominal Bases formed by their means are called PRIMARY NOMINAL BASES; while TADDHITA affixes are those that are added to substantives, primary or derived from roots, and the bases formed with them are called SECONDARY NOMINAL BASES ( vide @ref[179] ).
