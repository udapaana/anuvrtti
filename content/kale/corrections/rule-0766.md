The gerund in @deva[अम्] of the root @deva[कृ] is used[^1]

(a) with the noun governed by it, if it be compounded with it prepositionally when censure is implied; @deva[चौरकारं आक्रोशति] (@deva[चौरशब्दं उच्चार्येत्यर्थः]) 'cries out (he is) a thief, a thief.' In this case @deva[म्] is added to the noun governed.

(b) with the words @deva[स्वादु], @deva[लवण], and @deva[संपन्न], @deva[म्] being added to these as before; @deva[अस्वादुं स्वादुं कृत्वा भुंक्ते] @deva[स्वादुं-लवणं-सम्पन्नं—कारं भुंक्ते] 'he eats having sweetened or seasoned his food.'

(c) with the words @deva[अन्यथा], @deva[एवं], @deva[इत्थं], and @deva[कथं], provided the root @deva[कृ] loses its sense; @deva[अन्यथाकारं ब्रूते] 'he speaks in a different manner;' @deva[एवंकारं भुंक्ते] 'he eats thus;' similarly @deva[इत्थंकारं], @deva[कथंकारम्], but @deva[शिरोन्यथा कृत्वा भुंक्ते].

(d) with the particles @deva[यथा] and @deva[तथा] when an angry reply is given; @deva[यथाकारं भोक्ष्ये तथाकारं भोक्ष्ये किं तवानेन] (Sid. Kau.); 'I will eat in this manner, I will eat in that manner, what have you to do with that?'
