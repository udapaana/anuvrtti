**Irregular Benedictives.**

## Special Cases: @deva[ई] and @deva[ऊह्] in Benedictive

@deva[ई] 'to go'—@deva[ईयासम्]; but shortens its @deva[ई] after a preposition; @deva[समियासम्]; Ātm. @deva[एषीय]. @deva[ऊह्] also, when joined with prepositions, shortens its @deva[ऊ] before weak terminations beginning with @deva[य्]; @deva[समुह्यासम्].
