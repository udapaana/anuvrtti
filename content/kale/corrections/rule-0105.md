## Irregular Bases.

@deva[क्रुञ्च्] a heron &c. (derived from @deva[क्रुञ्च् कौटिल्याल्पीभावयोः]), @deva[खञ्ज्] a lame man, and @deva[सुवल्ग्] beautifully bounding. These become @deva[क्रुङ्], @deva[खन्] and @deva[सुवल्] before consonantal terminations; e. g.

| N. sing. | N. dual | I. dual. | Loc. plu. |
|---|---|---|---|
| @deva[क्रुङ्] | @deva[क्रुञ्चौ] | @deva[क्रुङ्भ्याम्] | @deva[क्रुङ्क्षु] or- |
| @deva[खन्] | @deva[खञ्जौ] | @deva[खन्भ्याम्] | @deva[खन्सु] |
| @deva[सुवल्] | @deva[सुवल्गौ] | @deva[सुवल्भ्याम्] | @deva[सुवल्सु] |

The remaining forms should be similarly made up.

Neu.

| N. V. A. Sing. | N. V. A. Dual | N. V. A. Plural |
|---|---|---|
| @deva[क्रुङ्] | @deva[क्रुञ्ची] | @deva[क्रुञ्चि] |
| @deva[खन्] | @deva[खञ्जी] | @deva[खञ्जि] |

The rest like the mas.
