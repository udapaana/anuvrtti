## V. INTERJECTIONS

§ 377. "The interjection is not properly a part of speech, as it does not enter into the construction of sentences. It is a sudden exclamation prompted by some strong feeling or emotion. There are various utterances suited to the different emotions of the mind":—*Prof. Bain.*

(a) These are—@deva[आ], @deva[इ], @deva[उ], @deva[ए], @deva[ऐ], @deva[ओ], @deva[अह], @deva[अहह], @deva[अहो], @deva[बत], @deva[ह], @deva[हा], @deva[हाहा], &c. expressive of wonder, grief or regret, &c.

(b) @deva[किम्], @deva[धिक्] &c. expressive of contempt.

(c) @deva[हा], @deva[बत], &c. expressing sorrow, dejection, &c.

(d) @deva[हा], @deva[हाहा], @deva[हन्त] showing grief.

(e) @deva[आ], @deva[हम्], @deva[हुम्], &c. expressive of anger, contempt, &c.

(f) @deva[हन्त], &c. expressing joy.

(g) There are some interjections used to call attention. Of these,

(1) some show respect; such as @deva[अङ्ग], @deva[अये], @deva[अहो], @deva[अहो], @deva[बत], @deva[उ], @deva[ए], @deva[ओ], @deva[प्याट्], @deva[भोः], @deva[हंहो], @deva[हे], @deva[है], @deva[हो], &c.

(2) and others, disrespect or contempt; as @deva[अङ्ग], @deva[अरे], @deva[अवे], @deva[रे], @deva[रेरे], @deva[अरेरे] &c.

(3) The exclamations @deva[श्रौषट्], @deva[वौषट्] and @deva[वषट्] are used when offering oblations to gods or manes; and

(4) @deva[स्वाहा] and @deva[स्वधा] when offering oblations to gods and manes respectively.
