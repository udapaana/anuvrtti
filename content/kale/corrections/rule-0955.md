  - "§ 955 fn: print reads 'कामप्रवेदने कच्चिति' with no avagraha (the sūtra is कामप्रवेदनेऽकच्चिति): kept as printed."
---
[^1]When hope is expressed without the use of the word @deva[कच्चित्] the potential is generally used; @deva[कामो मे भुञ्जीत भवान्] it is my desire (I hope) that you will eat; but @deva[कच्चिज्जीवति] I hope he lives.

---

## Footnotes

[^1]: @deva[कामप्रवेदने कच्चिति।] Pān III. 3. 153.
