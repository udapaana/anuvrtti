## Genitive with Verbs of Business/Gambling

[^1] The roots @deva[व्यवह्] (i. e. @deva[ह्] with @deva[वि] and @deva[अव]), @deva[पण्] and @deva[दिव्] when they all mean to transact business, or to stake in gambling, govern the Genitive of their object; @deva[शतस्य व्यवहरति] invests a hundred rupees in business; @deva[प्राणानामपणिष्टासौ] he staked his life; @deva[अदेवीद् बन्धुभोगानां] lost his brothers and pleasures in gambling, &c; but when @deva[दिव्] is preceded by a preposition, the Accusative may also be used; @deva[शतस्य शतं वा प्रतिदीव्यति] (Sid. Kau.)

---

[^1]: @deva[व्यवहृपणोः समर्थयोः । दिवस्तदर्थस्य । विभाषोपसर्गे ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 57–59.
