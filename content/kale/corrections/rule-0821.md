## Dative with @deva[श्लाघ्], @deva[ह्नु], @deva[स्था], @deva[शप्]

The roots @deva[श्लाघ्][^1] 'to praise,' @deva[ह्नु] 'to hide,' @deva[स्था] 'to stand,' and @deva[शप्] 'to swear,' govern the Dative of the person to whom some object or feeling is to be conveyed; @deva[गोपी स्मरात् कृष्णाय श्लाघते-ह्नुते-तिष्ठते-शपते वा] 'a cowherdess being incited by love flatters Krishna or apparently conceals her feeling from him or waits for him or swears before him;' (Vide Bhatti. VII. 73. 74.): but @deva[राजानं श्लाघते मन्त्री] 'a minister praises his king.'

[^1]: @deva[श्लाघह्नुङ्स्थाशपां ज्ञीप्स्यमानः ।] Pāṇ. I. 4. 34.
