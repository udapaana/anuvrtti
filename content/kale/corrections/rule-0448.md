---
key: rule:448
title: "Long Vowels Shortened and @deva[ऋ] to @deva[अ] in Reduplicative Syllable"
pages: [282.png]
verdict: approved
---
A radical long vowel becomes short and @deva[ऋ] is changed to @deva[अ] in the reduplicative syllable; as @deva[धा]-@deva[दधा], @deva[नी]-@deva[निनी], @deva[कृ]-@deva[चकृ] &c.
