## THE TWO FUTURES.

The difference between the Two Futures is the same as that between the Imperfect and the Aorist, the only difference being that the former refers to a future time and the latter to a past one. The First or Periphrastic expresses futurity definitely but not of this day; the Second or Simple Future expresses futurity indefinitely as also that of to-day; it is also employed to denote recent and future continuous time; as @deva[अयोध्यां श्वः प्रयातासि कपे भरतपालिताम्] (Bhaṭṭi XXII,) oh monkey, to-morrow you will go to Ayodhyā governed by Bharata; @deva[आनन्दितारस्त्वां दृष्ट्वा प्रष्टारश्चावयोः शिवम्। मातरः सह मैथिल्या तोष्टा च भरतः परम्] (Bhaṭṭi. XXII. 14.) they will be delighted on seeing you and will ask you questions about the welfare of us two and Sītā; and Bharata also will be greatly pleased; @deva[एते…उन्मूलितारः कपिकेतनेन] (Kir. III. 22) they will be extirpated by the monkey-bannered one (Arjuna); @deva[यास्यत्यद्य शकुन्तला] (S'ak. IV.) Śakuntalā will go (goes) to-day; @deva[मरिष्यामि विजेष्ये वा हताश्चेत्तनया मम] (Bhaṭṭi XVI. 13) if my sons are killed I will die or kill the enemy, &c.
