key: rule:765
title: "Gerunds Used with @deva[अग्रे], @deva[प्रथमम्], and @deva[पूर्वम्]"
pages: [445.png]
verdict: approved
---
This gerund or the one in @deva[त्वा] is used with the words @deva[अग्रे], @deva[प्रथमं] and @deva[पूर्वं], used as prepositions, although the notion of repetition is not present in these compounds; @deva[अग्रेभोजं]-@deva[भुक्त्वा वा व्रजति] 'having first eaten he goes out.' so @deva[प्रथमंभोजं] or @deva[भुक्त्वा पूर्वभोजं] or @deva[भुक्त्वा व्रजति].
