**IRREGULAR SPECIAL BASES OF THE NINTH CLASS.**

In the case of the root @deva[क्षुभ्] the @deva[न्] of @deva[ना] is not changed to @deva[ण्]; as @deva[क्षुभ्नाति], @deva[क्षुभ्नीतः], @deva[क्षुभ्नन्ति], &c.
