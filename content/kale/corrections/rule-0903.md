## THE FUTURE PARTICIPLES.

The future participle denotes that a person or thing is doing, or is about to do the action or to undergo the condition, expressed by the root; @deva[करिष्यन्] going or about to do; @deva[करिष्यमाण] about to do or what is about to be done.
