key: rule:41
title: "Retroflexion of @deva[न्] to @deva[ण्] after @deva[र्], @deva[ष्], @deva[ऋ] (Ṇatva)"
pages: [026.png]
verdict: approved
doubts:
  - "पितॄणाम् first letters poorly printed; reading agrees with the visible remainder"
---
@deva[न्] coming after @deva[र्], @deva[ष्], or @deva[ऋ] short or long, in the same word is changed to @deva[ण्] even though a vowel, a semi-vowel except @deva[ल्], a nasal, or a letter of the guttural or labial class or @deva[ह्] comes between @deva[र्], @deva[ष्] or @deva[ऋ] and @deva[न्];[^1] e. g. @deva[रामेन] = @deva[रामेण]; @deva[पूष्] + @deva[ना] = @deva[पूष्णा] by the sun, @deva[पितॄणाम्] &c.; but @deva[राम] + @deva[नाम] = @deva[राम नाम] (as these are two separate words). This change does not take place when @deva[न्] is at the end of a word[^2] @deva[रामान्].

---

[^1]: @deva[रषाभ्यां नो णः समानपदे। अट्कुप्वाङ्नुम्व्यवायेऽपि] — Pāṇ. VIII. 4. 1. 2.

[^2]: @deva[पदान्तस्य न] — Pāṇ. VIII. 4. 37.
