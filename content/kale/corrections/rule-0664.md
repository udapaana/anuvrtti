## Alphabetical List of Roots Changing Pada

The following is an alphabetical list of the roots that change their proper Pada after certain prepositions under the circumstances given in each case.

@deva[अस्]—when preceded by a preposition is conjugated in either Pada; @deva[बन्धं निरस्यति-ते].

@deva[इ] with @deva[अधि]—in the causal takes the Parasm. @deva[अध्यापयति].

@deva[ऊह्]—when preceded by a preposition is conjugated in either Pada; @deva[पापं अपोहति-ते] 'he destroys sin;' @deva[तदपोहति] 'discards;' @deva[समूहति-ते] 'he gathers together.'

@deva[ऋ] with @deva[सम्] is Atm.; @deva[समारन्त ममाभीष्टाः] Bh. VIII. 16; 'all my desires have come to me *i. e.* are gratified.'

@deva[ऋच्छ्] with @deva[सम्]—is Par. when used transitively, and Atm. when used intransitively; @deva[समृच्छति] 'he collects;' @deva[समृच्छते] 'is collected.'

@deva[कृ]—without a preposition admits of either Pada. It is Parasm. with @deva[अनु] and @deva[परा];[^1] @deva[अनुकरोति भगवतो नारायणस्य] । (Kād.); @deva[तां हनुमान्पराकुर्वन्] &c. (Bhaṭṭi. VIII. 50.); it is Atm. with preposition in following senses:—(1) @deva[गन्धन] [^2] or 'hurting,' 'killing,' as @deva[उत्कुरुते] 'informs against (with a view to injure);' (2) @deva[अवक्षेपण] or 'censuring, overcoming;' @deva[उदाकुरुते श्येनो वर्तिकाम्] 'the hawk reproves the snail;' (3) @deva[सेवन] or 'serving, attending upon;' as @deva[हरिमुकुरुते] 'he serves Hari;' (4) @deva[साहसिक्य] or 'acting violently,' 'outraging;' as @deva[परदारान् प्रकुरुते] 'outrages another's wife;' (5) @deva[प्रतियत्न] or 'imparting an additional quality' (@deva[सतो गुणान्तराधानम्] Kâshikâ); as @deva[एधः उदकस्य उपकुरुते] 'fuel imparts heat to water;' (6) @deva[प्रकथन] or reciting;' as @deva[गाथाः प्रकुरुते] 'recites stories from the Vedas;' (7) @deva[उपयोग] or 'applying to use;' as @deva[शतं प्रकुरुते] (@deva[धर्मार्थं शतं विनियुंक्ते इत्यर्थः]) 'devotes a hundred (Rupees, &c) to holy purposes;' *cf.* also Bhaṭṭi. VIII. 18;—with @deva[अधि][^3] it is Atm. in the sense of 'forgiving or overpowering;' @deva[शत्रुं अधिकुरुते] 'forgives or overpowers his enemy;' but @deva[मनुष्यानधिकरोति शास्त्रं] 'the S'âstra authorises men.' With @deva[वि][^4] it is Atm when used transitively; @deva[छात्रा विकुर्वते] 'pupils study;' @deva[स्वरान् विकुरुते गायकः] 'the musician varies the tones;' but @deva[चित्तं विकरोति कामः] 'Love affects the mind;' @deva[विकुर्वे नगरे तस्य] Bh. VIII. 21. @deva[कृ] with @deva[उप] in the sense of 'helping or doing good to' is used in both the Padas; @deva[न हि प्रदीपौ परस्परस्य उपकुरुतः] (Shár. Bhâ.) 'two strong lights do not indeed help (*i. e.* serve to intesify) each other;' @deva[सा लक्ष्मीरुपकुरुते यया परेषाम्] 'that is wealth by means of which one obliges another' (Kir VII. 28.).

The Causal of @deva[कृ] is Atm. when @deva[मिथ्या] is prefixed to it; @deva[पदं मिथ्याकारयते] 'mispronounces the accent on the syllable.'

@deva[कॄ]—'to scatter' with @deva[अप][^5] is Atm. in the sense of 'turning up or scratching with joy,' or turning up with the intention of making an abode or for maintenance (by quadrupeds or birds;)' in this sense @deva[स्] is prefixed to @deva[कॄ]; @deva[अपस्किरते वृषो हृष्टः] 'the bull turns up the ground in joy;' similarly @deva[अपस्किरते कुक्कुटो भक्ष्यार्थी]; @deva[अपस्किरते] (digs a hole for lying in) @deva[श्वा आश्रयार्थी]; *cf.* @deva[छायापस्किरमाणविष्किर] &c. (Up. II. 9).

When the root has its original sense, the Par. is used and @deva[स्] is not inserted: @deva[कुसुमानि अपकिरति स्त्री] 'the woman scatters flowers;' @deva[अपकिरति गजो धूलिम्] ।

@deva[क्रम्][^6]—when not preceded by a preposition is conjugated in both the Padas. But it is used in Atm. by itself when the meaning is 'free movement, energy, development or increase,' &c;. @deva[ऋचि क्रमते बुद्धिः] 'his intellect moves freely in (*i. e* proves very powerful in mastering) the Rigveda;' @deva[क्रममाणोरिसंसदि] 'moving unobstructed in the assembly of the enemy' (Bh. VIII. 22); @deva[अध्ययनाय क्रमते] 'shows ability or energy for studying,' @deva[न रञ्जनाय क्रमते जडानाम्] । Vikra. I. 16 @deva[क्रमन्तेऽस्मिन् शास्त्राणि] 'the Shástras find enlargement in him or are satisfactorily mastered by him.' In the same senses it is Atm. when preceded by @deva[उप] and @deva[परा] only (*i. e.* is Par. if preceded by any other prep.) @deva[उपक्रमते], @deva[पराक्रमते] *cf.* @deva[इत्युक्त्वा खे पराक्रंस्त] (showed his might); @deva[परीक्षितुमुपाक्रंस्त] (made bold) @deva[राक्षसी तस्य विक्रमम्] । Bh. VIII. 22–23; but @deva[संक्रामति] (@deva[शास्त्रेषु बुद्धिः]). When preceded by @deva[आ] it is Atm. in the sense of 'the ascending or the rising of a heavenly body;' @deva[आक्रमते सूर्यः] 'the sun rises;' but @deva[आक्रामति धूमो हर्म्यतलात्] 'the smoke issues forth from the upper terrace.' Also when preceded by @deva[वि], in the sense of 'a graceful movement of the feet, or ascending,' @deva[साधु विक्रमते वाजी] 'the horse moves gracefully;' but @deva[विक्रामति सन्धिः] 'the joint splits,'—and by @deva[प्र] and @deva[उप] in the sense of 'beginning,' @deva[प्रक्रमते], as in @deva[वक्तुं मिथः प्राक्रमतैवमेनम्] । Kum. III. 2. 'thus began to talk with him in private;' but @deva[प्रक्रामति] 'goes,' @deva[उपक्रामति] 'comes near.'

@deva[क्री][^7]—'to buy' is Atm. when the prepositions @deva[अव], @deva[परि] and @deva[वि] are prefixed to it (with @deva[वि] it means 'to sell'), @deva[अवक्रीणीते], @deva[परिक्रीणीते] *cf.* Bh. VIII. 8. @deva[कृतेनोपकृतं वायोः परिक्रीणानमुत्थितम्] ।

@deva[क्रीड्][^8]—'to play' is Atm. after the prepositions @deva[अनु], @deva[आ], @deva[परि] and @deva[सम्], @deva[अनु-आ-परि-सं-क्रीडते], but not when @deva[अनु] governs a noun, as @deva[माणवकमनुक्रीडति] *i. e.* 'plays with Mâṇavaka.' @deva[क्रीड्] with @deva[सम्] is Par. when it means 'to creak as a wheel,' as @deva[संक्रीडति चक्रम्].

@deva[क्षिप्][^9]—'to throw' is Par when preceded by the prepositions @deva[अभि], @deva[प्रति] and @deva[अति]; @deva[अभिक्षिपति] 'throws up,' @deva[अतिक्षिपति] 'throws out,' @deva[प्रतिक्षिपति] 'throws back.'

@deva[क्ष्णु]—with @deva[सम्] is Atm; @deva[संक्ष्णुते शस्त्रं] 'whets or sharpens his weapon;' @deva[उत्कंठां संक्ष्णुते] 'dispels anxiety.'

@deva[गम्][^10]—with @deva[सम्] is Atm. in the sense of 'to be proper, unite with, join'; @deva[वाक्यं संगच्छते], @deva[सखीभिः संगच्छते], &c., but @deva[ग्रामं संगच्छति] goes to a village.' In the Causal it takes the Atm. in the sense of 'having patience or waiting,' @deva[आगमयस्व तावत्] 'have patience first'.

@deva[गृध्]—'to be greedy' is Atm in the Cau. when the sense is 'to deceive,' @deva[माणवकं गर्धयते] 'he deceives Mâṇavaka;' but @deva[श्वानं गर्धयति] 'he makes the dog greedy.'

@deva[गॄ][^11]—with @deva[सम्] is Atm. when the meaning is 'to pledge one's word, to promise, to proclaim;' @deva[संगिरते शब्दं] 'he pledges his word; @deva[शतं संगिरते] 'he promises 100 (Rupees, &c.),' @deva[संगिरते स्वामिनो गुणान्] 'proclaims the merits of his master;' but @deva[संगिरति ग्रासं] 'he swallows down a mouthful.' If it (*i. e* @deva[गॄ] 6 Con.) be preceded by @deva[अव], it is Atm. @deva[अवगिरते शोणितं पिशाचः] 'the fiend drinks blood.'

@deva[चर्][^12]—'to walk' with @deva[उद्] is Atm. when used transitively; @deva[धर्ममुच्चरते] 'he transgresses his duty.' @deva[पानशौण्डाः पयःक्षीबा वृन्दैरुदचरन्त च] । Bhaṭṭ. VIII. 31; but @deva[बाष्पमुच्चरति] 'vapour rises up.' With @deva[सम्] or @deva[समुदा] it is Atm. when used with the instrumental of a vehicle; @deva[रथेन संचरते] 'he moves in a chariot' (See Bh. VIII. 32); @deva[क्वचित्पथा संचरते सुगणां] Rag. XIII. 19. 'now passes through the path of gods;' @deva[रथेन समुदाचरते].

@deva[जन्]—in the Causal takes the Parasm. @deva[जनयति].

@deva[जि][^13]—when preceded by @deva[वि] and @deva[परा] in the sense of 'to conquer,' and 'to defeat' or 'to find unbearable,' respectively, is Atm. @deva[विजयते], @deva[शत्रून् पराजयते], @deva[अध्ययनात् पराजयते] 'gets tired of study;' @deva[खं पराजयमानोसौ] 'filling up the sky, &c' @deva[तां पराजयमानां स प्रीतेः] 'her who was getting disgusted with,' &c. Bhaṭṭ. VIII. 9. 71.

@deva[ज्ञा][^14]—'to know' used intransitively by itself is Atm. @deva[सर्पिषो जानीते] (@deva[सर्पिषा उपायेन प्रवर्तते] Sid. Kau. *i. e.* 'proceeds to perform a sacrifice having obtained ghee for it); with the preposition @deva[अप] it is Atm. in the sense of 'denying;' as @deva[शतं अपजानीते] denies a hundred;' with @deva[प्रति] in the sense of 'acknowledging or promising,' and with @deva[सम्] in the sense of 'expecting,' it is also Atm.; @deva[शतं प्रतिजानीते] 'acknowledges a hundred;' @deva[हरचापारोपणेन कन्यादानं प्रतिजानीते] 'promises the hand of his daughter by (*i. e.* on the condition of) the drawing of Hara's bow;' @deva[शतं संजानीते] 'looks for a hundred;' @deva[मातरि मातुर्वा संजानाति] 'thinks of his mother.' When this is used without a preposition and when the fruit accrues to the agent it takes the Atm. @deva[गां जानीते]; when a preposition is prefixed to it and it is used transitively, it takes the Par. @deva[स्वर्गलोकं न प्रजानाति मूर्खः]. In the Desiderative this root takes the Atm.

@deva[तप्][^15]—'to heat' with @deva[वि] or @deva[उद्], when used intransitively, is

Atm. @deva[उत्तपते] or @deva[वितपते सूर्यः]; when used transitively it is Atm. if it has a limb of the body of the agent for its object; @deva[उत्तपते] or @deva[वितपते पाणिं] 'he warms his hand, but @deva[उत्तपति सुवर्णं सुवर्णकारः] 'a goldsmith heats gold', @deva[चैत्रो मैत्रस्य पाणिमुत्तपति]; when it means 'to practise penance' it is Atm. and is conjugated like a root of the fourth class.

According to some @deva[तप्] with @deva[अनु] is Atm; @deva[अनुतपते] 'repents.'

@deva[दा][^16]—'to give' (3 cl.) by itself takes either pada; but when preceded by @deva[आ] it is Atm. in any other sense than opening (the mouth, &c.); @deva[धनं आदत्ते] 'accepts money;' @deva[विद्यां आदत्ते] 'acquires knowledge;' @deva[नादत्ते भवतां स्नेहेन या पल्लवं] (Śâk.) 'does not pluck your foliage through affection;' but @deva[मुखं व्याददाति] 'opens his mouth;' @deva[विपादिकां व्याददाति वैद्यः] 'the doctor opens the tumour on the root;' @deva[नदीकूलं व्याददाति] 'breaks open the bank of a river;' but if the mouth belongs to another the exception is removed; @deva[व्याददते पतङ्गस्य मुखं पिपीलिकाः] 'ants break open the mouth of a moth' (Mâh. Bhâr.).

@deva[दा]—'to give' (I cl.) when preceded by the preposition @deva[सम्] singly or coupled with any other preposition takes the Atm. provided it is used with the Instrumental in the sense of the Dative; @deva[दास्या संयच्छते] or @deva[संप्रयच्छते] 'gives (something) to the maid-servant;' but @deva[दास्या धनं संप्रयच्छति विप्राय] 'gives wealth to a Brâhmaṇa through his maid-servant'

@deva[दृश्]—'to see preceded by @deva[उप] and used intransitively takes the Atm.; @deva[संपश्यते] 'sees (thinks) well,' this root takes the Atm. in the Desiderative; @deva[दिदृक्षते] 'wishes to see.'

@deva[द्रु]—'to run' is Par. in the Cau.

@deva[नह्]—with @deva[सम्] is Atm. in the sense of 'preparing, being ready for,' @deva[युद्धाय संनह्यते] 'prepares for battle; *cf.* @deva[छेत्तुं वज्रमणीन् शिरीषकुसुमप्रान्तेन संनह्यते] Bha.

@deva[नाथ्][^17]—'to beg or solicit for any thing' is used in the Par. in the sense of 'to hope for', 'to wish well' or 'to give blessings to,' it is exclusively Atm. @deva[सर्पिषो नाथते सर्पिर्मे स्यादित्याशास्ते इत्यर्थः] । Sid. Kau. @deva[मोक्षाय नाथते मुनिः] (Bhop.)

In "@deva[नाथसे किमु पतिं न भूभृताम्]" Kir. XIII. 59. the root is used in the Atmanepada. But Bhaṭṭoji Díkshit supposes that the reading here should be @deva[नाधसे] and not @deva[नाथसे]. Mammata also in his Kâvyaprakâsha finds fault with the Atm. use of this root when critising the verse @deva[दीन त्वामनुनाथते कुचयुगं पत्रावृतं मा कृथाः] &c. and says that @deva[नाथते] should be @deva[नाथति].

@deva[नी][^18]—'to lead or carry' with a preposition or with the prepositions @deva[उद्], @deva[उप] or @deva[वि] is Atm in the following senses.—

(1) @deva[सम्मानन] or 'showing regard for;' @deva[शास्त्रे नयते] 'gives instruction in the conclusions of the Śâstra' (@deva[तेन च शिष्यसम्माननं फलितम्] Sid Kau.), (2) @deva[उत्सञ्जन] or 'raising up;' @deva[दण्डमुन्नयते उत्क्षिपतीत्यर्थ] ; (3) @deva[आचार्यकरण] or 'initiating into sacred rites,' @deva[माणवकं उपनयते विधिना आत्मसमीपं प्रापयतीत्यर्थः । उपनयनपूर्वकेणाध्यापनेन हि उपनेतरि आचार्यत्वं क्रियते] (Sid. Kau.); (4) @deva[ज्ञान] or 'ascertaining the real nature of;' @deva[तत्त्वं नयते निश्चिनोतीत्यर्थ] ; (5) @deva[भृति] or 'employing on wages;' @deva[कर्मकरानुपनयते] 'employs labourers on wages; (6) @deva[विगणन] or 'paying off as a debt, taxes' &c; @deva[करं विनयते राज्ञे देयं भागं परिशोधयतीत्यर्थः], and (7) @deva[व्यय] or 'spending or applying to good use;' @deva[शतं विनयते धर्मार्थं विनियुंक्ते इत्यर्थः] (Sid. Kau.). @deva[नी] with @deva[वि] is Atm. when the object is something other than a limb of the body but exists in the agent; as @deva[कोपं विनयते]; but @deva[गुरोः क्रोधं विनयति शिष्यः]; @deva[गण्डं विनयति] (turns aside).

@deva[नु]—with @deva[आ][^19] is Atm. @deva[आनुते] 'he praises.'

@deva[प्रच्छ्]—with @deva[आ] is Atm. in the sense of 'taking leave of'—"@deva[आपृच्छस्व प्रियसखममुम्]" Meg. 10. 'take leave of this thy dear friend,' also with the preposition @deva[सम्] when used intransitively, @deva[संपृच्छते] 'he ascertains.'

@deva[भुज्][^20]—is Atm. except in the sense of 'protecting,' @deva[ओदनं भुंक्ते] 'eats food,' @deva[बुभुजे पृथिवीपालः पृथिवीमेव केवलाम्] 'the protector of the earth enjoyed (experienced the pleasure from the possession of) the earth alone;' @deva[वृद्धो जनो दुःखशतानि भुंक्ते] 'old people suffer hundreds of miseries;' @deva[महीं भुनक्ति] 'protects the world.'

@deva[मृष्]—with @deva[परि] is Par.; @deva[परिमृष्यति] 'endures;' but @deva[आमृष्यते] 'he touches.'

@deva[यम्][^21]—with @deva[आ] is Atm. either when used intransitively or has a limb of the body of the agent for its object; @deva[आयच्छते तरुः] 'the tree spreads;' @deva[आयच्छते पाणिम्] 'stretches forth the hand;' but @deva[आयच्छति कूपाद्रज्जुं] 'draws up the rope from the well'; preceded by @deva[सम्], @deva[उद्] and @deva[आ] it is Atm. except when it has a literary work for its object; @deva[वस्त्रं आयच्छते] 'puts on a garment;' @deva[भारमुद्यच्छते] 'lifts up a load;' @deva[व्रीहीन् संयच्छते] 'gathers rice;' but @deva[उद्यच्छति वेदम्] 'tries hard to learn the Veda'. @deva[यम्]—with @deva[उप] is Atm. in the sense of 'accepting, or espousing a girl;' @deva[दानं उपयच्छते] 'accepts the gift given;' @deva[उपयच्छते कन्यां] 'marries a girl'; in the Aorist this drops its nasal optionally; @deva[राम सीतां उपायत] (See Uttar. III. 112). @deva[उपायंस्त]; but @deva[परस्य भार्यां उपयच्छति] 'makes another's wife his own.'

@deva[युज्][^22]—preceded by @deva[प्र] or @deva[उप], or generally by a prep. beginning or ending with a vowel, and does not refer to sacrificial vessels, is Atm. @deva[प्रयुंक्ते], @deva[उपयुंक्ते], @deva[प्रयुञ्जानः प्रिया वाचः] (Bh. VIII. 39): but @deva[यज्ञपात्राणि प्रयुनक्ति] 'arranges the sacrificial vessels;' @deva[य इमां आश्रमधर्मे नियुंक्ते] (S'âk.) 'who appoints her to the duties of the hermitage'; @deva[अन्वयुंक्त गुरुमीश्वरः क्षितेः] Rag. XI 62. 'the lord of the earth asked his preceptor;' @deva[पणबन्धमुखान्गुणानजः षडुपायुंक्त] 'Aja employed the six expedients beginning with peace.' Rag. VIII. 21.

@deva[युध्]—in the Cau. takes the Par.

@deva[रम्][^23]—'to sport' changes its Pada when preceded by the prepositions @deva[वि], @deva[आ] and @deva[परि]; @deva[वत्सैतस्माद्विरम] 'cease, oh child, from this' (Uttar. I. 33). @deva[रात्रिरेव व्यरंसीत्] Ibid. I. 27. @deva[आरमति], @deva[विरामोस्त्विति चारमेत्] Manu II 79. @deva[परिरमति]; @deva[क्षणं पर्यरमत्तस्य दर्शनात्] 'was for a moment delighted with his sight.' With @deva[उप] it is Par.; @deva[यज्ञदत्तं उपरमति रमयतीत्यर्थ] (Sid. Kau.) When intransitively used it takes either Pada; @deva[उपरमति-ते] 'sports'; cf. @deva[उपारंसीच्च सम्पश्यन्]; @deva[नात्र सीतेत्युपारंस्त], &c. Bhaṭṭ. VIII. 54. 55.

@deva[ली]—'to melt, to embrace' is Atm. in the cau. in the sense of 'adoring, defeating or deceiving;' @deva[जटाभिर्लापयते] 'is adorable on account of his matted hair;' @deva[दण्डेन लापयते श्वा] 'the dog is defeated by means of a stick;' @deva[श्येनो वर्तिकामुल्लापयते वञ्चयतीत्यर्थः] (overpowers); @deva[मौर्ख्येण लापयते ब्राह्मणः] 'the Brâhmaṇa is deceived by reason of his folly.' @deva[बालमुल्लापयते वञ्चयतीत्यर्थः] ।

@deva[वञ्च्][^24]—in the cau is Atm. in the sense of 'deceiving;' @deva[माणवकं वञ्चयते] 'deceives Mâṇavaka;' but @deva[अहिं वञ्चयति] 'avoids a serpent.'

@deva[वद्][^25]—'to speak' is Atm. in the following senses:—(1) @deva[भासन] or 'showing brightness (proficiency in);' @deva[शास्त्रे वदते] 'is adept in the Sâstra;' (2) @deva[उपसंभाषा] or 'conciliating or coaxing' (generally preceded by @deva[उप]); @deva[भृत्यानुपवदते सांत्वयतीत्यर्थः]; (3) @deva[ज्ञान] or 'knowledge' @deva[शास्त्रे वदते] 'knows the Śâstra;' (4) @deva[यत्न] or 'effort, toil;' @deva[क्षेत्रे वदते] 'labours in the field;' (5) @deva[विमति] or 'disagreement, quarrel,' (generally preceded by @deva[वि] in this sense); @deva[विवदन्ते]; @deva[परस्परं विवदमानानां शास्त्राणाम्] 'of mutually conflicting Śâstras;' and (6) @deva[उपमन्त्रण] or 'coaxing, requesting;' @deva[दातारं उपवदते] 'praises the donour,' &c; with @deva[संप्र] it is Atm. in the sense of 'distinct and loud speech (as that of men gathered together);' @deva[संप्रवदन्ते ब्राह्मणाः] 'the Brâhmaṇas are speaking aloud together;' but @deva[संप्रवदन्ति पक्षिणः]; @deva[वरतनु संप्रवदन्ति कुक्कुटाः] 'oh beautiful one, the cocks are crowing;' with @deva[अनु] it is Atm. when used intrasitively; @deva[अनुवदते कठः कलापस्य] 'the Katha Brâhmaṇa imitates (speaks on the side of or recites like) the Kalâpa Brâhmaṇa;' but @deva[उक्तं अनुवदति] 'reproduces what is said;' @deva[अनुवदति वीणा] 'the lute imitates the notes (indistinctly);' with @deva[विप्र] it is optionally Atm. in the sense of 'disagreeing or disputing;' @deva[विप्रवदन्ति-न्ते वैद्याः] the physicians disagree; with @deva[अप] it is Atm. in the sense of 'reviling, reproaching, refusing,' when the fruit of the action refers to the agent; @deva[अपवदते धनकामो अन्यायं] 'greedy of wealth reviles others unjustly;' so @deva[न्यायमपवदते] 'refuses justice;' but @deva[अपवदति] 'reproaches' (when the result does not refer to the agent); *cf.* @deva[नार्तोप्यपवदेद्विप्रान्] Manu. IV. 236; when the fruit of the action is indicated as referring to the agent the Atm. is optional; @deva[स्वपुत्रं अपवदति-ते वा] (Sid. Kau. on Pan. 1. 3. 77); @deva[वद्] with @deva[उप] in the sense of 'advising or speaking stealthily,' when used transitively, is Atm. @deva[शिष्यं उपवदते] 'gives advice to his pupil;' @deva[परदारान् उपवदते] 'speaks stealthily with another's wife.

@deva[वह्]—is both Par. and Atm. but with @deva[प्र] is restricted to the Par; @deva[प्रवहति].

@deva[विद्][^26]—'to know' (2nd cl.) with @deva[सम्] is Atm. when used intransitively in the sense of 'knowing of, being aware of,' and adds @deva[र्] to @deva[द्] optionally in the 3rd per. plural; @deva[संविदते] or @deva[संविद्रते] 'they know well;' *cf.* @deva[के न संविद्रते वायोर्मैनाकाद्रिर्यथा सखा] Bh. VIII. 17.; 'who do not know that the mountain Maināka is the friend of Vâyu ?;' but @deva[संवित्तः सहयुध्वानौ तच्छक्तिं खरदूषणौ] Bhaṭṭ. V. 37, as it is used transitiveiy. It is also Atm. with @deva[सम्] in the sense of 'recognising;' as @deva[संवित्ते].

@deva[विश्][^27]—with @deva[नि] is Atm. @deva[निविशते]; @deva[किष्किन्धाद्रिं न्यविशत] &c. Bhaṭṭ VI. 143. Also when @deva[अभि] is prefixed to @deva[नि], @deva[अभिनिविशते सन्मार्गं] (Sid. Kau.) 'takes to a good path;' see Bh. VIII. 80.

@deva[शप्][^28]—meaning 'to reproach or to abuse' takes the Atm. even when the fruit of the action does not accrue to the agent; @deva[कृष्णाय शपते] ।

@deva[शिक्ष्][^29]—takes the Atm. in the sense of 'wishing to study;' @deva[धनुषि शिक्षते] 'wishes to study archery.'

@deva[श्रु][^30] 'to hear' with @deva[सम्] is Atm. when used intransitively; @deva[संशृणुते] 'hears well;' @deva[संशृणुष्व कपे] (Bhat. VII. 16.) 'Listen with heed, oh monkey;' *cf.* @deva[हितान्न यः संशृणुते स किं प्रभुः] Kir. 1. 5: but @deva[शब्दं संशृणोति] 'he hears the sound.' This root takes the Atm. in the Desiderative, except when @deva[आ] or @deva[प्रति] precedes it, @deva[शुश्रूषते]; but @deva[आ—प्रति—शुश्रूषति].

@deva[स्था][^31]—with @deva[सम्], @deva[अव], @deva[प्र] and @deva[वि] is Atm., @deva[संतिष्ठते]: @deva[मृदौ परिभवत्रासान्न] @deva[संतिष्ठते] (Mud. 1. 36.) 'fearing an outrage does not abide by one who is mild,' see Mrch. 1. 36. (In the sense of standing still, &c. it is used in the Par. @deva[क्षणं न संतिष्ठति जीवलोकः क्षयोदयाभ्यां परिवर्तमानः] । Hariv.); @deva[क्षणमप्यवतिष्ठते श्वसन् (जन्तुः)] 'if a being remains breathing though only for a moment;' @deva[अनीत्वा पङ्कतां धूलिमुदकं नावतिष्ठते] । Sis. 11. 34. @deva[प्रतिष्ठते] See Ragh. IV. 6; Kum. III. 22. @deva[वितिष्ठते]; @deva[पदैर्भुवं व्याप्य वितिष्ठमानं] Sis. IV. 4; with @deva[आ] it is Atm. in the sense of 'laying down a proposition, asserting solemnly;' @deva[शब्दं नित्यं आतिष्ठते] 'Affirms that sound is eternal;' @deva[जलं विषं वा तव कारणादास्थास्ये] (Mah. Bhâ.) 'for thy sake I will drink water or poison;' in the sense of 'observing' it takes the Par. when used transitively; @deva[विधिमातिष्ठति] 'observes the vow.' @deva[स्था] is Atm. by itself in the sense of 'disclosing one's intention or abiding by;' @deva[गोपी कृष्णाय तिष्ठते, आशयं प्रकाशयति इत्यर्थः] । @deva[संशय्य कर्णादिषु तिष्ठते यः] Kir. III. 14; 'who, when in doubt, takes recourse to Karṇa and others (as the deciders of the matter in hand).' With @deva[उद्] it is Atm. except in the sense of 'getting up or getting by right;' @deva[मुक्तावुत्तिष्ठते] 'earnestly thinks of (aspires to) absolution;' (See Kir. XI. 13. and Sis. XIV. 17); but @deva[पीठादुत्तिष्ठति]; @deva[ग्रामाच्छतमुत्तिष्ठति] 'a hundred is yielded to him by a village (as tax, &c.).' With @deva[उप] it is Atm. in the sense of:—(1) 'worshipping with the recital of holy texts,' @deva[आग्नेय्याग्नीध्रमुपतिष्ठते] 'worships the Âgnidhra fire with the recital of Vedic texts,' @deva[ये सूर्यमुपतिष्ठन्ते मन्त्रैः] Bh. VIII. 13, but @deva[भर्तारमुपतिष्ठति यौवनेन] (or @deva[पतिमुपतिष्ठति नारी] Vop.) where the meaning is 'to approach for intercourse, to serve,' See Bhaṭṭ. V. 68, (2) 'waiting upon, worshipping (a divine being)' @deva[आदित्यमुपतिष्ठते]; (the Atm. use of the root in '@deva[स्तुत्यं स्तुतिभिरर्थ्याभिरुपतस्थे सरस्वती]' Rag. VI. 6. is to be explained, thinks Bhattoji Dîkshita, by the fact that the king is considered to be a divine being), (3) 'uniting or joining; @deva[गङ्गा यमुनामुपतिष्ठते]; (4) 'forming friendship with' @deva[रथिकानुपतिष्ठते मित्रीकरोतीत्यर्थः] (Sid. Kau.); and (5) 'to lead to' @deva[पन्थाः स्रुघ्नं उपतिष्ठते] 'this way leads to Srughna.' When a desire to get something is implied @deva[स्था] with @deva[उप] takes either Pada; @deva[भिक्षुकः प्रभुमुपतिष्ठति-ते] 'a beggar waits on a lord (with desire of getting something).' When it is used intransitively it takes the Atm; @deva[भोजनकाल उपतिष्ठते] 'comes at the dinner time.'

@deva[स्मृ]—is Atm. in the Desiderative; @deva[सुस्मूर्षते].

@deva[स्रु]—is Par. in the causal; @deva[स्रावयति].

@deva[स्वृ]—with @deva[सम्] and @deva[आ] is Atm.; @deva[संस्वरते] 'roars so as to terrify;' @deva[श्रुतं संस्वरिषीष्ठास्त्वं] Bhaṭṭ. IX. 28; @deva[आस्वरते] 'utters a loud sound.'

@deva[हन्][^32]—with @deva[आ] when used intransitively or when it has a limb of the body of the agent for its object is Atm.; @deva[आहते] 'kills or dashes down;' @deva[स्वशिर आहते] 'strikes his own head;' but @deva[परस्य शिर आहन्ति] (Sid. Kau.)

@deva[हृ][^33]—with @deva[अनु] is Atm. in the senses of 'following the habits of, acquiring the natural quality of;' @deva[पैतृकमश्वा अनुहरन्ते] 'horses always follow the gait of their progenitors;' so @deva[मातरं गावः अनुहरन्ते]; in the sense of 'acquiring a new quality by imitation' it takes the Par. @deva[पितरमनुहरति] 'imitates his father.'

@deva[ह्वे][^34]—@deva[उप], preceded by @deva[उप], @deva[नि], @deva[वि], and @deva[सम्] and used intransitively is Atm. @deva[उप—नि—वि—सं—ह्वयते]; with @deva[आ] it is Atm. in the sense of 'challenging;' @deva[कृष्णश्चाणूरमाह्वयते] 'Krishṇa challenges Châṇura to battle;' @deva[आह्वत चेदिराण्मुरारिं] Sis. XXI. 1; but @deva[पुत्रमाह्वयति].
