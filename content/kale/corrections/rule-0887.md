## COMPARATIVE AND SUPERLATIVE DEGREES.

Adjectives in the Comparative degree are used with the Ablative; @deva[वर्धनाद्रक्षणं श्रेयः] protection (of one's subjects) is better than aggrandizement. @deva[अर्जुनाद्युधिष्ठिरो ज्यायान्] Udhisthira was older than Arjuna.

(a) Sometimes the comparative is used with the Instrumental; @deva[प्राणैः प्रियतरः] dearer than life. See also @ref[863] *Note*.
