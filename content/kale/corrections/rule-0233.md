## 5 Gati.

Compounds of the following words with verbal indeclinables are called Gati.

**(a)**[^1] The particles @deva[ऊरी], @deva[उररी], @deva[वौषट्], @deva[वषट्], @deva[स्वाहा], @deva[स्वधा], @deva[प्रादुः], @deva[आविस्] and @deva[श्रत्] and the word @deva[कारिका] meaning an action; as @deva[ऊरीकृत्य], @deva[उररीकृत्य] 'having accepted; @deva[वषट्कृत्य] 'having uttered the word *Vashaṭ*;' @deva[प्रादुर्भूय], @deva[कारिकाकृत्य] 'having done the act.'

**(b)**[^2] Onomatopoetic words not followed by @deva[इति], as @deva[खाट्कृत्य]; but @deva[खाडिति कृत्वा] (@deva[निरटीवत्]).

**(c)**[^3] The words @deva[सत्] and @deva[असत्] showing respect and disrespect respectively, the prepositions @deva[अलं] in the sense of 'adorning,' @deva[पुरः], @deva[अदः], @deva[अन्तः], @deva[कणे], @deva[मनः], @deva[अस्तं], @deva[अच्छ], and @deva[तिरः]; as @deva[अलंकृत्य] 'having adorned', but @deva[अलं कृत्वा] doing enough (@deva[पर्याप्तमित्यर्थः] Sid. Kau.); @deva[पुरस्कृत्य] 'placing in the front'; @deva[अदःकृत्य] (@deva[अदःकृतम्]) @deva[अन्तर्हत्य] (@deva[मध्ये हत्वा] Sid. Kau.); @deva[कणेहत्य], as @deva[कणेहत्य पयः पिबति] 'he drinks milk to his heart's content'; @deva[अच्छोद्य] 'having gone up to and spoken' (@deva[अभिमुखं गत्वा उक्त्वा च इत्यर्थः] Sid. Kau.); @deva[तिरोभूय], @deva[मनोहत्य] (till disgust is produced); @deva[अस्तंगत्य], @deva[अच्छगत्य] 'going towards.'

**(d)**[^4] The words @deva[हस्ते], @deva[पाणौ] and @deva[प्राध्वं]; as @deva[हस्तेकृत्य], @deva[पाणौकृत्य] 'having married'; @deva[प्राध्वंकृत्य] 'having forced to yield.'

**(e)**[^5] The particles @deva[उपाजे], @deva[अन्वाजे], @deva[साक्षात्], @deva[मिथ्या], @deva[अमा], @deva[प्रादुः], @deva[आविः] and @deva[नमस्], the words @deva[मनसि], @deva[उरसि] (except when there is the sense of juxtaposition or extreme contact), @deva[मध्ये], and the word @deva[पदे] form compounds with verbal indeclinables optionally; @deva[उपाजेकृत्य], @deva[उपाजे कृत्वा], @deva[अन्वाजेकृत्य] @deva[अन्वाजे कृत्वा] 'giving strength to the weak' [@deva[दुर्बलस्य बलमाधाय इत्यर्थः] Sid. Kau.]; @deva[साक्षात्कृत्य-त्वा], @deva[लवणंकृत्य-त्वा], @deva[उरसिकृत्य-त्वा] (having accepted) @deva[मनसिकृत्य कृत्वा]; but @deva[उरसि कृत्वा पाणिं शेते]; @deva[मध्येकृत्य-त्वा], @deva[पदेकृत्य], @deva[पदे कृत्वा], &c.

**(f)** These compounds are also formed with nouns derived from roots for their second members; e. g. @deva[अस्तमयः] 'setting of the sun,' @deva[पुरस्कारः] 'reception, showing respect,' @deva[तिरस्कारः], @deva[सत्कारः], @deva[अलंकृतिः], &c.
