---
key: rule:312
title: "Insertion of @deva[आन्] Before @deva[ई]"
pages: [185.png, 186.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] The words @deva[इन्द्र], @deva[वरुण], @deva[भव], @deva[शर्व], @deva[रुद्र], @deva[मृड], and @deva[आचार्य]. @deva[हिम] and @deva[अरण्य] both implying 'vastness'; @deva[यव] meaning 'bad or spoiled barley', @deva[यवन] when the *feminine* denotes the alphabet of the Yavanas, @deva[मातुल] and @deva[आचार्य] form their *fem* in @deva[ई] but insert @deva[आन्] before this @deva[ई] *e. g.* @deva[इन्द्राणी] 'the wife of Indra;' @deva[वरुणानी] 'the wife of Varuna', &c.; @deva[हिमानी] 'a vast sheet of ice,' @deva[अरण्यानी] 'an immense expanse of forests,' @deva[दुष्टो यवो यवानी], @deva[यवनानां लिपिर्यवनानी], but @deva[यवनी] 'the wife of a Yavana or Yavana woman;' @deva[आचार्यानी][^2] ( and not @deva[णी] ) 'the wife of an Achârya or a holy teacher'; but @deva[आचार्या] 'a woman who teaches; a spiritual preceptress'.

**(a)**[^3] The words @deva[मातुल] and @deva[उपाध्याय] insert this @deva[आन्] optionally; @deva[मातुलानी], @deva[मातुली]; @deva[उपाध्यायानी], @deva[उपाध्यायी] 'the wife of a preceptor,' but @deva[उपाध्यायी] or @deva[उपाध्याया] 'a female preceptor', 'a woman who herself performs the work of an @deva[उपाध्याय]'; in the case of @deva[अर्य] and @deva[क्षत्रिय], @deva[आन्] is optionally inserted before the @deva[ई], when no wife is meant; @deva[अर्या—अर्याणी], 'a mistress or a woman of the Vaisya or trader class', @deva[क्षत्रिया], @deva[क्षत्रियाणी] 'a woman of the Kshatriya or warrior class', @deva[अर्यी] 'the wife of a Vaishya'; @deva[क्षत्रियी] 'the wife of a Kshatriya.'

[^1]: @deva[इन्द्रवरुणभवशर्वरुद्रमृडहिमारण्ययवयवनमातुलाचार्याणामानुक्] । Pān. IV. 1. 49, @deva[हिमारण्ययोर्महत्वे] । @deva[यवाद्दोषे] । @deva[यवनाल्लिप्याम्] । Vârtikas.

[^2]: @deva[आचार्यादणत्वं च] । Vârtika.

[^3]: @deva[मातुलोपाध्याययोरानुक् वा] । @deva[या तु स्वयमेवाध्यापिका तत्र वा वाच्यः] । @deva[अर्यक्षत्रियाभ्यां वा स्वार्थे] । Vârtikas.
