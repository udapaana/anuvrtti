## PRESENT PARTICIPLES.

The present participle is to be used when contemporaneity of action is to be indicated. It is often idiomatically used to express the sense of 'while' or 'whilst' in English; @deva[अरण्ये चरन्] while wandering in the forest; @deva[विवाहकौतुकं बिभ्रत एव] while he yet wore the marriage string.
