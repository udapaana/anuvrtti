## Light and Heavy Syllables

A syllable is @deva[लघु] 'light,' or @deva[गुरु] 'heavy' according as its vowel is short or long.

(a) The vowels @deva[अ], @deva[इ], @deva[उ], @deva[ऋ] and @deva[लृ] are short; and the vowels @deva[आ], @deva[ई], @deva[ऊ], @deva[ॠ], @deva[ए], @deva[ऐ], @deva[ओ] and @deva[औ] are long.[^1] When a short vowel is followed by an Anuswāra or Visarga or by a conjunct consonant, it is converted into what is called 'a prosodially long vowel;' as @deva[गन्ध], @deva[अच्छ], &c.

[^1]: @deva[सानुस्वारश्च दीर्घश्च विसर्गी च गुरुर्भवेत् ।]
