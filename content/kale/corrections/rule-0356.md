title: "Neuter Fruits and Numerals"
pages: [221.png]

Words denoting fruits, and all numerals from @deva[शत] upwards, except @deva[शङ्कु] *mas.*, @deva[लक्ष] which is also *fem.* and @deva[कोटि] *fem.*; @deva[आम्रं], @deva[आमलकं], &c. @deva[शतं], @deva[सहस्रं] &c.
