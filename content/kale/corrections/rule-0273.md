---
key: rule:273
title: "@deva[परि] Compounds for Loss in Games"
pages: [165.png]
verdict: approved
---
The words @deva[अक्ष], and @deva[शलाका] and a numeral, are compounded with @deva[परि] and placed first to express 'loss in a game';[^1] @deva[अक्षेण विपरीतं वृत्तं अक्षपरि], 'so as to be a loser by an unlucky throw of dice'; @deva[शलाकापरि] 'an unlucky throw or movement of one of the pieces at a game played with Śalākās'; @deva[एकपरि] 'loss of one throw of dice', &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[अक्षशलाकासंख्याः परिणा] । Pāṇ. II. 1. 10. @deva[द्यूतव्यवहारे पराजये एवायं समासः] । Sid. Kau.
