title: "Feminine @deva[ई] for Wife of Male"

[^1] The *feminine* of nouns denoting 'a male' is formed by adding @deva[ई] when the wife of that male is meant; @deva[गोपस्य स्त्री गोपी]; @deva[शूद्री] ( sometimes @deva[शूद्राणी] also ) 'wife of a Śudra'.

**(a)** but not of nouns ending in @deva[पालक]; as @deva[गोपालिका] 'the wife of a cowherd' ( but @deva[गोपाल] has @deva[गोपाली] ), @deva[अश्वपालिका] 'the wife of a horse-groom'.

**(b)** @deva[सूर्य] has @deva[सूर्या] 'the divine wife of Śurya;' but @deva[सूरी] *i. e.* @deva[कुन्ती] who was a mortal
