**(c) The Infinitive.**

---

The Infinitive is formed by the affix @deva[तुम्] with the same effect as the @deva[ता] of the Future; e. g.

| Roots | Infinitive | Roots | Infinitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| @deva[इ] to go | @deva[एतुम्] | @deva[ग्रन्थ्] to compose | @deva[ग्रन्थितुम्] |
| @deva[एध्] to grow | @deva[एधितुम्] | @deva[पच्] to cook | @deva[पक्तुम्] |
| @deva[दा] to give | @deva[दातुम्] | @deva[व्रश्च्] to cut | @deva[व्रश्चितुम्] or @deva[व्रष्टुम्] |
| @deva[नी] to carry | @deva[नेतुम्] | @deva[गुह्] to cover | @deva[गूहितुम्] or @deva[गोढुम्] |
| @deva[कृ] to do | @deva[कर्तुम्] | @deva[सह्] to bear | @deva[सहितुम्] or @deva[सोढुम्] |
| @deva[भू] to be | @deva[भवितुम्] | @deva[चुर्] to steal | @deva[चोरयितुम्] |
| @deva[धू] to shake | @deva[धवितुम्] or @deva[धोतुम्] | @deva[बुध्] Cau. | @deva[बोधयितुम्] |
| @deva[वृ] to choose | @deva[वरितुम्] or @deva[वरीतुम्] | Desi. | @deva[बुबोधिषितुम्] |
| @deva[गै] to sing | @deva[गातुम्] | Ātm. Freq. | @deva[बोबुधितुम्] |
| @deva[गम्] to go | @deva[गन्तुम्] | &c. | &c. |
