## Causals with @deva[य्] Augment

@deva[शो] 'to pare, to sharpen,' @deva[छो] 'to cut,' @deva[सो] 'to finish,' @deva[ह्वे] 'to call,' @deva[व्ये] 'to cover,' @deva[वे] 'to weave,' @deva[सै] 'to waste away,' and @deva[पा] 'to drink' insert @deva[य्] instead of @deva[प्], @deva[शाययति], @deva[साययति], @deva[वाययति], @deva[पाययति], &c.

(a) @deva[पा] 'to protect' and @deva[वे] when it means 'to shake,' insert @deva[ल्] and @deva[ज्] respectively before @deva[अय]; @deva[पालयति] 'he protects,' @deva[वाजयति] 'he shakes.'
