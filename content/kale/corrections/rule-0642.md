## Vowel Changes Before @deva[य] in Nominal Verbs

Before this @deva[य],

(1) final @deva[अ] and @deva[आ] are changed to @deva[ई]; @deva[पुत्रं आत्मनः इच्छति]—@deva[पुत्रीयति] (@deva[पुत्र]+@deva[य]=@deva[पुत्री]+@deva[य]+@deva[ति]) 'he wishes for a son.'

(2) final @deva[इ] and @deva[उ] are lengthened; @deva[कवि]—@deva[कवीयति] 'he wishes for a poet.'

(3) final @deva[ऋ] is changed to @deva[री]; @deva[कर्तृ]—@deva[कर्त्रीयति].

(4) final @deva[ओ] and @deva[औ] are changed to @deva[अव्] and @deva[आव्]; @deva[गो]—@deva[गव्यति]; @deva[नौ]—@deva[नाव्यति].

(5) a final nasal is dropped and the preceding vowel is changed like an original final vowel; @deva[राजन्]—@deva[राजीयति] 'he wishes for a king.'

(6) in other cases the final consonant remains unchanged; @deva[वाच्]—@deva[वाच्यति] 'he wishes for words,' @deva[दिव्]—@deva[दिव्यति] (@deva[दीव्यति] according to some) 'he wishes for heaven,' @deva[समिध्]—@deva[समिध्यति] 'he wishes for holy sticks,' &c.

(7) The Tad. affix expressing 'a descendant of' is dropped and then the changes mentioned above take place; @deva[गार्ग्य] (the son of @deva[गर्ग]) @deva[आत्मनः इच्छति] @deva[गार्गीयति] (@deva[गार्ग्य]+@deva[य]+@deva[ति]=@deva[गार्ग]+@deva[य]+@deva[ति]=@deva[गार्गी]+@deva[य]+@deva[ति]), &c.
