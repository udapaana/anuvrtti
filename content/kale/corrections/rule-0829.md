## Dative with @deva[नमः], @deva[स्वस्ति], @deva[स्वाहा], @deva[स्वधा], @deva[अलं], @deva[वषट्]

The particles[^1] @deva[नमः], @deva[स्वस्ति], @deva[स्वाहा] and @deva[स्वधा] (exclamations used in offering oblations to gods and manes respectively), @deva[अलं] meaning 'equal to, a match for' and @deva[वषट्] (an exclamation accompanying an oblation to a deity) govern the Dative case; @deva[तस्मै नमः शंभवे] 'bow to that Śambhu;' @deva[प्रजाभ्यः स्वस्ति] 'may it be well with the subjects;' @deva[स्वस्त्यस्तु ते] (Rag. V. 17.) 'Farewell to thee;' @deva[अग्नये स्वाहा] 'this offering to Agni,' similarly @deva[पितृभ्यः स्वधा]; @deva[दैत्योभ्यो हरिः अलं] 'Hari is a match for the demons;' so @deva[अलं मल्लो मल्लाय] (Mah. Bhās.) 'one wrestler is a match for another; (see Rag. II 39. Bhatti. VIII. 98.); @deva[इन्द्राय वषट्] 'this oblation to Indra.'

(a) But when @deva[कृ] is used with @deva[नमः], it becomes the principal verb [^2] and therefore governs the Accusative case; @deva[नमस्करोति देवान्] 'bows down to gods' (when the sense of an infinitive is suppressed, of course the Dative will be used; see @ref[828]).

(b) Words having the sense of @deva[अलं], such as @deva[प्रभुः], @deva[समर्थः], @deva[शक्तः], &c. and also the verb @deva[प्रभू], are used with the Dative (Sid Kau.) @deva[दैत्योभ्यो हरिः प्रभुः समर्थः शक्तो वा]; @deva[प्रभुः—समर्थः—शक्तः मल्लो मल्लाय]; @deva[प्रभवति मल्लो मल्लाय]; @deva[विधिरपि न येभ्यः प्रभवति] (Bhar. II. 94.); @deva[प्रभु] and the other words may also be used with the Genitive (Sid. Kau.) @deva[प्रभवति निजस्य कन्यकाजनस्य महाराजः] (Māl. Mād. IV) the great king has power over his daughter.

(c) Verbs meaning 'to salute' such as @deva[प्रणम्], @deva[प्रणपत्], &c. may govern the Dative or the Accusative; @deva[न प्रणमन्ति देवताभ्यः] (Kād.) they do not bow down to deities; @deva[तां भक्तिप्रवणेन चेतसा प्रणनाम] saluted her with a mind bowed down with devotion; @deva[प्रणिपत्य सुरास्तस्मै शमयित्रे सुरद्विषां] (Rag. X. 15.) the gods bowed respectfully to him, the annihilator of the enemies of the immortals; @deva[वागीशं] (@deva[वाग्भिरर्थ्याभिः]) @deva[प्रणिपत्य] (Kum. II. 3.) having bowed to the lord of speech &c.
