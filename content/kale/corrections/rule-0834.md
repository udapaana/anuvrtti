## Ablative with Fear and Protection

In the case of words expressing fear or protection from fear,[^1] that from which or whom the fear proceeds is put in the Ablative case; @deva[चोराद्बिभेति] is afraid of a thief; @deva[भीतो रणे श्वेतवाहात्] I was afraid of the white-horsed one i. e. Arjuna; @deva[स्वल्पमप्यस्य धर्मस्य त्रायते महतो भयाद्] (Bhag. II. 40) even a little of this (kind of) piety saves one from great fear; @deva[कपेरत्रासिषुर्नादात्] (Bhatt. IX. 11.) were afraid of the roar of the monkey.

(a) [^2] That from which one is kept off is also put in the Ablative; @deva[पापान्निवारयति] wards off from sin; @deva[यवेभ्यो गां वारयति] keeps off the cow from barley.

---

[^1]: @deva[भीत्रार्थानां भयहेतुः] Pāṇ. I. 4. 25.
[^2]: @deva[वारणार्थानामीप्सितः ।] Pāṇ. I. 4. 27.
