[^1]When there is the idea of hope implied in a conditional form, the Aorist, the Present or the Simple Future may be used in both the clauses to denote a future time; @deva[देवश्चेदवर्षीत् वर्षति वर्षिष्यति वा धान्यमवाप्स्म वपामः वप्स्यामः वा।] (Sid. Kau). if it were to rain we would sow corn.
