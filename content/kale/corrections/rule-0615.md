## Desiderative Forms Without Desiderative Sense

There are a few primitive roots, which, though they take a Desiderative form, do not convey a desiderative sense (See @ref[396]). As these are looked upon as primitive roots, new Desiderative bases may be derived from them according to the rules given below; as @deva[जुगुप्सिषते] 'he wishes to censure' from @deva[जुगुप्स्] &c.
