---
key: rule:322
title: "Feminine of @deva[ऊरु] Compounds"
pages: [189.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] If a compound has for its last member @deva[ऊरु] and for its first, words signifying a standard of comparison, or one of the words @deva[संहित], @deva[शफ], @deva[लक्षण], @deva[वाम], @deva[सहित] and @deva[सह], it forms its *feminine* in @deva[ऊ]; @deva[रम्भोरूः] @deva[रम्भे इव ऊरू यस्याः] plantain-thighed; @deva[करभोरूः] having thighs ( beautifully tapering ) like the fore-arm or the trunk of an elephant; @deva[संहितोरूः] having well-turned thighs; @deva[शफौ खुरौ ताविव संश्लिष्टत्वादूरू यस्याः सा शफोरूः]; [ @deva[हितेन सह सहितौ ऊरू यस्याः सा] ] @deva[सहितोरूः], [ @deva[सहेते इति सहौ ऊरू यस्याः सा] ] @deva[सहोरूः] 'a woman whose thighs are capable of enduring great fatigue or pain'; or 'one having excellent thighs.'

---

[^1]: @deva[ऊरूत्तरपदादौपम्ये] । @deva[संहितशफलक्षणवामादेश्च] । Pân. IV. 1. 69. 70. @deva[सहितसहाभ्यां चेति वक्तव्यम्] । Vârtika.
