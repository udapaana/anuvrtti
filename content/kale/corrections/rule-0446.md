---
key: rule:446
title: "Aspirates Become Unaspirates in Reduplicative Syllable"
pages: [282.png]
verdict: approved
---
A radical aspirate (2nd or 4th letter of a class) is changed to its corresponding unaspirate in the reduplicative syllable; as @deva[छिद्]–@deva[चिच्छिद्], @deva[धु]–@deva[दुधु], @deva[भुज्]–@deva[बुभुज्], &c.
