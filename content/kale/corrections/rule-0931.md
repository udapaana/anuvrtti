[^1]The Aorist ought to be used when the idea of the continuousness or nearness of an action is to be implied; @deva[यावज्जीवमन्नमदात्] (Sid. Kau.) gave food throughout his life; @deva[येयं पौर्णमातिक्रान्ता तस्यामग्नीनाधित सोमेनायष्ट] (Sid. Kau) he consecrated the fire on the last Purṇimā day (the full-moon day) and offered Soma, &c.
