## Treatment of Final Syllable in a Pāda

The last syllable of a pāda is either heavy or light according as the exigence of the metre requires it, whatever be its natural length; [^1]as in @deva[वक्षस्थली रक्षतु सा जगन्ति], &c. (Vik. I.); @deva[तस्याः खुरन्यासपवित्रपांसुम्] (Rag. II. 2.)

[^1]: Vide Vṛttaratnākara I. 9. @deva[वर्णः संयोगपूर्वश्च तथा पादान्तगोपि वा ॥] Chhandomañjarī.
