@deva[जॄ] takes this affix with the negative particle prefixed to it and forms @deva[अजर्य] 'what cannot grow old' but this must qualify @deva[संगतं] 'friendship;' cf. @deva[तेन संगतमार्येण रामाचर्यं कुरु द्रुतम् ।] Bhaṭṭi. VI. 53. In @deva[मृगैरजर्यं जरसोपदिष्टमदेहबन्धाय पुनर्बबन्ध ।] Rag. XVIII. 7, the word @deva[संगत] must be considered as understood. If it does not qualify @deva[संगत], it will take the affix @deva[तृ]; as @deva[अजरिता कम्बल].
