@deva[अहन्] n. meaning 'a day' changes its final to @deva[र्] (and finally to *visarga*) when followed by consonantal terminations or by nothing; in other cases it is declined like @deva[नामन्].

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. V. A. | @deva[अहः] | @deva[अह्नी], @deva[अहनी] | @deva[अहानि] |
| I. | @deva[अह्ना] | @deva[अहोभ्याम्] | @deva[अहोभिः] |
| D. | @deva[अह्ने] | ,, | @deva[अहोभ्यः] |
| Ab. | @deva[अह्नः] | ,, | ,, |
| G. | @deva[अह्नः] | @deva[अह्नोः] | @deva[अह्नाम्] |
| L. | @deva[अह्नि-हनि] | ,, | @deva[अहस्सु-ःसु] |

Obs :—@deva[दीर्घाहन्] when mas. is declined like @deva[चन्द्रमस्] when it takes the consonantal terminations and like @deva[राजन्] when it takes the vowel terminations; when neu. it is declined like @deva[अहन्].
