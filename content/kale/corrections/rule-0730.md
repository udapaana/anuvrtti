title: "River Names and Constellation Names with @deva[क्यप्]"
pages: [435.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "सिध्यः/सिध् in (b): the print shows ि and a conjunct that may be ष्य (सिष्य); written from the root सिध्"
---
(a) The following two words which are the names of two rivers are derived by means of this affix; @deva[भिनत्ति कूलं भिद्यः] from @deva[भिद्+क्यप्]; @deva[उज्झत्युदकं उज्झ्यः], @deva[उज्झ्+क्यप्]; see Rag. XI. 8. In other cases these roots take the affix @deva[तृ]; @deva[भेत्ता], @deva[उज्झिता].

(b) Similarly the words @deva[पुष्यः] and @deva[सिध्यः]; both the names of the constellation Pushya, are derived from @deva[पुष्] and @deva[सिध्] respectively with the affix @deva[क्यप्]; @deva[पुष्यन्त्यस्मिन्नर्थाः पुष्यः], @deva[सिध्यन्त्यस्मिन् सिध्यः].
