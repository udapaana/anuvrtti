## Relative Pronoun यत् Introducing a Clause

The relative pronoun @deva[यत्] neu. sing. is used like the English 'that' to introduce a clause, the gender of the demonstrative pronoun being the same as that of the antecedent noun; @deva[यद्विद्वान् अपि नरः अन्यान् विगणयति स धनमद एव] it is the pride of wealth that even a learned man slights another; @deva[सत्योयं जनप्रवादः यत्संपत्संपदमनुबध्नातीति] it is a true saying that one good fortune follows another.

*Obs*:—Sometimes the antecedent noun or pronoun is omitted and has to be inferred from the gender and number of the relative; e. g. @deva[धनेन किं यो न ददाति याचके] i. e. @deva[तस्य धनेन], &c. what is the use of wealth to him who does not bestow it on mendicants, &c.
