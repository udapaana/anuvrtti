## Prefixing Augment @deva[इ] to Desiderative @deva[स्]

To this @deva[स्] the augment @deva[इ] is to be prefixed after Seṭ roots, optionally after Veṭ ones and not after those that are Aniṭ, subject to the following exceptions:—

(1) Roots ending in @deva[उ] or @deva[ऊ], @deva[ऋ] and @deva[ऌ] short, and the roots @deva[ग्रह्] and @deva[गुह्] do not take @deva[इ]; @deva[नु]—@deva[नुनूष्]; Vide @ref[618]. (*d*); @deva[भू]—@deva[बुभूष्], &c.

Exceptions:—@deva[ऋ] 'to go,' @deva[दृ] A. 'to respect,' @deva[धृ] 6 A. 'to hold,' and @deva[पू] A. 'to purify,' admit of @deva[इ] (see also 4 below)

(2) @deva[स्मि], @deva[अञ्ज्], @deva[प्रच्छ्], @deva[अश्] take @deva[इ] necessarily.

(3) @deva[वृत्], @deva[वृध्], @deva[शृध्], @deva[स्यन्द्] and @deva[क्लृप्] do not admit of @deva[इ] in the Par. (see @ref[484]) In the Atm. they admit @deva[इ], the last two do so optionally; @deva[वृत्]—@deva[विवृत्सति], @deva[विवर्तिषते], &c.

(4) Roots ending in long @deva[ॠ] and @deva[इव्] and the roots @deva[दरिद्रा], @deva[श्रि], @deva[ऊर्णु], @deva[यु], @deva[भृ], @deva[वृ], @deva[स्तृ], @deva[ऋध्] 'to prosper,' @deva[दम्भ्], @deva[भ्रस्ज्], @deva[ज्ञप्] (i. e. @deva[ज्ञप्] of the 10 cl. and optional Cau. Base of @deva[ज्ञा]), @deva[सन्] 'to give,' @deva[तन्], @deva[पत्], @deva[कृत्], @deva[चृत्], @deva[छृद्], @deva[तृद्], and @deva[नृत्] (Vide @ref[485].) take @deva[इ] optionally.

Exceptions:—@deva[कॄ] 'to scatter,' @deva[गॄ] 'to swallow,' take @deva[इ] necessarily. The intermediate @deva[इ] is not lengthened in the case of these roots; @deva[चिकरिष्], &c.

(5) The roots @deva[क्रम्], @deva[गम्] and @deva[स्नु] take @deva[इ] in the Par. and reject it in the Atm.
