## Locative with @deva[साधु] and @deva[निपुण] (adoration sense)

[^1] The words @deva[साधु] and @deva[निपुण], not preceded by the prepositions @deva[अनु], @deva[परि] and @deva[प्रति], are used with the Loc. when the sense of adoration is to be conveyed; @deva[मातरि साधुर्निपुणो वा] reverentially disposed towards his mother; but @deva[निपुणः राज्ञः भृत्यः] a clever servant of the king. When preceded by the prepositions @deva[अनु], @deva[परि] and @deva[प्रति] these are used with the Accusative; @deva[साधुर्निपुणो वा मातरं प्रति पर्यनु वा].

---

[^1]: @deva[साधुनिपुणाभ्यामर्चायां सप्तम्यप्रतेः ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 43. @deva[अप्रत्यादिभिरिति वक्तव्यम् ।] Vārt.
