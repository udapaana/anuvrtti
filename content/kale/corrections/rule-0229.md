title: "Madhyamapadālopi: Dropping Middle Member"
pages: [141.png]
verdict: approved
---
In some Karmadhāraya compounds the last member of the first word which is itself a compound word is dropped;[^1] e. g. @deva[शाकप्रियः पार्थिवः शाकपार्थिवः] 'a king who likes vegetables', @deva[देवपूजको ब्राह्मणः देवब्राह्मणः] &c. These compounds, though they should be properly called "*Uttamapadalopi*" are popularly designated "*Madhyamapadalopi*". Any compound requiring a similar explanation is classed under this group (@deva[शाकपार्थिवादि]) which is an @deva[आकृतिगण].

---

[^1]: @deva[शाकपार्थिवादीनां सिद्धये उत्तरपदलोपस्योपसंख्यानम् ।] Vārt on Pāṇ. II. 1. 60.
