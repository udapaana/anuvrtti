# DECLENSION OF THE NUMERALS (CARDINALS AND ORDINALS)

@deva[एक] (@deva[एका] fem.), @deva[द्वि] (@deva[द्वा] fem.), @deva[त्रि] (@deva[तिसृ][^1] fem.), and @deva[चतुर्] (@deva[चतसृ] fem.), are all adjectives and takes the gender, number and case of the nouns they qualify.

---

[^1]: @deva[त्रिचतुरोः स्त्रियां तिसृचतसृ ।] Pāṇ. VII. 2. 99.
