## IV. CONJUNCTIONS

§ 376. The following are the principal conjunctions in Sanskrit:—

(a) Copulative @deva[अथ], @deva[अथो], @deva[उत], @deva[च], @deva[किंच], &c.

(b) Disjunctive @deva[वा], @deva[वा]...@deva[वा], &c.

(c) Adversative @deva[अथवा], @deva[तु], @deva[किंतु], @deva[किंवा], &c.

(d) Conditional @deva[चेत्], @deva[यदि], @deva[यदापि], @deva[नेत्], @deva[नोचेत्], @deva[वेट्] (used in sacrificial ceremonies), &c.

(e) Causal @deva[हि], @deva[तत्], @deva[तेन], &c.

(f) Interrogative @deva[आहो] @deva[अहोस्वित्], @deva[उत], @deva[उताहो], @deva[किं], @deva[किन्नु], @deva[किमुत], @deva[किंस्वित्], @deva[ननु], @deva[नवा], @deva[नु], &c.

(g) Affirmatives and negatives @deva[अङ्ग], @deva[अथ किम्], @deva[आम्], @deva[अद्धा] &c.

(h) Conjunctions of time @deva[यावत्]—@deva[तावत्], @deva[यदा], @deva[तदा] &c.

(i) @deva[अथ] and @deva[इति] are used to mark the beginning and the close of a work respectively.
