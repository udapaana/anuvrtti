**By means of the affix @deva[क्यप्] (@deva[य])**

The roots @deva[इ] 1. 2. P. 'to go,' @deva[स्तु], @deva[शास्], @deva[वृ] 5. P. A., @deva[दृ], @deva[जुष्], and roots having @deva[ऋ] short for their penultimate, except @deva[कॢप्] and @deva[चृत्], take the affix @deva[क्यप्] (@deva[य]) in the same sense as @deva[यत्]. When a root ends in a short vowel, @deva[त्] is inserted between the final vowel and the affix @deva[य]; e. g. @deva[इ]—@deva[इत्य] 'worthy of being approached;' @deva[स्तुत्य] 'deserving praise;' @deva[शास्]—@deva[शिष्य] 'fit to be instructed;' @deva[वृत्]—@deva[वृत्य]; @deva[दृ]—@deva[आदृत्य]; @deva[जुष्]—@deva[जुष्य] 'worthy of being served;' @deva[वृत्]—@deva[वृत्य]; @deva[वृध्]—@deva[वृध्य] 'fit to be increased as wealth, &c.' but @deva[कल्प्य] (@deva[कॢप्+यत्]) 'able;' @deva[चृत्]—@deva[चर्त्य] (@deva[चृत्+ण्यत्]) 'that ought to be teased or hurt.'
