title: "Second Person Singular Imperative Termination @deva[धि]"
pages: [262.png]
verdict: approved
---
(1)[^1] The second person sing. termination of the Parasm. Imperative is @deva[धि] when the base ends in any consonant except a nasal or a semivowel; also in the case of the root @deva[हु] 3 P. 'to sacrifice.'

[^1]: @deva[हुझल्भ्यो हेर्धिः ।] Pāṇ. VI. 4 101
