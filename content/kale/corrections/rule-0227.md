---
key: rule:227
title: "Compounds with @deva[तव्य], @deva[अनीय], @deva[य], @deva[तुल्य]"
pages: [140.png]
verdict: approved
---
Words ending in the terminations @deva[तव्य], @deva[अनीय], @deva[य] and @deva[तुल्य] and words having a similar meaning, may be compounded with any subanta not importing a particular kind;[^1] e. g. @deva[भोज्योष्णम्] 'any hot eatable,' @deva[तुल्यश्वेतः] 'of a similar white colour,' @deva[सदृशश्वेतः], &c.; but @deva[भोज्यः ओदनः] as the latter word refers to a kind of food here.

---

[^1]: @deva[कृत्यतुल्याख्या अजात्या ।] Pāṇ. II. 1. 68.
