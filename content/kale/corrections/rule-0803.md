## Root @deva[वस्] with Prepositions Governs Accusative

The root @deva[वस्][^1] preceded by the prepositions @deva[उप], @deva[अनु], @deva[अधि] and @deva[आ] governs the Accusative case; @deva[उप-अनु-अधि-आ-वसति वैकुंठं हरिः]. Hari dwells in Vaikuntha; @deva[शून्यमन्ववसद्वनं] he dwelt in a dreary forest; but @deva[वस्] with @deva[उप] meaning 'to abstain from food' is used with the Loc; @deva[उपवसति वने रामः] Rāma observes a fast in the forest.

---

[^1]: @deva[उपान्वध्याङ्वसः ।] Pāṇ. I. 4. 48.
