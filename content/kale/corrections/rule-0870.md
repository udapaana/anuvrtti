## Locative/Instrumental with Nakshatra Names (Time)

[^1] When the names of *Nakshatras* are used as showing a particular time the Locative or the Instrumental is used; @deva[मूलेनावाहयेद्देवीं श्रवणेन विसर्जयेत् । मूले श्रवणे इति वा ।] (Sid. Kau.)

---

[^1]: @deva[नक्षत्रे च लुपि ।] Pāṇ. II 3. 45.
