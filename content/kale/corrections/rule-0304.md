[^1] The @deva[न] of @deva[पान] is changed to @deva[ण] necessarily when the compound implies a country and optionally when it signifies merely the act of drinking; as @deva[सुरापाणाः] ( @deva[प्राच्याः] ); @deva[क्षीरपाणाः] ( @deva[उशीनराः] ); but @deva[क्षीरपाणम्] or @deva[पानम्].

**(a)** @deva[न] is also optionally changed to @deva[ण] in the case of the following compounds:—@deva[गिरि-णदी-नदी], @deva[गिरि-णख-नख], @deva[गिरि-णड्य-नड्य], @deva[गिरिनितम्ब णितम्ब], @deva[चक्र-णदी-नदी], @deva[चक्र-णितम्ब-नितम्ब], &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[पान देशे] । @deva[वा भावकरणयोः] । Pān. VIII. 4. 9-10. @deva[गिरिनद्यादीनां वा] । Vârt.
