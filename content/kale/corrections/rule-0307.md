A *Prâtipadika* ending in @deva[अ] and the words included in the Ajâdi group[^1] form their *feminine* in @deva[आ];[^2] as @deva[भुञ्जान-भुञ्जाना]; @deva[अज-अजा], @deva[एडका], @deva[अश्वा], @deva[चटका], @deva[मूषिका]; @deva[बाला], @deva[वत्सा], @deva[होडा], @deva[मन्दा], @deva[विलाता], all meaning 'a young girl'; ( of these the first five form an exception to @ref[313], and the second to @ref[308], *c* ); @deva[आ] is also added to @deva[फल] when compounded with @deva[सत्], @deva[भस्त्रा], @deva[अजिन], @deva[पण] and @deva[पिण्ड]; to @deva[पुष्प] when compounded with @deva[सत्], words ending in @deva[अच्], to @deva[काण्ड], @deva[प्रान्त], @deva[शत] and @deva[एक]; to @deva[शूद्र] not preceded by @deva[महत्], when class is implied, and to @deva[मूल] preceded by @deva[अ]; @deva[संफला]; @deva[भस्त्राफला], @deva[पणफला], &c all 'kinds of creeper'; @deva[सत्-पाक्-काण्ड-प्रान्त-शत-एक-पुष्पा], 'kinds of creeper' @deva[शूद्रा] 'a woman of the @deva[शूद्र] class;' @deva[अमूला].

**(a)**[^5] But if the *Prâtipadika* end in @deva[क] ( the member of the termination ) the preceding @deva[अ] is changed to @deva[इ]; @deva[सर्विका], @deva[कारिका], &c.; the words @deva[मामक], @deva[नरक], and those ending in the Tad. affix @deva[त्य] with @deva[क] suffixed to it, changed their @deva[अ] to @deva[इ] similarly; @deva[मामिका], @deva[नरान् कायति] calls out to @deva[इति नरिका], @deva[दाक्षिणात्यिका], @deva[इहत्यिका] a woman of this place.

Exceptions:—[^3] The @deva[अ] is not changed to @deva[इ] when the @deva[क] is added to the pronouns @deva[यद्] and @deva[तद्], or when it is a part of the Tad. affix @deva[त्यक], or when there is a compound, or when there is a word included in the Kshipaka group[^6] ( @deva[क्षिपकगण] ); as @deva[यका], @deva[सका], @deva[अधित्यका] 'a tableland', @deva[उपत्यका] 'the land at the foot of a mountain', @deva[बहुपरिव्राजका नगरी], @deva[क्षिपका], @deva[ध्रुवका], @deva[कन्यका], &c.

**(b)** [^4] @deva[अ] is optionally changed to @deva[इ] in the following cases.—

**(1)** @deva[तारका] 'a star', @deva[तारिका] 'able to protect'; @deva[वर्णका] 'a cloak, a garment,' @deva[वर्णिका] ( in other cases ), @deva[वर्तक-वर्तका शकुनौ प्राचाम्] @deva[उदीचां तु वर्तिका]; @deva[अष्टका] 'kind of Shrâddha,' @deva[अष्टिका] ( in other cases ).

**(2)** @deva[सूतिका-सूतका], a woman recently delivered; @deva[पुत्रिका] or @deva[पुत्रका], @deva[वृन्दारका] or @deva[वृन्दारिका], a goddess.

**(3)** when the @deva[क] follows upon the *fem.* termination @deva[आ] shortened to @deva[अ] and is preceded by @deva[य] or @deva[क]; as @deva[आर्या+क=आर्यक+आ=आर्यिका] or @deva[आर्यका]; @deva[चटका+क=चटकक+आ=चटकिका] or @deva[चटकका] &c. but @deva[संकाश्ये भवा सांकाश्यिका], @deva[अश्विका], @deva[शुभंयातीति शुभंयाः]; @deva[अज्ञाता शुभंयाः शुभंयिका].

**(c)** This change of @deva[अ] to @deva[इ] is necessary when the @deva[क] follows the @deva[य] or @deva[क] of a root @deva[सुनयिका], @deva[सुपाकिका], &c.

[^6]: The following words are included in the @deva[क्षिपकगण]:—@deva[क्षिपक] 'an archer,' @deva[ध्रुवक], @deva[चरक] 'a spy,' @deva[सेवक], @deva[करक] 'a kind of bird, hail,' @deva[चटक]; @deva[अवक] 'a kind of plant,' @deva[हलक], @deva[अलका], @deva[कन्यका], and @deva[एडक].
