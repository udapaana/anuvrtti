## Third Aorist of Tenth Class Roots and Causals

Roots of the tenth Class and Causals:—

(a) The @deva[अय] of the base is dropped (the vowel changes taking place before it being retained) and a short vowel substituted for the long one (@deva[ए] and @deva[ऐ] being shortened to @deva[इ], and @deva[ओ] and @deva[औ] to @deva[उ]).

The base so modified is then reduplicated according to the general rules; @deva[भावय] (Cau. B. of @deva[भू])=@deva[भाव्]=@deva[भव्]=@deva[बभव्] after reduplication; @deva[चेतय्] (from @deva[चित्])=@deva[चेत्]=@deva[चित्]=@deva[चिचित्], &c.

(b) @deva[इ] is substituted for the @deva[अ] of the reduplicative syllable if the syllable following it be short, and not prosodially long; the @deva[इ] of the reduplicative syllable is lengthened if not followed by a long syll. or a conjunct consonant; thus @deva[बभव्]=@deva[बिभव्]=@deva[बीभव्]; @deva[चिचित्]=@deva[चीचित्]; @deva[स्खल्]=@deva[चस्खल्]=@deva[चिस्खल्], the @deva[इ] is not lengthened as it is followed by a conjunct consonant; @deva[स्पन्द्]=@deva[पस्पन्द्] only, as @deva[प] is followed by a syllable prosodially long.

(c) Roots having a penultimate @deva[ऋ], short or long, optionally preserve it, the long @deva[ॠ] being changed to the short one; @deva[वृत्] causal base @deva[वर्तय]=@deva[वर्त्] after dropping @deva[अय], and @deva[वृत्] by this; @deva[वर्त्]=@deva[ववर्त्]; @deva[वृत्]=@deva[ववृत्]=@deva[विवृत्]=@deva[वीवृत्]; @deva[कृत्]—@deva[कीर्तय]=@deva[कीर्त्] and @deva[कृत्] by this; @deva[कीर्त्]—@deva[चिकीर्त्]; @deva[कृत्]—@deva[चीकृत्].

(d) To the base so prepared the augment @deva[अ] is to be prefixed and the terminations added, P. & A., as in the 2nd Variety:—@deva[अबीभवत्]-@deva[त] from @deva[भू]; @deva[अचीचितत्] from @deva[चित्], @deva[अचिस्खलत्]-@deva[त] from @deva[स्खल्], @deva[अपस्पन्दत्]-@deva[त] from @deva[स्पन्द्]; @deva[अववर्तत्]-@deva[त], @deva[अवीवृतत्]-@deva[त] from @deva[वृत्]; @deva[अचिकीर्तत्]-@deva[त], @deva[अचीकृतत्]-@deva[त] fr. @deva[कृत्]; @deva[अपपर्थत्]-@deva[त], @deva[अपीपृथत्]-@deva[त] from @deva[पृथ्], &c.

@note[type=note]{N. B. The Ātm. form in @deva[त] when not given should be supposed to be understood.}
