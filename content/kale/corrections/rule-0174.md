@deva[ईयस्] is another termination of the comparative degree and @deva[इष्ठ] of the superlative. These are added to adjectives of quality only[^1]. Before these the last vowel of a word with the following consonant if any is dropped; e. g. @deva[लघु]-@deva[लघीयस्], @deva[लघिष्ठ]; @deva[पटु], @deva[पटीयस्], @deva[पटिष्ठ]; @deva[महत्] @deva[महीयस्], @deva[महिष्ठ]; &c. but @deva[पाचकतर], @deva[पाचकतम] only.

---

[^1]: @deva[अजादी] (i. e. @deva[ईयम्] and @deva[इष्ठ]) @deva[गुणवचनादेव ।] Pāṇ. V. 3. 58.
