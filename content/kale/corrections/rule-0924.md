title: "Present for All Tenses with @deva[जातु] and @deva[आत्]"
pages: [519.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1]When @deva[जातु] or @deva[अपि] is used in a sentence and condemnation or censure is implied, the Present may be used in the sense of the three tenses; @deva[अपि जायां त्यजसि जातु गणिकामादत्से] where @deva[त्यजसि] and @deva[आदत्से] may have also the sense of the Past or the Future tense; @deva[जातु तत्रभवान् वृषलान् याजयति] (you will even make a S'udra perform a sacrifice).
