[^1] If *prâtipadika* end in @deva[वन्] it changes its @deva[न्] to @deva[र्] before @deva[ई]; @deva[शक्वन्-शक्वरी] the strong one; @deva[पीवन्-पीवरी], @deva[शर्वन्-शर्वरी] the night, @deva[सुत्वानमतिक्रान्ता अतिसुत्वरी], @deva[अतिधीवरी], &c.

**Exception.**—[^2] But if a base end in @deva[वन्] applied to roots ending in a soft consonant; or if a compound has such a base for its latter member, the *fem.* is formed by adding @deva[आ] only, before which the preceding @deva[अन्] is dropped, *e. g.* @deva[अवावन्+आ=अवावा] a Brâhmaṇa woman ( @deva[ब्राह्मणी] ) or a female thief; @deva[राजयुध्वा].

---

[^1]: @deva[वनोरच] । Pân. VI. 1. 77, 7.

[^2]: @deva[अनो बहुव्रीहे:] । @deva[अन उपधालोपिनोऽन्यतरस्याम्] Pân. IV. 1. 12. 28.
