---
key: rule:258
title: "Transformation of @deva[हृदय]"
pages: [159.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[हृदय][^1] is changed to @deva[हृद्] when preceded by @deva[सु] or @deva[दुस्] meaning 'a friend', or an 'enemy' respectively; @deva[शोभनं हृदयं यस्य स सुहृद्] 'a friend', @deva[दुर्हृद्] 'an enemy'; but @deva[सुहृदयः] 'one who has got a good heart' i.e. 'one who is able to appreciate merits', &c.; @deva[दुर्हृदयः] 'a man of wicked intentions.'

---

[^1]: @deva[सुहृद्दुर्हृदौ मित्रामित्रयोः ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 150.
