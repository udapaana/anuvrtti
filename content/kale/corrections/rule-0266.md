---
key: rule:266
title: "Exceptions Where @deva[क] Is Not Affixed"
pages: [162.png]
verdict: approved
---
No @deva[क] is affixed[^1]

**(a)** when the whole compound is used as a name or when it ends in @deva[ईयस्], as @deva[विश्वे देवा यस्य विश्वदेवः] 'whose deities are the Viswadevas'; @deva[बहवः श्रेयांसः यस्य बहुश्रेयान्]. A Bah. ending in @deva[ईयस्] *f.* (i. e. @deva[ईयसी][^2]) does not shorten its final vowel; e. g. @deva[बह्व्यः श्रेयस्यो यस्य बहुश्रेयसीः] 'one having many excellent wives.' But @deva[अतिश्रेयसिः] as a Tatpurusha.

**(b)** to the word @deva[भ्रातृ] preceded by a term of praise; @deva[प्रशस्तो भ्राता यस्य स प्रशस्तभ्राता]; but @deva[मूर्खभ्रातृकः] one whose brother is a fool.

**(c)** to the words @deva[नाडि] and @deva[तन्त्री] both referring to the body of an animal; @deva[बहुनाडिः कायः] 'the body having many arteries'; @deva[बहुतन्त्रीग्रीवा] 'the many-veined neck'; but @deva[बहुनाडीकः स्तम्भः] 'a pillar with many ornamental lines'; @deva[बहुतन्त्रीका] (many-stringed) @deva[वीणा].

**(d)** to the final of the compound @deva[निष्प्रवाणिः] (@deva[निर्गता प्रवाणी] 'a weaver's shuttle' @deva[यस्य]) @deva[पटः] 'a new, unbleached garment.'

**(e)** to compounds formed according to rules @ref[251], @ref[252], and @ref[253]; as @deva[सपुत्रः], @deva[उपबहवः], @deva[दक्षिणपूर्वा], &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[न संज्ञायाम् । ईयसश्च । वन्दिते भ्रातुः । नाडीतन्त्र्योः स्वाङ्गे । निष्प्रवाणिश्च ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 155-57, 159-60.

[^2]: @deva[ईयसो बहुव्रीहेर्नेति वाच्यम् ।]
