---
key: rule:407
title: "Rules for Final @deva[उ] of @deva[नु] and @deva[उ] Bases"
pages: [256.png]
verdict: approved
---
The final @deva[उ] of the base is optionally dropped before @deva[व्] and @deva[म्] if it be not preceded by a conjunct consonant. It is changed to @deva[उव्] before a weak termination beginning with a vowel, if preceded by a conjunct consonant and to @deva[व्] in other cases. The @deva[हि] of the Imperative 2nd per. sing. is dropped after @deva[उ] not preceded by a conjunct consonant.
