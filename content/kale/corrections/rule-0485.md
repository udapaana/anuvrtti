## Optional @deva[इ] before Ārdhadhātuka with @deva[स्]

The roots @deva[कृत्], @deva[चृत्], @deva[छृद्], @deva[तृद्] and @deva[नृत्] take @deva[इ] optionally when followed by an *ârdhadhâtuka* (Non-conjugational termination) beginning with @deva[स्] except in the Aorist; @deva[कृत्]-@deva[कर्तिष्यामि], @deva[कर्त्स्यामि]; @deva[अकर्तिष्यम्], @deva[अकर्त्स्यम्] Cond. &c.
