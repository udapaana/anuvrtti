**(*a*)** All the numerals from 20-99, monosyllabic words in @deva[ई] and such as are formed by the affix @deva[ता]; @deva[विंशतिः], @deva[श्रीः]; @deva[तनुता] &c.

**(*b*)** The synonyms of @deva[भूमि], @deva[सरित्], @deva[लता] and @deva[वनिता] and these words themselves.

**Exceptions:**—@deva[स्रोतस्] *n.* and @deva[यादस्] *n.* both meaning a 'river'.
