## Samprasāraṇa in Reduplicative Syllable

Before the strong terminations of the Perfect Samprasâraṇa takes place in the reduplicative syllable only. In this case the initial conjunct consonant of a root is reduplicated as it is; i. e. @deva[स्वप्]-@deva[सस्वप्] &c.

(a) The vowel following the Samprasâraṇa is dropped.
