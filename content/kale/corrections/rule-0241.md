---
key: rule:241
title: "Dropping @deva[न्] from @deva[ब्रह्मन्]"
pages: [147.png, 148.png]
verdict: approved
---
The @deva[न्] of @deva[ब्रह्मन्] is optionally dropped when preceded by @deva[कु] and @deva[महत्];[^1] @deva[कुब्रह्मा-ह्मः] a bad Brāhmaṇa, @deva[महाब्रह्मा-ह्मः]. The @deva[न्] is dropped necessarily when the compound implies the country in which the Brāhmaṇa dwells; e. g. @deva[सुराष्ट्रब्रह्मा] a Brāhmaṇa dwelling in Surāshṭra.

---

[^1]: @deva[ब्रह्मणो जानपदाख्यायाम् । कुमहद्भ्यामन्यतरस्याम् ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 104. 105.
