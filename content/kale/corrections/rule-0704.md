---
key: rule:704
title: "Affix @deva[त] Showing Place of Habitual Action"
pages: [428.png]
verdict: approved
---
The affix @deva[त][^1] added to roots having the sense of 'sitting or going, or eating' shows the place where the action takes place; @deva[इदं मुकुन्दस्य आसितं] 'this is the place where Mukunda to sit;' @deva[इदं यातं रमापतेः] 'this is the path by which the husband of Ramā used to pass;' @deva[भुक्तं एतत् अनंतस्य] 'this is the place where Ananta took his food,' &c.

[^1]: @deva[अधिकरणे च ध्रौव्यगतिप्रत्यवसानार्थेभ्यः ।] Pāṇ. III. 4, 48.
