[^1]The Simple Future is alone used when the idea of hope is conveyed by @deva[क्षिप्र] and words having the sense of @deva[क्षिप्र]; @deva[वृष्टिश्चेत्क्षिप्रं आशु त्वरितं वा यास्यति शीघ्रं वप्स्यामः] if a shower were to come quickly we would at once sow corn.

[^1]: @deva[क्षिप्रवचने लृट्।] Pān. III. 3. 133.
