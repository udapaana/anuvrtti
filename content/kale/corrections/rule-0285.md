---
key: rule:285
title: "Unchanged Finals with @deva[सु], @deva[अति], @deva[किम्]"
pages: [170.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] The finals of compounds having for their first member @deva[सु] or @deva[अति] in the sense of 'praise' and @deva[किं] in the sense of 'censure', remain unchanged; @deva[सुराजा] 'a good king' @deva[अतिराजा] 'a pre-eminent king'. @deva[अतिगौ] 'an excellent bull' @deva[अतिश्वा] &c. but @deva[परमराजः]; @deva[गामतिक्रान्तः अतिगवः]; @deva[कुत्सितो राजा किंराजा] 'a bad king', @deva[किंसखा] 'a bad friend'; but @deva[किंराजः], @deva[किंसखः] in other cases. This prohibition does not apply to Bah. compounds @deva[सुसक्थः]; @deva[स्वक्षः].

---

[^1]: @deva[न पूजनात्] । Pān. V. 4. 69. @deva[स्वतिभ्यामेव] । Vārt. @deva[किमः क्षेपे] । Pān. V. 4. 70.
