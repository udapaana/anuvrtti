title: "Present for Past with @deva[ननु] Particle"
pages: [517.png, 518.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1]When a question is asked and an answer is given to it, the Present is used in the sense of the Past tense when the particle @deva[ननु] is used; @deva[कटं अकार्षीः किम्—ननु करोमि भोः] where @deva[करोमि] is equivalent to @deva[अकार्षम्]; when the particles @deva[न] and @deva[नु] are used the Present may be optionally used; @deva[कटमकार्षीः किम्—न करोमि] or @deva[नाकार्षम्—नु करोमि] or @deva[न्वकार्षम्].

## Footnotes
