---
key: rule:647
title: "Dropping of @deva[य] in General Tenses"
pages: [392.png]
verdict: approved
---
In the general tenses the affix @deva[य] (@deva[क्यच्] and @deva[क्यङ्] to be given hereafter) is dropped when preceded by a consonant, @deva[समिधांचकार] Perf. 1 sing. @deva[समिधिता], @deva[समिधिष्यति] 1st. and 2nd Fu. 3 sing. from @deva[समिध्यति], but @deva[पुत्रीयांचकार] from @deva[पुत्रीयति].
