Words ending in @deva[उ] and those having @deva[क्], @deva[ट्], @deva[ण्], @deva[थ्], @deva[न्], @deva[भ्], @deva[म्], @deva[य्], @deva[र्], or @deva[स्], for their penultimate; as @deva[प्रभुः], @deva[भानुः], @deva[इक्षुः], @deva[स्तबकः] 'a bunch of flowers' &c; @deva[घटः], @deva[पाषाणः], @deva[शोथः] 'a swelling', @deva[फेनः], @deva[दीपः], @deva[स्तम्भः], @deva[सोमः], @deva[समयः], @deva[क्षुरः] a razor, @deva[अङ्कुरः], @deva[वृषः], @deva[वायसः] &c.

**Exceptions:**—Words ending in—

**(*a*)** @deva[उ]—@deva[धेनु], @deva[रज्जु] ( except when it ends a compound, in which case it is both *mas.* and *fem.* ) @deva[कुहु]—@deva[हू] 'the last day of a lunar month on which the moon is invisible,' @deva[सरयु] 'name of a river,' @deva[तनु], @deva[करेणु] and @deva[प्रियंगु] 'a kind of creeper,' which are all *fem.*; @deva[श्मश्रु], @deva[जानु], @deva[वसु] 'wealth,' @deva[अश्रु], @deva[जतु] 'lac,' @deva[त्रपु] 'tin or lead', @deva[तालु], @deva[दारु], @deva[मधु] 'honey,' @deva[स्वादु] 'relish', @deva[वस्तु] and @deva[मस्तु] 'sour cream', which are all *neuter.*

**(*b*)** @deva[क]—@deva[चिबुक] 'the chin', @deva[शाल्लूक], @deva[प्रातिपदिक], @deva[अंशुक] 'a garment,' @deva[उल्मुक] 'a fire brand.'

**(*c*)** @deva[ट] and @deva[ण]—@deva[किरीट], @deva[मुकुट], @deva[ललाट], @deva[शृङ्गाट] 'a place where four roads meet;' @deva[ऋण], @deva[लवण], @deva[पर्ण], @deva[उष्ण].

**(*d*)** @deva[थ] and @deva[न]—@deva[काष्ठ], @deva[पृष्ठ], @deva[रिक्थ] property left at death, @deva[उक्थ] 'a hymn of the Sāmaveda,' 'a kind of sacrifice,' @deva[जघन], @deva[अजिन] 'the hairy skin of a black antelope', @deva[तुहिन] 'snow', @deva[कानन], @deva[विपिन], @deva[वन], @deva[वृजिन] 'sin,' @deva[वेतन], @deva[शासन], @deva[सोपान] 'a flight of steps', @deva[मिथुन], @deva[श्मशान], @deva[रत्न], @deva[चिह्न].

**(*e*)** @deva[प], @deva[भ], @deva[म]—@deva[पाप] 'sin' @deva[रूप], @deva[शिल्प], @deva[पुष्प], @deva[शष्प] 'tender grass', @deva[अन्तरीप] 'an island', @deva[कुङ्कुम] 'saffron', @deva[रुक्म] gold, iron, @deva[सिध्म] 'a leprous spot', @deva[युग्म] 'a battle', @deva[इध्म], @deva[गुल्म] ( generally *mas.* ), @deva[अध्यात्म] knowledge of the soul.

**(*f*)** @deva[य] and @deva[र]—@deva[हृदय], @deva[इन्द्रिय], @deva[उत्तरीय] 'an upper garment', @deva[द्वार], @deva[अग्र], @deva[तक्र], @deva[वक्त्र], @deva[वप्र] 'lead', @deva[छिद्र], @deva[नीर], @deva[कृच्छ्र], @deva[रन्ध्र], @deva[श्वभ्र], @deva[अस्त्र], @deva[तिमिर], @deva[विचित्र], @deva[केयूर], @deva[उदर], @deva[शरीर], @deva[कन्दर] 'dry ginger', @deva[पञ्जर] 'a cage', @deva[जठर], @deva[अजिर] 'courtyard,' @deva[वैर], @deva[चत्वर], @deva[पुष्कर], @deva[गह्वर], @deva[कुहर] 'a cave', @deva[कुटीर] 'a hut' ( *m.* also ), @deva[कुलीर] 'a crab', @deva[काश्मीर] 'name of a country,' @deva[अम्बर], @deva[शिशिर], @deva[तन्त्र] a loom, the ritual &c.; @deva[यन्त्र], @deva[क्षत्र], @deva[क्षेत्र], @deva[मित्र], @deva[कलत्र], @deva[चित्र], @deva[सूत्र], @deva[नेत्र], @deva[गोत्र] a family, @deva[अङ्गुलित्र] a finger-armour, @deva[शस्त्र], @deva[शास्त्र], @deva[वस्त्र], @deva[पत्र], @deva[पात्र], @deva[शुक्र].

**(*g*)** @deva[ष] and @deva[स]—@deva[ऋजीष] 'a frying pan,' @deva[अम्बरीष] 'a frying pan,' @deva[पीयूष], @deva[पुरीष], @deva[किल्बिष] 'sin, guilt,' @deva[कल्मषः] 'sin, stain,' ( sometimes *mas.* ), @deva[बिस], @deva[बुस] 'chaff' @deva[साहस] which ( *b. g.* ) are all *neuter.*
