title: "@deva[तथा] as Substitute for @deva[च]"
pages: [532.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[तथा] 'likewise' often supplies the place of @deva[च]; @deva[रामस्तथा लक्ष्मणश्च] Rāma and Lakshmaṇa; @deva[अनागतविधाता च प्रत्युत्पन्नमतिस्तथा] both Anāgatavidhāta and Pratyutpannamati, @deva[तथा हि] means 'for instance, to be more plain;' @deva[तथा च] 'likewise;' both are often used introducing quotations.
