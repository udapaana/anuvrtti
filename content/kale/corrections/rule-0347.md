The words @deva[देव], @deva[दैत्य], @deva[मनुष्य], @deva[पर्वत], @deva[समुद्र], @deva[स्वर्ग], @deva[मेघ], @deva[किरण], @deva[दिवस], @deva[असि], @deva[शर], @deva[यज्ञ], @deva[आत्मा], @deva[नख] ( also *neu.* ), @deva[केश], @deva[दन्त], @deva[कण्ठ], @deva[गल], @deva[स्तन], @deva[भुज], @deva[गुल्फ], and their synonyms, and words expressive of the measures of corn &c., such as @deva[कुडव] &c.

**Exceptions:**—@deva[द्यो] *fem.*, @deva[दिव्] *fem.*, @deva[खारी] *fem.*, @deva[मानिका] *fem.* 'a kind of weight', @deva[त्रिविष्टप] *neu*; @deva[दिन] *neu.*; @deva[अहन्] *neu.*; and @deva[अभ्र] *neu.*
