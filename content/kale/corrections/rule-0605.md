## Special Causal Forms with @deva[प्] Augment

(a) @deva[मि] 'to throw,' @deva[मी] 'to destroy,' @deva[दी] 'to perish,' @deva[जि] 'to conquer,' and @deva[क्री] 'to buy,' also insert @deva[प्], but after changing their final vowel to @deva[आ]; @deva[मापयति], @deva[दापयति], @deva[जापयति], @deva[क्रापयति].

(b) The roots @deva[क्षै], @deva[श्रा] or @deva[श्रै] 'to cook' and @deva[ज्ञा] (mit.), shorten their vowel necessarily, and @deva[ग्लै] and @deva[स्ना] optionally, before the inserted @deva[प्] when not preceded by prepositions; @deva[क्षपयति], &c. @deva[ज्ञपयति], (@deva[पशुं संज्ञपयति] slays; @deva[प्रज्ञपयति] @deva[शरं] &c.); but @deva[ज्ञापयति], @deva[ग्लपयति], @deva[ग्लापयति]; @deva[स्नपयति], @deva[स्नापयति]; but @deva[प्रग्लापयति], @deva[उपस्नापयति] only.
