---
key: rule:42
title: "Retroflexion of @deva[स्] to @deva[ष्] after Vowels (Ṣatva)"
pages: [029.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[स्] belonging to a substitute (@deva[आदेश]) or a termination (@deva[प्रत्यय]) and not at the end of a grammatical form is changed to @deva[ष्] when preceded by any vowel except @deva[अ] or @deva[आ], a semi-vowel, a letter of the guttural class or @deva[ह्];[^1] e. g. @deva[रामे] + @deva[सु] = @deva[रामेषु]; but @deva[रामस्य] as @deva[स्] is preceded by @deva[अ]. @deva[सुपीः] @deva[सुपिसौ] &c., as @deva[स्] belongs to the word @deva[सुपिस्]. This change takes place even if an *anuswāra* substituted for @deva[न्], a *visarga* or @deva[श्], @deva[ष्] or @deva[स्] intervene;[^2] e. g. @deva[धनून्] + @deva[सि] = @deva[धनूंषि] nom. pl. of @deva[धनुस्] neu. a bow. @deva[पिपठीष्] + @deva[सु] = @deva[पिपठीष्षु].

---

[^1]: @deva[अपदान्तस्य मूर्धन्यः। इण्कोः। आदेशप्रत्यययोः] — Pāṇ. VIII. 3. 55, 57, 59.

[^2]: @deva[नुम्विसर्जनीयशर्व्यवायेऽपि] — Pāṇ. VIII. 3. 58.
