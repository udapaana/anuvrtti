---
key: rule:205
title: "Instrumental Tatpurusha"
pages: [123.png, 124.png]
verdict: approved
---
A noun in the Instrumental may be compounded—

**(a)**[^1] with another expressive of an effect produced by what is denoted by the noun in the Instrumental case, and with @deva[अर्थ], as @deva[शङ्कुलया खण्डः शङ्कुलाखण्डः] severed by means of a knife; @deva[धान्येन अर्थः धान्यार्थः] wealth obtained by means of grain.

**(b)**[^2] with verbal derivatives when the Instrumental has the sense of the agent or the instrument or means of the action; as @deva[हरिणा त्रातः हरित्रातः] saved by Hari; @deva[नखैर्भिन्नः नखभिन्नः] torn with the nails &c.

**(c)** with the words[^3] @deva[पूर्व], @deva[सदृश], @deva[सम], @deva[ऊन] and words having the sense of @deva[ऊन], @deva[कलह], @deva[निपुण], @deva[मिश्र], @deva[श्लक्ष्ण] and @deva[अवर]; as @deva[मासेन पूर्वः मासपूर्वः]; @deva[मात्रा सदृशः मातृसदृशः] like his mother; @deva[पितृसमः] equal to his father; @deva[माषेण ऊनं माषोनं]; so @deva[माषविकल] (less by one māsha, a particular weight); @deva[वाचा कलहः वाक्कलहः] a quarrel (in words); @deva[आचारनिपुणः]; @deva[गुडमिश्रः]; @deva[आचारश्लक्ष्णः] 'thin by the discharge of religious duties'; @deva[मासेन अवरः मासावरः] 'younger by a month.'

**(d)**[^4] With another noun signifying an article of food or something eatable, the noun in the Instrumental case denoting some kind of condiment used to season it or another article of food mixed with it; as @deva[दध्ना ओदनः दध्योदनः] rice mixed with curds; @deva[गुडेन धानाः गुडधानाः] fried rice mixed with treacle.

**(e)** Sometimes @deva[स्वयं] may take the place of the Instrumental as @deva[स्वयंकृतः] done by one's self.

[^2]: @deva[कर्तृकरणे कृता बहुलम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 1. 32.

[^3]: @deva[पूर्वसदृशसमोनार्थकलहनिपुणमिश्रश्लक्ष्णैः ।] Pāṇ. II. 1. 31.

[^4]: @deva[अन्नेन व्यञ्जनम् । भक्ष्येण मिश्रीकरणम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 1. 34. 35.
