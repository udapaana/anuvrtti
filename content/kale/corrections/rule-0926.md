## IMPERFECT.

[^1]The Imperfect according to Pāṇini denotes past action not done to-day i. e. done at some time prior to the current day, @deva[तानभाषत पौलस्त्यः] (Bhaṭṭi.) Bibhīshaṇa spoke to them.
