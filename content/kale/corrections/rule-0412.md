title: "Forms of @deva[ज्ञा] and @deva[ज्या] (@deva[जा], @deva[जि])"
pages: [260.png, 261.png]
verdict: approved
---
The roots @deva[ज्ञा] 'to know' and @deva[ज्या] 'to become old' assume the forms @deva[जा] and @deva[जि] respectively; as @deva[जानाति]-@deva[नीते], @deva[जिनाति], &c.
