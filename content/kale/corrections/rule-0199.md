---
key: rule:199
title: "@deva[राजदन्तादि] and @deva[धर्मादि]: Reverse Order Exceptions"
pages: [121.png]
verdict: approved
---
In the case of words of the @deva[राजदन्तादि] class that which ought to be placed first is placed last; while in the case of words of the @deva[धर्मादि] group (a sub-division of @deva[राजदन्तादि]) the rule is observed optionally;[^1] @deva[शूद्रार्यम्] a Śudra and an Arya, @deva[धर्मश्च अर्थश्च धर्मार्थौ] or @deva[अर्थधर्मौ]; so @deva[अर्थशब्दौ] or @deva[शब्दार्थौ], @deva[अर्थकामौ] or @deva[कामार्थौ], &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[राजदन्तादिषु परम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 2. 31. @deva[धर्मादिष्वनियमः ।] Vārt.
