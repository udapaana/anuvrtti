title: "@deva[उपानह्] (Shoe): Special Declension"
verdict: approved-with-doubts

  - "Nom. sing. printed 'उपानत,' (halant faint); written उपानत्."

The @deva[ह्] of @deva[उपानह्] f. 'a shoe' is changed to @deva[त्] before @deva[स्] and to @deva[द्] before the @deva[भ्] of the termination; e. g. Nom. @deva[उपानत्], @deva[उपानहौ], @deva[उपानहः], Ins. sing. and dual @deva[उपानहा], @deva[उपानद्भ्याम्]; Loc. @deva[उपानहि], @deva[उपानहोः], @deva[उपानत्सु].
