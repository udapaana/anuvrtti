## Dropping of Penultimate Nasal in Benedictive

A penultimate nasal is generally dropped. Some of the roots dropping their nasal thus are:

@deva[अञ्च्], @deva[अञ्ज्], @deva[भञ्ज्], @deva[रञ्ज्], @deva[सञ्ज्], @deva[स्वञ्ज्], @deva[ग्रन्थ्], @deva[मन्थ्], @deva[उन्द्], @deva[स्कन्द्], @deva[स्यन्द्], @deva[इन्ध्], @deva[बन्ध्], @deva[दम्भ्], @deva[स्तम्भ्], @deva[दंश्], @deva[भ्रंश्], @deva[स्रंस्] and @deva[तृंह्].
