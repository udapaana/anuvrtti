## Ablative with Learning and Origin

(a) [^1] The teacher from whom something is learnt regularly is put in the Ablative case; @deva[उपाध्यायादधीते] learns from the preceptor; but @deva[नटस्य गाथां शृणोति].

(b) Similarly the prime or original cause in the case of @deva[जन्] to be born, and the source in the case of @deva[भू] are put in the Ablative; @deva[ब्रह्मणः प्रजाः प्रजायन्ते] the creation proceeds from Brahman; @deva[गोमयाद्वृश्चिको जायते] the scorpion is born from cowdung; @deva[हिमवतो गङ्गा प्रभवति] The Ganges rises from the Himalayas; @deva[कामात्क्रोधोऽभिजायते] from desire anger is produced.

*Note*:—Verbs meaning 'to be born' or 'to be begotten upon' are often used with the Loc.; @deva[तस्यां शतानन्द आङ्गिरसोऽजायत] from her was born Satananda Angirasa; @deva[मेनकायामुत्पन्नां] begotten upon Menakā; See Manu Smr. 154. I. 9.

---

[^1]: @deva[आख्यातोपयोगे ।] Pāṇ. I. 4. 29. @deva[जनिकर्तुः प्रकृतिः । भुवः प्रभवः ।] Pāṇ. I. 4. 30. 31.
