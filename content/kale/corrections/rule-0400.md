The following roots of the 10th class preserve their vowel unchanged; @deva[अघ्] to sin, @deva[कथ्] to tell, @deva[क्षप्] to send, to pass; @deva[गण्] to count, @deva[गल्] U. to filter, A. to throw, @deva[वर्] to choose or seek, to get, @deva[ध्वन्] to sound, @deva[मह्] to honour, @deva[रच्] to compose, @deva[रस्] to taste, @deva[रह्] to forsake, @deva[शठ्] to speak ill of, deceive, @deva[रट्] to scream, @deva[पट्] to weave, (but @deva[पाटयति] when it means to tear &c.), @deva[स्तन्] to thunder, @deva[गद्] to sound, @deva[पत्] to go, @deva[कल्] to count, @deva[स्वर्] to sound, @deva[पद्] A. to go, @deva[अंस्] to divide, @deva[वट्] to separate, @deva[लज्] to shine, @deva[कर्ण्] to bore, @deva[छद्] to conceal, @deva[चप्] to cheat, @deva[वस्] to dwell, @deva[श्रथ्] or @deva[श्लथ्] to be weak or lax, @deva[व्यय्] to give, to spend, @deva[स्पृह्] to desire, @deva[मृग्] to seek, @deva[मृष्] to bear, @deva[कृप्] to pity, to be weak, @deva[कुण्], @deva[गुण्] to converse with, @deva[ग्रह्] A. to take (also @deva[ग्राहयति] when caus. of @deva[ग्रह्]), @deva[कुह्] A. to astonish, to deceive, @deva[पुट्] to bind or string together, @deva[स्फुट्] to become manifest, @deva[सुख्] to make happy, and others less common.
