title: "Passive Imperative Third Person for Courtesy"
pages: [525.png, 526.png]
verdict: approved
---
The third person singular of the Imperative in the passive voice is often used and sometimes as a courteous form of expression; @deva[आनीयतां राजपुत्रः] the prince should be brought; @deva[श्रूयतां भो पण्डिताः] may you hear, ye Pandits; @deva[एतदासनमास्यतां] take this seat.
