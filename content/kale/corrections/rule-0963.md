## SECTION V. Indeclinables.

## ADVERBS.

The *neu.* singulars of the Nominative and other cases of several nouns are used as adverbs; @deva[चिरं] or @deva[चिरेण] or @deva[चिराद्ध्यात्वा] having contemplated for a long time; @deva[दुःखं] or @deva[दुःखेन तिष्ठति] he is in distress; so @deva[सुखं] or @deva[सुखेन] &c.

(a) The word @deva[विधा] is used adverbially in combination with several words such as @deva[बहु, नाना], &c.; @deva[बहुविधं, नानाविधं] in various ways. The word @deva[पूर्वं] is also used adverbially as the latter member of a compound when some action is to be expressed as having happened before; @deva[सान्त्वपूर्वं] having said something by way of consolation; @deva[बुद्धिपूर्वम्] thoughtfully, (*i. e.* thought preceding a certain action); @deva[अबुद्धिपूर्वं भगवन्धेनुरेषा हता मया] O venerable Sir, I killed this cow unwittingly; @deva[शपथपूर्वं अकथयत] &c.
