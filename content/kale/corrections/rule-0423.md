@deva[अन्] P. 'to breathe,' @deva[जक्ष्] 'to eat,' @deva[रुद्] P. 'to weep,' @deva[श्वस्] P. 'to sigh' and @deva[स्वप्] P. 'to sleep,' insert the augment @deva[इ] between their final and the terminations beginning with any consonant except @deva[य्]; in the case of 2 and 3 sing. Imperf. they insert @deva[ई] or @deva[अ]; e. g.
