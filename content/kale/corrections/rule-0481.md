## Augment @deva[इ] in Second Future and Conditional

@deva[इ] is to be prefixed, or not, or optionally to the terminations given above, according as the root is Seṭ or Aniṭ or Veṭ. Before the terminations the final vowel and the penultimate short take their Guṇa substitute.
