## @deva[पृथक्], @deva[विना], @deva[नाना] with Ablative/Accusative/Instrumental

[^1] The indeclinables @deva[पृथक्], @deva[विना] and @deva[नाना] govern the Ablative, the Accusative and the Instrumental cases; @deva[पृथक् रामात्-रामं-रामेण वा] different from or without Rāma; so @deva[नाना रामं] &c. @deva[नाना नारीं निष्फला लोकयात्रा] (Vop.) worldly life is vain without a woman (wife).

---

[^1]: @deva[पृथग्विनानानाभिस्तृतीयान्यतरस्याम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 32.
