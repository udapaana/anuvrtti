## Genitive/Instrumental with Potential Passive Participles

[^1] In the case of Potential passive participles the agent of the action is put in the Genitive or in the Instrumental case; @deva[मया मम वा सेव्यो हरिः] Hari ought to be served by me; @deva[राक्षसेन्द्रस्य संरक्ष्यं मया लव्यमिदं वनं] (Bhatti. VIII. 129.) this forest which the lord of the demons ought to preserve must be destroyed by me; @deva[गन्तव्या ते वसतिरलका] &c. (Meg.) Thou should go to Alakā.

---

[^1]: @deva[कृत्यानां कर्तरि वा ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 71.
