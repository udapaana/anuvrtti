---
key: rule:762
title: "Usage of the Gerund in @deva[अम्] at End of Compounds"
pages: [444.png]
verdict: approved
---
This Participle is generally used at the end of compounds; @deva[स लोष्टघातं हतः] 'he was pelted to death;' @deva[बन्दिग्राहं गृहीता] (Vik. I.) 'She was taken a captive;' @deva[स मूलघातं न्यवधीदरींश्च] (Bhaṭṭi. I. 2.) 'He totally exterminated his enemies, &c.'
