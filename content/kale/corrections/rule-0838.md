## Ablative when the Sense of an Indeclinable Participle is Suppressed

When the sense of an indeclinable participle is suppressed in a sentence[^1] the object governed by that ptc. or the place at or on which the action takes place is put in the Ablative case; @deva[प्रासादात् प्रेक्षते] sees from a palace (@deva[प्रासादमारुह्य प्रेक्षते] Sid. Kau.); similarly @deva[आसनात् प्रेक्षते]=@deva[आसने उपविश्य प्रेक्षते]; @deva[श्वशुराज्जिह्रेति]=@deva[श्वशुरं वीक्ष्य जिह्रेति], Sid. Kau

---

[^1]: @deva[ल्यब्लोपे कर्मण्यधिकरणे च ।] Vārt.
