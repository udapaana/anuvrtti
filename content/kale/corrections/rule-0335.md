---
key: rule:335
title: "Dwigu with Measurement Words"
pages: [193.png]
verdict: approved
---
**(a)** A Dwigu ending in @deva[काण्ड][^1] ( a particular measure ) and qualifying a word denoting 'a field' has its *fem.* formed by the addition of @deva[आ], but when a Taddhita affix has been first added to it and then dropped; as @deva[द्वे काण्डे प्रमाणं यस्याः असौ द्विकाण्ड+मात्रा=द्विकाण्डा क्षेत्रभक्ति] 'a piece of land 30 hands in extent'; but @deva[द्विकाण्डी रज्जुः] 'a rope, 30 hands in length'; also when the ending word is one that does not denote a measure, except the words @deva[विस्त] 'a tola', @deva[आचित] 'the load of a cart,' and @deva[कंबल्य] ( a weight equal to 3⅓ tolas ); @deva[पञ्चभिरश्वैः क्रीता पञ्चाश्वा], @deva[द्वौ विस्तौ पचतीति द्विविस्ता स्थाली]; so @deva[द्व्याचिता], @deva[द्विकंबल्या].

**(b)**[^2] When the word @deva[पुरुष] expressive of measurement ends a Dwigu and the Taddhita affix is added and dropped as before, its *fem.* is formed both in @deva[आ] and @deva[ई]; @deva[द्वौ पुरुषौ प्रमाणं अस्याः द्विपुरुषा-षी परिखा] 'a ditch two purushas ( 13 feet ) in depth'.

---

[^1]: @deva[काण्डान्तात्क्षेत्रे] । Pân. IV. 1. 23.

[^2]: @deva[पुरुषात्प्रमाणेऽन्यतरस्याम्] । Pân. IV. 1. 24.
