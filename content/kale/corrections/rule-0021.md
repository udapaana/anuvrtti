When @deva[अ] or @deva[आ] is followed by @deva[ए] or @deva[ऐ] and @deva[ओ] or @deva[औ], @deva[ऐ] and @deva[औ] are respectively substituted for both;[^1] @deva[कृष्ण] + @deva[एकत्वम्] = @deva[कृष्णैकत्वम्]; @deva[देव] + @deva[ऐश्वर्यं] = @deva[देवैश्वर्यम्] the majesty of god; @deva[सा] + @deva[एव] = @deva[सैव] she alone; @deva[भव] + @deva[ओषधं] = @deva[भवौषधम्] medicine against birth and re-birth; @deva[विद्या] + @deva[औत्सुक्यं] = @deva[विद्यौत्सुक्यम्] longing for knowledge.

**Exceptions:**

**(a)** If a preposition ending in @deva[अ] be followed by a root beginning with @deva[ए] or @deva[ओ], @deva[ए] or @deva[ओ] is substituted;[^2] @deva[प्र] + @deva[एजते] = @deva[प्रेजते] quakes very much; @deva[उप] + @deva[ओषति] = @deva[उपोषति] burns something near; but if the root be a denominative, vṛddhi is optional; @deva[उप] + @deva[एडकीयति] = @deva[उपेडकीयति] or @deva[उपैडकीयति]; @deva[प्र] + @deva[ओघीयति] = @deva[प्रोघीयति] or @deva[प्रौघीयति].

**Counter-exception:** If a form of the root @deva[इ] to go, or @deva[एध्] to grow, with an initial @deva[ए], comes after @deva[अ], and @deva[एष] or @deva[एष्य] derived from @deva[इष्] of the 4th, 6th or 9th conj., after @deva[प्र], vṛddhi takes place; @deva[उप] + @deva[एति] = @deva[उपैति]; @deva[उप] + @deva[एधते] = @deva[उपैधते]; (but @deva[उप] + @deva[इतः] = @deva[उपेतः]; @deva[अवेहि] know; from @deva[अव] + @deva[एहि] which is equal to @deva[अव] + @deva[आ] + @deva[इहि], and not @deva[अवैहि]; @deva[मा भवान् प्र] + @deva[इदिधत्] = @deva[प्रेदिधत्]); @deva[प्र] + @deva[एषः] = @deva[प्रैषः] sending or directing; @deva[प्र] + @deva[एष्यः] = @deva[प्रैष्यः] a servant. But @deva[प्रेषः], @deva[प्रेष्यः] (from @deva[ईष्]).

**(b)** When @deva[अ] is followed by @deva[एव] in an indefinite sense, @deva[ए] is substituted for both:[^3] @deva[क] + @deva[एव] = @deva[केव भोक्ष्यसे] where will you dine to-day? (The place being not definitely known); but @deva[तवैव] (why, I shall dine at your house).

**(c)** @deva[अ] followed by @deva[ओम्] and @deva[आ] (prep.) is dropped[^4] e. g. @deva[शिवाय] + @deva[ओं नमः] = @deva[शिवायों नमः]; @deva[शिव] + @deva[एहि] (@deva[आ] + @deva[इहि]) = @deva[शिवेहि].

**(d)** The vṛddhi is optional when the ending @deva[अ] of a word is followed by @deva[ओतु] a cat, or @deva[ओष्ठ] the lower lip, in a compound:[^5] @deva[स्थूल] + @deva[ओतुः] = @deva[स्थूलोतुः] or @deva[स्थूलौतुः]; @deva[बिम्बोष्ठः], @deva[बिम्बौष्ठः].

**(e)** The final vowel or the final consonant with the preceding vowel is dropped in the case of the following words when followed by certain words in a compound:[^6][^7] @deva[शक] the country of the Śakas + @deva[अन्धुः] a well = @deva[शकन्धुः]; @deva[कर्क] name of a country + @deva[अन्धुः] = @deva[कर्कन्धुः]; @deva[कुल] + @deva[अटा] = @deva[कुलटा] one who moves from one mansion to another, a woman of bad character; @deva[सीमन्] + @deva[अन्तः] = @deva[सीमन्तः] (the white line left by the parting of the hair on each side of the head); but @deva[सीमान्तः] when the meaning of the word is 'The last line or limit of a boundary.' @deva[मनस्] + @deva[ईषा] = @deva[मनीषा] wish; so @deva[लाङ्गलीषा] the pole of a plough; @deva[हलीषा]; @deva[पतन्] + @deva[अञ्जलिः] = @deva[पतञ्जलिः] name of the celebrated author of the Mahābhāṣya (a vast commentary on the sūtras of Pāṇini); lit. 'fit to be adored with joined hands; or according to tradition 'who fell down from the hands of a certain sage as he was offering water to the sun at the time of Sandhyā adoration.' @deva[सार] + @deva[अङ्गः] = @deva[सारङ्गः] an antelope with a spotted skin or a kind of bird; but @deva[साराङ्गः] one with a beautiful skin. This is an Ākṛtigaṇa i.e. words of similar formation and requiring grammatical explanation but not actually found in the gaṇa may be classed under it; e.g. @deva[मार्त] + @deva[अण्डः] = @deva[मार्तण्डः] (derived from @deva[मृताण्ड]) lit. born of a dead egg, the sun.

[^3]: @deva[एवे चानियोगे] Vārtik.

[^4]: @deva[ओमाङोश्च] Pāṇ. VI. 1. 95.

[^5]: '@deva[ओत्वोष्ठयोः समासे वा ।]'

[^6]: @deva[शकन्ध्वादिषु पररूपं वाच्यम्] Vārt.

[^7]: And also in the case of an onomatopœic wording in @deva[अत्] followed by @deva[इति] e.g. @deva[पटत्] + @deva[इति] = @deva[पटिति]; but not in the case of a monosyllabic word @deva[श्रद्] + @deva[इति] = @deva[श्रदिति]; and when the word is repeated, only the @deva[त्] is optionally dropped, as @deva[पटत्पटत्] + @deva[इति] = @deva[पटत्पटेति] or @deva[पटत्पटदिति].
