title: "Affix @deva[य] for Roots with Penultimate @deva[अ] and Labial Ending"
pages: [432.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "\"killed\" is the print's own wording, kept"
---
Roots having @deva[अ] for their penultimate and ending in a consonant of the labial class take the affix @deva[य]; @deva[शप्]—@deva[शप्य]; @deva[लभ्]—@deva[लभ्य]; @deva[रम्]—@deva[रम्य], &c.

(a) When @deva[लभ्] is preceded by @deva[आ], @deva[न्] (changed to @deva[म्]) is inserted between @deva[ल] and @deva[भ्]; @deva[आलभ्]—@deva[आलंभ्य] 'what ought or is fit to be killed;' @deva[न्] is also inserted when @deva[लभ्] is preceded by @deva[उप] if the meaning be 'to praise;' @deva[उपलंभ्यः साधुः] 'a Sādhu ought to be praised;' but @deva[उपलभ्यं धनं] 'wealth ought to be acquired.'
