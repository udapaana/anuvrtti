---
key: rule:195
title: "Adding @deva[अ] to Samāhāra Dwandwa Endings"
pages: [119.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "प्रावृट्शरदौ: second akṣara (वृ) blurred; ट्, शरदौ clear"
---
When a Samāhāra-Dwandwa ends in a consonant of the palatal class or @deva[द्], @deva[ष्] or @deva[ह्], @deva[अ] is added to it;[^1] @deva[वाक् च त्वक् च वाक्त्वचम्] speech and skin, @deva[त्वक्स्रजम्] a bark and a garland, @deva[शमीदृषदम्], @deva[वाक्त्विषम्], @deva[छत्रोपानहम्] an umbrella and shoes, &c, But @deva[प्रावृट्शरदौ] ( as it is not a Sam. Dwa. ).

---

[^1]: @deva[द्वन्द्वाच्चुदषहान्तात्समाहारे ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 106.
