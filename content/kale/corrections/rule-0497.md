## Final @deva[उ] and @deva[इ] Changes before Weak Terminations

The final @deva[उ], short or long, is changed to @deva[उव्] before a vowel weak termination and @deva[इ] to @deva[इय्] or @deva[य्] as it is preceded by a conjunct consonant or not; as @deva[दुधू]+@deva[इव]=@deva[दुधुविव] 1 dual; @deva[निनी]+@deva[इव]=@deva[निन्य्]+@deva[इव]=@deva[निन्यिव], @deva[शिश्रि]+@deva[इव]=@deva[शिश्रियिव], &c.
