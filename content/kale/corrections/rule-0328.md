title: "Feminine of @deva[ऊधस्] and Age-Related Compounds"
pages: [190.png, 191.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] If a Bah. end in @deva[ऊधस्] it takes the suffix @deva[ई] in the *fem.* before which @deva[न्] is substituted for the final @deva[अस्]; @deva[पीनं ऊधः यस्याः सा पीनोध्नी] having a large udder: @deva[कुण्डोध्नी] ( see Rag. I. 84. ); also when it is preceded by a numeral or an indeclinable; as @deva[द्व्युध्नी], @deva[अत्युध्नी] having an udder exceedingly large; but @deva[ऊधः अतिक्रान्ता अत्यूधाः].

**(a)** Similarly a Bah. ending in @deva[दामन्], and @deva[हायन] denoting age, and having a numeral for its first member takes @deva[ई] in the *fem.*; @deva[द्विदाम्नी], @deva[द्विहायनी] 'a girl two years old;', @deva[त्रिहायणी], &c; but @deva[द्विहायना शाला] a building built two years since.

*N. B.*—the @deva[न] of @deva[हायन] is changed to @deva[ण] after @deva[त्रि] and @deva[चतुर्] only when it takes @deva[ई]; @deva[चतुर्हायणी बाला]; but @deva[त्रिहायना], @deva[चतुर्हायना शाला].

---

[^1]: @deva[ऊधसोऽनङ्] । Pân V. 4. 131. @deva[बहुव्रीहेरूधसो ङीष्] । @deva[संख्याव्ययादेर्ङीष्] । @deva[दामहायनान्ताच्च] Pân. IV. 1. 25-27. @deva[वयोवाचकस्यैव हायनशब्दस्य ङीष्णत्वं चेष्यते] । Vârtika.
