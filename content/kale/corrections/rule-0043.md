The @deva[म्] of @deva[सम्] is changed to an Anuswāra and Visarga when followed by a form of the root @deva[कृ], the Anuswāra again is optionally changed to an Anunāsika; e. g. @deva[सम्] + @deva[कर्ता] = @deva[संस्कर्ता] and @deva[सँस्कर्ता]; @deva[स्] is affixed to the ending of the words @deva[सम्], @deva[पुम्] and @deva[कान्];[^1] so @deva[संस्कर्ता] and @deva[सँस्कर्ता].

*N. B.* The word @deva[संस्कर्ता] is capable of having 108 different forms as several *sūtras* are brought to bear upon it; but they are not given here as being tedious and of no material value.

Sections *a*, *b*, *c*, and *d* may be omitted by beginners.

**(a)** The @deva[म्] of @deva[पुम्] is optionally nasalised and has @deva[स्] added to it as above, when it is followed by a hard consonant except a sibilant, preceding a vowel, a semivowel, a nasal or @deva[ह्];[^2] @deva[पुम्] + @deva[कोकिलः] = @deva[पुम्] + @deva[स्] + @deva[कोकिलः] = @deva[पुंस्कोकिलः], @deva[पुँस्कोकिलः] a male *kokila* bird; so @deva[पुंस्पुत्र]; and @deva[पुँस्पुत्र]; the son, a grown up man; but @deva[पुंक्षीरम्] milk for a man; @deva[पुंदासः] a man-servant. Exception @deva[पुंख्यानम्] the account of a man.

**(b)** @deva[न्] at the end of a word (except @deva[प्रशान्]) followed by @deva[च्], @deva[छ्]; @deva[ट्], @deva[ठ्]; or @deva[त्], @deva[थ्] which is itself followed by a vowel, a semi-vowel, a nasal or @deva[ह्], undergoes the same changes;[^3] e. g. @deva[शार्ङ्गिन्] + @deva[छिन्द्धि] = @deva[शार्ङ्गिन्] + @deva[स्] + @deva[छिन्द्धि] = @deva[शार्ङ्गिन्] + @deva[श्] + @deva[छिन्द्धि] (by @ref[28], sec. *a*) @deva[शार्ङ्गिंश्छिन्द्धि] and @deva[शार्ङ्गिँश्छिन्द्धि] Oh Kṛṣṇa, cut off; similarly @deva[चक्रिन्] + @deva[त्रायस्व] = @deva[चक्रिंस्त्रायस्व] and @deva[चक्रिँस्त्रायस्व]; save, Oh Kṛṣṇa; but @deva[हन्ति] as the @deva[न्] is not at the end of a word. @deva[सन्त्सरुः] a fine handle of a sword. @deva[प्रशान्तनोति] a good man spreads.

**(c)** The @deva[न्] of @deva[नॄन्] when followed by @deva[प्] undergoes this change optionally; @deva[नॄन्] + @deva[पाहि] = @deva[नॄन्पाहि], @deva[नॄंःपाहि], and @deva[नॄँःपाहि].

**(d)** The @deva[न्] of @deva[कान्] followed by @deva[कान्] also undergoes this change optionally; @deva[कान्] + @deva[कान्] = @deva[कांस्कान्], @deva[काँस्कान्] to whom? @deva[स्] or @deva[ष्] is substituted for the visarga when the following sets are combined: @deva[कः] + @deva[कः] = @deva[कस्कः]; similarly @deva[कौतस्कुतः] wherefrom, @deva[भ्रातुष्पुत्रः], @deva[सद्यस्कालः] the present time; @deva[सर्पिष्कुण्डिका] a ghee-pot; @deva[धनुष्कपालम्] a bowstaff; @deva[यजुष्पात्रम्] a sacrificial vessel; @deva[अयस्कान्त] a magnet; @deva[तमस्काण्डः] great darkness; @deva[अयस्काण्डः]; @deva[भास्करः]; @deva[अहस्करः] the sun.
