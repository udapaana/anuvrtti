title: "@deva[उदक] → @deva[उद्] Transformations"
pages: [171.png, 172.png]
verdict: approved
---
**(a)** @deva[उदक][^1] becomes @deva[उद्] necessarily (1) when it forms a proper name, also when it is final; or (2) when followed by the words @deva[पेषं], @deva[वास], @deva[वाहन], and @deva[धि]; @deva[उदमेघः] 'a particular cloud filled with water; @deva[उदधिः], @deva[क्षीरोदः] the sea of milk; @deva[लवणोदः] &c.; @deva[उदपेषं पिनष्टि], @deva[उदवासः] standing in water; @deva[उदवाहनः], @deva[उदधिः] 'a vessel for holding water' @deva[घटः], @deva[समुद्रे तु पूर्वेण सिद्धम्] Sid. Kau.;

**(b)** and optionally when followed by a word beginning with a simple consonant and denoting 'a vessel to be filled up with water', and by @deva[मन्थ], @deva[ओदन], @deva[सक्तु], @deva[बिन्दु], @deva[वज्र], @deva[भार], @deva[हार], @deva[वीवध] 'a yoke with a pan at each end for carrying loads' ( @deva[कावड] Marâthi ) and @deva[गाह]; @deva[उदककुम्भः] or @deva[उदकुम्भः]; but @deva[उदकस्थाली] ( as @deva[स्थाली] begins with a conjunct consonant ), @deva[उदकपर्वतः]; @deva[उदमन्थः] or @deva[उदकमन्थः] barley-water; @deva[उदौदनः] or @deva[उदकौदनः] rice boiled with water, water-meal; @deva[उदवीवधः] or @deva[उदकवीवधः], 'a yoke for carrying water;' @deva[उदगाहः] or @deva[उदकगाहः] 'plunging or bathing in water' &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[उदकस्योदः संज्ञायाम्] । @deva[उत्तरपदस्य चेति वक्तव्यम्] । Vârt. @deva[पेषंवासवाहनधिषु च] । @deva[एकहलादौ पूरयितव्येऽन्यतरस्याम्] । @deva[मन्थौदनसक्तुबिन्दुवज्रभारहारवीवधगाहेषु च] । Pān. VI. 3. 57–60.
