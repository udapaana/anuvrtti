key: rule:31
title: "Dental to Nasalised ल् Before ल्"
pages: [022.png, 024.png]
verdict: approved
---
A letter of the dental class, followed by @deva[ल्] is changed to @deva[ल्], the nasalised @deva[ल्ँ] taking the place of @deva[न्][^1]; @deva[तद्] + @deva[लयः] = @deva[तल्लयः] the destruction of that; @deva[विद्वान्] + @deva[लिखति] = @deva[विद्वाँल्लिखति] a learned man writes.

---

[^1]: @deva[तोर्लि] । Pāṇ. VIII. 4. 60.
