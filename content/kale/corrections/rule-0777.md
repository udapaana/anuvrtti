In the following list are alphabetically arranged almost all the common Kṛt affixes forming nouns (substantive and adjective) with various significations from roots or derivative bases. @deva[अ]—(@deva[अच्], @deva[अण्], @deva[अप्], @deva[कञ्], @deva[खच्], @deva[खश्], @deva[खल्], @deva[घ], @deva[घञ्], @deva[ट], @deva[टक्], @deva[ड], @deva[ण], and @deva[श]):—

@deva[अच्]—denoting 'the agent' is added to—@deva[पच्] and other roots; @deva[पचतीति पचः] 'one who cooks;' @deva[चर्]—@deva[चरः], @deva[चुर्]—@deva[चोरः], @deva[भू]—@deva[भवः], @deva[नद्]—@deva[नदः] (@deva[नदी]);—to @deva[भृ] and @deva[पच्], when the words @deva[जार] and @deva[श्वन्] are prefixed to them respectively; @deva[जारभरा] 'an adulteress;' @deva[श्वपचः] 'a Chāndāla'—to @deva[हृ] when the noun governed by it is used prepositionally and when the idea of difficulty is not present or when the whole compound denotes 'age;' @deva[अंशं हरतीति अंशहरः] 'one entitled to a share, a heir'; but @deva[भारहारः] 'a load-bearer' (@deva[भार+हृ+अण्]); @deva[कवलहरः] 'a young child;' also when @deva[आ] is prefixed to it in the sense of 'in the habit of.' @deva[पुष्पाणि हर्तुं शीलमस्य असौ पुष्पहरः];—to the root @deva[ग्रह्] when the words @deva[शक्ति] 'a missile,' @deva[लाङ्गल] 'a plough,' @deva[अङ्कुश] 'an iron hook,' @deva[तोमर] 'a javelin,' @deva[यष्टि] 'a stick,' @deva[घट], @deva[घटी] and @deva[धनुष्] are prefixed to it; @deva[शक्तिं गृह्णातीति शक्तिग्रहः] 'one armed with a spear,' &c; @deva[लाङ्गलग्रहः] &c.; also when @deva[सूत्र] is prefixed to it and the root is used in the sense of 'wearing'; @deva[सूत्रग्रहः] 'wearing a sacred thread;' but @deva[सूत्रग्राहः] (@deva[सूत्र+ग्रह्+अण्]) 'one who takes in his hand a sacred thread;'—to @deva[अर्ह्] when a noun used objectively is prefixed to it; @deva[पूजामर्हतीति पूजार्हो ब्राह्मणः] 'a Brāhmaṇa deserving worship;'—to @deva[रम्] and @deva[जप्] when the words @deva[स्तम्ब] and @deva[कर्ण] in the Loc. are prefixed to them respectively; @deva[स्तम्बेरमः] 'an elephant,' @deva[कर्णेजपः] 'one who whispers into the ear, a spy;'—to any root when the word @deva[शं] is prefixed, @deva[शंकरः], @deva[शंभवः], @deva[शंवदः] &c.,—to @deva[शी] when a word showing the place of action precedes, @deva[खे शेते] @deva[ख-(खे)शयः] 'dwelling in the sky,' so @deva[हृच्छयः] 'dwelling in the heart, i. e Madana'; also when words like @deva[पार्श्व], @deva[उदर], @deva[पृष्ठ], &c. and @deva[उत्तान], &c. precede, @deva[पार्श्व—उदर—पृष्ठ—शयः] 'sleeping on the sides,' &c., @deva[उत्तानशयः] 'lying on the back with the face upwards.' so @deva[अवमूर्धशयः] (@deva[अवनतो मूर्धा अस्य तथा शेते]) i. e. 'with the face downwards.' When added to roots ending in @deva[इ] and to some others it forms abstract nouns, @deva[चि]—@deva[चयः] 'a collection,' @deva[जि]—@deva[जयः], @deva[भी]—@deva[भयं], @deva[वृष्]—@deva[वर्षः] 'a shower of rain.' &c.

@deva[अण्]—is added to roots when the words forming their objects are prefixed to them, @deva[कुंभकारः] 'a potter,' @deva[भारहारः]—When a subanta is prefixed to @deva[हन्] with @deva[सम्], the @deva[न्] of this root is changed to @deva[ट्] optionally; @deva[वर्णसंघातः] or-@deva[टः] 'a collection of words.'

@deva[अप्]—is added to roots ending in @deva[उ] and @deva[ऋ] short or long; it sometimes forms abstract nouns, sometimes shows the place or the instrument of action denoted by the root; @deva[स्तु]—@deva[स्तवः] praise, @deva[यु]—@deva[यवः] barley, @deva[पू]—@deva[पवः], @deva[भू]—@deva[भवः], @deva[कृ]—@deva[करः] the instrument of doing anything i. e. the hand, @deva[गृ]—@deva[गरः] poison, @deva[दृ]—@deva[दरः] fear, @deva[वृ]—@deva[वरः] a boon &c., @deva[स्तृ] with @deva[वि]—@deva[विष्टरः] a tree or a seat, @deva[विस्तरः] otherwise; @deva[हन्] with @deva[सम्] takes this affix; @deva[संघः] a collection; @deva[गम्] also takes it, @deva[गमः] : @deva[अद्] preceded by a preposition takes this affix and is changed to @deva[घस्]; @deva[निघसः], @deva[प्रघसः], @deva[विघसः] &c ; food or eating; (when no preposition precedes it takes @deva[घञ्]—@deva[घासः]); the roots @deva[जप्] and @deva[व्यध्] not preceded by a preposition take @deva[अप्]: @deva[जपः] muttering prayers, @deva[व्यधः] the act of piercing; (but when a preposition precedes, they take @deva[घञ्]; as @deva[उपजापः] secret whispering into the ear, separation, &c.); the roots @deva[स्वन्] and @deva[हस्] by themselves take @deva[अप्] or @deva[घञ्]; @deva[स्वन्]—@deva[स्वनः] or @deva[स्वानः] sound; @deva[हस्]—@deva[हसः] or @deva[हासः]; with a preposition they take the latter only; @deva[प्रस्वानः]; @deva[प्रहासः] &c.; @deva[यम्] without any preposition or with the preposition @deva[उप], @deva[नि], @deva[वि] and @deva[सम्] takes either @deva[अप्] or @deva[घञ्]; @deva[यमः] or @deva[यामः] restraint, control, &c, @deva[उपयमः] or @deva[यामः] marriage; similarly @deva[नियमः] or @deva[नियामः] &c.; the roots @deva[गद्], @deva[नद्], @deva[पद्] and @deva[स्वन्], with @deva[नि] take @deva[अप्] or @deva[घञ्]; @deva[निगदः] or @deva[निगादः] speech, @deva[निनदः] or @deva[निनादः] sound, &c @deva[क्वण्] with or without @deva[नि] takes @deva[अप्] or @deva[घञ्]; @deva[क्वणः] or @deva[क्वाणः], @deva[निक्वणः] or-@deva[क्वाणः] the sound of a lute; the root @deva[मद्] when a word other than a preposition is prefixed to it takes @deva[अप्]; with a preposition it takes @deva[घञ्]; @deva[धनमदः] the pride of wealth, @deva[उन्मादः] madness, insanity; but when preceded by @deva[प्र] or @deva[सम्] it takes @deva[अप्] when the meaning is joy; @deva[प्रमदः] or @deva[संमदः]; in other cases it takes @deva[घञ्]; @deva[प्रमादः], @deva[संमादः] carelessness, oversight, a blunder. The affixes @deva[अप्] and @deva[घञ्] are added to several other roots too numerous to note here; the difference between these is that @deva[घञ्] occasions Vṛddhi of the preceding vowel, @deva[अप्] does not

@deva[क]—is added to roots having @deva[इ], @deva[उ], @deva[ऋ], or @deva[ऌ] for their penultimate, and to the roots @deva[प्री] and @deva[कृ] and denotes the agent; @deva[लिख्]—@deva[लिखः] one who writes, @deva[क्षिप्]—@deva[क्षिपः] one who throws, @deva[बुध्]—@deva[बुधः] &c. @deva[प्री]—@deva[प्रियः] one who pleases; @deva[कृ]—@deva[किरः] one who scatters; it is also added to roots ending in @deva[आ] with or without any preposition, the final @deva[आ] being dropped; @deva[ज्ञा]—@deva[ज्ञः] or @deva[प्रज्ञः] one who knows, wise; @deva[ह्वे]—@deva[ह्वः] or @deva[आह्वः] one who calls; also when a subanta is used prepositionally with it; @deva[दा]—@deva[गोदः] one who gives cows or cuts the hair; @deva[पा]—@deva[द्विपः] (@deva[द्वाभ्यां पिबतीति]) an elephant; when added to @deva[स्था] it has various meanings; @deva[समस्थः] happy, @deva[विषमस्थः] placed in difficulties; @deva[प्रस्थः] a measure of corn, &c. This is also added to @deva[ग्रह्]—@deva[गृहं] a house, @deva[गृहाः] a wife, a house.

@deva[कञ्]—is added to @deva[दृश्] not meaning 'to see' when a pronoun precedes it; @deva[तद्+दृश्=अ=तादृशः] like that; also when the words @deva[समान] and @deva[अन्य] precede; @deva[सदृशः] like to, @deva[अन्यादृशः] like another. @deva[क्स] is added similarly; @deva[सदृक्षः], @deva[तादृक्षः] &c

@deva[खच्] and @deva[खश्]—Before these the @deva[अ] of the noun forming the object of the root with which it is used prepositionally inserts the syllable @deva[म्] after it. @deva[खच्] is added to the root @deva[वद्] after @deva[प्रिय] and @deva[वश]; @deva[प्रियं वदतीति प्रियंवदः] 'one who speaks sweetly;' @deva[वशंवदः] 'subject to the influence of, obedient to the will of;'—@deva[कृ] after the words @deva[क्षेम], @deva[प्रिय], @deva[मद्र], and @deva[भय]; @deva[क्षेमं]—@deva[प्रियं]-@deva[मद्रं]-@deva[करः] doing good, &c.; @deva[भयंकरः] causing fear, dreadful, @deva[अभयंकरः];—to the root @deva[गम्] after a subanta; @deva[विहङ्गमः] passing through the sky, a bird;—to the roots @deva[भृ], @deva[तृ], @deva[दृ], @deva[जि], @deva[धृ], @deva[सह्], @deva[तप्] and @deva[दम्] when the whole is a name; @deva[विश्वंभरः] God; @deva[रथंतरं] a portion of the Sāma Veda; @deva[पतिंवरा] a bride who chooses her husband; @deva[शत्रुंजयः] an elephant, @deva[युगंधरः] name of a mountain; @deva[परंतपः] name of a king; @deva[अरिंदमः] name of a king;—to @deva[यम्] after @deva[वाच्], @deva[वाचंयमः] one who restrains his speech for the observation of a vow;—to the roots @deva[सह्] and @deva[दृ] after the words @deva[सर्व] and @deva[पुर] respectively; @deva[सर्वंसहा] the earth, @deva[पुरंदरः] Indra;—to @deva[कष्] after the words @deva[सर्व], @deva[कूल], @deva[अभ्र] and @deva[करीष]; @deva[सर्वंकषः] all destroying, all powerful; also a rogue; @deva[कूलंकषा] (a river) sweeping away its banks; @deva[अभ्रंकषः] dashing against, coming in collision with the clouds as the wind; @deva[करीषंकषा] blowing away dry cowdung, as a strong wind or gale. @deva[खश्] is added to @deva[एज्] cau. as in @deva[जनमेजयः] making the people tremble with fear; name of a king,—to @deva[अज्], @deva[धे], @deva[तुद्] and @deva[हा] 'to go' after the words @deva[वात], @deva[शुनी], @deva[तिल] and @deva[शर्द्ध] flatulence; @deva[वातमजः] facing the wind, a kind of deer; @deva[शुनिंधयः] a kitten; @deva[तिलंतुदः] an oilman; and @deva[शर्द्धंजहाः] causing flatulence (@deva[माषाः]) a kind of bean;—to @deva[धे] and @deva[ध्मा] after the words @deva[स्तन] and @deva[नाडी], respectively @deva[स्तनंधयः] a baby sucking breast; @deva[नाडिं-(डीं)धमः] a goldsmith;—to @deva[तुद्] after @deva[विधु] and @deva[अरुष्]; @deva[विधुंतुदः] the afflictor of the moon i e. Rāhu; @deva[अरुंतुदः] (@deva[अरूंषि मर्माणि तुदतीति]) wounding the vital parts, painful;—to @deva[पच्] after words showing measure as @deva[प्रस्थंपचा स्थाली] @deva[खारिंपचः कटाहः] and after @deva[मित] and @deva[नख]-@deva[मितंपचः] one who cooks measured corn, a miser; @deva[नखंपचा] nail-scorching (as @deva[यवागूः]);—to @deva[दृश्] and @deva[तप्] after the words @deva[असूर्य] and @deva[ललाट]; @deva[असूर्यंपश्या] those who do not see the sun i e the queens of a king who are shut up in the harem; @deva[ललाटंतपः] scorching the forehead;—to @deva[दृश्], @deva[मद्] and @deva[ध्मा] after @deva[उग्र], @deva[इरम्] and @deva[पाणि] respectively; @deva[उग्रंपश्य] of a fierce aspect, @deva[इरंमदः] lightning; @deva[पाणिंधमः] a road (shrouded in such darkness that one has to clap one's hands for driving away serpents &c. that may be in the way); to @deva[मन्] meaning 'to consider oneself' as, @deva[पण्डितंमन्य], a pedant, who considers himself a Pandita; @deva[गांमन्यः] one who considers himself a cow i e. who is very humble; &c.

@deva[खल्]—is added to any root with @deva[ईषत्], @deva[दुर्] or @deva[सु] prefixed to it when the idea of ease or difficulty is present, @deva[ईषत्करः] done easily; @deva[दुष्कर] done with difficulty; @deva[सुकर] done easily, so @deva[दुःशासन], @deva[दुर्योधन], &c. &c.

@deva[घ]—is added in the sense of the place or the instrument of the action or forms abstract nouns; @deva[कृ] with @deva[आ]—@deva[आकरः] a mine; @deva[खन्] with @deva[आ], @deva[आखनः] a spade; @deva[पण्] with @deva[आ], @deva[आपणः] a place of traffic; @deva[कष्]—@deva[निकषः] a touch-stone; @deva[चर्]—@deva[गोचरः] a pasture-ground; @deva[संचरः] a path; @deva[वह्]—@deva[वहः] a shoulder; @deva[निगमः] that by which the people are guided, the Veda; @deva[व्रजः] and @deva[व्यजः] a fan.
