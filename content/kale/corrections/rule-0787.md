## Nominative Case with Indeclinables Used Adjectivally

When an indeclinable used with an adjectival force takes the place of the verb, the object governed by it is put in the Nominative case.[^1] @deva[विषवृक्षोपि संवर्ध्य स्वयं छेत्तुमसांप्रतम्] (Kum. II. 55.) it is not fit to cut down even a poisonous tree having first reared it up; here the indeclinable @deva[असांप्रतं] is equivalent to @deva[न युज्यते] and the whole sentence to @deva[वृक्षं संवर्ध्य तं छेत्तुं असाम्प्रतं] (@deva[न युज्यते]) @deva[योपि विषवृक्षः स्यात्।]

**Concord of the Adjective with the Substantive.**

---

[^1]: @deva[निपातेनाभिहिते कर्माणि न विभक्तिपरिगणनस्य प्रायिकत्वात्।] Vāman.
