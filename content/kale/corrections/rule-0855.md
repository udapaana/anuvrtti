## Genitive with Words Denoting Frequency of Time

[^1] Words having the senses of @deva[कृत्वः] i. e denoting frequency of time, such as @deva[द्विः], @deva[त्रिः], @deva[पञ्चकृत्वः] &c. govern the Genitive of the time in the sense of the Locative; @deva[पञ्चकृत्वोह्नो भोजनम्] taking food five times a day; @deva[द्विरह्नो भुङ्क्ते], &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[कृत्वोर्थप्रयोगे कालेऽधिकरणे ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 64.
