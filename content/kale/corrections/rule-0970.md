## The Particles @deva[अथ] and @deva[इति].

[^1]@deva[अथ] is used in the following senses:—(1) as a sign of auspiciousness [^2]; @deva[अथातो ब्रह्मजिज्ञासा] now begins the inquiry about Brahma; *cf.* the Bhāṣya on this sūtra (2) marks the beginning or commencement of a work; @deva[अथेदमारभ्यते प्रथमं तन्त्रम्] now is begun the 1st Tantra; so @deva[अथ योगानुशासनम्], &c; (3) 'then' after that,' &c; @deva[अथ प्रजानामधिप.] &c. (Rag. II. 1.) After that i. e. passing of the night) the lord of the earth, &c. (4) asks a question @deva[अथ भगवान् लोकानुग्रहाय कुशली काश्यपः] is the venerable Kashyapa all right that he may oblige the world? @deva[अथ शक्नोषि भोक्तुं] are you able to eat? (5) 'and, including'; @deva[भीमः अथ अर्जुनः] Bhīma and also Arjuna; (6) 'if;' @deva[अथ मरणमवश्यमेव जन्तोः] if death is sure to befall a creature, &c.

## Footnotes

[^1]: @deva[मङ्गलानन्तरारम्भप्रश्नकात्स्न्येष्वथो अथ।] Amara
