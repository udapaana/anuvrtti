title: "Genitive Compounds with @deva[पूर्व], @deva[अपर], @deva[अधर], @deva[उत्तर], @deva[अर्ध]"
pages: [127.png, 128.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1]@deva[पूर्व], @deva[अपर], @deva[अधर], @deva[उत्तर] and @deva[अर्ध] n. are compounded with nouns in the Genitive expressive of the whole and are placed first; e. g. @deva[पूर्वं कायस्य पूर्वकायः] the forepart of the body; @deva[अपरकायः] @deva[अधरकायः] &c. @deva[अर्धं पिप्पल्याः अर्धपिप्पली]; but @deva[ग्रामार्धः] as (@deva[अर्ध] is mas. here).

**N. B.** In order that the above rule should hold the noun expressive of the whole must denote a single object; hence @deva[पूर्वः] the first @deva[छात्राणां] among pupils, @deva[अर्धं पिप्पलीनाम्] half of a number of pippalis &c. and not @deva[पूर्वछात्रः] &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[पूर्वापराधरोत्तरमेकदेशिनैकाधिकरणे । अर्धं नपुंसकम् ।] Pāṇ. II. 2. 1, 2.
