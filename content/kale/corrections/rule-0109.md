The Nom. sing. of @deva[अनेहस्] time, @deva[पुरुदंसस्] name of Indra, and @deva[उशनस्] name of Śukrāchārya is @deva[अनेहा], @deva[पुरुदंसा] and @deva[उशना] respectively; the Voc. sing. of @deva[उशनस्] is @deva[उशनन्], @deva[उशन], and @deva[उशन]; the other forms are regular.
