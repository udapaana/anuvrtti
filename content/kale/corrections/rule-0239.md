The following words, when at the end of a Tatpurusha, drop the final vowel, together with the following consonant if there be any, and add @deva[अ] under the circumstances mentioned:—

**(a)**[^1] @deva[रात्रि] when preceded by a numeral, an indeclinable, a word expressive of a part of the whole, such as @deva[पूर्व], @deva[अपर], &c., or by the words @deva[सर्व], @deva[संख्यात] and @deva[पुण्य]; as @deva[द्वयोः रात्र्योः समाहारः द्विरात्रम्] an aggregate of two nights (Dwi.); @deva[अतिक्रान्तो रात्रिमतिरात्रः] that has passed a night, the dead of night; @deva[पूर्वं रात्रेः पूर्वरात्रः] the first part of the night; @deva[सर्वा रात्रिः सर्वरात्रः], @deva[संख्याता रात्रिः संख्यातरात्रः], @deva[पुण्यरात्रः] an auspicious night.

The same happens when @deva[रात्रि] is preceded by @deva[अहन्] in a Dwandwa; as @deva[अहश्च रात्रिश्च अहोरात्रः].

**(b)**[^2] @deva[राजन्], @deva[अहन्] and @deva[सखि]; as @deva[परमः राजा परमराजः] a great king; @deva[मद्राणां राजा मद्रराजः], @deva[उत्तमं अहः उत्तमाहः] an excellent day, @deva[एकं च तदहः एकाहः]; @deva[द्वयोरह्नोः समाहारः द्व्यहः], so @deva[त्र्यहः], @deva[सप्ताहः], &c. @deva[पुण्यं अहः पुण्याहं] a holy day, @deva[सुदिनाहं], (vide @ref[245], b.), @deva[कृष्णस्य सखा कृष्णसखः] &c.

**(c) Exceptions**[^3]—@deva[अहन्] when preceded by an indeclinable, or a word denoting a part of the whole, or by @deva[सर्व] or by a numeral with a Taddhita affix added to the whole compound, becomes @deva[अह्न]; as @deva[अतिक्रान्तमहः] exceeding a day (in duration) @deva[अत्यह्नः], @deva[पूर्वाह्नः] forenoon, @deva[सर्वाह्नः] the whole day, @deva[संख्याताह्नः], @deva[द्वयोरह्नोः भवः द्व्यह्नः] (@deva[-ह्ना] fem.), @deva[द्व्यह्नप्रियः] &c.; with @deva[संख्यात] the change is optional; as @deva[संख्याताह्नः], or @deva[हः].

**N. B.** The[^4] @deva[न्] of the substitute is changed to @deva[ण्] when preceded by @deva[र्] belonging to the first member ending in @deva[अ]; as @deva[सर्वाह्णः]; but @deva[परागतं अहः पराह्नः] (as @deva[परा] ends in @deva[आ]).

**(d)**[^5] @deva[तक्षन्] when preceded by @deva[ग्राम] or @deva[कौट], and @deva[श्वन्] by @deva[अति] or an inanimate object with which it is compounded; e. g. @deva[ग्रामस्य तक्षा ग्रामतक्षः] 'a village carpenter i. e. one not very skilful;' @deva[कुट्यां भवः कौट] (@deva[स्वतन्त्रः]) @deva[स चासौ तक्षा च कौटतक्षः] 'an independent carpenter'; @deva[अतिश्वः] [@deva[वराहः]] 'fleeter than a dog'; @deva[अतिश्वी] (worse than a dog's life) @deva[सेवा]; @deva[आकर्षः श्वा इव आकर्षश्वः] 'a dog like i. e. bad or unlucky throw of dice;' but @deva[वानरश्वा] 'a monkey like a dog'.

**(e)**[^6] @deva[सक्थि] when preceded by @deva[उत्तर], @deva[मृग] or @deva[पूर्व] or by a word denoting an inanimate object with which it is compounded; @deva[उत्तरसक्थम्] 'the upper part of the thigh', @deva[मृगसक्थम्] 'the thigh of a deer,' @deva[पूर्वसक्थम्], @deva[फलकामिव सक्थि फलकसक्थम्] 'a thigh like a board'.

**(f)** A numeral when compounded in a Tat. @deva[निर्गतानि त्रिंशतः निस्त्रिंशानि वर्षाणि] (@deva[चैत्रस्य]) more than thirty; @deva[निर्गतः त्रिंशतांगुलिभ्यः निस्त्रिंशः खड्गः] a sword more than thirty fingers in length.
