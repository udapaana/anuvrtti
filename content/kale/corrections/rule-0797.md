## Accusative Case Denotes the Object

The Accusative denotes the object i. e. the person or thing upon whom or which the effect of an action takes place; @deva[हरिं सेवते] he worships Hari; @deva[ग्रामं गच्छन् तृणं स्पृशति] going to a village he touches grass.[^1]

---

[^1]: But when the relation of object and verb is expressed by the passive termination the noun forming the object is put in the nominative case; @deva[हरिः सेव्यते].
