Nouns ending in @deva[अत्], @deva[मत्], and @deva[वत्]:—

@deva[अ] is lengthened in the Nom. sing.[^1], and @deva[न्] is inserted between @deva[अ] and the final @deva[त्] in the first five inflections. The final @deva[त्] is dropped in the Nom. sing.; the @deva[अ] of @deva[महत्] is lengthened before this @deva[न्] except in the Voc. sing.
