Words ending in @deva[ए] and @deva[ऐ].

Here simply the terminations should be added and *Sandhi* rules observed.

Decline similarly @deva[स्मृते] (@deva[स्मृतः तत् इ. येन]) one who has thought of *Kāma*.

The neu. @deva[प्ररै] (@deva[प्रकृष्टा रै यस्य]) changed to @deva[प्ररि] (by @deva[एच इग्घ्रस्वादेशे] Pāṇ. I. 1. 48. and I. 2. 47.) should be declined like @deva[वारि] except before the consonantal terminations, when it should be declined like @deva[रै] *m. f.*

[^1]: The Kau. does not specially mention this form. Just as it stops after giving the Nom. forms in the case of words like @deva[रै], @deva[गो], @deva[स्मृतो] &c., so does it do in the case of @deva[से] also, implying thereby that the Voc. forms of @deva[से] are also like the Nom. ones. But the Sutra @deva[एङ्ह्रस्वात्संबुद्धेः] Pāṇ. VI. 1. 69 (@deva[एङन्तात् ह्रस्वान्ताच्च अङ्गात् हल् लुप्यते सम्बुद्धेः चेत्]) (Kau.) seems to apply in the present case and by dropping the final @deva[स्] we get the form @deva[से].
