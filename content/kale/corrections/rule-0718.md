The roots @deva[वद्] implying reproach, @deva[पण्] meaning 'to transact business,' and @deva[वृ] 9 Ātm. not denoting limit or restraint, take the suffix @deva[य]; as @deva[अवद्यं पापं] 'sin is reprehensible or merits condemnation;' but @deva[अनुद्यं] (@deva[अन्+वद्+क्यप्] i. e. @deva[य]) @deva[गुरुनाम] 'the name of a preceptor ought not to be uttered (out of respect),' @deva[पण्या गौः] 'a cow is saleable;' but @deva[पाण्यः] (@deva[पण्+ण्यत्] i. e. @deva[य]) @deva[ब्राह्मणः] 'a Brāhmaṇa deserving praise;' @deva[वर्य] 'that can be chosen or sought after;' as @deva[शतेन वर्या कन्या] 'hundred men (i. e. any one) can seek the hand of a girl;' but @deva[वृत्या] (@deva[वृ+क्यप्] i. e. @deva[य]) @deva[कन्या] 'to be married by a particular man.'
