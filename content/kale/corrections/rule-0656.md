## Formation with @deva[क्यष्] (@deva[य]) Affix

The affix @deva[य] is added to @deva[लोहित] and some other words and to words ending in the affix @deva[आ] (@deva[डाच्]) and the nominal base thus formed is conjugated in the Parasm. and Atmane.; *e. g.* @deva[लोहित]—@deva[लोहितायति-ते] 'becomes red.' @deva[पटपटायते] 'utters the sound *Patpat*.'

**(f) By means of the affix @deva[इ] (@deva[णिङ्] and @deva[णिच्]) and conjugated in Parasm. and Atm.**
