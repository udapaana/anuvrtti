## Genitive/Dative with Benediction Words

[^1] The words @deva[आयुष्यं], @deva[मद्रं], @deva[भद्रं], @deva[कुशलं], @deva[सुखं], @deva[अर्थः] and @deva[हितं] and words having the same sense, when used in a sentence containing a benediction, govern the Dative or the Genitive case; @deva[आयुष्यं चिरंजीवितं कृष्णाय कृष्णस्य वा भूयात्] (Sid. Kau.); may Krishna live long; similarly @deva[मद्रं], @deva[भद्रं], @deva[कुशलं], @deva[निरामयं], @deva[सुखं], @deva[शं], @deva[अर्थः], @deva[प्रयोजनं], @deva[हितं], @deva[पथ्यं वा भूयात्] (Sid. Kau.).

---

[^1]: @deva[चतुर्थी चाशिष्यायुष्यमद्रभद्रकुशलसुखार्थहितैः ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 73.
