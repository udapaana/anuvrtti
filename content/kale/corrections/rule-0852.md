## Genitive with @deva[नाथ्] (to wish) in Benedictions

[^1] @deva[नाथ्] meaning 'to wish' governs the Genitive when the idea of a benediction is implied; @deva[धृत्या नाथस्व] wish to have patience; @deva[धनस्य नाथते] desires to have wealth. So @deva[सर्पिषः नाथनम्].

---

[^1]: @deva[आशिषि नाथः] Pāṇ. II. 3. 55.
