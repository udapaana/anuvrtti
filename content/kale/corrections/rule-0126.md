## WORDS OF IRREGULAR DECLENSION.

@deva[अस्थि] n. a bone, @deva[दधि] n. curds, @deva[सक्थि] n. a thigh and @deva[अक्षि] n. an eye, become @deva[अस्थन्], @deva[दधन्], @deva[सक्थन्], and @deva[अक्षन्] respectively before the vowel terminations beginning with the I. sing.[^1] and then they follow the declension of nouns in @deva[न्]; in other respects they are declined like @deva[वारि].

@deva[अस्थि].

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. | @deva[अस्थि] | @deva[अस्थिनी] | @deva[अस्थीनि] |
| V. | @deva[अस्थे-स्थि] | ,, | ,, |
| A. | @deva[अस्थि] | ,, | ,, |
| I. | @deva[अस्थ्ना] | @deva[अस्थिभ्याम्] | @deva[अस्थिभिः] |
| D. | @deva[अस्थ्ने] | @deva[अस्थिभ्याम्] | @deva[अस्थिभ्यः] |
| Ab. | @deva[अस्थ्नः] | ,, | ,, |
| G. | ,, | @deva[अस्थ्नोः] | @deva[अस्थ्नाम्] |
| L. | @deva[अस्थ्नि-स्थनि] | ,, | @deva[अस्थिषु] |

@deva[दधि] and the rest should be similarly declined.
