## Dative with Verbs of Telling and Sending

With verbs of telling such as @deva[कथ्], @deva[ख्या], @deva[शंस्], @deva[चक्ष्], @deva[निविद्] cau. &c. and of sending such as @deva[हि] with @deva[प्र], @deva[सृज्] with @deva[वि], &c. the Dative of the indirect object is used; @deva[रामभिष्वसनदर्शनोत्सुकं मैथिलाय कथयांबभूव सः ।] (Rag. XI. 37.); He told the king of Mithilā that Rāma was eager to see the bow; @deva[आख्याहि मे को भवानुग्ररूपः] (Bhag. XI. 31.) Tell me, who thou art in this fierce form, &c., @deva[उपस्थितां होमवेलां गुरवे निवेदयामि] (S'ak. IV.) I will tell my preceptor that it is time to offer the morning oblations, @deva[हरिरस्मै सुरांगनां प्रजिघाय] (Rag. VIII. 79.) Indra sent a heavenly damsel against him (i. e. to disturb his contemplations); @deva[रक्षस्तस्मै महोपलं प्रजिघाय] (Rag. XV. 21.)
