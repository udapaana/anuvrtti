When @deva[इ], @deva[उ], @deva[ऋ] and @deva[ऌ], short or long, are followed by a dissimilar vowel, @deva[य्], @deva[व्], @deva[र्] and @deva[ल्] are respectively substituted for them[^1]; e. g. @deva[इति] + @deva[आह] = @deva[इत्याह], @deva[सुधी] + @deva[उपास्यः] = @deva[सुध्युपास्यः] adored by the wise; @deva[मधु] + @deva[अरिः] = @deva[मध्वरिः] the enemy of @deva[मधु] i. e. Vishṇu, @deva[धातृ] + @deva[अंशः] = @deva[धात्रंशः] a portion of the creator; @deva[ऌ] + @deva[आकृतिः] = @deva[लाकृतिः] a figure like that of @deva[ऌ] &c.

**Ob.**—Several of the above words may have more than one form when combined; e. g. @deva[सुधी] + @deva[उपास्यः] = @deva[सुध्य्] + @deva[उपास्यः] by the general rule:—

**N. B.** The following rules and the two (a), (b) given under @ref[20], though they properly form the subject of the next section, are given here to avoid confusion. Ordinary students may pass over these except sec. (b) of the present art.

**(a)** A consonant except @deva[ङ्], preceded by a vowel, is optionally doubled, if no vowel follows[^2]; @deva[सुध्य्] + @deva[उपास्यः] = @deva[सुध्युपास्यः] and @deva[सुध्ध्य्] + @deva[उपास्यः]:—

**(b)** When a consonant, except a nasal or a semivowel, is followed by a soft unaspirate or aspirate (3rd or 4th letter of a class) it is changed to the soft unaspirate (3rd letter) of its class[^3]; @deva[सुध्ध्य्] + @deva[उपास्यः] = @deva[सुद्ध्युपास्य]

**(c)** Semivowels, when preceded by a letter of the first five classes except @deva[ञ्] are optionally doubled[^4]; thus @deva[सुध्य्] + @deva[उपास्यः] = @deva[सुध्य्य्] + @deva[उपास्यः] and @deva[सुध्ध्य्य्] + @deva[उपास्यः]; @deva[सुद्ध्य्] + @deva[उपास्यः] = @deva[सुद्ध्य्य्] + @deva[उपास्यः] and @deva[सुद्ध्य्य्] + @deva[उपास्यः]। Thus there are four forms ultimately, viz. @deva[सुध्युपास्यः], @deva[सुद्ध्युपास्यः] and @deva[सुद्ध्य्युपास्यः]।

Similarly @deva[मधु] + @deva[अरिः] = @deva[मध्वरिः], @deva[मध्ध्वरिः], @deva[मद्ध्वरिः], and @deva[मद्ध्व्वरिः]; @deva[धातृ] + @deva[अंशः] = @deva[धात्रंशः] and @deva[धात्त्रंशः]. In @deva[ऌ] + @deva[आकृतिः] of course there is no possibility of any other form.

**(d)** A consonant except @deva[ङ्], coming after @deva[र्] or @deva[ह्] preceded by a vowel is optionally doubled[^5]; e. g. @deva[हरि] + @deva[अनुभवः] = @deva[हर्य्] + @deva[अनुभवः] = @deva[हर्यनुभवः] by the general rule; and optionally @deva[हर्य्य्] + @deva[अनुभवः] = @deva[हर्य्यनुभवः] (Hari's experience) by this rule; so @deva[न हि] + @deva[अस्ति] = @deva[न ह्यस्ति] and @deva[न ह्य्यस्ति]।
