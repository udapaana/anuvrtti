Words ending in @deva[ॠ] and @deva[ऌ].

There are no words ending in @deva[ॠ] or @deva[ऌ]; therefore, the imitative sounds of the roots @deva[कॄ] and @deva[तॄ] and @deva[गम्ऌ] and @deva[शक्ऌ] are taken to show what the forms of such words will be, if there be need to use such words.
