Nouns expressive of relationship like @deva[पितृ] m. a father, @deva[मातृ] f. a mother, @deva[देवृ] m. a husband's brother, &c. with the exception of @deva[नप्तृ] a grandson, @deva[भर्तृ] a husband and @deva[स्वसृ] a sister, as also the words @deva[शंस्तृ] one who praises (Un. II. 92), @deva[नृ] a man (Un. II. 98) and @deva[सव्येष्टृ] a charioteer, do not change their @deva[ऋ] to @deva[आर्] in the N. dual and plural and Acc. sing. and dual; e. g.

@deva[पितृ]

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. | @deva[पिता] | @deva[पितरौ] | @deva[पितरः] |
| V. | @deva[पितः (र्)] | ,, | ,, |
| A. | @deva[पितरम्] | ,, | @deva[पितॄन्] |

The rest like @deva[धातृ].

Decline similarly @deva[भ्रातृ], @deva[जामातृ], @deva[देवृ], @deva[शंस्तृ], @deva[सव्येष्टृ] and… @deva[नृ]. The Gen. plu. of @deva[नृ] is @deva[नृणाम्] or @deva[नॄणाम्][^1]

@deva[मातृ]

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. | @deva[माता] | @deva[मातरौ] | @deva[मातरः] |
| V. | @deva[मातः (र्)] | ,, | ,, |
| A. | @deva[मातरम्] | ,, | @deva[मातॄः] |

The rest like @deva[स्वसृ].

Decline similarly @deva[यातृ] a husband's brother's wife, @deva[दुहितृ] a daughter, and @deva[ननान्दृ] or @deva[ननन्दृ][^2] a husband's sister.
