[^1] @deva[हृदय] becomes @deva[हृद्] necessarily when followed by @deva[लेख] ( formed with the affix @deva[अण्] ), and @deva[लास] and the Tad. affixes @deva[य] ( @deva[यद्] ) and @deva[अ] ( @deva[अण्] ), and optionally when followed by @deva[शोक] and @deva[रोग] and the Tad. @deva[य] ( @deva[ष्यञ्] ); @deva[हृदयं लिखतीति हृल्लेखः] 'heart-ache'; ( @deva[घञि तु हृदयलेख] Sid. Kau. ), @deva[हृल्लासः] 'disquietude,' 'hiccough'; @deva[हृदयस्य प्रियं हृद्यं] 'agreeable to the heart'; @deva[हृदयस्येदं हार्दं], @deva[हृच्छोकः] or @deva[हृदयशोकः] 'the pang of the heart;' @deva[हृदयरोगः], @deva[हृद्रोगः].
