title: "Insertion of @deva[ना] in Ninth Class"
pages: [256.png]
verdict: approved
---
In the ninth class @deva[ना] is inserted between the root and the terminations. @deva[ना] becomes @deva[न्] before the weak terminations beginning with a vowel, and @deva[नी] before the weak terminations beginning with a consonant.[^1]

[^1]: @deva[क्र्यादिभ्यः श्ना] । Pāṇ. III. 1. 81.
