---
key: rule:445
title: "Reduplication of Conjunct Consonants"
pages: [282.png]
verdict: approved
---
If a conjunct consonant begins a root, the first consonant only with the following vowel is reduplicated; e. g. @deva[प्रच्छ्]–@deva[पप्रच्छ्].

(a) If the first member of a conjunct consonant, however, be a sibilant (@deva[श्], @deva[ष्] or @deva[स्]) and the second a hard consonant, the hard consonant is reduplicated. @deva[स्पर्ध्]–@deva[पस्पर्ध्], @deva[श्चुत्]–@deva[चुश्चुत्]; but @deva[स्वन्]–@deva[सस्वन्] &c.
