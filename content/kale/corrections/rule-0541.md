## Root Changes in Second Aorist

The roots @deva[अस्], @deva[ख्या], @deva[पत्], @deva[वच्], @deva[शास्], @deva[श्वि] and @deva[ह्वे] become @deva[अस्थ्], @deva[ख्य], @deva[पप्त्], @deva[वोच्], @deva[शिष्], @deva[श्व], and @deva[ह्व], respectively; e. g. @deva[आस्थत्], @deva[अख्यत्], @deva[अवोचत्], @deva[अशिषत्] &c. 3rd sing.
