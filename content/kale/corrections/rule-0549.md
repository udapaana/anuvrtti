## Third Aorist of Bases with Initial Vowels

Bases with initial vowels:—

(a) If a root begins with a vowel and ends in a single consonant, the consonant is reduplicated and @deva[इ] added to it in the reduplicative syllable: @deva[अद्]=@deva[अद्द्]=@deva[आदिद्]=@deva[आदिदत्]-@deva[त]; @deva[आप्]=@deva[आपिपत्]-@deva[त]; @deva[ऊह्]=@deva[औजिहत्]-@deva[त], &c.

(b) If it ends in a conjunct consonant with @deva[न्], @deva[द्] or @deva[र्] for its first member, the second member of the conjunct is reduplicated: @deva[उन्द्]=@deva[उन्द्द्]=@deva[उन्दिद्] and finally @deva[औन्दिदत्]-@deva[त] 3 sing; similarly @deva[आट्टिट्टत्]-@deva[त] from @deva[अट्ट्] (considered as @deva[अद्द्], otherwise @deva[आटिट्टत्]), @deva[आर्जिहत्]-@deva[त] from @deva[अर्ह्]; @deva[आर्जिजत्]-@deva[त] from @deva[अर्ज्], &c.

(c) The roots @deva[ऊर्ण्], @deva[अङ्क्], @deva[अङ्ग्], @deva[अन्ध्], @deva[अंस्], @deva[अर्थ्] Ātm. and some others, substitute @deva[अ] for @deva[इ] in the reduplicative syllable; 3 sing. @deva[औननत्], @deva[आब्रकत्], @deva[आञ्जगत्], @deva[आन्दधत्], @deva[आंससत्], @deva[आर्तथत्]; &c.
