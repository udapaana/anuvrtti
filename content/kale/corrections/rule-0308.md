**(a)** A *prâtipadika*[^1] ending in @deva[कर], except @deva[यत्कर], @deva[तत्कर], @deva[किंकर][^6] and @deva[बहुकर]; @deva[घ्न], @deva[सर], preceded by @deva[पुरः], @deva[अग्रतः], @deva[अग्रे] and @deva[पूर्व]; and @deva[चर] preceded by @deva[सेना], @deva[दाय] and words expressive of place; the words @deva[नद], @deva[चोर], @deva[देव], @deva[ग्राह], @deva[गर], @deva[प्लव], and @deva[नद], nominal bases ending in @deva[एय], nominal and verbal derivatives formed by the affix @deva[अ] causing Vṛiddhi or Guṇa, such as @deva[औपगः], @deva[औत्सः], @deva[कुंभकारः], @deva[भारहारः], @deva[यादृशः], @deva[तादृशः], &c. and words ending in the Tad. affixes @deva[द्वयस], @deva[दघ्न], @deva[मात्र] and @deva[इक] ( with some exceptions ) and in the kṛit affix @deva[त्वर] form their *feminine* in @deva[ई] when they are not used adjectively; *e. g.* @deva[भोगकरी] conducing to pleasures; @deva[एककरी] &c.; @deva[पातिघ्नी], @deva[पित्तघ्नी], &c ; @deva[अग्रेसरी], &c.; @deva[सेनाचरी], @deva[कुरुचरी], a woman of the Kuru country; @deva[मत्स्यचरी], &c.; @deva[नदी], @deva[देवी], @deva[सूदी], &c.; @deva[सौपर्णेयी], @deva[वैनतेयी], &c.; @deva[ऐन्द्री], @deva[औत्सी], &c ; @deva[कुंभकारी], @deva[नगस्कारी], &c.; @deva[उरुद्वयसी-दघ्नी-मात्री], measuring or reaching as far as the knee; &c. @deva[आक्षिकी], @deva[लावणिकी], &c.; @deva[यादृशी], @deva[तादृशी], @deva[इत्वरी], evanescent, an unchaste woman &c.; @deva[गत्वरी] &c.; but @deva[किंकरा], @deva[बहुकुरुचरा नगरी].

**(b)** [^2] Also words ending in the Tad. affixes @deva[न], @deva[स्न], @deva[ईक], @deva[य] ( causing Vṛiddhi ) and the words @deva[तरुण] and @deva[तलुन]; @deva[स्त्रैणी], @deva[पौंस्नी] fit for a man; @deva[शाक्तीकी], @deva[तरुणी], @deva[तलुनी], &c. also words ending in the krit. affix @deva[अन] causing a nasal to be inserted before it; @deva[आढ्यंकरणी].

**(c)** [^3] Words indicative of the stages of life except the last take the *fem.* @deva[ई]; @deva[कुमारी], @deva[किशोरी], @deva[बर्करी], @deva[वधूटी], @deva[चिरण्टी] ( both meaning a 'a young woman' ); but @deva[वृद्धा], @deva[स्थविरा] &c. as these imply old age; @deva[कन्या] is an exception.

**(d)** **Obs.**—[^4] The words @deva[केवल], @deva[मामक], @deva[भागधेय], @deva[पाप], @deva[अपर], @deva[समान], @deva[आर्यकृत], @deva[सुमङ्गल] and @deva[भेषज] form their *fem.* in @deva[ई] when they are used as names ( or in the Veda ); @deva[केवली], @deva[मामकी], @deva[समानी], @deva[आर्यकृती] &c; but @deva[केवला], @deva[समाना], &c. when they are not names.

**(e)** The words @deva[नर्तक], @deva[खनक], @deva[रञ्जक] and @deva[रजक], those derived by adding the Krit affixes @deva[आक] and @deva[त्र] ( added to certain roots ) and the words included in the @deva[गौरादिगण][^5] form their *fem.* in @deva[ई]; @deva[नर्तकी], @deva[रजकी] &c. @deva[कुट्टाकी] one who divides or cuts; @deva[लुंटाकी] robbing or stealing, @deva[धात्री], &c.; @deva[गौरी], @deva[मनुषी], @deva[शृंगी], @deva[हरिणी], @deva[मातामही], @deva[पितामही] &c.; @deva[सुंदर] has @deva[सुंदरा] and @deva[सुंदरी].
