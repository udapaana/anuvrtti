## Insertion of @deva[र्], @deva[रि], or @deva[री] in Parasmaipada Frequentatives

@deva[र्] or the syllable @deva[रि] or @deva[री] is inserted between the @deva[अ] of the reduplicative syllable and the radical consonant of roots ending in short @deva[ऋ] or having it for their penultimate; similarly @deva[ल्] or @deva[लि] or @deva[ली] is inserted in the case of @deva[क्लृप्]; @deva[वृत्]=@deva[ववृत्]—@deva[वर्वृत्] or @deva[वरिवृत्] or @deva[वरीवृत्]; @deva[कृ]—@deva[चर्कृ], or @deva[चरिकृ] or @deva[चरीकृ]; @deva[क्लृप्]—@deva[चल्क्लृप्], or @deva[चलिक्लृप्] @deva[चलीक्लृप्]; @deva[दृश्]—@deva[दर्दृश्], @deva[दरिदृश्] or @deva[दरीदृश्].
