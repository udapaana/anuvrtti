@deva[तर] and @deva[तम] when attached to verbs, and indeclinables forming adverbs, assume the forms of @deva[तराम्] and @deva[तमाम्][^1]; @deva[पचतितराम्], @deva[पचतितमाम्]; @deva[उच्चैस्तराम्]; @deva[उच्चैस्तमाम्]; @deva[नितराम्] @deva[नितमाम्]; or @deva[सुतराम्] &c.; but @deva[उच्चैस्तरः] adj. (higher).

---

[^1]: @deva[किमेत्तिङव्ययघादाम्वद्रव्यप्रकर्षे ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 11. @deva[आम्] is added to @deva[तर] and @deva[तम] after @deva[किम्], or a word ending in @deva[ए] or a subanta or tiñanta (@deva[तिङन्त]) except when they form adjectives.
