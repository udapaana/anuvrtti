## Genitive with Verbs of Incorrect Knowledge, Memory, Control, Compassion

The root @deva[ज्ञा][^1] meaning 'to have an incorrect knowledge of' governs the Genitive; @deva[तैलं सर्पिषो जानीते] supposes oil to be ghee; but @deva[सर्पिर्जानीते].

(a) Verbs implying 'to think of, to remember,' such as @deva[स्मृ], @deva[इ] with @deva[अधि], 'to be master of,' such as @deva[ईश्], @deva[भू] with @deva[प्र], &c. and to have compassion on such as @deva[दय्] &c., govern the genitive of their object; @deva[कच्चिद्भर्तुः स्मरसि] (Meg. 90) dost thou remember the lord? @deva[स्मरन् राघवबाणानां विव्यथे राक्षसेश्वरः] Rām. VI. 60. 3. @deva[अध्येति तव लक्ष्मणः] (Bhatti VIII. 119.) Lakshmana remembers thee; @deva[प्रभवति निजस्य कन्यकाजनस्य महाराजः] (Māl. Mād. 4.) the great king has mastery over his daughter; @deva[यदि तं प्रेक्षमाणा आत्मनः प्रभविष्यामि] (Uttar.) if after I see him I shall have control over myself; @deva[गात्राणामनीशोऽस्मि संवृत्तः] (S'ak. II.) I have lost all power over my limbs; @deva[कथंचिदीशा मनसां बभूवुः] (Kum. III. 34.) with great difficulty they could control their minds; @deva[शौवस्तिकत्वं विभवा न येषां व्रजन्ति तेषां दयसे न कस्मात्] (Bhatt. II. 33.) why dost thou feel no compassion for those whose wealth does not see the morrow? @deva[रामस्य दयमानः] (Ibid. VIII 119) taking pity on Rāma.

(b) @deva[कृ] meaning 'to impart additional properties' governs the Genitive; @deva[एधोदकस्य उपस्कुरुते] 'fuel imparts heat to water.' @deva[मा कस्यचिदुपस्कृथाः] Bhatti. VIII. 119.

[^1]: @deva[ज्ञोऽविदर्थस्य करणे । अधिगर्थदयेशां कर्मणि । कृञः प्रतियत्ने ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 51–53.
