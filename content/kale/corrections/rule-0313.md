---
key: rule:313
title: "Feminine @deva[ई] for Class and Kind"
pages: [186.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] Nouns ending in @deva[अ] and not having @deva[य्] for their penultimate, except @deva[हय], @deva[गवय] a wild ox, @deva[मुकय], @deva[मनुष्य] and @deva[मत्स्य] take @deva[ई] when 'class' is implied *e. g.* @deva[वृषली] 'A Śudra female' ( also the wife of a @deva[वृषल], see @ref[311]. above ); similarly @deva[ब्राह्मणी], @deva[महाशूद्री], &c., @deva[हरिणी], @deva[मृगी], @deva[औपगवी] 'a woman of the @deva[औपगव] class of Brâhmaṇas,' @deva[कठी] 'a woman of the @deva[कठ] class of Brâhmaṇas', &c., @deva[हयी]; @deva[गवयी], @deva[मुकयी], @deva[मनुषी], and @deva[मत्सी] ( vide @ref[306]. *d.* ); but @deva[देवदत्ता] 'a woman of that name'; @deva[अश्वा] as it is one of the words included in the Ajâdi group; ( see @ref[307]. and foot-note ); @deva[शूद्रा] 'a woman of the Śudra class ( see @ref[307] above ).'

**(a)** Words ending in @deva[पाक], @deva[कर्ण], @deva[पर्ण], @deva[पुष्प], @deva[फल], @deva[मूल] and @deva[वाल] form their fem. in @deva[ई] when kind is implied; @deva[ओदनपाकी], @deva[शङ्कुकर्णी], @deva[शालपर्णी], @deva[शंखपुष्पी], @deva[दासीफली], @deva[दर्भमूली] and @deva[गोवाली] all names of particular herbs.

**(b)** Also words ending in @deva[इ] and denoting mankind; @deva[दाक्षी] 'a woman of the @deva[दाक्षि] family,' @deva[औदमेयी] ( @deva[उदमेयस्यापत्यं] ), but @deva[तित्तिरिः].

[^1]: @deva[जातेरस्त्रीविषयादयोपधात्] । Pān IV. 1. 63. @deva[योपधप्रतिषेधे हयगवयमुकयमनुष्यमत्स्यानामप्रतिषेधः] । Vârtika. @deva[पाककर्णपर्णमूलवालोत्तरपदाच्च] । @deva[इतो मनुष्यजातेः] । Pān. IV. 1. 64. 65.
