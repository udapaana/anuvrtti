title: "@deva[वह्] and @deva[ऋ] Taking @deva[य] in Specific Senses"
pages: [433.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "वह्यं: the print may have a different vowel mark"
---
The root @deva[वह्] not denoting an instrument for carrying, and @deva[ऋ] in the sense of 'a master or a Vaishya' take @deva[य]; @deva[वह्यं] 'a carriage or a vehicle;' but @deva[वाह्य] (@deva[वह्+ण्यत्]) 'what can be borne;' @deva[अर्यः] 'a master or a Vaishya;' but @deva[आर्य] (@deva[ऋ+ण्यत्]) 'fit to be approached or adored.'
