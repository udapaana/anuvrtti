## Formation with @deva[इ] Affixes (@deva[णिङ्] and @deva[णिच्])

Verbal bases are formed from the nouns given below by the addition of the affixes @deva[इ] (@deva[णिङ्]), and @deva[इ] (@deva[णिच्]) with various senses; those derived by means of @deva[णिङ्] are conjugated in the Atm. and those by @deva[णिच्] in the Par; *e. g.* @deva[पुच्छं] with @deva[वि], @deva[उत्], and @deva[परि]—@deva[उत्पुच्छयते] 'raises the tail,' @deva[विपुच्छयते], @deva[परिपुच्छयते]; @deva[सम्भाण्डयते] 'collects together the vessels'; @deva[सञ्चीवरयते भिक्षुः] 'the mendicant collects together or wears tattered garments;' @deva[मुण्ड]—@deva[मुण्डयति माणवकम्] 'he shaves Māṇavaka', @deva[मिश्र]—@deva[मिश्रयति अन्नम्] 'he mixes boiled rice with (curds, curry, &c.);' @deva[श्लक्ष्ण]—@deva[श्लक्ष्णयति वस्त्रम्] 'he weaves cloth of a very thin texture,' @deva[लवणयति व्यञ्जनम्] 'mixes salt with condiments;' @deva[व्रतयति पयः] 'he observes the vow of living upon milk only;' @deva[व्रतयति शूद्रान्नम्] 'he observes the vow of abstaining from eating food at a Shudra's house;' @deva[वस्त्र]—@deva[संवस्त्रयति] 'he clothes with a garment;' @deva[हल]—@deva[हलयति] 'he uses a large plough;' @deva[कलि]—@deva[कलयति] 'he quarrels!' @deva[कृत]—@deva[कृतयति] (@deva[कृतं गृह्णाति] Sid. Kau.). @deva[तूस्त] 'sin or hair or matted hair,' (@deva[तूस्तं केशा इत्येके जटीभूता केशा इत्यन्ये पापमित्यपरे] । Sid. Kau.)—@deva[तूस्तयति] 'ties into a braid the hair,' &c.
