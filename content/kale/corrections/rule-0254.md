The following words drop their final vowel or the final consonant with the preceding vowel and take @deva[अ] when at the end of a Bah. compound:—

**(a)**[^1] @deva[अक्षि] and @deva[सक्थि] 'both meaning parts of the body'; @deva[जलजवत् अक्षिणी यस्य स जलजाक्षः] 'one whose eyes are like lotuses'; @deva[दीर्घे सक्थिनी यस्य स दीर्घसक्थः] 'one having long thighs', 'spindlelegged'; @deva[कमले इव अक्षिणी यस्याः सा कमलाक्षी] (@deva[स्त्री]); but @deva[दीर्घसक्थि शकटम्] 'a cart with long poles'; @deva[स्थूलाक्षा वेणुयष्टिः] 'a bamboo stick with big eyelike holes'; (where @deva[अ] is added by @ref[282] (c) and hence the *feminine* ends in @deva[आ]; see N. B. For @deva[सक्थि] see also (e) below.

**N. B.** The *fem.* of Bah. compounds ending in @deva[अक्ष] is formed by adding @deva[आ] when it qualifies an inanimate object.

**(b)**[^2] @deva[अङ्गुलि], when the compound is an attribute of @deva[दारु]; @deva[पञ्च अङ्गुलयो यस्य तत्पञ्चाङ्गुलं दारु] (@deva[अङ्गुलिसदृशावयवं धान्यादिविक्षेपणकाष्ठम्]); but @deva[पञ्चाङ्गुलिः हस्तः] the hand having five fingers.

**(c)**[^3] The words @deva[मूर्धन्] preceded by @deva[द्वि] or @deva[त्रि], @deva[लोमन्] preceded by @deva[अन्तर्] and @deva[बहिः], and @deva[नेतृ] used in apposition to the name of a constellation of stars; @deva[द्वौ मूर्धानौ यस्य स द्विमूर्धः] two-headed; @deva[त्रिमूर्धः]; but @deva[दशमूर्धा]; @deva[अन्तर्लोमः]; @deva[बहिर्लोमः]; @deva[मृगो नेता यासां ताः मृगनेत्रा रात्रयः] 'nights of which the *Mṛiga* constellation is the leader' i. e. 'whose position in the heavens marks their advance;' so @deva[पुष्यनेत्राः] &c.

**(d)**[^4] *Feminine* words ending in a termination showing 'a complement of,' and the word @deva[प्रमाणी]; e. g. @deva[कल्याणी पञ्चमी यासां रात्रीणां ताः कल्याणीपञ्चमा रात्रयः]; 'nights whose complement is an auspicious fifth night.' @deva[स्त्री प्रमाणी यस्य असौ स्त्रीप्रमाणः] 'one whose authority is a woman.'

**(e)**[^5] @deva[हलि] and @deva[सक्थि] optionally become @deva[हल] and @deva[सक्थ] when preceded by @deva[अ], @deva[दुस्] or @deva[सु]; @deva[अहलः-लिः] having no plough; @deva[असक्थः-] 'one who has no thighs,' @deva[दुःसक्थः-क्थिः] 'one having deformed thighs'; @deva[सुसक्थः-क्थिः]; &c. @deva[शक्ति] is another reading for @deva[सक्थि] in the sūtra; so @deva[अशक्तः-क्तिः], &c.

**(f)**[^6] @deva[प्रजा] and @deva[मेधा] under similar circumstances drop their @deva[आ] and add @deva[अस्]; as @deva[अविद्यमाना प्रजा यस्य असौ अप्रजाः]; having no progeny, @deva[दुष्टा प्रजा यस्य स दुष्प्रजाः], having bad progeny, @deva[शोभना मेधा यस्य स सुमेधाः] of good intellect; similarly @deva[दुर्मेधाः], @deva[अमेधाः].
