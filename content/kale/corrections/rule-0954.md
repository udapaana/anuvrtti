[^1]With interrogative words such as @deva[किं], @deva[कतर], @deva[कतम], &c. the Potential or the Simple Future may be used when censure is implied (see @ref[937]); @deva[कः कतरो वा हरि निन्देत् निन्दिष्यति वा।]

(a) When wonder is implied the simple Future is used in preference to the Potential if the word @deva[यदि] be not used; @deva[आश्चर्यं अन्धो नाम कृष्णं द्रक्ष्यति] it is a wonder that a blind man should see Hari, but @deva[आश्चर्यं यदि साध्वधीयीत] it is wonder if he study.
