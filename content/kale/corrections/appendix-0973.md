## Prose and Verse in Sanskrit Poetry

This appendix treats of **Prosody**.[^1]

Poetical composition in Sanskrit may be in the form of @deva[गद्य] prose, or '@deva[पद्य]' verse or metrical composition.[^2]

[^1]: The earliest writer on Prosody is Pingalāchārya. His work is known as "The Pingalachhandas-s'āstra." It is written in Sūtras and is divided into eight books. The Agni Purāṇa also deals with the subject very fully. The present chapter, however, is chiefly based on the Vṛttaratnākar and Chhandomañjarī.

[^2]: @deva[काव्यं गद्यं च पद्यं च तद्द्विधैव व्यवस्थितम्] । Dandin. Kāv. Pr. I.
