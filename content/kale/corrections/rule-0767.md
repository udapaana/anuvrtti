The Gerund of the roots @deva[दृश्] and @deva[विद्][^1] is compounded with their objects and express totality; @deva[कन्यादर्शं वरयति] 'chooses as many girls as he sees, i. e. all of them;' @deva[ब्राह्मणवेदं भोजयति, यं यं ब्राह्मणं जानाति लभते विचारयति वा तं सर्वे भोजयति इत्यर्थः] (Sid. Kau.) 'he feeds every Brāhmaṇa that he knows or comes across or remembers i. e. all of them.'

(a) [^2]The gerunds of @deva[विद्] 'to get' and @deva[जीव्] 'to live' are joined with @deva[यावत्] in the same sense; @deva[यावद्वेदं भुंक्ते] 'eats what he gets.'

(b) [^3]With the words @deva[चर्मन्] and @deva[उदर] the gerund of @deva[पूर्] is used; @deva[चर्मपूरं स्तृणाति]; @deva[उदरपूरं भुंक्ते] 'eats so as to fill his belly.'
