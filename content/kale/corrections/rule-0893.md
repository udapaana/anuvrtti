---
key: rule:893
title: "Present Participle for Manner, Cause, or Object"
pages: [509.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] The present participle is used to denote the manner in which an action is done or the cause or object of an action; @deva[शयाना भुञ्जन्ते यवनाः] the Yavanas dine by lying down; @deva[हरिं पश्यन् मुच्यते] a man is absolved by (reason of his) seeing Hari; similarly @deva[तिष्ठन् मूत्रयति, गच्छन् भक्षयति] (Mb.).

---

[^1]: @deva[लक्षणहेत्वोः क्रियायाः] Pāṇ. III. 2. 126 @deva[हेतुः फलं कारणं च।] Sid. Kau.
