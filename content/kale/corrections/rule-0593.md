## Special Cases in Passive Base Formation

### (a) Nasal Dropping with Vowel Lengthening

The roots @deva[खन्], @deva[जन्], @deva[तन्], and @deva[सन्], optionally drop their @deva[न्] and at the same time lengthen their @deva[अ]; @deva[खन्]—@deva[खन्यते], @deva[खायते], &c.

### (b) Special Base Forms

@deva[शी] 'to lie down' and @deva[श्वि] form their bases as @deva[शय्य] and @deva[शूय].

### (c) Vowel Shortening with Prepositions

@deva[ऊह्] shortens its @deva[ऊ] when a preposition is prefixed to it.

### (d) Final Vowel Elision

@deva[दरिद्रा], @deva[दीधी] and @deva[वेवी] drop their final vowel before @deva[य].

### (e) Special Substitutions

@deva[ब्रू] and @deva[अस्] substitute @deva[वच्] and @deva[भू] for themselves; @deva[घस्] and @deva[अज्] also substitute @deva[अद्] and @deva[वी] respectively.
