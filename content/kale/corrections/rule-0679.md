**(c) Past Passive Participles.**

## Formation of Past Passive Participle

The Past Passive Participle is formed by the addition of the affix @deva[त] to the root; *e. g.* @deva[स्ना]—@deva[स्नात] 'bathed,' @deva[जि]—@deva[जित] 'conquered,' @deva[नी]—@deva[नीत] 'carried,' @deva[श्रु]—@deva[श्रुत] 'heard,' @deva[भू]—@deva[भूत] 'become,' @deva[हृ]-@deva[हृत] 'taken away,' @deva[त्यज्]—@deva[त्यक्त] 'abandoned,' @deva[चित्]—@deva[चित्त] 'thought or meditated upon,' &c.
