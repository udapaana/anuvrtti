The indeclinables forming Avyayībhāva compounds have various senses[^1]; *e. g.* (1) the meaning of case; as @deva[गोपि इति अधिगोपम्] 'in the protector of the cowherd,' so @deva[हराविति अधिहरि], @deva[अध्यात्मम्] &c.; (2) @deva[सामीप्य] or 'vicinity;' as @deva[कृष्णस्य समीपमुपकृष्णम्] near Krishna; so @deva[उपगवम्] &c., (3) @deva[समृद्धि] or 'prosperity' as @deva[मद्राणां समृद्धिः सुमद्रम्], 'in a country wherein the Madras are in a prosperous condition,' (4) @deva[व्यृद्धि] ( @deva[वि+ऋद्धिः] ) or 'bad condition;' as @deva[यवनानां व्यृद्धिः दुर्यवनम्] 'when the Yavanas are in a bad condition;' (4) @deva[अभाव] or absence; as @deva[मक्षिकाणामभावः निर्मक्षिकम्] 'where flies are utterly absent' i. e. 'perfect loneliness,' or 'quiet;' so @deva[निर्जनम्] &c. (6) @deva[अत्यय] or 'the close or the transgression of' as @deva[हिमस्य अत्ययः अतिहिमम्] 'after the wintry season,' so @deva[अतिवसन्तम्], @deva[अतियौवनम्], @deva[अतिमात्रम्] 'transgressing the proper limits,' &c.; (7) @deva[असम्प्रति] or 'unfitness of time; as @deva[निद्रा संप्रति न युज्यते इति अतिनिद्रम्] past sleeping time; as @deva[अतिनिद्रम् उत्तिष्ठति पुरुषः]; (8) @deva[प्रादुर्भाव] or 'manifestation' as @deva[हरिशब्दस्य प्रकाशः इतिहरि] in which the name Hari is manifested (loudly uttered); (9) @deva[पश्चात्] or 'after'; as @deva[विष्णोः पश्चाद् अनुविष्णु]; (10) @deva[योग्यता][^2] or 'fitness' as @deva[रूपस्य योग्यं अनुरूपम्] in a corresponding manner; so @deva[अनुगुणम्] agreeably or comformably to, favourably; &c. (11) @deva[वीप्सा] or 'repetition' as @deva[अर्थम् अर्थं प्रति प्रत्यर्थम्] in every case; @deva[अहन्यहनीति प्रत्यहम्-ह] every day; so @deva[प्रतिपर्वतम्], &c. (12) @deva[अनतिवृत्ति] or the 'non-violation of;' so @deva[शक्तिमनतिक्रम्य यथाशक्ति] 'not going beyond' i. e. 'according to one's power'; so @deva[यथाविधि][^3] &c. (13) @deva[सादृश्य] or 'similarity'; as @deva[हरेः सादृश्यं सहरि] like Hari. (14) @deva[आनुपूर्व्य] or 'succession in order, priority'; as @deva[ज्येष्ठस्य आनुपूर्व्येण अनुज्येष्ठम्] 'according to seniority,' so @deva[अनुक्रमम्] according to the proper order; &c. (15) @deva[यौगपद्य] or 'simultaneousness', as @deva[चक्रेण युगपत् सचक्रम्] 'along with the wheel'. (16) @deva[सम्पत्ति] or 'power, influence; as @deva[क्षत्राणां सम्पत्तिः सक्षत्रम्] 'with the Kshatriyas in a satisfactory condition,' or 'very influencial' or 'powerful'. (17) @deva[साकल्य] or 'entirety, fulness'; as @deva[तृणमपि अपरित्यज्य सतृणम्] 'in a manner not leaving out even grass' @deva[अत्ति]; and (18) @deva[अन्त] or 'end'; as @deva[अन्ते अग्निग्रन्थपर्यन्तमधीते साग्नि] including the work on fire (which is studied last of all); so @deva[सभाष्यम्], &c.

[^3]: @deva[यथाऽसादृश्ये ।] Pāṇ. II. 1. 7. @deva[यथा] is not compounded when it indicates similarity; @deva[यथा हरिः तथा हरः] &c.
