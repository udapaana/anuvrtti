key: rule:734
title: "@deva[युज्] Taking @deva[क्यप्] with Final Consonant Change"
pages: [436.png]
verdict: approved
---
@deva[युज्] in the sense of 'to be harnessed' takes the affix @deva[क्यप्] and changes its final to @deva[ग्]; @deva[युग्यः गौः] 'a bull to be harnessed to the yoke;' in other senses it takes the affix @deva[ण्यत्]—@deva[योज्य].
