## Special Uses of @deva[क्यङ्] Affix

The following are the different senses in which the affix @deva[क्यङ्] is used in the particular cases given below.

(a) It is added to @deva[सत्र], @deva[कष्ट], @deva[कृच्छ्र] and @deva[गहन] in the sense of 'desirous of committing sin;' @deva[पापं चिकीर्षति]—@deva[सत्रायते], @deva[कष्टायते], &c., added to @deva[कष्ट] it has also the sense of 'prompt in', @deva[कष्टाय क्रमते]—@deva[कष्टायते] (@deva[पापं कर्तुमुत्सहते इत्यर्थः], Sid Kau.).

(b) to @deva[रोमन्थ] used objectively, @deva[रोमन्थायते] 'is ruminating.'

(c) to @deva[बाष्प] 'tears,' @deva[ऊष्मन्] 'heat,' and @deva[फेन], in the sense of 'sending forth, vomiting;' @deva[बाष्पायते] 'he sheds tears,' @deva[ऊष्मायते] 'gives out heat,' @deva[फेनायते] 'sends forth foam.'

(d) to @deva[सुख] and others in the sense of 'experiencing or enjoying,' @deva[सुखं वेदयते] @deva[सुखायते] 'he enjoys happiness'; but @deva[परस्य सुखं वेदयते] 'he shows the happiness of another.'

(e) to @deva[शब्द], @deva[वैर], @deva[कलह], @deva[अभ्र], @deva[कण्व] 'sin', @deva[सुदिन] 'a fair day', @deva[दुर्दिन] 'a cloudy day' and @deva[नीहार] 'fog, heavy dew,' in the sense of 'making or doing;' @deva[शब्दं करोति] @deva[शब्दायते] 'he makes a sound' (also @deva[शब्दयति] cau.), @deva[सुदिनायते] &c.

**(e) By means of the affix @deva[क्यष्] (@deva[य]) and conjugated in Parasm. and Atm.**
