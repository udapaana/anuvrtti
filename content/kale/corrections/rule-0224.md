The particle @deva[कु] forms a Karm. compound with any other word, @deva[कुपुरुषः] (@deva[कुत्सितः पुरुषः]) a bad man; @deva[कुपुत्रः] &c.

**(a)**[^1] @deva[कु] is changed to (1) @deva[कत्] when followed by a noun with an initial vowel in a Tat. by @deva[त्रि] and the nouns @deva[रथ] and @deva[वद] and by @deva[तृण] 'denoting a species;' @deva[कुत्सितोऽश्वः कदश्वः] 'bad horse;' so @deva[कदन्नं] 'coarse food;' but @deva[कूष्ट्रः] 'having bad camels' (a Bah. comp.) @deva[राजा]; @deva[कुत्सिताः त्रयः कत्रयः] 'three inferior things;' @deva[कत्रथः] 'a bad chariot,' @deva[कद्वदः] 'speaking ill, a bad speaker,' @deva[कत्तृणम्] 'a kind of fragrant grass.'

(2) To @deva[का] when followed by @deva[पथिन्] and @deva[अक्ष] and when meaning 'a little,' and optionally when prefixed to @deva[पुरुष]; @deva[कापथम्], @deva[काक्षः] 'a sidelong look or a frown of displeasure;' see Bh. V. 54 (@deva[अक्षशब्देन तत्पुरुषः अक्षिशब्देन बहुव्रीहिर्वा] Sid. Kau.). @deva[ईषज्जलं काजलं] 'a little water,' @deva[ईषत्पुरुषः कापुरुषः], but @deva[कुत्सितःपुरुषः कुपुरुषः] or @deva[कपुरुषः].

(3) And to @deva[का] or @deva[कव] when followed by @deva[उष्ण]; @deva[कोष्णं], @deva[कवोष्णं] and @deva[कदुष्णं] by (1), all meaning 'luke-warm'.

[^1]: @deva[कोः कत्तत्पुरुषेऽचि ।] ('@deva[त्रौ च]' Vārt:) @deva[रथवदयोश्च । तृणे च जातौ । कापथ्यक्षयोः । ईषदर्थे । विभाषा पुरुषे । कवं चोष्णे ।] Pāṇ. VI. 3. 101-107.
