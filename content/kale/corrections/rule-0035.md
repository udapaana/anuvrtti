@deva[श्] preceded by a word ending in any of the first four letters of a class and followed by a vowel, a semi-vowel, a nasal or @deva[ह्], is optionally changed to @deva[छ्];[^1] e. g. @deva[तद्] + @deva[शिवः] = @deva[तद्] + @deva[शिवः]; and @deva[तद्] + @deva[छिवः]. and then @deva[तत्] + @deva[शिवः], and @deva[तत्] + @deva[छिवः] by the above, and finally @deva[तच्शिवः] and @deva[तच्छिवः] that Śiva (see a. @ref[28].). Similarly @deva[तच्श्लोकेन], @deva[तच्छ्लोकेन]; but @deva[वाक्श्चोतति] speech falters (as @deva[श] is not followed by any of the letters mentioned in the rule).

[^1]: @deva[शश्छोऽटि । खरि च ।] Pāṇ. VI. I. 4. 63, 55.
