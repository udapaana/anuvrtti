---
key: rule:256
title: "Transformations of @deva[जानु], @deva[जाया], @deva[गन्ध], and @deva[नासिका]"
pages: [158.png]
verdict: approved
---
At the end of a Bah. compound—

**(a)**[^1] @deva[जानु] is changed to @deva[ज्ञु] necessarily when preceded by @deva[प्र] or @deva[सम्] and optionally when preceded by @deva[ऊर्ध्व]; @deva[प्रगते जानुनी यस्य स प्रज्ञुः] 'one having the knees wide apart' i. e. 'bandylegged'; @deva[संज्ञुः] 'one having well-turned or beautiful knees'; @deva[ऊर्ध्वजानुः-ज्ञुः]; 'long-shanked'.

**(b)**[^2] @deva[जाया] becomes @deva[जानि]; @deva[युवती जाया यस्य स युवजानिः] 'one whose wife is young;' @deva[भूजानिः] lit. one whose wife is the earth; i. e. 'a king', &c.

**(c)**[^3] @deva[गन्ध] becomes @deva[गन्धि] when preceded by @deva[उत्], @deva[पूति] or @deva[सु]; @deva[उद्गतः गन्धः यस्य स उद्गन्धिः] 'whose smell is spread above,' @deva[पूतिगन्धिः] 'having a repulsive smell', @deva[सुगन्धिः]. This change of @deva[गन्ध] is possible only when the smell referred to is inseparably connected with (looks like a part and parcel of) the object denoted by the other member; as @deva[सुगन्धि पुष्पं, सलिलं च] (which receives its odour from the lotuses &c. growing in it); @deva[सुगन्धिवायुः]; but @deva[शोभना गन्धा अस्य सुगन्ध आपणिकः] 'a perfumer dealing in sweet smells.' @deva[गन्ध] is also changed to @deva[गन्धि][^4] when it means 'a particle of', 'a little of,' or when the compound involves comparison; as @deva[सूपस्य गन्धः यस्मिन् तत् सूपगन्धि भोजनम्]; so @deva[घृतगन्धि] a dinner in which there is a scanty supply of ghee, &c.; @deva[पद्मस्य इव गन्धः यस्य तत् पद्मगन्धि] 'that which has the smell of a lotus.'

**(d)**[^5] @deva[नासिका] becomes @deva[नस] when it is preceded by a preposition or when the compound is used as a distinguishing name and it is preceded by any word except @deva[स्थूल]; @deva[उन्नता नासिका यस्य स उन्नसः] 'one having a prominent nose,' @deva[प्रणसः] 'one having a good nose,' @deva[द्रुरिव नासिका यस्य द्रुणसः][^6] 'one whose nose is like a tree,' 'large-nosed;' but @deva[स्थूलनासिकः]. @deva[नस] becomes @deva[नस्] optionally when preceded by @deva[खुर] or @deva[खर]; as @deva[खुरणसः] or @deva[खुरणाः] 'having a nose like a horse's hoof i. e. flat;' @deva[खरणसः-णाः] 'sharp-nosed.' @deva[नासिका] when preceded by @deva[वि] becomes @deva[ग्र] or @deva[ख्य]; as @deva[विगता नासिका यस्य स विग्रः] or @deva[विख्यः] 'one having a deformed nose.'

---

[^1]: @deva[प्रसंभ्यां जानुनोर्ज्ञुः । ऊर्ध्वाद्विभाषा ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 129, 130.

[^2]: @deva[जायाया निङ् ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 134. @deva[लोपो व्योर्वलि ।] Pāṇ. VI. 1. 66. @deva[जाया] at the end of a Bah. substitutes @deva[नि] for its final @deva[आ]. The preceding @deva[य्] or @deva[व्] is dropped before a consonant except @deva[य्].

[^3]: @deva[गन्धस्येदुत्पूतिसुसुरभिभ्यः ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 135. @deva[गन्धस्येत्वे तदेकान्तग्रहणम् ।] Vārt. There is a difference of opinion among critics as to the meaning of the Vārt. @deva[गन्धस्य] &c. Some hold that the smell must be the natural property of a thing to necessitate the final @deva[इ]. Cf. Jayamaṅgala on Bhaṭṭi. @deva[आघ्रायि वान्गन्धवहः सुगन्धः] &c. II. 10, and Mallinātha on Rag. IV. 45. But the view of such eminent grammarians as Kaiyaṭa, Bhaṭṭoji, Nagesha, &c. is as given above.

[^4]: @deva[अल्पाख्यायाम् । उपमानाच्च ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 136-137.

[^5]: @deva[अत्र नासिकायाः संज्ञायां नसं चास्थूलात् । उपसर्गाच्च ।] Pāṇ. V. 4. 118. 119. @deva[वेर्ग्रो वक्तव्यः । ख्यश्च ।] Vārtikas.

[^6]: @deva[पूर्वपदात्संज्ञायामगः । उपसर्गाद्बहुलम् ।] Pāṇ. VIII. 4. 3. 28.
