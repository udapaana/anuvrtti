title: "Shortening @deva[ई] and @deva[ऊ] in First Member"
pages: [172.png]
verdict: approved-with-doubts
doubts:
  - "श्रीमत्: the print shows \"श्रीमर ,\" (final letter broken); kept श्रीमत्"
---
**(a)** [^1] When the first member of a compound ends in @deva[ई] or @deva[ऊ] not capable of changing into @deva[इय्] or @deva[उव्] and not belonging to a *fem.* termination or an indeclinable, the @deva[ई] or @deva[ऊ] is optionally shortened; @deva[ग्रामणीपुत्रः] or @deva[ग्रामणिपुत्रः] the son of a leader of a village; &c.; but @deva[गौरीपतिः], @deva[श्रीमत्], @deva[भ्रूभङ्गः], @deva[शुक्लीभावः] &c.

**(b)** But the word @deva[भ्रू] followed by @deva[कुंस] and @deva[कुटि] is an exception; @deva[भ्रू+कुंस=भ्रू-भ्रुकुंस] ( @deva[भ्रुवा कुंसो भाषणं शोभा वा यस्य स स्त्रीवेषधारी नर्तकः] Sid. Kau. ) an actor; @deva[भ्रू-भ्रुकुटिः] the knit eyebrow; according to some, @deva[भ्रू] optionally becomes @deva[भ्र] when followed by @deva[कुंस] or @deva[कुटि]: as @deva[भ्रकुंसः] and @deva[भ्रकुटिः] ( see ft. note ).

[^1]: @deva[इको ह्रस्वोऽङ्यो गालवस्य] । Pān. VI. 3. 61. @deva[इयङुवङ्भाविनामव्ययानां च नेति वाच्यम्] । @deva[अभ्रुकुंसादीनामिति वक्तव्यम्] Vârtikas. @deva[अकारोऽनेन विधीयते इति व्याख्यान्तरम्] । Sid. Kau.
