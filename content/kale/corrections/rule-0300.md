doubts:
  - "मस्करमस्करिणौ in fn: our print may read मस्कारिणौ (blurred); alt clearly मस्करिणौ."
  - "वर्चस्कमन्नमलम्: our scan blurred, read with alt."
---
@deva[स्][^1] is inserted in the case of the following compounds:—

**(a)** @deva[अपर] followed by @deva[पर] when continuity of an action is implied; @deva[अपरस्पराः सार्था गच्छन्ति] । @deva[सततमविच्छेदेन गच्छन्तीत्यर्थः]; but @deva[अपरपरा गच्छन्ति] । @deva[अपरे च परे च सकृदेव गच्छन्तीत्यर्थः] । and in the case of @deva[आ + चर्य] when wonder is meant; @deva[आश्चर्यं यदि स भुञ्जीत] । But @deva[आचर्यं कर्म शोभनम्] ।

**(b)** @deva[अवकीर्यते इति अवस्करः] when it means @deva[वर्चस्कं] ( @deva[कुत्सितं वर्चः वर्चस्कमन्नमलम्] । Sid. Kau. ) otherwise @deva[अवकरः]; @deva[अपस्करः] when it means @deva[रथाङ्गम्] 'part of a carriage'; @deva[विष्किरः] ( or @deva[विकिरः] ) 'a bird'. @deva[प्रतिष्कशः] ( @deva[सहायः पुरोयायी वा] Sid. Kau. ), but @deva[प्रतिगतः कशां प्रतिकशः] 'one who boldly faces the whip, a disobedient servant' &c. @deva[मस्करः] 'a bamboo', but @deva[मकरः] 'a shark', @deva[मस्करिन्] 'an ascetic' but @deva[मकरिन्] 'the sea'; @deva[कारस्करः] when it means 'a particular kind of tree,' otherwise @deva[कारकरः] ।

**(c)** in the case of the words of the @deva[पारस्करादि] group, such as @deva[पारस्करः], @deva[किष्कुः], @deva[किष्किन्धा], &c. when they are names.

**(d)** and when the words @deva[तद्] and @deva[बृहत्] are followed by @deva[कर] and @deva[पति] and the meaning is 'a thief', and 'a deity' respectively, and @deva[द्] and @deva[त्] being dropped before @deva[स्]; @deva[तस्करः], @deva[बृहस्पतिः]. Also @deva[प्रायश्चित्तम्], @deva[प्रायश्चित्तिः], @deva[वनस्पतिः] &c.
