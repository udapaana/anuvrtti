**I. SVARASANDHI, OR THE COMBINATION OF FINAL AND INITIAL VOWELS.**

If a simple vowel, short or long, be followed by a similar vowel, short or long, the substitute for them both is the similar long vowel; e.g.[^1] @deva[दैत्य] + @deva[अरिः] = @deva[दैत्यारिः]; @deva[अत्र] + @deva[आसीत्] = @deva[अत्रासीत्]; @deva[यदा] + @deva[अभवत्] = @deva[यदाभवत्]; @deva[विद्या] + @deva[आतुरः] = @deva[विद्यातुरः] eager to gain knowledge; @deva[इति] + @deva[इवः] = @deva[इतीवः] @deva[अपि] + @deva[ईक्षते] = @deva[अपीक्षते]; @deva[श्री] + @deva[ईशः] = @deva[श्रीशः]; @deva[भानु] + @deva[उदयः] = @deva[भानूदयः]; @deva[साधु] + @deva[ऊचुः] = @deva[साधूचुः]; @deva[चमू] + @deva[ऊर्जः] = @deva[चमूर्जः] the strength of the army; @deva[कर्तृ] + @deva[ऋजुः] = @deva[कर्तृजुः]; @deva[कृ] + @deva[ॠकारः] = @deva[कॄकारः]; @deva[होतृ] + @deva[ऌकारः] = @deva[होतॄकारः]; the ḷkāra pronounced by the sacrificer (since there is no long @deva[ऌ] long @deva[ॠ] is substituted for both).

(a) If @deva[ऋ] or @deva[ऌ] be followed by a short @deva[ऋ] or @deva[ऌ] short @deva[ऋ] or @deva[ऌ] is optionally substituted for both;[^2] @deva[होतृ] + @deva[ऋकारः] = @deva[होतृकारः], and also @deva[होतृऋकारः] (vide @ref[23]. sec. b.); so three forms altogether @deva[होतॄकारः] @deva[होतृकारः], and @deva[होतृऋकारः]; @deva[होतृ] + @deva[ऌकारः] = @deva[होतॢकारः]. and also @deva[होतृऌकारः].

---

[^1]: @deva[अकः सवर्णे दीर्घः ।] Pāṇ. VI. 1. 101.

[^2]: @deva[ऋति सवर्णे ऋ वा । ऌति सवर्णे ऌ वा ।] Vārtikas on the above.
