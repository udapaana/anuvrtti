title: "Gender in First and Second Person Pronouns"
pages: [506.png, 507.png]
verdict: approved
---
The pronouns of the first and second person viz. @deva[अस्मद्] and @deva[युष्मद्] have no gender. The other pronouns follow the gender of the nouns they refer to. For the uses of the shorter forms of @deva[अस्मत्] and @deva[युष्मत्] see chapter IV.
