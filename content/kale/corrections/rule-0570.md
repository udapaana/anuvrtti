## Optional Nasal Dropping in @deva[गम्] and @deva[यम्]

@deva[गम्], and @deva[यम्] with @deva[उप] 'to marry,' optionally drop their nasal, when they take the Ātm. terminations. @deva[यम्] when it means 'to give out' (as the faults of others) necessarily drops its nasal.
