[^1]With the particle @deva[पुरा] not joined with @deva[स्म], the Aorist, the Imperfect, the Perfect or the Present may be used; @deva[वसन्तीह पुरा छात्रा अवात्सुरवसन्नूषुर्वा।] (Sid. Kau.) here formerly dwelt pupils. But when @deva[स्म] is used with @deva[पुरा] the present alone can be used; @deva[यजति स्म पुरा] he formerly sacrificed.
