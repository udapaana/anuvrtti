## Three Types of Passive Verbs

There are three kinds of Passive Verbs:

1. **The Passive properly so called** (@deva[कर्मणि प्र॰]), as @deva[रामेण द्रव्यं दीयते]

2. **An Impersonal Passive** (@deva[भावे प्र॰]), as @deva[गम्यते] 'it is gone'

3. **A Reflexive** (@deva[कर्मकर्तरि प्र॰]), as @deva[ओदनः पच्यते] 'rice is cooked'
