The roots @deva[स्कु] 'to go by leaps, to raise,' @deva[स्तम्भ्] 'to obstruct,' @deva[स्तुम्भ्] 'to stop,' @deva[स्कम्भ्] and @deva[स्कुम्भ्] 'to obstruct' belong to the 5th and 9th classes; as @deva[स्कुनाति], @deva[स्कुनीते], @deva[स्कुनोति], @deva[स्कुनुते], &c.
