## Special Forms of @deva[आन] Participles

(a) The @deva[आ] of @deva[आन] is changed to @deva[ई] in the case of the root @deva[आस्] (2. cl.) 'to sit;' Pre. P. @deva[आसीन].

(b) @deva[आन] added to the roots @deva[पू] and @deva[यज्] forms nouns; *e. g.* @deva[पवमानः] 'that which purifies, hence, the wind' (*cf.* Rag. VIII. 9.) also, 'one of the sacred fires;' @deva[यजमानः] 'one who sacrifices.'
