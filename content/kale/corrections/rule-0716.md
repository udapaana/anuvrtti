The roots @deva[तक्] 'to laugh at,' @deva[शस्] 'to kill,' @deva[चत्] to 'look for, to ask,' @deva[यत्] 'to strive,' @deva[जन्], @deva[शक्] and @deva[सह्] take the affix @deva[य]; @deva[तक्यं] 'what is fit to be laughed at,' @deva[शस्यं] 'what ought to be killed;' &c.
