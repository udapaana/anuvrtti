The following seven roots form their bases like the Desiderative in the senses indicated. These are:—@deva[कित्] to administer medicine, to treat as a patient, @deva[चिकित्सति-ते]; @deva[गुप्] to censure, @deva[जुगुप्सते]; @deva[तिज्] to bear, to forgive, @deva[तितिक्षते]; @deva[बध्] to abhor, to act loathsomely, @deva[बीभत्सते]; @deva[दान्] to make straight, @deva[दीदांसति-ते]; @deva[मान्] to reason, to think, @deva[मीमांसते]; @deva[शान्] to sharpen, @deva[शीशांसति-ते]. But @deva[कित्] to desire @deva[केतति]; to dwell, @deva[केतयति]; @deva[दान्] to cut @deva[दानयति-ते] &c.
