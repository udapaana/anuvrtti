---
key: rule:928
title: "Augment Dropped with @deva[मा] and @deva[स्म] for Imperative"
pages: [520.png]
verdict: approved
---
When the particle @deva[मा] in combination with @deva[स्म] is used with the Imperfect in the sense of the Imperative, the augment @deva[अ] is dropped; @deva[मा स्म भवः, मा स्म करोत्, मा स्म प्ररुदितं युवाम्।]
