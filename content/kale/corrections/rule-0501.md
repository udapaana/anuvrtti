## Insertion of @deva[न्] after Reduplicative Syllable

@deva[न्] is inserted after the reduplicative syllable in the case of roots beginning with @deva[अ] and ending in a conjunct consonant and in that of @deva[अश्] 'to pervade' and @deva[ऋच्छ्] 'to go'. The reduplicative @deva[अ] is changed to @deva[आ]; @deva[अञ्ज्]. @deva[अअञ्ज्]+@deva[अ]=@deva[अ]+@deva[न्]+@deva[अञ्ज्]+@deva[अ]=@deva[आनञ्ज्]+@deva[अ]=@deva[आनञ्ज], similarly @deva[आनर्द] 1 sing. of @deva[अर्द्], @deva[आनशे] of @deva[अश्], &c.
