The optional forms of @deva[इदम्] and @deva[एतद्] in the Acc., Inst. Sing. and Gen. & Loc. Duals are to be used when there is anvádes'a[^1] i. e. their proper forms have already been used in a previous clause; e. g. @deva[अनेन व्याकरणमधीतं एनं छन्दोऽध्यापय] 'he has studied grammar, teach him prosody,' @deva[अनयोः पवित्रं कुलं, एनयोः प्रभूतं स्वम्], 'The family of them both is pure, and their wealth ample.'

---

[^1]: @deva[किंचित्कार्यं विधातुमुपात्तस्य कार्यान्तरं विधातुं पुनरुपादानमन्वादेशः ।] Sid. Kau.
