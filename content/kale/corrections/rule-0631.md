## Insertion of @deva[री] in Ātmanepada Frequentatives with Penultimate @deva[ऋ]

When a root contains a penultimate @deva[ऋ] (@deva[ऌ]) original or brought in by samprasāraṇa the syllable @deva[री] is inserted between the @deva[अ] of the reduplicative syll. (which remains short against @ref[629] b) and the radical consonant; @deva[वृत्]=@deva[वृत्य]=@deva[ववृत्य]=@deva[वरीवृत्यते], @deva[प्रच्छ्]=@deva[पृच्छ्य]=@deva[परीपृच्छ्यते], @deva[नृत्]=@deva[नरीनृत्यते], @deva[ग्रह्]=@deva[जरीगृह्यते].
