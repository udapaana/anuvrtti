[^1]The roots @deva[विश्], @deva[पत्], @deva[पद्], and @deva[स्कन्द्] are used in their gerundive forms in @deva[अम्] in the sense of 'complete occupation or pervasion or repetition' with nouns which if not compounded would have stood in the Acc.; @deva[गेहानुप्रवेशमास्ते गेहं गेहमनुप्रवेशम् ।] @deva[गेहमनुप्रवेशमनुप्रवेशम् ।] so @deva[गेहानुप्रपातम्], @deva[गेहानुप्रपादम्], @deva[गेहानुस्कन्दम्], &c.
