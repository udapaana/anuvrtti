## (e) Participle of the Future Tense.

The Participle of the Simple Future, Active as well as Passive, is formed from the third person singular form of the Second Future of a root. The Parasm. Ptc. is formed simply by dropping the final @deva[इ], and the Ātm. and the Passive one by substituting @deva[मान] for the final @deva[ते]; e. g.

**Participles.**

| Roots. | Par. | Ātm. | Passive. |
|---|---|---|---|
| @deva[दा]— | @deva[दास्यत्] | @deva[दास्यमान] | @deva[दास्यमान] or @deva[दायिष्यमाण] |
| @deva[भू]— | @deva[भविष्यत्] | @deva[भविष्यमाण] | @deva[भविष्यमाण] or @deva[भाविष्यमाण] |
