## Dative with @deva[क्रुध्], @deva[द्रुह्], @deva[ईर्ष्य्], @deva[असूय्] (Anger, Hatred, Jealousy)

The roots @deva[क्रुध्],[^1] @deva[द्रुह्], @deva[ईर्ष्य्], and @deva[असूय्], and others having the same sense govern the Dative of the person against whom the feeling of anger, hatred, &c. is directed; @deva[हरये क्रुध्यति द्रुह्यति-ईर्ष्यति-असूयति वा] (Sid Kau) 'he is angry with Hari, or bears hatred towards him, or is jealous of him or finds fault with him;' @deva[सीतायै नाक्रुध्यन्नाप्यसूयत] (Bhatt. VIII 75.) 'he did neither get angry with Sītā nor find fault with her;' but @deva[भार्यामीर्ष्यति] 'he keeps a jealous watch over his wife' (that others may not see her @deva[मैनामन्योऽद्राक्षीदिति] Sid. Kau)

(a) [^2] But @deva[क्रुध्] and @deva[द्रुह्] preceded by a preposition govern the Accusative case; @deva[किं मां संक्रुध्यसि] 'why do you get angry with me?' @deva[नित्यमस्मच्छरीरमभिद्रोग्धुं यतते] (Mud. I.) 'he always endeavours to do harm to my body.'

*Obs*:—@deva[द्रुह्] with @deva[अभि] is also found used with the Dat.; @deva[मया पुनरेभ्य एवाभिद्रुग्धमजेन] (Utta. VII).
