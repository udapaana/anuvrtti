title: "Special Senses of Pronoun तद्"

---
The pronoun @deva[तद्] has often the sense of 'well known, renowned,' &c. @deva[तौ पार्वतीपरमेश्वरौ] those (well-known) Pārvatī and Parameśvara; @deva[तान्येव वनस्थलानि] those well-known forest sites.

(a) When repeated, this pronoun has the sense of 'various, several;' @deva[तेषु तेषु रम्यतरेषु स्थानेषु] in those various highly delightful spots; @deva[कृतैरपि तैस्तैः प्रयत्नैः] notwithstanding several efforts–@deva[कामैस्तैस्तैर्हृतज्ञानाः] &c (Bhag. VII. 20).
