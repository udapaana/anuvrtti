The following is a list of such of the Taddhita affixes as are commonly to be met with.

## Section I. Miscellaneous Affixes

### @deva[अ]

is added in the sense of:—

**(1)** 'the son of'; as @deva[उपगोः अपत्यं पुमान् औपगवः] 'the son of Upagu'; so @deva[वासुदेवः] from @deva[वसुदेव]; @deva[पर्वतस्य अपत्यं स्त्री पार्वती] 'the daughter of the mountain,' &c.

**(2)** 'the descendant of'; as @deva[उत्सस्य गोत्रापत्यं पुमान् औत्सः] 'a descendant of Utsa', @deva[उत्सस्य गोत्रापत्यं स्त्री औत्सी] 'a female descendant of Utsa', ( vide @ref[311], @ref[313] );

**(3)** 'dyed with,' @deva[हरिद्रया रक्तं हारिद्रं वसनं] 'a garment dyed with turmeric';

**(4)** 'made of,' @deva[देवदारोर्विकारः दैवदारवः] 'made of the fir tree;'

**(5)** 'belonging to,' &c; @deva[देवस्य अयं दैवः] 'belonging to a god;' @deva[शर्कराया इदं शार्करं] 'of sand'; @deva[ऊर्णाया इदं और्णं वस्त्रं] 'a woolen garment,' @deva[ग्रैष्मः] 'belonging to the sultry season'; @deva[नैशः] 'nocturnal;' @deva[सांवत्सरः] 'yearly' &c.; when added to @deva[हेमन्त] the final @deva[त] is dropped, @deva[हैमनः] 'autumnal,' ( vide Sis. VI. 65; Kir. XVII. 12 ). @deva[हैमन्तः] means 'agreeable in the autumnal season;'

**(6)** 'lord of'; @deva[पृथिव्या ईश्वरः पार्थिवः] 'the lord of the earth'; @deva[पञ्चालानां स्वामी पाञ्चालः] 'the king of the Panchâlas'; @deva[ऐक्ष्वाकः][^1] 'the king of the Ikshvâku race',

**(7)** 'a collection of'; @deva[काकानां समूहः काकं]; @deva[बकानां समूहः बाकं] 'a flock of cranes'; similarly @deva[मायूरं] from @deva[मयूर] 'a peacock,' @deva[कापोतं] from @deva[कपोत] 'a pigeon', @deva[भिक्षाणां समूहो भैक्षम्]; @deva[गर्भिणीनां समूहो गार्भिणम्] &c.

**(8)** 'knowing or studying'; @deva[व्याकरणं अधीते वेद वा वैयाकरणः] 'one who studies grammar, grammarian', &c.

**(9)** and to form abstract nouns; @deva[मुनेः भावः मौनं] 'silence', @deva[युवन् यौवनं] 'youth;' @deva[सुहृद्-सौहार्दं] 'friendship'; @deva[पृथोर्भावः पार्थवं] 'greatness, width,' &c.

### @deva[अक]

forms derivatives with various significations:—

**(1)** @deva[उष्ट्रे भवः औष्ट्रकः] 'produced from or relating to a camel,' @deva[ग्रीष्मे भवः ग्रैष्मकः] 'produced in the hot season,'

**(2)** @deva[कुलालेन कृतं कौलालकं] 'made by a potter,' @deva[ब्रह्मणा कृतं ब्राह्मकं] 'made by Brahman,'

**(3)** @deva[आरण्यकः] 'a forester,' 'a wild man',

**(4)** @deva[राज्ञां योग्यं राजन्यकं] 'a place fit for kings to live in'; @deva[मानुष्यकं] 'a country fit for men to live in' @deva[कुरुषु जातः कौरवकः] ( also @deva[कौरवः][^2] ) 'an inhabitant of Kuru'; @deva[युगन्धरेषु जातः यौगन्धरकः] ( also @deva[यौगन्धरः][^2] ) 'inhabiting Yugandhara';

**(6)** @deva[पथि जातं पन्थकं] 'grown on a way';

**(7)** @deva[पन्थानं गच्छतीति पथिकः] 'a traveller';

**(8)** @deva[पूर्वाह्णे भवः पूर्वाह्णिकः] 'happening in the forenoon'; similarly @deva[अपराह्णिकः] 'happening in the afternoon';

**(9)** is added to denote 'enmity' @deva[काकोलूकयोः वैरं काकोलूकिका][^3] 'the antipathy between the crow and the owl'; similarly @deva[कुत्सकुशिकिका], &c;

**(10)** when added to words ending in the Tad. affixes signifying 'the child or the descendant', and to the words @deva[उक्षन्], @deva[उष्ट्र], @deva[उरभ्र] 'a ram', @deva[राजन्], @deva[राजन्य], @deva[राजपुत्र], @deva[वत्स], @deva[मनुष्य] and @deva[अज], it has the sense of 'a collection of'; @deva[उपगूनां समूहः औपगवकम्] 'a number of the descendants of Upagu'; @deva[औक्षकं] 'a herd of cattle', @deva[राजकं] 'a collection of kings', @deva[राजन्यकं] 'an assemblage of kshatriyas'; @deva[वात्सकं] 'a number of calves'; @deva[मानुष्यकं], @deva[अजकं] &c.;

**(11)** it is also added to @deva[क्रम], @deva[पद], @deva[शिक्षा], and @deva[मीमांसा] in the sense of 'one who has studied them'; @deva[क्रमकः] 'a student who goes through a regular course of study, or one who has studied the *krama* arrangement of a sacred text', @deva[मीमांसक] 'a student of the *mīmāṃsā* philosophy', &c.

### @deva[आमह]

is added to the words @deva[पितृ] and @deva[मातृ] in the sense of 'the father of'; @deva[पितुः पिता पितामहः] 'a paternal grandfather'; @deva[मातामहः] 'a maternal grandfather'.

**(1)** @deva[उल] is added to @deva[मातृ] in the sense of 'the brother of'; @deva[मातुर्भ्राता मातुलः] 'a maternal uncle';

**(2)** and @deva[व्य] is added to @deva[पितृ] and @deva[भ्रातृ] in the sense of 'the brother and the son of' respectively; @deva[पितुः भ्राता पितृव्यः] 'a paternal uncle'; @deva[भ्रातुः पुत्रः भ्रातृव्यः] 'a nephew.'

### @deva[आयन]

and @deva[आयनि], are added to patronymics formed by means of Tad. affixes in the sense of 'the child of,' @deva[दाक्षायणः-णि] 'the son of Dâkshi'; @deva[गार्ग्यायणः-णिः] 'the son of @deva[गार्ग्य], a descendant of Garga' @deva[आयन] is also added to the word @deva[कापिशी] 'name of a town' in the sense of 'produced in'—@deva[कापिशायनः], and optionally to @deva[द्रोण]; @deva[द्रोणायनः] or @deva[द्रौणिः] son of Drona.

### @deva[इ]

has the meaning of 'a son or a descendant of'; @deva[दाक्षिः] 'the son of Daksha'; @deva[वैयासकिः] 'the son of Vyâsa,' &c. ( In the case of the words @deva[व्यास], @deva[वरुड] 'name of a low caste,' @deva[निषाद], @deva[चण्डाल] and @deva[बिंब], the final @deva[अ] is changed to @deva[अक्] before this @deva[इ]. )

### @deva[इक] ( @deva[ठक्], @deva[ठञ्], @deva[ठन्] )

has various senses:—

**(1)** @deva[रैवत्याः अपत्यं पुमान् रैवतिकः][^4] 'the son of Revati;

**(2)** 'happening or given once a month, monthly, lasting for a month', &c. @deva[मासेन दीयते इति मासिकं वेतनं पुस्तकं वा]; similarly @deva[वार्षिकं आयुः] &c.

**(3)** 'gathering together', @deva[सैनिकाः];

**(4)** 'asking;' @deva[सुस्नातं पृच्छतीति सौस्नातिकः] 'one who asks another whether he had an auspicious ablution'; so @deva[सुखशयनं पृच्छतीति सौखशायनिकः] 'one who asks another whether he had comfortable sleep'. ( see Rag. VI. 61 X. 14. ); @deva[सौखसुप्तिकः] &c ;

**(5)** 'using an instrument', @deva[असिः प्रहरणमस्य असिकः] 'one who strikes with a sword, a swordsman'; @deva[धानुष्कः] 'an archer;'

**(6)** 'mixed with,' &c.; @deva[दध्ना संस्कृतं दाधिकं] 'mixed with curds', @deva[मारीचिकं] fr. @deva[मरीचि] 'black pepper',

**(7)** @deva[धर्मं चरतीति धार्मिकः] 'pious, religious', similarly @deva[अधार्मिकः].

**(8)** @deva[उडुपेन तरतीति औडुपिकः] 'a boatman'; @deva[नाविकः] &c.

**(9)** @deva[हस्तिना चरतीति हास्तिकः] 'one who rides an elephant'; @deva[शकटेन चरतीति शाकटिकः] 'one who drives in a carriage';

**(10)** @deva[दध्ना भक्षयतीति दाधिकः] 'one who eats with curds';

**(11)** 'living upon', @deva[वेतनेन जीवतीति वैतनिकः] 'one who lives upon wages', so @deva[वाहनिकः], @deva[औपदेशिकः] &c.

**(12)** 'carrying upon'; @deva[उत्संगेन हरतीति औत्संगिकः]

**(13)** @deva[अस्तीति बुद्धिः अस्य आस्तिकः] 'a believer in God and sacred writings'; @deva[नास्तिकः] &c.

**(14)** it is added to @deva[लाक्षा], @deva[रोचना], @deva[शकल] and @deva[कर्दम] in the sense of 'dyed with', @deva[लाक्षया रक्तं लाक्षिकं] 'dyed with lac'. @deva[रोचनिकः], @deva[शाकलिकः] 'chequered or spotted', @deva[कार्दमिकः];

**(15)** @deva[वेद], @deva[न्याय], @deva[वृत्ति], @deva[लोकायत] and words ending in @deva[सूत्र] except @deva[कल्पसूत्र] &c.; in the sense of 'one who studies them'; @deva[वेदमधीते वैदिकः] 'a student of the Veda'; @deva[नैयायिकः] 'one who studies Nyâya or logic', @deva[वृत्तिमधीते वार्तिकः] 'one who studies a commentary' &c., @deva[लौकायतिकः] 'an atheist, a student of the materialistic philosophy', @deva[सांग्रहसूत्रिकः]; but @deva[काल्पसूत्रः];

**(16)** to @deva[हस्तिन्], @deva[धेनु], @deva[केदार] and @deva[कवच] in the sense of 'a collection of'; @deva[हास्तिकं] 'a herd of elephants', @deva[धैनुकं] 'a herd of cows', @deva[कैदारिकं] 'a collection of fields,' @deva[कावचिकं] 'a collection of armours';

**(17)** to @deva[अध्यात्मन्], @deva[अधिदेव], @deva[अधिभूत], @deva[इहलोक], @deva[परलोक], &c. in the sense of 'relating to &c.;' @deva[आत्मानमधिकृत्य भवः आध्यात्मिक] 'relating to the Supreme spirit', 'spiritual'; @deva[आधिदैविक] 'relating to or coming from the governing deity'. @deva[आधिभौतिक] 'proceeding from the elements', @deva[ऐहलौकिक] 'temporal', @deva[पारलौकिकः] &c.

**(18)** to @deva[क्रय], @deva[विक्रय], @deva[क्रयविक्रय], and @deva[वस्न] in the sense of 'living upon ( in these cases no Vṛiddhi substitute takes place ); @deva[क्रयेण जीवतीति क्रायिकः] 'one who lives upon selling things, a trader', @deva[विक्रयिकः], @deva[वस्निकः], 'one who lives upon wages'.

**(19)** to words denoting musical instruments in the sense of 'playing upon'; &c. @deva[मृदंगवादनं शिल्पमस्य मार्दंगिकः] 'one whose profession is to play on a tabor', so @deva[वैणिकः] fr. Vīṇā; similarly @deva[वैणविकः], @deva[माडुकः] or @deva[माडुकिकः], @deva[झार्झरिकः], &c.

**(20)** to @deva[पर्प] and the remaining words[^5] of that group in the sense of 'walking with the assistance of;' @deva[पर्पिकः] [ @deva[पर्पेण चरति इति, येन पीठेन पङ्गवश्चरन्ति स पर्पः] Sid. Kau. ]; @deva[अश्वेन चरति आश्विकः], @deva[रथिकः] &c. @deva[पथा चरति पथिकः] 'a traveller'; this is also added to words denoting inanimate things. @deva[वारिपथिकं दारु] 'wood carried on by the force of water';

**(21)** to words included in the @deva[भस्त्रादि][^6] group in the sense of 'bears or carries by means of' @deva[भस्त्रया हरतीति भस्त्रिकः], and to the word @deva[वि-वीवध]; @deva[वि-वीवधेन हरति वि-वीवधिकः]; also @deva[वैवधिकः];—

**(22)** to @deva[कुसीद] and @deva[दशैकादशन्] in the sense of 'lending on interest' @deva[कुसीदिकः] 'a usurer', @deva[दशैकादशिकः] 'one who lends another ten rupees in order to get back eleven i. e. 'a usurer'; and

**(23)** to @deva[आकर्ष]; @deva[आकर्षेण चरति आकर्षिकः] 'magnetic, attractive'.

### @deva[इन]

**(1)** Added to @deva[पूर्व] or words ending in @deva[पूर्व] and to @deva[श्राद्ध] it has the sense of 'done and eaten by' respectively; @deva[कृतपूर्वी कटं]; @deva[श्राद्धमनेन भुक्तं श्राद्धी] 'one who has eaten at a Śrāddha';

**(2)** it is added to a few words, such as @deva[खल], @deva[कुडुम्ब], &c. in the sense of 'a collection of' with *fem.* term. @deva[ई] added to it; @deva[खलानां समूहः खलिनी] 'a multitude of thrashing floors or wicked men', @deva[कुडुम्बिनी] 'a number of families'. @deva[डाकिनी] 'a host of female goblins or imps,' @deva[शाकिनी], &c.

### @deva[इमन्] ( @deva[इमनिच्] )

forms abstract nouns when added to the words @deva[पृथु], @deva[मृदु], @deva[महत], @deva[तनु], @deva[पटु], @deva[लघु], @deva[बहु], @deva[साधु], @deva[आशु], @deva[उरु], @deva[गुरु], @deva[बहुल], @deva[खण्ड], @deva[दण्ड], @deva[अकिंचन], @deva[चण्ड], @deva[बाल], @deva[वत्स], @deva[होड], @deva[पाक], @deva[मन्द], @deva[स्वादु], @deva[ह्रस्व], @deva[दीर्घ], @deva[प्रिय], @deva[वृष], @deva[ऋजु], @deva[क्षिप्र], @deva[क्षुद्र], @deva[अणु], @deva[दृढ], @deva[वृढ], @deva[परिवृढ], @deva[कृश], @deva[भृश], @deva[वक्र], @deva[शीत], @deva[उष्ण], @deva[जड], @deva[बधिर], @deva[मधुर], @deva[पण्डित], @deva[मूर्ख], @deva[मूक], @deva[स्थिर], and words expressive of colour; before this term these undergo the same changes as before the @deva[ईयस्] of the comparative and are always *mas.*; as @deva[प्रथिमा] 'greatness', @deva[म्रदिमा] 'softness' @deva[तनिमा], 'thinness' @deva[पटिमा] 'dexterity,' sharpness.' &c.; @deva[बालिमा], @deva[वत्सिमा], @deva[होडिमा], @deva[पाकिमा] 'childhood'; @deva[ह्रसिमा], @deva[क्षोदिमा], @deva[द्रढिमा], @deva[उष्णिमा], @deva[क्राशिमा], @deva[जडिमा], @deva[शुक्लिमा], &c.

### @deva[इय] ( @deva[घ] )

—this is added to

**(1)** @deva[क्षत्र] in the sense of born of the race of; @deva[क्षत्रियः];

**(2)** @deva[राष्ट्र] in the sense of 'relating to'; @deva[राष्ट्रियः];

**(3)** @deva[महेन्द्र] in the sense of 'an oblation offered to'; @deva[महेन्द्रियः हविः];

**(4)** @deva[अग्र]; @deva[अग्रियः] 'foremost.'

### @deva[ईक] ( @deva[ईकक्] )

—is added to @deva[शक्ति] and @deva[यष्टि] in the sense of 'striking with'; @deva[शक्त्या प्रहरतीति शाक्तीकः]; 'a spearman' ( also @deva[शाक्तिकः] ); @deva[यष्टीकः] 'one who strikes with a stick'.

### @deva[ईन्] ( @deva[ख], @deva[खञ्] )

**(1)** added to @deva[कुल] and words ending in @deva[कुल] it means 'born of', @deva[कुले जातः कुलीनः] or @deva[कौलीनः] 'of a good family,' @deva[आढ्यकुलीनः—कौलीनः] 'sprung from a noble and rich family'; it is added to

**(2)** the words @deva[पार] and @deva[अवार] separately or taken together in any order in the sense of 'going'; as @deva[पारं गामीति पारीणः] 'going over to the other bank'; ( when used at the end of a comp. it means, versed or adept in; vide Bhaṭṭ. II.46 ). @deva[अवारीणः] 'coming over to this side ( of a river, &c. );' @deva[पारावारीणः] 'one who goes to both sides or one who crosses the sea'; @deva[अवारपारीणः] 'crossing a river,' &c.

**(3)** the word @deva[ग्राम] in the sense of 'rustic' as @deva[ग्रामीणः];

**(4)** the words @deva[आत्मन्], @deva[विश्वजन] and words ending in @deva[भोग] in the sense of 'beneficial to;' @deva[आत्मने हित आत्मनीनः]; @deva[विश्वजनीनः]; @deva[मातृभोगीणः] 'fit to be enjoyed by a mother'; @deva[पितृभोगीणः] &c.
