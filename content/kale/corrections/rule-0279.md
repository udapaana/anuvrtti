The following Avya. compounds are anomalous[^1]:—

@deva[तिष्ठन्ति गावः यस्मिन् काले स तिष्ठद्गु दोहनकालः] 'the time when cows stand for being milked;' see Bhaṭṭi. IV. 14. So @deva[वहद्गु] 'the time when cows conceive or bulls bear the plough; @deva[आयत्याः गावः अस्मिन् काले आयतीगवम्] 'the evening time,' 'the time when the cows come home'; @deva[खलेयवम्], 'the time when the barley is on the thrashing ground;' similarly @deva[खलेबुसम्]; @deva[लूनयवम्] 'when barley is reaped,' @deva[लूयमानयवम्], @deva[संहृतयवम्], &c. @deva[समभूमि] 'when the ground is even;' @deva[समपदाति] 'when the foot-soldiers are in right lines,; @deva[सुषमम्], @deva[विषमम्], @deva[अपसमम्] at the end of the year; @deva[आयतीसमम्], @deva[पापसमम्] in an evil year; @deva[पुण्यसमम्], @deva[प्राह्णम्], @deva[प्ररथम्] 'when the chariots move forth,' @deva[प्रमृगम्] 'when the deer comes,' @deva[विमृगम्], @deva[प्रदक्षिणम्], @deva[संप्रति] and @deva[असंप्रति].

**N. B.**—According to all followers of Pân. compounds of this group are precluded from being further compounded though poets do not seem to respect the prohibition except in so far as to place such words at the end of these comps.; *cf.* @deva[प्रदक्षिणक्रियार्हायाम्] । Rag. I. 76; also IV. 25, VII. 24, &c.

[^1]: @deva[तिष्ठद्गुप्रभृतीनि च] । Pân. II. 1. 17.
