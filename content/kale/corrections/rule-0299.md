The vowel of the first member is lengthened in the following cases:—[^1]

**(a)** When the nouns derived from the roots @deva[नह्], @deva[वृत्], @deva[वृष्], @deva[व्यध्], @deva[रुच्], @deva[सह्], and @deva[तन्] with the aff. @deva[क्विप्] ( o ) follow prepositions termed Gati or nouns which they govern; @deva[उपानह्], @deva[नीवृत्] an inhabited country, a realm, @deva[प्रावृद्] the rainy season, @deva[मर्मावित्] piercing the vitals; so @deva[मृगावित्] a hunter ( *cf.* Bhaṭṭi II. 7 ). @deva[नीरुक्], @deva[अभीरुक्], @deva[ऋतीषद्] overpowering an assailant @deva[परीतत्]. But @deva[परिणहनम्] as @deva[नह्] is not followed by *Kvip.*

**(b)** When followed by the aff. @deva[वल], and the whole is a name; @deva[कृषीवलः] a husbandman.

**(c)** Words containing more than two vowels when followed by @deva[मत्] ( @deva[वत्] ) except in the case of @deva[अजिर], @deva[खदिर], @deva[पुलिन], @deva[हंस], @deva[कारण्डव] and @deva[चक्रवाक] and the whole is a name; @deva[अमरावती] and @deva[इरावती] which are names; but @deva[अजिरवती]; @deva[व्रीहिमती], @deva[वलयवती] which is not a name. Also in the case of @deva[शर], @deva[वंश], @deva[धूम], @deva[अहि], @deva[कपि], @deva[मुनि], @deva[शुचि], and @deva[हनु]; @deva[शरावती] &c.

**(d)** When a verbal derivative with the aff. @deva[अ] ( @deva[घञ्] ) follows a preposition, in most cases, when the comp. does not signify a human being; @deva[परिपाकः]-@deva[परीपाकः]; but @deva[निषादः] one of a mountain-tribe. Similarly @deva[प्रति-ती-वंशः]; @deva[प्रति-ती-कारः] &c.

**(e)** When @deva[काश] follows a preposition ending in @deva[इ]; @deva[वीकाशः], @deva[नीकाशः]; but @deva[प्रकाशः]

**(f)** When @deva[अष्टन्] is the first, and @deva[नर] the second member of a compound, and the whole is a name; @deva[अष्टापदम्] gold, @deva[अष्टापदः] a spider; but @deva[अष्टपुत्रः], @deva[विश्वानरः] an epithet of Savitṛi.

**(g)** When @deva[मित्र] is the latter member and the whole is the name of a ṛishi; @deva[विश्वामित्रः], but @deva[विश्वमित्रो माणवकः] ।
