key: rule:685
title: "Seṭ Roots and Derivative Bases Admitting @deva[इ]"
pages: [421.png, 422.png]
verdict: approved
---
Seṭ roots (subject to @ref[684]), and all derivative bases admit of @deva[इ]; roots of the Tenth Class and causals reject their final @deva[अय], Par. Frequentatives their final @deva[अ], and Ātm. Frequentatives their @deva[य]:—

| Roots. | P. P. Ptc. | | Roots. | P. P. Ptc. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| @deva[शङ्क्] | @deva[शङ्कित] 'suspected.' | | @deva[कथ्] | @deva[कथित] 'told.' |
| @deva[वद्] | @deva[उदित] 'spoken.' | | @deva[प्रथ्] | @deva[प्रथित] 'spread.' |

| Roots. | P. P. Ptc. | | Roots. | P. P. Ptc. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| @deva[एध्] | @deva[एधित] 'increased.' | | @deva[बोधय्] cau. Base of @deva[बुध्], @deva[बोधित] 'made to know.' |
| @deva[कम्प्] | @deva[कम्पित] 'shaken.' | | @deva[चिकीर्ष्] des. b. of @deva[कृ], @deva[चिकीर्षित] 'desired to do.' |
| @deva[मुष्] | @deva[मुषित] 'stolen; deceived.' | | @deva[बोबुध्य] Ātm. Fre. Base of @deva[बुध्], @deva[बोबुधित] 'frequently known.' |
| @deva[ग्रह्] | @deva[गृहीत] 'taken.' | | @deva[बोभूय]—Par. Fre. Base of @deva[भू], @deva[बोभूयित]. |

**Exceptions:—**@deva[इन्ध्], @deva[ऋष्], 'to go, to kill,' &c., @deva[चित्] 'to know, to observe,' &c., @deva[जुष्], @deva[त्रस्], @deva[दीप्], @deva[मद्] and @deva[यत्]; @deva[इद्ध], @deva[ऋष्ट], @deva[चित्त], @deva[जुष्ट], @deva[त्रस्त], @deva[दीप्त], @deva[मत्त], @deva[यत्त].

N. B. There are many Seṭ roots which reject the intermediate @deva[इ] but as some of them form their P. P. participles in @deva[न] and others irregularly, they will be given in their proper places.
