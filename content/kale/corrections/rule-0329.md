---
key: rule:329
title: "Feminine of Limb-Denoting Compounds"
pages: [191.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] When a compound has for its second member a word denoting a limb of the body of an animal and having no conjunct consonant preceding its final vowel, the *fem.* is formed by the addition of @deva[आ] or @deva[ई]; @deva[अतिकेशा-शी]; 'a woman having abundant hair;' @deva[सुकेशा-शी], @deva[चंद्रमुखा-खी]; but @deva[सुगुल्फा] having beautiful ankles; @deva[सुस्तनी] or @deva[सुस्तना] ( @deva[स्त्री वा प्रतिमा वा] ; ) @deva[सुमुखा शाला] 'an edifice with a beautiful front'.

**(a)** [^2] If the word, denoting the limb, however, be one of the words @deva[क्रोड], @deva[नख], @deva[खुर], @deva[उखा], @deva[शिखा], @deva[बाल], @deva[शफ], @deva[शुक], @deva[भुज], @deva[कर] &c. or have more than two syllables, the *fem.* is formed in @deva[आ] alone; @deva[कल्याणक्रोडा] ( @deva[अश्वानामुरः क्रोडा] Sid. Kau. ), @deva[पृथुजघना], having large hips; @deva[चटुलनयना], &c.

**(b)** [^3] Also when the first member is one of @deva[स], @deva[सह], or @deva[विद्यमान], the *fem.* is formed in @deva[आ] necessarily; @deva[सकेशा], @deva[अकेशा], @deva[विद्यमाननासिका], @deva[सहनासिका] &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[स्वाङ्गाच्चोपसर्जनादसंयोगोपधात्] । Pân. IV. 1. 54.

[^2]: @deva[न क्रोडादिबह्वचः] । Pân. IV. 1. 56.

[^3]: @deva[सहनञ्विद्यमानपूर्वाच्च] । Pân. IV. 1. 57.
