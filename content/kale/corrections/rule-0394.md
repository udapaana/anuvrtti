The[^1] @deva[ॠ] (long) of a root, penultimate or final, when it does not take Guṇa or Vṛddhi, is changed to @deva[इर्], and to @deva[उर्] if a labial or @deva[व्] precedes; and the @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] is lengthened when @deva[इर्] or @deva[उर्] is followed by a consonant; e. g. @deva[जॄ] 4. P. 'to become old,' @deva[जीर्यति], @deva[अजीर्यत्], &c.; @deva[कॄ] 6. P. @deva[किरति], @deva[अकिरत्], &c.; here @deva[कि] does not become long as @deva[किर्] is followed by @deva[अ]; @deva[कृत्] 10. P. A. @deva[कीर्तयति-ते], @deva[अकीर्तयत्-त], &c.

[^1]: @deva[ऋत इद्धातोः] । @deva[उरण् रपरः] । Pāṇ. VII. 1. 100, I. 1. 51. @deva[हलि च] Pāṇ. VII. 2. 77.
