---
key: rule:95
title: "Special Rules for @deva[ह्] Changes"
pages: [056.png]
verdict: approved
---
**(a)** @deva[ह्] is changed to @deva[ढ्] when at the end of a *pada* or when followed by a consonant except a nasal or a semi-vowel. **(b)** In the case of roots beginning with @deva[द्] it is changed to @deva[घ्] under the same circumstances; **(c)** when forming part of the roots @deva[द्रुह्], @deva[मुह्], @deva[स्नुह्] and @deva[स्निह्], it is optionally changed to @deva[घ्] i. e. to @deva[ढ्] or @deva[घ्]; **(d)** and in the case of the root @deva[नह्] it is changed to @deva[ध्] under the above-mentioned circumstances.[^1]

**(e)** The @deva[ह्] of @deva[उष्णिह्] f. a kind of metre is changed to @deva[क्] when followed by a hard consonant or by nothing, and to @deva[ग्] before a soft consonant (Pāṇ. III. 2, 59).

---

[^1]: @deva[हो ढः । दादेर्धातोर्घः । वा द्रुहमुहष्णुहष्णिहाम् । नहो धः] — Pāṇ. VIII. 2. 31-34.
