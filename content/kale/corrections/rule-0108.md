## Bases ending in @deva[स्]:—

The penultimate @deva[अ] of nouns ending in @deva[स्] is lengthened in the Nom. sing. if it does not belong to root nouns.[^1]

@deva[चन्द्रमस्] m. the moon.

Decline similarly @deva[वेधस्] god Brahmā, @deva[सुमनस्] good-minded, @deva[दुर्मनस्] ill-minded, @deva[उन्मनस्] anxious-hearted &c.

Decline similarly @deva[पयस्], milk, @deva[वयस्] age, @deva[अवस्] protection, fame &c. @deva[श्रेयस्] good, @deva[सरस्], @deva[वचस्], &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[अत्वसन्तस्य चाधातोः] — Pāṇ. VI. 4. 14. The penultimate vowel of nouns ending in @deva[मत्] or @deva[वत्] and in @deva[अस्] not belonging to a root is lengthened when followed by @deva[सु] (@deva[स्]) not belonging to the Vocative.
