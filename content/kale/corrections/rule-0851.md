## Genitive with Verbs of Affliction (Disease)

[^1] Verbs meaning 'to be afflicted with a disease' govern the Genitive of their object when used impersonally or when they have for their subject names of diseases; @deva[चौरस्य ज्वरस्य रुजा] the thief is afflicted with the pain of fever; @deva[पुरुषस्य रुजयत्यतिसारः] dysentery inflicts pain on the man; except when @deva[ज्वर] and @deva[संताप] are used as subjects; (vide Bhatti. VIII. 120;) @deva[तं रुजयति ज्वरः] or @deva[संतापः] fever or affliction pains him.

[^1]: @deva[रुजार्थानां भाववचनानामज्वरेः ।] Pāṇ. II. 3. 54. @deva[अज्वरिसंताप्योरिति वाच्यम्] ; Vārt.
