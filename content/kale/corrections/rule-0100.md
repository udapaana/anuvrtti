The @deva[वा] of root-nouns ending in @deva[वाह्] is changed to @deva[ऊ] before the vowel terminations beginning with the Acc. plu.[^1]; e. g. @deva[विश्ववाह्] m. the sustainer of the universe, a lord:—

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. V. | @deva[विश्ववाट्-ड्] | @deva[विश्ववाहौ] | @deva[विश्ववाहः] |
| A. | @deva[विश्ववाहम्] | ,, | @deva[विश्वौहः] |
| I. | @deva[विश्वौहा] | @deva[विश्ववाड्भ्याम्] | @deva[विश्वशाड्भिः] |
| D. | @deva[विश्वौहे] | ,, | @deva[विश्ववाड्भ्यः] |
| Ab. | @deva[विश्वौहः] | ,, | ,, |
| G. | ,, | @deva[विश्वौहोः] | @deva[विश्वौहाम्] |
| L. | @deva[विश्वौहि] | ,, | @deva[विश्ववाट्सु] |

Decline similarly @deva[हव्यवाह्], @deva[भारवाह्], @deva[भूवाह्], @deva[श्वेतवाह्] &c. Acc. plu., Inst. sing. &c. of @deva[भूवाह्], are @deva[भूहः], @deva[भूहा] &c.

[^1]: @deva[वाह ऊठ् । सप्रसारणाच्च] — Pāṇ. VI. 4. 132. VI. 1. 108. @deva[आ] and @deva[ऊ] combine into @deva[औ] by Pāṇ. VI. 1. 89. See @ref[20].
