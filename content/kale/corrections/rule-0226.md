---
key: rule:226
title: "@deva[ईषत्] 'Little' in Compounds"
pages: [140.png]
verdict: approved
---
The word @deva[ईषत्] 'little' is compounded with any other word except a verbal derivative, and even with a verbal derivative when it expresses a quality;[^1] as @deva[ईषत्पिङ्गलः] 'a little brown', @deva[ईषद्रक्तम्] 'reddish,' &c.

---

[^1]: @deva[ईषदकृता ।] Pāṇ. II. 2. 7. @deva[ईषद्गुणवचनेनेति वाच्यम् ।] Vārt.
