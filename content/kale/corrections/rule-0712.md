## (1) Participles in @deva[तव्य] and @deva[अनीय].

The affixes @deva[तव्य] or @deva[अनीय] are added to roots or derivative bases in the sense of 'must be fit to be,' &c. Before these the ending vowel and the penultimate short of a root take their Guṇa substitute. Before @deva[तव्य] Seṭ roots take @deva[इ], Aniṭ roots do not, and Veṭ roots take it optionally. Before @deva[अनीय] penultimate @deva[ऋ] is always changed to @deva[अर्] and not to @deva[र्] (as it sometimes does); e. g.

| Roots. | | Pot. Ptc. |
|---|---|---|
| @deva[दा]— | @deva[दातव्य], @deva[दानीय] | what must or ought to be given. |
| @deva[चि]— | @deva[चेतव्य], @deva[चयनीय] | what must or ought to be searched into, &c. |
| @deva[नी]— | @deva[नेतव्य], @deva[नयनीय] | ,, ,, carried. |
| @deva[श्रु]— | @deva[श्रोतव्य], @deva[श्रवणीय] | ,, ,, heard. |
| @deva[भू]— | @deva[भवितव्य], @deva[भवनीय] | ,, ,, been. |
| @deva[कृ]— | @deva[कर्तव्य], @deva[करणीय] | ,, ,, done. |
| @deva[बुध्]— | @deva[बोधितव्य], @deva[बोधनीय], @deva[बोद्धव्य] | ,, ,, known. |
| @deva[मुच्]— | @deva[मोक्तव्य], @deva[मोचनीय] | ,, ,, released. |
| @deva[मृज्]— | @deva[मार्ष्टव्य][^1], @deva[मार्जनीय] | ,, ,, wiped. |
| @deva[सृज्]— | @deva[स्रष्टव्य], @deva[सर्जनीय] | ,, ,, created. |
| @deva[भ्रस्ज्]— | @deva[भर्ष्टव्य], @deva[भर्जनीय], @deva[भ्रष्टव्य], @deva[भ्रज्जनीय] | ,, ,, fried. |
| @deva[भिद्]— | @deva[भेत्तव्य], @deva[भेदनीय] | ,, ,, split. |
| @deva[निन्द्]— | @deva[निन्दितव्य], @deva[निन्दनीय] | ,, ,, censured. |
| @deva[गुह्]— | @deva[गोढव्य], @deva[गूहनीय], @deva[गूहितव्य][^2] | ,, ,, concealed. |

[^1]: The @deva[ऋ] of @deva[मृज्] is changed to @deva[आर्] against § 712.

[^2]: The @deva[उ] of @deva[गुह्] is lengthened instead of being Guṇated before a strong termination beginning with a vowel.
