## 1. Masculine Words

Verbal derivatives formed by the affixes @deva[अ] and @deva[न], and @deva[इ] added to the roots @deva[दा] and @deva[धा]; *e. g.* @deva[पाकः], @deva[त्यागः], @deva[करः], @deva[गरः] any beverage or drink, poison, @deva[गोचरः] range, scope, @deva[यज्ञः], @deva[विघ्नः], @deva[आधिः] mental pain or anguish, @deva[निधिः] a treasure, &c.

**Exceptions:**—@deva[याञ्चा] *fem.* and @deva[भय], @deva[लिङ्ग], and @deva[भग] all *neu.*
