The *Feminine* of @deva[नृ] and @deva[नर] is @deva[नारी] and that of words included in the *Shârṅgaravâdi* ( @deva[शार्ङ्गरवादिगण] ) group[^1] is formed by adding @deva[ई]; as @deva[शार्ङ्गरवी], @deva[गौतमी], @deva[आतिथेयी], @deva[आशोकेयी], @deva[बैदी], @deva[पुत्री], &c.

---

[^1]: This group consists of the following words—@deva[शार्ङ्गरव], @deva[कापटव], @deva[ब्राह्मण], @deva[गौतम], @deva[आतिथेय], @deva[आशोकेय], @deva[वात्स्यायन], @deva[मौआयन], @deva[शैव्य], @deva[आश्मरथ्य], @deva[चाण्डाल], @deva[पुत्र] and some others not very important.
