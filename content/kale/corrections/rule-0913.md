[^1]The infinitive is used with verbs and verbal nouns meaning 'to wish or desire' provided the agent of it is the same as that of the verb; @deva[को हर्तुमिच्छति हरेः दंष्ट्राम्] (Mud. I.) who wishes to snatch away the jaws of the lion; @deva[माधुर्यं मधुबिन्दुना रचयितुं क्षाराम्बुधेरीहते;] Bhartṛ. II. 6. But @deva[तमेतत्कर्तुमहमिच्छामि] I wish him to do this, is wrong.
