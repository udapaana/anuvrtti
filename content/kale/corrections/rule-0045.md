## Visargasandhi

@deva[स्] at the end of a word followed by any letter or not, the @deva[ष्] of @deva[सजुष्] and @deva[र्] followed by a hard consonant or by nothing, are changed to a *Visarga*; as @deva[रामः पठति] Rāma reads. @deva[पितर्] = @deva[पितः] oh father. @deva[भ्रातुः कन्यका] a brother's daughter.
