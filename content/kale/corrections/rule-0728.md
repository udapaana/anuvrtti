@deva[भृ] 1. cl. takes this affix when the participle so derived does not form a name; @deva[भृत्याः] 'those who ought to be nourished or maintained, hence servants,' &c; but @deva[भार्याः] (@deva[भृ+ण्यत्]) 'a class of Kshatriyas;' when @deva[सम्] precedes @deva[भृ], @deva[क्यप्] and @deva[ण्यत्] are added optionally @deva[संभृत्याः] or @deva[संभार्याः].

*N. B.* The word @deva[भार्या] meaning 'a wife' ought to be derived from @deva[भृ] of the 3rd class and affix @deva[ण्यत्].
