title: "Impersonal Use of Past Passive Participles"
pages: [510.png]
verdict: approved
---
The past passive participles are often used impersonally, the agent being put in the Instrumental case; @deva[प्रद्युतितं] or @deva[प्रद्योतितं सूर्येण] it is shone by the sun; @deva[जितं पुत्रप्रेम्णा] victorious (all powerful) is the affection for sons; @deva[पण्डितायितं तत्रभवता] he showed his learning; @deva[प्रमुदितं] or @deva[प्रमोदितं साधुना], &c.
