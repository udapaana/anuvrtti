---
key: rule:264
title: "Mandatory @deva[क] for Certain Feminine Endings"
pages: [161.png]
verdict: approved
---
But if the last word of a Bah. compound be a *fem.* noun ending in @deva[ई] or @deva[ऊ] not capable of being changed into @deva[इय्] or @deva[उव्] before the vowel case termination, or if it ends in @deva[ऋ], @deva[क] is necessarily affixed; @deva[ईश्वरः कर्ता यस्य तद् ईश्वरकर्तृकं जगत्], @deva[बहुनदीको देशः], @deva[रूपवती वधूः यस्य स रूपवद्वधूकः], &c.; but @deva[सुधीः स्त्री] is an exception; @deva[बहुस्त्रीकः], @deva[सस्त्रीकः] &c.
