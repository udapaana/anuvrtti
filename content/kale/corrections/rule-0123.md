Irregular Bases.

The first five forms of @deva[पथिन्] a road, @deva[मथिन्] a churning handle, and @deva[ऋभुक्षिन्] name of Indra are irregular.[^1] They drop their @deva[इन्] before vowel terminations beginning with the Acc. plu.

@deva[पथिन्].

| | Sing. | Dual | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| N. V. | @deva[पन्थाः] | @deva[पन्थानौ] | @deva[पन्थानः] |
| A. | @deva[पन्थानम्] | ,, | @deva[पथः] |
| I. | @deva[पथा] | @deva[पथिभ्याम्] | @deva[पथिभिः] |
| D. | @deva[पथे] | ,, | @deva[पथिभ्यः] |
| Ab. | @deva[पथः] | @deva[पथिभ्याम्] | @deva[पथिभ्यः] |
| G. | @deva[पथः] | @deva[पथोः] | @deva[पथाम्] |
| L. | @deva[पथि] | ,, | @deva[पथिषु] |

Decline similarly @deva[मथिन्] and @deva[ऋभुक्षिन्]; the latter word, however, does not insert the @deva[न्] in the first five inflections; e. g. Nom. @deva[मन्थाः] @deva[मन्थानौ] @deva[मन्थानः], @deva[ऋभुक्षाः-क्षाणौ-क्षाणः]; Acc. @deva[मन्थानम्] @deva[मन्थानौ-मथः], @deva[ऋभुक्षाणम्-ऋभुक्षाणौ-ऋभुक्षः]
