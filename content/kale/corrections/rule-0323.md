title: "@deva[ऐ] Before @deva[ई] for Specific Words"
pages: [189.png]
verdict: approved
---
[^1] The *fem.* of @deva[वृषाकपि], Vishnu or Shiva, @deva[अग्नि], @deva[कुसित] or @deva[कुसिद] 'a usurer' is formed in @deva[ई] before which the final vowel of these is changed to @deva[ऐ]; @deva[वृषाकपायी], @deva[अग्नायी], @deva[कुसितायी] or @deva[कुसिदायी].

---

[^1]: @deva[वृषाकप्यग्निकुसितकुसिदानामुदात्तः] । Pân. IV. 1. 37.
