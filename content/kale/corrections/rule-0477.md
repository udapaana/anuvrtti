@deva[वी] is substituted for @deva[अज्] 1 P. 'to go' necessarily before any non-conjugational termination, and optionally before such as begin with any consonant except @deva[य्]; @deva[वेता], @deva[अजिता]; @deva[वेष्यति], @deva[अजिष्यति] &c.
